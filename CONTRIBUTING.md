# Contributing to Kenshi Wiki

## Suggest a correction

[Open the article correction form](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/issues/new?template=article-correction.yml) and sign in to GitHub. Paste the page link or title and tell us what you would change. A maintainer can make the edit. A typo and a one-sentence correction are both welcome.

For game mechanics, tell us what you observed. The game version, whether mods were enabled, a source, or steps another player could try can help. [teppuM’s notes on Skeleton Repair Beds and the Robotics job](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/pull/8/files) show how a small edit can document a repeatable observation.

## Edit an article in your browser

1. Open the article on [kenshi.wiki](https://kenshi.wiki/) and choose **Edit on GitHub**. On a small screen, look under **Wiki tools**. Sign in and, if GitHub offers to fork the repository, accept to create your own working copy.
2. Change the relevant words in the article. Most articles are Markdown. Keep the page metadata and existing source credits, and use **Preview** to read your edit.
3. Choose **Commit changes** or **Propose changes**, give your edit a short title, and follow GitHub’s prompt to open a pull request against `mattresspad/kenshi-wiki` on `main`. Say what you corrected and include a source if it would help another editor check the change.

GitHub runs the site checks. A maintainer reviews the wording and can help with formatting or build errors.

## Reuse a wiki article

Building something for Kenshi players? We welcome independent projects that link to the wiki or reuse its articles under the license shown on each page.

When you reuse article text, link back to the article and its license, keep its contributor and source credits, and tell readers what you changed. On pages adapted from the Kenshi Fandom wiki, those credits lead back to the original revision and its authors. See [Licenses and reuse](https://kenshi.wiki/contributors#licensing) for the terms that apply to your version.

## Work on the website

For a problem with layout, navigation, search, or other site behavior, [open a website issue](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/issues/new?template=website.yml). Include the page and what you expected to happen. A screenshot can help with visual changes.

If you already have a fix, open a pull request with the affected page and how you checked it. The [README](README.md#develop) covers local development, the article import tools, and release checks. The [Contributors page](https://kenshi.wiki/contributors#licensing) explains the credits and licenses for text, software, and artwork.
