**Traders Guards** are members of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) faction, acting as a basic security force. They can be found in the [Bank](/bank) and the Trader's Guild HQ. If [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) takeover, they can be found under their faction instead.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by Catun Scrapmaster.
