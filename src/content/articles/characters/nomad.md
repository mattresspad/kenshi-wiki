**Nomads** are members of the [Nomads](/nomads) faction. They can be found in [Settled Nomads](/settled-nomads) and [Settled Nomad Village](/settled-nomad-village).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
