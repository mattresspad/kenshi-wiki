**Rane the Giant** is a former [Hundred Guardian](/hundred-guardian) and [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She was named by the Shek leader, the [Stone Golem](/stone-golem). Her position was challenged, and she lost the duel, lowering her status in Shek society.

She can be found in either a [Squin](/squin), [Admag](/admag) or [Last Stand](/last-stand) bar and it costs c.7,500 to recruit her.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
