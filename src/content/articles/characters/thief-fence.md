**Thief Fences** are members of the [Shinobi Thieves.](/shinobi-thieves) They can be found in random [Bars](/bars) within cities. They will trade with the player even if you are not part of their faction. Everything they buy and sell is marked down 50% no matter your relationship with the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander

-   Scorchlander

-   Hive Prince
