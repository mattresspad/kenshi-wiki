**Swamp Ninja Chunin** are members of the [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas). They can be found in the [Swamp Ninja Base](/swamp-ninja-base) or wandering with a squad of [Swamp Ninja Genin](/swamp-ninja-genin) in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) and the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Statistics

-   Greenlander

-   Scorchlander

-   Hive Prince
