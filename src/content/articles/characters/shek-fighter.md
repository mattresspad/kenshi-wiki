Shek Fighters are lone characters of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) wandering the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert). They have no notable dialog or interactions.

They have a "duel wandering fighter starter" dialogue and a "not fearful" personality.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (25% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Trivia

-   Originally, they were going to have a duel event - where the character would challenge you to a duel, and if accepted it would increase your relations with the Shek Kingdom. The event was scrapped though, and mostly reworked into the [Shek Warrior](/shek-warrior)'s dialogue.
