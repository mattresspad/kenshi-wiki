**Ronin Weapon Traders** are unnamed members of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) or [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) depending on where the city their [Ronin Weapon Shop](/ronin-weapon-shop) is located. They can also be members of [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction) if they reside in the [Mongrel Weapon Shop](/mongrel-weapon-shop). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
