Catun is a fortified [major town](/major-towns) in the [Bonefields](/bonefields), controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities). The small, isolated town stands on a plateau in the middle of a valley. Its watchtowers overlook the surrounding wildlife.

[Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro) represents the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) here. His fate can change who holds the town and whether its buildings survive. [See the Fishmen takeover](#town-override).

## Location and defences

Explore the Bonefields or read the **[Map of the United Cities (Extended Territories)](/map-of-the-united-cities-extended-territories)** to find Catun. Outpost s-IIIs serve as wall towers. Samurai with polearms regularly defend the town against [skin spiders](/skin-spider), [boneyard wolves](/boneyard-wolf), [garrus](/garru) and [beak things](/beak-thing). One or two members of the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones-character) occasionally turn up. The imported account describes the local samurai as able to overwhelm these intruders.

## Fishmen takeover

If [Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro) is killed or imprisoned, the [Fishmen](/fishmen) take over Catun. **Every building is destroyed** in this [world state](/world-states).
