## Amplified Generators 2

## Details

### **Estimated Time**

6 Hours

### **Tech Level**

5

### **Costs**

2 [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book)

### **Requirements**

[Fuel Generator Theory](/fuel-generator-theory-tech), Amplified Generators

## Results

### **Improvements**

+15 Power to [Generator II](/generator-ii)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>A further 15kw upgrade to all generators.</blockquote><figcaption>In-game description</figcaption></figure>

**Amplified Generators** are [Electrics Tech](/electrics-tech) which instantly upgrades [Power](/power) generators for all of the player's outposts.

Contrary to the description, this upgrade only applies towards the 2nd version of fuel generator.
