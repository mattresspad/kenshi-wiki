**Double-Barrel Turret Spotlight Fitting** is [Defense Tech](/defense-tech) which allows players to construct [Double-Barrel Harpoon Turrets](/double-barrel-harpoon-turret) with [Lighting](/lighting). Having proper lighting at night improves accuracy when using ranged weapons.
