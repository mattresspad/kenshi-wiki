**Fishmen**, also known as **Gurglers**, are fishlike abominations who live on an island south of [Catun](/catun) called [Fishman Island](/fishman-island). Their leader, [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler), can be found in the south-westernmost corner of the island in the [Island Lab](/island-lab). **Fishmen** control and infest the ruins of the city [Fish Isle](/fish-isle) which is located in the center of the island.

Despite being relatively weak on their own, they should not be underestimated when in large numbers (much akin to [Fogmen](/fogmen)). Just like the Fogmen, they will attempt to carry injured organic characters and bring them back to their nests, where they will be tied to a pole and eaten alive by them.

Gurglers presumably hatch from [Gurgler Eggs](/gurgler-egg) and sleep in Fish Beds. They stop spawning in the Fishman Island once the King Gurgler is killed.

## Lore

The game never explains where the Fishmen come from. None of their race, faction, character or location records has a description, and no book mentions them. What the game does say comes from a few items and from what other characters say about them.

-   The King Gurgler's [wanted poster](/wanted-posters) calls him "neither man nor animal", "an enormous beast with more similarity to a hiveman-crossed-crab, ten-fold in size", wanted for the kidnapping and cannibalism of [Catun](/catun) residents.
-   The [Gurgler Egg](/gurgler-egg) is "a creepy Fishman egg, stolen from the Gurglers."
-   Fishmen never speak. Every line they have is a random noise such as "GLURRRGH", "\*squelch\*", "\*chomp\*" or "\*gargle\*".
-   When the player's squad first enters [Fishman Island](/fishman-island), its members can talk about the island:
    -   [Horse](/horse) or [Shryke](/shryke): "Pretty sure the Tech Hunters cleared the island out long ago though, last I heard." Later: "For one, they don't have ears so I'll talk all I want."
    -   [Sadneil](/sadneil): "Ugh, not like I haven't seen a million gurglers before... Still, I've had better conversations with them than I have with most humans, I'll give them that." The squad leader answers: "I thought they could only make gurgling sounds, totally incapable of intelligible speech?" Sadneil: "Correct."
    -   [Lumi](/lumi): "Nonsense, let me handle those fish men... I'm fluent in crustacean, you know."
-   When the squad first enters the [Bonefields](/bonefields), a [Skeleton](/skeleton) can say "Not surprised with all the beak things around. And that immortal fish guy." While the King Gurgler is alive, a [Greenlander](/greenlander) or [Shek](/shek) asks "'Immortal fish guy'? What?", the Skeleton warns "Don't go to the south", and a [Hive](/hive) member asks "I am interested in the fish guy. Is he made of fish?" Once he is dead, the squad can instead remember "when we killed that giant fishman king", and a Greenlander adds: "We won't see many more of those fishmen gurgler things raiding the south cost now".

## Trivia

-   In the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set), aside from the racial groups Fishman and Alpha Fishman, there is an unused racial group called King Fishman. Most likely this was intended to be used for the King Gurgler but was discarded in favor of making the character an Alpha Fishman.
    -   King Fishman is far tougher than Alpha Fishman: 400 blood, 600 health for the head, chest, stomach and each arm, and 800 for each leg. Its legs are also hit three times as often (a hit chance of 30, against 10 for the other Fishmen). It uses the ordinary Fishman's head rather than the Alpha Fish Head.
-   The FCS also holds a second, unused Alpha Fishman race. It has the Alpha Fish Head but the ordinary Fishman's health (75 to 125 blood, 150 for each body part and 200 for each leg). An unused squad named Alpha Fishman, led by an [Alpha Gurgler](/alpha-gurgler), sits beside it.
-   Two more Fishmen squads in the FCS are never used: "Fish Squad!", made of [Gurglers](/gurgler), and "\_\_fish", which holds a single [Skimmer](/skimmer).
-   In the FCS, the [Gurgler Egg](/gurgler-egg)'s egg field names the [Crab](/crab), exactly as the [Crab Egg](/crab-egg)'s does, and the two eggs share the same material and the same value of 400. This suggests the Gurgler Egg began as a copy of the Crab Egg.
-   When fighting Fishmen if you were to severe one of their limbs, it would immediately become invisible. This is because they do not have a limb assigned to drop as an item.
    -   The following mod, **[Fishsticks - Gurgler Limb Mod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1752014748)**, adds Gurgler limbs to the game.
