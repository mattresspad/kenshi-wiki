**Copper Alloy Plates** are [items](/items) used in the construction of some [buildings](/category/buildings). They are crafted at [Copper Alloy Bench](/copper-alloy-bench) which can be constructed after researching [Copper Alloys](/copper-alloys-tech). Curiously, crafting these levels your [Engineer](/engineer) skill and not [Labouring](/labouring).

They can be found in many [ancient ruins](/ancient-ruins) in varying quantities.
