<div class="notice loading-tip" role="note"><span class="loading-tip-label visually-hidden">Loading screen tip:</span> If a character keeps food in his backpack then it will be automatically shared with the rest of the squad.</div>

**Backpacks** are carrying equipment worn on the back. Equipping one gives a character extra space and can reduce combat stats. Some bags also let the wearer stack [Trade Goods](/trade-goods) that would normally occupy separate slots.

Garru and Bull backpacks apply the stack multiplier directly. Trader and Wooden backpacks first raise smaller stacks to a minimum size (2 for Trader and 3 for Wooden), then multiply the stack size by 3.

Backpacks are obtained differently depending on their category. Normal Backpacks are widely for sale in [Travel and Repairs Shops](/travel-and-repairs-shop). Thieves Backpacks are sometimes found at normal Travel Shops but are also sold by [Shinobi Traders](/shinobi-trader). Animal Backpacks are sold by Travel Shops, [Farm Shops](/farm-shop) and some independent animal traders. [Tech Hunter Shops](/tech-hunter-shop) offer a large selection of backpacks. [Traders Backpack Large](/traders-backpack-large) and [Shopkeeper Goods Bag](/shopkeepers-goods-bag) are not for sale and can only be obtained by stealing one off of a shopkeeper's back.

[Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Mourn](/mourn) and [Squin](/squin) are good locations to find a large selection of backpacks in various shops.

Occasionally you can find some of the animal Backpacks, Scavenger Basket and the Wooden Backpacks inside of containers found in town ruins such as [Old Village](/old-village-empire-peasants), [Expedition 4](/expedition-4), [Expedition 5](/expedition-5), [Bast](/bast) and [Free Settlement](/free-settlement) to name a few.

## Loading screen tips

<div class="notice loading-tip" role="note"><span class="loading-tip-label visually-hidden">Loading screen tip:</span> Backpacks are a good way to carry more stuff, but the larger ones will hinder you in combat.</div>

<div class="notice loading-tip" role="note"><span class="loading-tip-label visually-hidden">Loading screen tip:</span> If you don't have a backpack, you can feed your animals by dropping some food on the floor nearby.</div>

<div class="notice loading-tip" role="note"><span class="loading-tip-label visually-hidden">Loading screen tip:</span> You need to eat to survive. Your character simply needs food in his inventory in order to stay fed. Food in a backpack will be shared among the squad members.</div>

## Trivia

-   The combat skill penalty which many backpacks have only reduces a unit’s attack and defense. Martial Arts and Dodge are unaffected. This means that a Martial Artist can wear a Thieves Backpack without having any of their stats reduced.
-   As having lower attack (than your opponent's defense) and defense (than your opponent's attack) multiplies the XP gain you receive in combat, the Large Backpack and Traders Backpack Large can speed up leveling massively.
-   You can hide items from smuggling checks by placing them inside of your Backpack and putting your Backpack in your inventory.
-   When a Backpack is dropped, items inside may become bugged and only sell for 1/4th of their normal value. If mousing over the items after selling them they will be labeled as "Stolen from \*player name\*". You can fix this bug by simply dropping the items on the ground and picking them back up. Just be sure if the items inside were originally stolen that you do so outside of a shop and away from prying eyes as looting stolen items inside a shop may cause NPCs to believe you just looted from their store.
-   If you drop a Backpack, you can loot it from the ground by holding down right click (without holding alt) and selecting loot.
    -   This can be very useful if you wish to train Strength (or Martial Arts by encumbering yourself) as you can stack many cheap heavy items like water inside of a Garru Backpack. A Garru Backpack filled with (96) water will weigh a massive 966 kg.
