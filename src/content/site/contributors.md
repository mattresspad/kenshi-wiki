**Prd ([mattresspad](https://github.com/mattresspad)) is Kenshi Wiki’s project lead and maintainer.** Prd founded the wiki, manages its domain and deployments, and directs the independent rebuild and its editorial work. Corrections and credited pull requests are welcome.

The articles retain their individual authors, original sources, licenses and revision histories. Kenshi Wiki’s Fandom contributors wrote much of the imported corpus. **[teppuM](https://github.com/teppuM)** contributed testing and corrections, including [Skeleton Repair Bed evidence](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/pull/8) and the [Blender plugins guide](/modding/blender-plugins). Additional authors and technical evidence are credited on the relevant articles.

To help, choose a task from [Pages needing work](/pages-needing-work), use the article’s correction link, or propose an edit to its actual FreshWiki file. Formatting acceptance and substantive editorial review are recorded separately. Bring bulk imports and architectural proposals to Prd before implementation.

<h2 id="outland">Historical prototype and Outland sources</h2>

[VlcekM](https://github.com/VlcekM) built the original Astro prototype and proposed RimWorld Wiki as its visual model. Prd subsequently developed its navigation, publishing and reader experience. This historical implementation is preserved at [revision 83abc179](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/tree/83abc179fafa63b826d7f2662c4a18a134a9e6c1).

The prototype’s Outland styling adapted legacy Vector and material from RimWorld Wiki. Trevor Parscal, Roan Kattouw and the other [Vector contributors](https://github.com/wikimedia/mediawiki-skins-Vector/tree/1e084256a129ca0e57a356c263808f98f3fdf3a4), MediaWiki contributors and RimWorld Wiki contributors retain their historical credits and notices. FreshWiki’s layouts and styling were written independently; the following links describe the archived prototype.

- [Prototype base and article stylesheet](/sources/outland.css.txt) and [prototype ribbon stylesheet](/sources/wiki-header.css.txt).
- [Historical Vector/MediaWiki source archive](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/83abc179fafa63b826d7f2662c4a18a134a9e6c1/public/notices/upstream-skin-source.zip).
- [Vector notice](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/83abc179fafa63b826d7f2662c4a18a134a9e6c1/public/notices/Vector-COPYING.txt), [MediaWiki notice](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/83abc179fafa63b826d7f2662c4a18a134a9e6c1/public/notices/MediaWiki-COPYING.txt), and [MediaWiki credits](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/83abc179fafa63b826d7f2662c4a18a134a9e6c1/public/notices/MediaWiki-CREDITS.txt).

<h2 id="artwork">Artwork and trademarks</h2>

Lo-Fi Games created Kenshi and the retained game artwork. Community project icons, platform marks, inventory images and the wiki’s line drawings have their individual sources recorded in the [artwork inventory](/notices/artwork.txt). GitHub’s mark uses [MIT-licensed Octicons artwork](/notices/Octicons-MIT.txt). Trademarks remain with their respective owners; unresolved reuse questions stay in the artwork record.

The About Kenshi images come from the prototype’s recorded Steam-art collection. The saddle guide retains Prd’s fitting and playtest evidence; the Transport example’s CC0 grant applies to its sample mesh and texture. See each article’s credits for its scope.
