- **Artwork, screenshots, icons and fonts:** have individual sources and notices in the [artwork inventory](/notices/artwork.txt). ## Permission records and open questions

A source record explains where material came from. A license or permission record explains the scope of an authorization. They are different: credit alone does not establish permission, and a missing permission record does not by itself establish infringement.

Some artwork records explicitly leave reuse terms unconfirmed. Those questions remain open; this guidance does not clear them or promise that every use has been resolved. Permission information is welcome at the contact address above. A grant for one file or purpose must not be extended to unrelated material or uses.

Noncommercial operation, online availability and AI-assisted redrawing do not automatically establish permission. Applicable legal exceptions may matter for a particular use; there is no site-wide assertion that all wiki material is fair use. The [U.S. Copyright Office’s fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html) explains the case-specific assessment.

## Report a concern or share permission information

Use the [private copyright, permission or removal form](/contact/copyright), or email [admin@kenshi.wiki](mailto:admin@kenshi.wiki), with the details you have:

- The affected wiki URL, image address or repository file.
- The underlying work and its creator or rights holder, with a source link where available.
- An explanation of the concern, correction or permission being offered, including its scope.
- A reply address so the maintainer can follow up.

Anyone may raise an informal concern; you do not need to prepare a formal legal notice to ask a question. You may also suggest a practical resolution. Please send sensitive details by email rather than posting them in a public issue or pull request.

## How concerns are reviewed

Prd reviews the identified material and available evidence, and may ask for clarification. Depending on the issue, the response may include clarifying a credit, recording permission, correcting material, seeking permission or removing particular content. Unresolved questions remain identified as such; a report is not automatically treated as a finding of infringement.

Source records, qualifications and resolution history are preserved. Public notes describe the issue and outcome without publishing private correspondence or personal contact details. Relevant information may need to be shared with a contributor, service provider or adviser when handling a concern; please share only the personal information needed for that purpose. There is no guaranteed response deadline.

This inbox is a general wiki contact and is not presented as a registered DMCA agent. Formal notices concerning material hosted by a provider follow that provider’s procedures. Direct contact with the wiki is not a prerequisite for using them: see [GitHub’s copyright process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy), [Cloudflare’s abuse reporting information](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/abuse-approach/) and the [Copyright Office’s notice-and-takedown guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/512/).

## Contribute with sources and credits

Keep each page’s recorded license, author credits, source revision and change notices. New original articles use CC BY-SA 4.0; edits to existing articles follow their recorded license. Independent website software contributions use MIT.

For imported text, quotations, code or media, include the creator, source, applicable reuse basis and necessary credit. A short source note is enough when the evidence is straightforward. If permission or compatibility is unclear, describe the proposed material and question before adding it; do not upload it merely to ask whether it can be used.

Ordinary typo and factual corrections need no additional rights declaration. The [contribution guide](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains browser edits and pull requests. Sensitive security concerns should continue through the separate [private security reporting route](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
