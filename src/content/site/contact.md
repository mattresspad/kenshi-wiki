Send **Prd, the wiki maintainer**, a private question, correction or proposed contribution. This contact represents Kenshi Wiki, not Meitou or other linked projects.

For copyright, permission or removal concerns, use the [copyright contact form](/contact/copyright). You can always email [admin@kenshi.wiki](mailto:admin@kenshi.wiki).

## Sources, credit and review

Include sources and your preferred credit when proposing material. Contributions remain subject to our [contribution guidance](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the page’s recorded license. New original articles use CC BY-SA 4.0; imported material retains its own terms. A submission is not automatically published.

[GitHub corrections](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/issues/new?template=article-correction.yml) remain available for public, routine changes. Use the separate [private security reporting route](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/main/SECURITY.md) for sensitive security concerns.
