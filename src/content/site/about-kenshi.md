**Kenshi** is a single-player, open-world game by **Lo-Fi Games**, combining squad control, role-playing and base building. You choose your own activities rather than follow a fixed story. [Lo-Fi Games’ overview](https://lofigames.com/about-kenshi/).

<h2 id="Gameplay">Gameplay</h2>

Start with [Getting Started](/getting-started), the [Controls](/controls), or the [Gameplay portal](/gameplay). The [Guides portal](/guides) collects longer player walkthroughs.

<h3 id="Squads_and_outposts">Squads and outposts</h3>

Control one character or a squad. Characters can train skills, research technologies and build a base. [Official feature overview](https://lofigames.com/about-kenshi/). Read about [recruitment](/unique-recruits), [jobs](/jobs), [construction](/construction) and [research](/technology).

<h3 id="Combat_and_injuries">Combat and injuries</h3>

Injuries affect movement and fighting; severe wounds can require treatment or robotic limb replacements. [Official medical-system description](https://lofigames.com/about-kenshi/). The wiki’s [health guide](/guide-to-health) explains practical care, with its own qualifications and source history.

<h2 id="Development">Development</h2>

Chris Hunt founded Lo-Fi Games in 2006 and initially developed Kenshi alone. Steam Early Access followed in 2013, and the team expanded in 2014. [Lo-Fi Games’ studio history](https://lofigames.com/about/about-us/). Kenshi’s full release was **6 December 2018**. [Steam release record](https://store.steampowered.com/app/233860/Kenshi/).

The [Changelog](/changelog) preserves the wiki’s historical release record. Use [Steam’s update history](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/233860) for published announcements. For creating content, start at [Modding](/modding) and the [Forgotten Construction Set](/forgotten-construction-set).
