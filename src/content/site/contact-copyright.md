Anyone may raise an informal copyright, permission or removal concern with **Prd, the Kenshi Wiki maintainer**. You do not need a formal legal notice to ask a question.

Use [general contact](/contact) for other questions or contributions. Direct email remains available at [admin@kenshi.wiki](mailto:admin@kenshi.wiki).

## Information that helps

Identify the wiki page or file, describe the original work and its creator where known, and explain the concern or permission being offered. Include source links and the resolution you would like. Please share only the personal information needed for review.

Our [copyright and permissions guidance](/copyright-and-permissions#how-concerns-are-reviewed) explains how concerns are assessed. This is an informal wiki contact, not a registered DMCA-agent service. It does not replace a hosting provider’s formal procedures.
