**Okran's Gulf** is a zone which comprises the western edge of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s Territory. It is likely given its name "Gulf" for bordering the alien-like [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), and like many other territories which the HN controls, it is named after their deity [Okran](/okran).

It's characterized by an extensive amount of flat and arid (though mostly fertile) lands, which gradually transitions to a more mountainous look when it's closer to [Rebirth](/rebirth-zone). It is also home to [Stack](/stack), one of the [Major Towns](/major-towns) of HN, where [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), the officer responsible for countering the invasion of various [Shek](/shek) factions to the [south of this region](/border-zone) and its neighboring region [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), can be found.

This place is often times infiltrated by dangers of [Sheks](/shek) and [Fogmen](/fogmen), but the defenses of [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> prevents much of their penetration into the region. The security of this region appears to be heavily fortified in its southern<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and western<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> parts but lacking in southeast<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and northern<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> areas. Two [Holy Military Bases](/holy-military-base) can be found somewhat near the edge of this region, likely built to prevent widespread infiltration of [Fogmen](/fog-deathyard) from the Fog Islands next to this region.

This zone is comparatively much less heavily patrolled than their neighboring [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), but patrols are nonetheless still a fairly common sight here, who police the region in an attempt to clear it from [outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws). Occasionally, players may see wandering [Caravan Trader Bosses](/caravan-trader-boss) (affiliated with HN) [Nomads](/nomads) and [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild) roaming this region. Animals such as [Bulls](/wild-bull) and [Wolves](/bonedog) can be spotted here. Some lone individuals such as [Drifters](/drifters) and [Lost Drones](/lost-drone) can also be occasionally spotted.

A series of settlements and [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) can be found in this region, with one of the former settlements close to the [Obedience](/obedience) overrun by Fogmen to become a [Deadhive Overrun](/deadhive-overrun).

## Trivia

-   Patrols of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) and [Berserkers](/berserkers) are constantly attempting to invade into this region but have been blocked off as a result of [Seta's forces](/high-inquisitor-seta). This can be seen in how when he is gone, their presence in this region starts to become noticable as spawns. And, in the case of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) specifically, how even after their assaults are clearly fully under way if [Seto](/seto) is imprisoned/dead, killing off either [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) or [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) still increases their presence, indicating that they were always there, just blocked off.
    -   [Phoenix's](/holy-lord-phoenix) status likely serves as a 2nd line of defense or reserve force aimed at eradicating the rest of [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) that weren't cleared off in the 1st round by [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) as his status is directly tied with certain [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) spawns in this region.
-   [Fog Deathyard](/fog-deathyard) has 2 [World States](/world-states) to cause it to start spawning form this region, one determined by [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) status which determines whether or not the army will withdraw from [Holy Military Base](/holy-military-base) causing the [Fogmen](/fogmen) from the northeastern [Fog Islands](/fog-islands) to spill in. The other, determined by [The Queen's](/the-queen) status, determines whether the [turned](/fogmen) [Hivers](/western-hive) will spill in from the southeastern corner of this region from [Vain](/vain) and [Dreg](/dreg)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, which doesn't have as much stationed military base presence to prevent their infestation.
    -   While logically this should cause Fogmen to spawn much more often in the northern/southern part of the region depending on the source of infestation, the game mechanics are incapable of simulating this, causing the nests to spawn with equal chance across the entire region.
    -   Because the spawn mechanics uses a weight system to determine the nest picked for spawn chance (as opposed to increasing/decreasing spawn rates depending on the level of density) triggering either source for Fogmen massively reduces the level of [Wolf Den](/wolf-den) presence in the region, while having both sources stacked ([Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and [The Queen](/the-queen) dead) would only increase Fogmen presence by a miniscule amount rather than doubling it.
-   [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) likely similarly traversed into this region from [Vain](/vain)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and some may have wandered off into the neighboring region [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
