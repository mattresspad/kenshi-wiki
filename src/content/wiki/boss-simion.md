**Boss Simion** is the leader of the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers).

He resides in [Fort Simion](/fort-simion), with 8 [Outlaw Swordsman Captains](/outlaw-swordsman-captain), 4 ~ 6 [Outlaw Heavies](/outlaw-heavy), and 0 ~ 2 [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman) acting as his bodyguards.

He has a Dumb [Personality](/personality).

## History

Boss Simion's history is taken from his diary, the [Men of Dirt](/men-of-dirt).

He was formerly a farmer in the [United Cities](/united-cities), along with his wife and daughter. Due to competing with the massive [slave](/slavery) workforce that functioned effectively as free labor, he and his family could no longer afford to stay in business, eventually becoming poverty stricken and forced out by the Empire to wander [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

After scavenging a caravan that was feasted on by [skimmers](/skimmer), he acquired enough materials to get through the season, using these supplies he would go on to build [Fort Simion](/fort-simion). From here, he began taking in and protecting the outcasts of the United Cities as if they were his children, stealing from the [nobility](/noble-circle) that took everything from them and staying out of sight.

It is implied, though unconfirmed, that prior to this his wife may have left with their daughter. There is also a possibility that his wife may have surrendered both herself and their daughter to slavery in order to survive.

## Interactions

Players can go inside Fort Simion and speak to Boss Simion if the player is at least neutral with Rebel Farmers, the dialogue options can be to attack him for his bounty, leave him alone or ask if the player can join the Rebel Farmers. In which the latter he will reply with that if the player truly wishes to join them, they must declare themselves hostile with United Cities (with preferred actions such as kicking a noble in the gut) and then speak to them again.

### Ways to ally with the Rebel Farmers/Swordsmen

-   Be enemies with the United Cities and talk to Boss Simion.
-   Kill a Noble and talk to Boss Simion.
-   Be allied with the Anti-Slavers and ask Boss Simion to team up.
    -   Could fail if choosing the wrong dialogue option.
-   Be malnourished and ask Boss Simion if you can join.
    -   Results in free bread as well.
-   Have a damaged (probably means below 0 HP) leg, arm or head and ask Boss Simion that you want to join.

## [World States](/world-states)

(Requires Boss Simion to be alive and free)

If [Nagata](/lord-nagata) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are killed/imprisoned while Boss Simion is alive and free, [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) will be taken over by rebels. If [Kana](/lady-kana) is killed as well, [Port North](/port-north) will be taken over by Empire Peasants.

(Requires Boss Simion to be dead/imprisoned)

The squads of Rebel Farmers will be reduced in the following regions by: [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) (94%), [Heng](/heng-zone) (71%), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun) (76%), [Bast](/bast-zone) (58%), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert) (82%) and be completely removed in [Skimsands](/skimsands).

[Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) will be overrun by [Cannibals](/sho-battai/cannibals) if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Nagata](/lord-nagata) are killed.

His death will have no impact if [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai/empire-peasants) and [Port North](/port-north/empire-peasants) are already taken over by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).
