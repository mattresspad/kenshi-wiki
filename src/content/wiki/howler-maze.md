The **Howler Maze** is an island connected to the mainland with water covered land bridges to the north. The neighboring regions are [Heng](/heng-zone) and [Gut](/gut).

The island is known to be uninhabited but is instead dominated by [Crabs](/crab), with a few escaped slaves wandering in from the surrounding UC territory. A noticeable number of ruins are located here.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in the Howler Maze. This zone does not spawn Camps.
