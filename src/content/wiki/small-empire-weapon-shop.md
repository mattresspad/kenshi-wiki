**Small Empire Weapon Shops** are weapon shops owned by the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) found in [United Cities](/united-cities).

This shop is the smaller version of [Empire Weapon Shop](/empire-weapon-shop), and therefore also contains a smaller variety and quantities than it's counterpart.

### Possible Locations

-   [Bark](/bark)
-   [Brink](/brink)
-   [Clownsteady](/clownsteady)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)

### Residents

-   [UC Weapon Trader](/uc-weapon-trader)
-   [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) (1 to 2)
