**Sand Ninjas** are members of the [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) faction. They can be found in [Cactus Den](/cactus-den) and in small squads of 3-5 patrolling [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon), [The Eye](/the-eye), and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

They have a 20% chance of spawning with a bounty of c.4,000. They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

A character with their name can also be found as a part of the [Drifters](/drifters) faction hanging out in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), some Waystations, and [World's End](/worlds-end).
