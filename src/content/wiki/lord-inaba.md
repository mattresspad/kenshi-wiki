**Lord Inaba** is a nobleman, writer, and military commander of the [United Cities](/united-cities). He can be found in [Stoat](/stoat) at his [Noble House](/noble-house) or patrolling with his 5 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/hooded-empire-noble-guard). Lord Inaba is the author of [The Blessing of Slavery](/the-blessing-of-slavery).

He appears to be the governor of **Stoat** and is responsible for implementing conscription and organizing patrols around [Bast](/bast-zone); for if Lord Inaba is captured or killed, several Samurai patrols will cease to spawn in that region.

He has the Fearful [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Lord Inaba** is killed or imprisoned, then town overrides can apply to different locations under his influence.

Notably, [Stoat](/stoat) will become half destroyed or will be handed over to the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) if [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is also dead or imprisoned. If [Yabuta of the Sands](/yabuta-of-the-sands) is free and alive, Stoat can instead fall under the control of the [Yabuta Outlaws](/yabuta-outlaws) if Tengu and Inaba are both dead or imprisoned.

[Cannibal](/cannibals)patrols will start appearing in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert)region if Lord Inaba, Emperor Tengu and Yabuta of the Sands are all killed/imprisoned.

If Tengu and Inaba are dead while Valtena is alive, Drin will fall under The Holy Nation or ruined otherwise.

UC patrols will decrease frequency in [Bast](/bast-zone) (80%).

His death/kidnapping can also cause a potential spawn of Rebel Farmers 'Upgraded Heavy Squad' patrol consisted of up to 4 more Outlaw Heavies compared to the usual 'Heavy Squad' patrol if Tengu is still alive and free in [Bast](/bast-zone), [Heng](/heng-zone) and [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

Lord Inaba's death/imprisonment along with [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Sanda](/lady-sanda), [Nagata](/lord-nagata), and [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga), will cause patrols of 4 to 13 [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) start spawning in the regions of [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

## Trivia

-   Lord Inaba is one of the more influential or special nobles in the noble circles, as he is the only one who uses the largest "Empire Army Force" squad, and has significantly increased rebel presence at multiple areas tied with his death.
-   **Lord Inaba**, [Lord Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) and [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata) all use [Wakizashi](/wakizashi) as their choice of weapon.
-   **Lord Inaba** and [Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro) have the exact same clothing outfit except for the lack of [Square Goggles](/square-goggles).
