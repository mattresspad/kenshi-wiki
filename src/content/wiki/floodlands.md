Located between [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf). As the name implies, the **Floodlands** is a flooded swamp with a never-ending downpour making this a gloomy area to travel. The region is also known for the ancient structures that remain suggesting the area was industrialized and might explain why the environment is now dead. The only thing roaming these lands are [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) and Skeletons.

The constant rain is probably the source of the main river which flows through [Wend](/wend) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) before it gets deposited into the lowlands of [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in this zone. There are no [Nests](/nest) or Camps which spawn in the Floodlands.

## Lore

Floodlands bears the scars visible from the vast amount of dead area due to exploitation, it has been delegated to feed the industrial maw of the Old Empire, which always hungers yet is never satisfied.

The water that rains here also gets transported to Okran's Pride via Wend and then to Border Zone and ultimately leaving the water to The Swamps.
