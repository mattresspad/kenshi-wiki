**Samurai Armor** is a Heavy Armour that is equipped in the Body Armour slot. It can be bought from Armour Shops in Mongrel, UC, Shek and Tech Hunter towns. It is part of the samurai armour series together with [Samurai Helmet](/samurai-helmet), [Samurai Legplates](/samurai-legplates) and [Samurai Boots](/samurai-boots).

It is the uniform of the soldiers of the [United Cities](/united-cities), which are [Samurais](/samurai), [Samurai Sergeants](/samurai-sergeant), [Samurai Heavies](/samurai-heavy), [Samurai Captains](/samurai-captain), [Samurai Elites](/samurai-elite), and [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout). The colored Empire version are worn by [Samurai Gate Guards](/samurai-gate-guard), [Samurai Gate Sergeants](/samurai-gate-sergeant), [Samurai Police](/samurai-police), [Police Chiefs](/police-chief), [Noble Captains](/noble-captain), and [Empire Noble Guards](/empire-noble-guard-hooded). It is also worn by mercenaries like [Cannibal Hunter Captains](/cannibal-hunter-captain), [Mercenaries](/mercenary), [Mercenary Heavies](/mercenary-heavy), and [Mercenary Captains](/mercenary-captain).

A rusted and stat-wise inferior version of Samurai Armour also [exists](/ancient-samurai-armour). Yet another [version, with an emblem](/empire-samurai-armour), is employed only by the Empire.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

## Appearance

The Samurai Armor is built with average steel. It features several splint-mail segments, those being the pauldrons, rerebraces, vambraces, and a single culet. The only piece that isn't in this fashion is the breastplate, which is a single, large plate.
