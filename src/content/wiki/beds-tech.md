**Beds** is a Level II [Core Tech](/core-tech) which allows the player to construct [Beds](/bed).

This research is a must-have for all players, as even [Skeleton](/skeleton) characters can benefit from beds. Having player-owned beds can save a small number of Cats if [built in town](/player-owned-buildings-in-town) and can be the difference between life and death for players who have built a secluded [outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost).
