**Greyshelf** is a zone in the southeast. It comprises the entirety of the highlands above [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) and is separated from [Royal Valley](/royal-valley) by a mountain range. It is primarily inhabited by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive), with their heartlands to the south of this area.

It is notable for its abundance of [Gorillos](/gorillo) and [Black Gorillos](/black-gorillo). Most locations that can be found here are [Armouries](/armoury-ruin), some of which have been [claimed](/hiver-claimed-armoury) by the Southern Hive.

Care should be taken as Acid Rain weather effect can happen in this area.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in Greyshelf. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
