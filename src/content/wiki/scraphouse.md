56154-rebirth.mod The **Scraphouse** is a weapons/merchant shop located in an ancient factory, just to the east of [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city) in the [Deadlands](/deadlands), be aware of the [Acid Rain](/weather-effects#acid-rain) surrounding the place. It can be marked on a player's map by reading the **[Old Treasure Map](/maps)**, indicating that the bulk of the shop's stock may have come from an ancient stash of valuable items.

The shopkeeper at this location is [Quin](/quin), who often talks with their partner [Dack](/dack).

The shop is guarded by 8 [Soldierbot Guards](/soldierbot-guard) and 5 (0.9x to 1.0x age) [Security Spider II](/security-spider-ii).

The Merchant offers a variety of mid to endgame goods which include

(Guaranteed one time spawns through [Artifact](/artifacts) system or as "special items")

-   3x [AI Cores](/ai-core) (c.25,000 each)
-   3x [Engineering Research](/engineering-research) (c.8,000 each)
-   13x [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) (c.5,000 each)
-   Map of the Ashlands (c.10,000)
-   [Ashlander Storm Goggles](/ashlander-stormgoggles) (c.120)
-   Skeleton Smiths \[Model # Mk I\] [Short-Cleaver](/short-cleaver)
-   Catun Scrapmaster \[Model # Catun No.3\] [Flesh Cleaver](/flesh-cleaver)
-   Edgewalkers \[Model # Edge Type 1\] [Falling Sun](/falling-sun)
-   Specialist [Armoured Rag Skirt](/armoured-rag-skirt)
-   Specialist [Stealth Leg (Right)](/stealth-leg-right)

Although it's possible to [steal](/thievery) those things from the merchant, Dack and many of the guards patrol around the crates make it very difficult to get away with a meaningful haul. A very high level character on thievery and lock-picking is recommended. Many of the general storage containers (including barrels, which are never locked) can be reached from specific corners. These will only work if they are either completely safe (e.g. from the outside of the walls, specific corners) or mostly safe with a high enough stealth level. You can also use complete darkness (some other corners where the guards' view is obstructed) to your advantage. Note that the security spiders can spot stealthy characters very quickly, In certain spots the guards' strict patrol patterns can make it difficult for them to sustain eye contact with a suspected thief long enough to trigger an alarm.

Total item count: 160-240 (Assuming all items fit)

Refresh time: 48 hours

Crossbows and Weapons have a 100 relative chance to spawn items from their pool per item, rare blueprints have a 7 and "Hi tech junk" (Items listed) have a 30 chance.

If a weapon spawns it has a 5 (relative) chance to be Catun Scrapmaster quality, a 30 to be Edgewalkers and 100 to be Skeleton Smiths.
