**Swamp Raptors** are docile reptilian creatures that waddle the land in large squads, primarily in and around [the Swamp](/the-swamp). They are stronger than their cousins the [River Raptors](/river-raptor), as well as somewhat pointier. However, they share the same gluttonous hunger for crops, making them the scourge of Swamper [hemp farms](/hemp-farm). They can also be found contentedly munching on fresh corpses, putting their ravenous hunger to a more beneficial use.

There is also a rare species called the [Megaraptor](/megaraptor) which is noticeably larger and much stronger than its River or Swamp counterparts.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Swamp Raptors can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 18 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 5 piercing damage which Swamp Raptors have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.77961008 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0039) + Strength (0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.83928 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0052) + Strength (0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.89894992 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt** **\=** Martial Arts (0.0065) + Strength (0.0195)
