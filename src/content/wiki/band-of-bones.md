The **Band of Bones** is a violent [Shek](/shek) bandit group led by [Tora](/tora-the-fearless) composed of denizens from the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

Roaming patrols can often decide to randomly attack your squad. They are in the 20's to 30's of combat and not terribly well armed. All members of the faction wield ancient quality Combat Cleavers.

Their HQ is located at the [Exile Camp](/exile-camp), members of this faction are commonly found in the south of the map such as [The Swamp](/the-swamp) and the southern [United Cities](/united-cities).

The members of the Band of Bones compose mostly of Shek warriors who have lost in battle but have refused to give up the sword. Members tend to be hornless and malnourished.
