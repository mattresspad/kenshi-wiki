**Moll's Speech** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) which can only be found in the Flotsam HQ in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village). This item is a draft speech written by the leader of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), [Moll](/moll).

This item itself is also considered an illegal item by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). If held in the player's inventory during a bag search at a Holy Nation town, that character will receive a [bounty](/bounty) for holding illegal goods and subsequently attacked and/or arrested.

This item is not unique though, as Flotsam HQ can also spawn in an [overwritten](/town-overrides) version of [Blister Hill](/blister-hill).
