**Bolts \[Heavies\]** are ammunition used with [Crossbows](/crossbow-class).

After researching [Crossbow Bolts](/crossbow-bolts-tech) and learning the [Blueprint](/blueprints), these bolts can be made on an [Arrow Making Bench](/arrow-making-bench). They can later be stored in a [Boltbox](/boltbox).

## Used with

-   [Spring Bat](/spring-bat)
