A large [backpack](/backpacks) that can only be equipped by domesticated [garrus](/garru) and can stack [trade goods](/trade-goods) up to 6. This backpack features a hanging lantern which will make sneaking while wearing one/by one less effective.

Can be purchased from Adventure Shops at [World's End](/worlds-end), or the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) as well as from Animal Traders/Farm Traders in towns for c.2,000.
