**Gorillos** are white ape-like creatures who roam wastelands in the central part of the map, particularly [Vain](/vain) and [Greyshelf](/greyshelf). They have enormous toothy maws and are aggressive to anything that passes by. They have intimidatingly high stats, but they travel alone and are somewhat slow and lumbering, so they can be kited with [crossbows](/crossbows) or simply outrun.

A larger, stronger version called the [Great White Gorrillo](/great-white-gorillo) can be found in one area.

In one of the [United Weekly Series](/united-weekly-series), the reporters claim that "Gorillos and golden pants" may be the next hot fashion trend. It is unclear if the Gorillos are the ones who would be wearing the golden pants or not. Sadly, no golden pants for either humanoids or Gorillos can be found in-game to resolve this pressing question.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Animals can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) Their "weapon" provides them with 5.2 cut damage and 13 blunt damage which is unaffected by stats. They have 3 attacking animations, 2 of which have 100 power and one which has only 70. To find the damage for the 70 power attack simply multiply the results for the variants below by 0.7.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.05844149 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.02925) + Strength (0.08775)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.06739199 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.039) + Strength (0.117)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.07634249 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.04875) + Strength (0.14625)
