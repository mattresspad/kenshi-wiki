Blood Spiders are horrible, disgusting little insectoids whose menacing hiss will haunt your nightmares. Despite their blood-red coloring, they can be hard to see as they are stick-thin and primarily patrol shadowy and vine-choked swampland. They are one of the many predators of Kenshi who will happily eat their prey while it is still alive, which they do by incapacitating organic beings and sucking them dry (draining the victim's hunger bar).

Blood Spiders are the true "glass cannons" of Kenshi. They are individually quite fragile, but they attack in groups, hit like a truck, and can walk along the bottom of bodies of water. Particularly in [The Swamp](/the-swamp), this means that being caught between Blood Spiders and a large lake can be a death sentence, as the spiders will simply follow a swimmer into the water and continue attacking them, unlike most pursuers. If faced on open terrain, however, it is relatively easy to outrun a pack. They are also quite weak to bleeding damage but can recover lost blood quickly. If you manage to down one, quickly looting its teeth to kill it is a good idea.

As if all this wasn't bad enough, there is also a stronger, larger variant known as the [Elder Blood Spider](/elder-blood-spider) found in one [location](/tower-of-spiders).

The [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) [Miu](/miu) hates spiders passionately and will often let loose with a string of expletives when they are nearby, potentially providing a valuable warning to a player who has not noticed them yet.

## Spawns

### Regions

Standard Blood Spider swarms spawn in [The Swamp](/the-swamp), the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands), and the [Burning Forest](/burning-forest). In The Swamp, there is a 20/75 chance of spawning a swarm of Blood Spiders, assuming that nothing has happened to nearby faction leaders. In the South Wetlands, there is a 1/9 chance of spawning a swarm of Blood Spiders, assuming that nothing has happened to the boss of the Red Sabres.

The regions [Shem](/shem) and [Venge](/venge) may also spawn Blood Spiders in small groups. The chances of spawning these groups in Shem is 1/26 and in Venge is 2/63.

### Spawn Groups

Standard Blood Spider swarms can contain from 1 to 10 spiders. These spiders will be roughly adult age.

Small groups of Blood Spiders can contain from 1 to 3 spiders.

### Other Locations

Besides the above spawns, Blood Spiders can also be found in set locations around the map, including several [Lost Library](/lost-library) outposts. These swarms consist of 6 to 15 almost fully-grown spiders, which will be nested and will not roam the rest of the region.

There are two [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs) that have nested Blood Spiders as well. The [Swamped Lab](/swamped-lab) contains anywhere from 5 to 25 Blood Spiders. The [Infested Lab](/infested-lab) has between 5 and 20 spiders.

The [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) has between 6 and 10 pet Blood Spiders.

## [World States](/world-states)

If [Big Grim](/big-grim), [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) and [Big Gray](/big-gray) are all dead, [Rot](/rot) will be destroyed and invaded by Blood Spiders.
