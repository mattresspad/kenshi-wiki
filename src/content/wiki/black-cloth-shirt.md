A **Black Cloth Shirt** is Clothing that is equipped in the Shirt slot.

There is also the [standard](/cloth-shirt) version of this shirt.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

## Appearance

**Black Cloth Shirt** is a black version of the [Cloth Shirt](/cloth-shirt).
