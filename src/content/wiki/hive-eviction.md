**Hive Eviction** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has defeated the [Southern Hive Visit](/southern-hive-visit) event. It cannot trigger if the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south) has been killed or captured.

Once the conditions are fulfilled, the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) will send an assault to the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). This assault will consist of 3 squads, each squad made up of 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard), 1 [War Gorillo](/war-gorillo), and led by a [Prince](/prince).

When the assault arrives the War Leader will give a short speech complaining about the player being in [King's](/king) territory, then order the attack to begin, attacking gates and characters, and liberating captives. If the assault is victorious, they will occupy the base until defeated.
