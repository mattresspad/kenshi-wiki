**Rebirth** is a labor camp in the pit of a large mountain where [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) sends criminals for punishment and repentance. All races can be present here, except for [Skeletons](/skeleton)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as The Holy Nation does not consider them to have a soul. It is located in an [eponymous region](/rebirth-zone) north of main Holy Nation territory.

This place functions as a concentration camp for religious re-education and penance, where the imprisoned are to perform various labour tasks and to build and maintain a statue resembling either [Okran](/okran) or [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) to redeem themsleves<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> until their passing, by which point their soul would then be believed to be reborn as a child of Okran<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, although it's suggested that the sentencing for some is temporary and could be let out once they are deemed to be cleansed sufficiently<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Much like [other prison camps in the HN](/holy-mines), this camp is established for religious reasons and a place to organize prisoners. The camp houses various undesirables such as hardened criminals<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and border trespassers<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the prevalence of [Sheks](/shek) in this area suggests that many were raiders of various Shek factions such as [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) and [Berserkers](/berserkers)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Players can sell or donate characters to Rebirth Shopkeep they consider in need of punishment<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Non-player characters, such as [Fishmen](/fishmen), [Cannibals](/cannibals) or [Bandits](/bandits), will be accepted for a price of c.500 if the player specifically asks for compensation, otherwise it will yield +1 reputation with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). Unlike the [similar practice](/slavery) of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) ([and](/slave-hunters) [their](/manhunters) [close](/traders-guild) [associates](/united-cities)), Rebirth isn't meant for personal gain or exploitation<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but rather for re-education and discipline<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as a result the player wouldn't be able to buy any inmates.

Players can potentially gain [recruits](/slave-recruits) by unlocking cages and shackles, depending on the [personality](/personality) of the inmate in question.

You can start your game in Rebirth with two non-Skeleton characters if you choose "The Slaves" [game start](/game-starts).

## Trivia

-   If the [player](/nameless) was enslaved by The Holy Nation or the [Slavemongers](/slavemonger), they will be sent here.
    -   Due to Slavemongers being supposed to be a removed content, they would just stand idly without doing anything.
-   Although lore-wise Skeletons are not supposed to be enslaved, gameplay mechanics would still make the HN enslave or imprison any Skeletons nearby.
-   [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) aid the escape of slaves, as could be seen in the shack ("[Flotsam Safehouse](/flotsam-safehouse)") right north of it.
-   [Bast's](/bast-zone) dialogue would indicate that their youngsters were captured and sent here.
-   [Maril's Diary Series](/marils-diary-series) is in large part related to this area.
