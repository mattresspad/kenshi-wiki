**Raid** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has a [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) within 5,000 meters of the [Exile Camp](/exile-camp). It has a 20% chance to trigger if within 2,500 meters, this chance scales down with distance until it hits 0% at 5,000 meters. The event cannot trigger if [Tora the Fearless](/tora-the-fearless) is killed or captured, or if the player has killed or captured [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem), the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), or the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster).

Once the conditions are fulfilled, the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) will send an assault to the player's base. This assault will consist of 2-4 squads, each made up of 4-7 [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones-character).

When the assault arrives, the War Leader will give a short speech before ordering the attack to begin, breaking down gates, attacking characters, and liberating captives. If the assault is defeated, a [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-band-of-bones) event will be triggered in 80-120 hours. If the assault is victorious, another **Raid** event will trigger in 200-300 hours.
