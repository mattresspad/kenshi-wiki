The **Megaraptor** is a massive version of the raptor species. It is a larger version of the [swamp raptor](/swamp-raptor) with increased stats and scale (health/size). It spawns with an age multiplier between 0.9x to 1.0x.

It is living in the Drowned Ancient Citadel Ruin in [Raptor Island](/raptor-island-zone). Interestingly enough it is the only animal in the game with a female chance of 100.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. The Megaraptor can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 18 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 5 piercing damage which the Megaraptor has.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.77961008 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0039) + Strength (0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.83928 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0052) + Strength (0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.89894992 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt** **\=** Martial Arts (0.0065) + Strength (0.0195)
