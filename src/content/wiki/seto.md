**Seto** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) and the daughter of the [Shek Kingdom's](/shek-kingdom) ruler, Queen [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem).

She can be given to the player provided the player has over 35 relations _and_ imprisoned/killed either the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) or [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), or alternatively if the player has 80 relations. They can then speak to Esata, which will boost the relations by a further +60 (if the condition achieved was the former), and then:

-   Summons squad for cheering and gives +10 relation
-   Adds a further +20 relations, at a cost of -15 relations with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

Once the cheering and war cries are done, Esata will grant Seto to your squad.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

**Seto** has a c.20,000 [Bounty](/bounty) from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

If the player is allied to [the Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), or [United Cities](/united-cities), Esata's dialog for awarding her to the player will be locked off, making her impossible to recruit. This doesn't apply when she is already recruited, however.

She has an Honorable [Personality](/personality).

## Bounty

### [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

When Seto is turned into The Holy Nation it will grant the following effects:

(Not allied to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom))

-   +50 relations with The Holy Nation
-   \-75 relations with Shek Kingdom
-   c.20,000

## [World States](/world-states)

If Seto is killed/imprisoned there are several World States that would trigger:

-   [Admag](/admag) would have a [Town Override](/town-overrides) under Shek Kingdom rule with a notable lack of military population.
-   [Hundred Guardian](/hundred-guardian) and [Shek Warrior](/shek-warrior) patrols will start invading [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) regions if **Esata** is alive and free.
-   If [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is alive and free, [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) will start spawning counter-attack patrols at the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) and [Border Zone](/border-zone) regions.

### [Base Events](/events)

**Seto's** death/imprisonment prevents [Tribute Round](/tribute-round), [Return For Blood](/return-for-blood), [Return for Tribute](/return-for-tribute), [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) from being launched.

It will also allow [Stone Golem's Revenge](/stone-golems-revenge) to be launched provided that the player isn't allied to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

## Trivia

-   Seto's state governing Shek Kingdom assaults into The Holy Nation persist even as part of your squad, and is completely irrelevant to who caused her death/imprisonment.
    -   This means that [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) will launch assaults into The Holy Nation once Seto is killed/imprisoned _even_ if her demise has nothing to do with the latter (this is unlike [Stone Golem's Revenge](/stone-golems-revenge) assaults against the player which checks conditions and only launches if the player is not allied).
