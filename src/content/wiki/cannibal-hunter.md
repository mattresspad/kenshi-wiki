**Cannibal Hunters** are the standard members of the faction [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters). They can be found roaming the area spanning from the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains) to [Sinkuun](/sinkuun). Many Cannibal Hunters can be found in [The Bastion](/the-bastion).

They patrol in groups of 5 ~ 7, or 6 ~ 9 Hunters with 0 ~ 2 [Mountain Dogs](/mountain-dog).

The leader of a Cannibal Hunters roaming group can be talked to in order to hire as a mercenary for revenge against Cannibals. Their rate is c.1500 a day to defend your outpost and c.3000 a day to bodyguard you.
