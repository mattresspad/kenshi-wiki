The **Mourn Barman** is an unnamed member of the [Trade Ninjas](/trade-ninjas). He resides in the [Bar](/bar-of-mourn) in [Mourn](/mourn).

Upon asking him about the place, he will tell the player a small story and warn them not to enter the HQ, calling the locked gates a 'pest control measure'.

Upon getting rid of the [pest](/great-white-gorillo) and returning with a [trophy](/great-white-claw), the Barman will be impressed and will give the player free [Grog](/grog) and [Sake](/sake).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).
