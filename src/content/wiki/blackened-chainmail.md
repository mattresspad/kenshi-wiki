**Blackened Chainmail** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Shirt slot.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

This chainmail also offers slightly more resistance than the short-sleeve chain shirts.

## Appearance

Blackened Chainmail covers the full torso of the character and is similar in appearance to Chainmail. The major difference between the two is that this one is made up of black chain links and the trim around the neck and wrists is made up of black colored material.
