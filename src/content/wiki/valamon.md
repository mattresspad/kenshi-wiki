## [World States](/world-states)

If **Valamon** is killed or kidnapped, some of the locations controlled by the Reavers will be [overtaken](/town-overrides) by other factions:

**Ark** becomes overtaken by the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) if the [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) is alive. If she is also killed or kidnapped, it becomes inhabited by a bunch of [squatters](/vagrants).

[Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp) will be overtaken by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) if the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south) is alive and free, if she is also killed or kidnapped, all Reaver Camps will become [ruins](/reaver-camp/destroyed).

If **Valamon** is killed or kidnapped, The [Blooded Raid](/blooded-raid) event will also cease to trigger.

[The Eye](/the-eye) and [Heng](/heng) if the world states for them to spawn there were already achieved beforehand. Crab Raider patrols will start spawning in Stormgap Coast and Heng if the Crab Queen is alive.

(Requires Valamon to be alive and free)

If [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) is dead, [Reavers](/reavers) will take over [Brink](/brink/reavers) and start patrolling [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and [The Eye](/the-eye). If [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) is also killed, they will start patrolling [Heng](/heng-zone).

If [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) is dead, Reavers will take over [Crab Village](/crab-village).

If [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi), [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are all dead, Reavers [will take over Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield/reavers).
