**Reavers** are a faction of bandits led by [Valamon](/valamon) who believe that they deserve to be in charge instead of the [United Cities](/united-cities). They have the same trade culture as the [Swampers](/swampers).

They are known for capturing their targets and putting them into forced conscription where they are sent to the fray to prove their worth or perish. It's likely that many higher-ranked Reaver members were former slaves themselves and are attempting to bring others into their fold in the same way they have been themselves.

Like the other factions of the southeastern region of Kenshi, they dislike and often times come into skirmish and border raids with their rivals [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders), [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) and [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits). They will stop [raiding](/blooded-raid) the [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) if their [leader](/valamon) is either killed or kidnapped.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
