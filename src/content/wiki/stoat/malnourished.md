If **Slave Master Haga** and **Slave Mistress Ren** are both dead/imprisoned, Stoat residents become malnourished.

The town is plagued by rampant famine and poverty after all the slave camps of [Stone Camp](/stone-camp) and [Slave Farm](/slave-farm) supplying this town have been cut off, and the ensuing chaos has destroyed and killed a part of the town and its inhabitants.

This override can flip towards [malnourished + half-destroyed](/stoat/civil-war), [Tech Hunters](/stoat/tech-hunters), or [Yabuta Outlaws](/stoat/yabuta-outlaws).
