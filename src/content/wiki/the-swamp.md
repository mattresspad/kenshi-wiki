**The Swamp** is a large region centered between the [Border Zone](/border-zone) and the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands) to the north and south, and the [Spider Plains](/spider-plains) and the [Burning Forest](/burning-forest) to its west and east. There is low visibility compared to most other regions due to the areas fog and foliage density. The region is fertile for hemp and riceweed farming. It also has an abundance of copper and iron resources.

It is home to the [Swampers](/swampers) faction and various crime syndicates. The criminal gangs are: [Hounds](/hounds), [Blackshifters](/blackshifters), [Grayflayers](/grayflayers), [Stone Rats](/stone-rats), and [Twinblades](/twinblades). The [Hounds](/hounds) control the city Shark and the village Rot through lucrative [Hemp](/hemp) and [Hashish](/hashish) trade networks. Aside from the Hounds, the gangs can be found in various surrounding swamp villages. The gangs deal in [Hashish](/hashish), which is illegal in many cities.

A few ancient and abandoned locations can be found scattered around the area and presents both potential loot and danger.

Both [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) and [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) can be seen patrolling the Swamp, but only the Red Sabres have a hideout within the Swamp while Swamp Ninjas have a hideout in the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands). Other than the abundance of bandits, The Swamp is also known for a large number of pests and hostile fauna such as [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor) and [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider), respectively.

The Swamp has great 100% fertility for any swamp crop farm and the water production is not a problem for most parts. 100% quality copper mines dots throughout its landscapes and there are a reasonable number of 100% quality iron mines. The stone mine is capped at 50% for The Swamp but the number of resources you can farm from other sources already make up your loss in stone production.

Your staple protein would be found from the unique [Swamp Turtles](/swamp-turtle) living here. Also, [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) and [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor) are a common sight in the region.

## Tips

1.  The Swamp is a haven for squads with 30+ stats due to abundance of resources and the constant spawn of resourceful enemies.
2.  The main threat usually comes from [blood spiders](/blood-spider) harassing you while you are fighting other enemies or when traversing through shallow waters.
3.  Like most environments with lots of hostile wildlife, walling up your entire base is the best way to prevent disruption of work and to protect your people from unnecessary loss.

## Trivia

    -   This is further supported by the presence of Hive Bandits, Lost Princes and Lost Drones in this region and Flats Lagoon when The Queen is dead.
