**Meatwrap** is a [food](/food) item. It can be purchased from relevant [traders](/traders), typically in [Bars](/bars). Players can cook Meatwraps on a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove) after researching [Advanced Cooking](/advanced-cooking-tech).

The Meatwrap is a very situational food that is hardly useful even in the early games when [technology](/technology) or resources are limited. It is the result of combining [Raw Meat](/raw-meat), 15nu, and [Bread](/bread), 30nu, resulting in the Meatwrap for 50nu, granting a very slight supplement of 5nu to the finished product.

It is absolutely outshined by almost any other type of food but can be made if no other options are available.
