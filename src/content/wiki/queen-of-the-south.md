**Queen of the South** is leader of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) and has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status). She sits upon her throne located in [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location) protected by her [Elite Droneguards](/elite-droneguard) who will attack any intruders. She rules over her subjects with the help of her consort, the [King](/king).

Despite all other members of the Southern Hive having a distinct pink coloration of their skin, the Queen of the South appears to be exactly of the same colour as her rival, [the Queen](/the-queen) of the [Western Hive](/western-hive).

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## [World States](/world-states)

**Queen of the South** is linked to several world states, most of which require for her to be alive and for the **Queen of the Western Hive** to be dead.

If Queen of the South is alive and not kidnapped and the Queen of the Western Hive is dead then Southern Hive patrols will spawn in the [Bonefields](/bonefields), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon-zone), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [The Hook](/the-hook), [The Swamp](/the-swamp) and in [Vain](/vain).

If Queen of the South is alive and not kidnapped, the [Savant](/savant) is dead or kidnapped, then Southern Hive patrols will spawn in [Sonorous Dark](/sonorous-dark) and the [Skinhouses](/skinhouse) will be under Hive control.

If Queen of the South is alive and not kidnapped, the [Elder](/elder) is dead or kidnapped, then Southern Hive patrols will spawn in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) and [The Crags](/the-crags). [Iron HQ](/iron-hq) will also be taken over by the Southern Hive.

If Queen of the South is alive and not kidnapped, the [Valamon](/valamon) is dead or kidnapped, then Southern Hive will overtake the [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp).

[Killing or kidnapping](/world-states) the Queen of the South will cause all Southern Hive locations to become [invaded](/town-overrides) by [Fogmen](/fogmen) and for the [Southern Hive Visit](/southern-hive-visit) and [Hive Eviction](/hive-eviction) events to stop happening.

## Trivia

-   **Queen of the South** has the exact same stats as her Western cousin, [The Queen](/the-queen). This may imply that the color of southern hivers is given by the biomass created by King from living creatures to generate new hives and that both queens were originally created in similar ways.
-   Although she is a robot, the Queen of the South has a Hunger rate of 3. If you were to place her inside of a cage you own, she would starve before too long.
    -   If you mouse over her hunger, it will state that she does not need to eat but this is simply a visual bug.
