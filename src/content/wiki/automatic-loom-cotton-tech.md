**Automatic Loom (cotton)** is [Crafting Tech](/crafting-tech) research which allows the player to construct a Cotton Auto-Loom. This is the cotton equivalent of [Automatic Loom (Hemp)](/automatic-loom-hemp-tech).

This research can be helpful for players who craft a lot of armour and create [Fabrics](/fabrics) from [Cotton](/cotton).
