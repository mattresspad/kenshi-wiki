The **Flotsam Ninjas** are a small<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> rebel [faction](/factions) mostly made up of women<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and escapees who have defected from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, although their organization is also composed of a significant proportion of volunteers<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, likely originating from [Deadcat](/deadcat)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They have taken refuge in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) under [Moll's](/moll)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> leadership, where some of them train to become ninjas<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Many of the Flotsam still worship [Okran](/okran), but follow a divergent path that also venerates Narko<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, seeing both gods as integral parts of the cosmic balance rather than mortal enemies. As a result, they are seen as heretics and traitors by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Additionally, they also engage in various subversive activities<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> inside the Holy Nation such as assassinations,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> aiding escapees,<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> fomenting unrest and riots,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> practicing in and also very likely spreading heresies<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and advocating for armed resistance against the Holy Nation in general.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

They live in a hidden village near the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), as the [Painted Tribe](/cannibals) provide a good deterrent from their pursuers.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, the Cannibals also present dangers to the ninjas themselves,<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> occasionally killing off their supply lines,<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so they have to regularly cull them to prevent them from becoming too big of a threat.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Attitudes on the subject of the Holy Nation within the Flotsam Ninjas do vary. Some seek to overthrow their<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> rule, some wish to move on and leave them, while others bear guilt over turning their backs on [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

If the player comes across their territory for the first time, they will interrogate the player about their reasons for being there. Women and non-humans can usually get through fairly easily, while human males have to go through a bit more trouble and dialogue.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Relations

### Faction Relations

_The factions this faction has special relations towards. For all others, it will use this faction's default (0)._

-   [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) (-100)
-   [Deadcat](/deadcat) (+100)

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Agreeing with Moll when she mentions cannibals being good target practice or reply that it's "all fun until you find yourself in a pot" will result in +1 Relations.
-   Agreeing with Moll when she goes off a rant about religion will give +2 relations. This is repeatable and can be done until you ally with them.
-   Attacking Moll through dialogue will result in -20 relations.
-   Backing off from swearing allegiance or having conversation go sour will result in -2 relations.
-   The Player has an option when talking to [Moll](/moll) to either side with the Flotsam Ninjas or the Holy Nation. Choosing one will negatively affect your relations with the other. (+/-75 for Flotsam) (+50/-75 for HN)
-   If the player turns in Moll to an [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) to earn her c.50,000 bounty, their relations with the Flotsam Ninjas will be severely damaged (-75).
-   If Moll gets imprisoned (not directly by the player) and gets freed, Flotsam Ninjas will be allied to the player.

## Trivia

-   The word Flotsam can mean "People who are considered to be worthless or to have been rejected by society", which adequately fits [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s view on them.
    -   Its default meaning is "Floating Junk", which could be a reference to being elevated as compared to the neighboring [Floodlands](/floodlands) or [Northern Coast](/northern-coast).
-   Flotsam's military could be estimated to be at less than 1% of HN's military, given that they only field roughly 1/3rd as many patrols as HN in only 2 zones<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and zero patrols in the core region of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride).
    -   Given the weight estimation for military presence in zones by putting them relative to bandits, it would be: [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) (1x), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) (0.07x), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) (0.09x), [Border Zone](/border-zone) (0.14x), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam) (0.17x), [Bast](/bast-zone) (0.81x), [Skimsands](/skimsands) (0.25x), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) (0.03x) and Flotsam would occupy 2% of it.
        -   This is discounting the garrison inside the towns and military outposts, which could make the numbers much lower.
    -   The force disparity is so glaring, that even Moll admits that numbers aren't "on our side"<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
    -   Even a single High Inquisitor's army is capable of completely blocking off Flotsam's advancement<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
    -   The places where Flotsam are capable of taking over is extremely limited, only taking a heavily devastated [Blister Hill](/blister-hill/flotsam-ninjas) and [Rebirth](/rebirth/flotsam-ninjas) after being completely abandoned.
        -   Places like [Stack](/stack), [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth), [Holy Military Bases](/holy-military-base), [Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist), [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield), [Holy Mines](/holy-mines), [Holy Farms](/holy-farms) are never recovered.
        -   No patrols (potential and not) in places like [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Bast](/bast-zone), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).
