**Strayed Paladins** are members of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They only exist if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are all either [killed or imprisoned](/world-states).

They appear to be [Paladins](/paladin) who are separated from Holy Nation's military leadership and roam aimlessly in the wilds. They tend to equip a variety of makeshift rags and armor, somewhat similar to [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits). Unlike [them](/hungry-bandit), however, they are _not_ malnourished and are still formidable in combat. They have the same stats as a [Paladin](/paladin), as well as a Smart [Personality](/personality).

These characters are likely stragglers separated from the main army force, since their military is still functional as evident by certain settlements<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Events such as [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> where they still have countless [Inquisitors](/inquisitor), [High Paladin](/high-paladin) and [Paladins](/paladin) to throw against the [outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) in military expeditions [covering the entire map](/world-of-kenshi).

They spawn in squads of 4-10 in these zones: [Bast](/bast-zone), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.
