Deep in the mountains, north-west of [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), there is a giant [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) mine bigger than any other slave labor mines. The zone named **Rebirth** extends slightly beyond the boundaries of the mine named [Rebirth](/rebirth).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or Ancient Locations in Rebirth. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps or have any Homeless Spawns.

### Minor Outposts

_These locations are typically small towns, villages, or faction bases._

-   [Flotsam Safehouse](/flotsam-safehouse)
-   [Rebirth](/rebirth)

## Tips

-   The Flotsam Safehouse provides many useful supplies, there are no repercussions from taking them.
-   If you are a non-human, avoid this area, unless you wish to be enslaved.
-   In the event you escape the Rebirth camp, head north to the Flotsam Safehouse. It is full of food, hacksaws to break shackles, and clothes to disguise yourself.
