51311-rebirth.mod **Shek Weapon Shops** are weapon shops found in [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) cities.

These shops mostly sell [Heavy Weapons](/heavy-weapons), a most common choice of weapons for sheks.

### Possible Locations

-   [Admag](/admag)
-   [Squin](/squin)
-   [Last Stand](/last-stand)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) Shek takeover
-   [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield) Shek takeover
-   [Stack](/stack) Shek takeover

### Residents

-   [Shek Retainer](/shek-retainer)
-   [Shek Warrior](/shek-warrior) (1 to 2)
