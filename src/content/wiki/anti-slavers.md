The **Anti-Slavers** are a faction led by the legendary [Tinfist](/tinfist) in a fight against [slavery](/slavery). As a result, they gained many powerful enemies such as [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) who engage in these practices. They are considered to be terrorists by slaver factions for launching raids, causing famine<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, unleashing criminals<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and generally causing chaos, death and destruction. Despite all this, they are favorably viewed by [Drifters](/drifters)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> from around the [world](/world-of-kenshi).

Their base, [Spring](/spring), is located atop a mountain in the southwest of region of [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble).

A notable amount of them don't use [weapons](/weapons) and are instead skilled [Martial Arts](/martial-arts) fighters. They have fighters from all kinds of races including [Hivers](/hive), [Humans](/human), [Shek](/shek), and [Skeletons](/skeleton).

Players can side with Anti-Slavers by talking to [Grey](/grey), or alternatively side against them by capturing [Tinfist](/tinfist).

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   The Anti-Slavers' Pacifier will not talk to you if you are allied with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), or the [United Cities](/united-cities).
-   The Anti-Slavers will hate you if you kill or imprison [Bo](/bo), [Grey](/grey), [Jaegar](/jaegar), or [Tinfist](/tinfist).
-   Players can ally with the Anti-Slavers by talking to [Grey](/grey).  This will give you +75 Anti-Slavers and -30 from [United Cities](/united-cities), [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) relations.
-   If you have captured and/or killed [Longen](/longen) and talk to Tinfist, you will get +75 reputation with the anti-slavers instantly, without the -30 to the UC, Slave traders or Traders Guild. (But capturing Longen will still give the normal penalty.)
-   Joining [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) by talking to [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) will grant +10 relations, or +15 relations if the player has already allied with Anti-Slavers.
-   Donating money to [Koin](/koin) will grant you -1/-2/-7/-15/-30 by donating 5k/10k/20k/50k/100k.
-   Talking to [Koin](/koin) about influence and having at least a level 2 [Outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) and paying 10k will grant you -30 relations.
-   Purchasing [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) membership from [Yamdu](/yamdu) will grant you -30 relations.

## Trivia

-   Despite being otherwise fanatically opposed to slavery, the Anti-Slavers do not have a negative relation with the [Reavers](/reavers).
-   Anti-Slavers are the only faction to have uniform presence of all possible starting races at equal chances, even if some races should be much more common in the land (e.g. [Hive Princes](/hive-prince) appear just as frequent as [Hive Worker Drones](/hive-worker-drone)).
-   Anti-Slavers may follow you as bodyguards randomly when allied.
