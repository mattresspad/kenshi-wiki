**Medic Recruit** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a medic looking for work. They can be hired for c.6,000.

They can be found in [Eyesocket](/eyesocket), [Slave Markets](/slave-markets), [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), any [Waystation](/waystation), and [World's End](/worlds-end). They can also be found in locations controlled by [Deadcat](/deadcat) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). They have a chance of spawning in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), including when the location is "malnourished".

Their race is determined by their faction ([Drifters](/drifters)). They commonly have a Smart [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player CIVILIAN" Dialogue Package.
