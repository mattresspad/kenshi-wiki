A **Topper** is a [Katana Class](/katanas) weapon that is aimed to inflict more blunt damage and sacrifice some cutting damage. While it has the same reach as the [Nodachi](/nodachi), it does not have the penalties or bonuses it has. This makes it a better choice for those trying to avoid them. The Topper is the only Katana without a Robotic damage reduction and also happens to have the lowest Armour Penetration debuff of the weapon class.

You can find the [Meitou](/meitou) model of this weapon on [Rhinobot](/rhinobot) in [Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **1x9**, meaning it can only be equipped as a primary weapon.
