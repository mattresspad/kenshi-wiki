**Reaver Slaves** are the unreliable [whelps](/reaver-whelp) of the [Reavers](/reavers). Reaver Slaves are members of the [Vagrants](/vagrants) faction. They can be found in small groups accompanying squads of [Blooded](/reaver-blooded) and [Ironclads](/reaver-ironclad), serving as their meat shields during fights, or locked in cages within [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp) or [Ark](/ark).

Like other slaves, Reaver Slaves can become [Slave Recruits](/slave-recruits) if they are freed from their [Prisoner Shackles](/prisoner-shackles) and/or [Prisoner Cage](/prisoner-cage). During a fight, if one is able to defeat their masters before killing the slaves, they will also have a chance to join the player, just take care not to accidentally attack them after the fight.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

## Trivia

-   They are referred to as "whelps" by the Reavers, though this is also [another rank](/reaver-whelp) entirely.
-   Due to a bug(?) it is much harder to recruit this slave type (though not unique to this slave) when attempting to free them from their cages.
-   The bug causes slaves of Maniacal personality to be stuck in their cages without attempting to join. As such, only Honorable or Smart personality slaves can join the player.
-   The 'bandit types' personality these slaves possess never allows them to be Honorable, and only be rarely Smart, with every other personality having an equal chance of appearing. Because of this, it is very hard to recruit them from the reaver camps.
    -   Slaves freed by killing their patrolling reaver masters are unaffected.
