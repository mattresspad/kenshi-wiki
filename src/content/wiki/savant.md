**Savant** is the crazed Leader of the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) with a [bounty](/bounty) of c.80,000 by the [United Cities](/united-cities), despite the listing of a c.50,000 on his [wanted poster](/wanted-posters). Savant can be found on his throne or sleeping in his bed in the [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq). Players can join his faction but only as a [Skeleton](/skeleton).

He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (25% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

Savant also holds a special blueprint named the "Skeleton-Human Transformation Bible" which allows the player to craft [Human Skin Suits](/human-skin-suit) and the [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine).

## [World States](/world-states)

If **Savant** is killed or imprisoned, it will cause the Skinhouse HQ to be completely destroyed. One of the [Skinhouses](/skinhouse) is also destroyed while the other is taken over by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) if the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south) is still alive.

The skin bandit patrols also stop and are replaced by a Southern Hive patrol of 1 [Prince](/prince) and 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard) if the **Queen of the South** is alive and free.

This will also cause the Skin Bandit raids ([Skin Harvest](/skin-harvest) and [The Big Skin Harvest](/the-big-skin-harvest)) on a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) to stop.

## Interactions

Talking to Savant as a non-human allows the player to discover more about the "philosophy" of the skinned ones.

Savant is of the opinion that the cinders that spread everywhere from the [Ashlands](/ashlands) are toxic to all minds. He claims human skin helps protect them against these cinders; However, because some still manage to slip through our wounds and by breathing, we humans engage in pleasurable activities such as eating and drinking to release "healing byproducts" in our bodies, purifying them from the cinders and "resetting our bad moral judgement".

Due to the skeletons having no skin, as well as their "extra components" and inability to laugh, eat or engage in any kind of pleasurable activity, Savant believes they are the race most prone to succumbing to the madness-inducing cinders, supporting his theory with the large number of rampaging "mad" skeletons present in the Ashlands. Thus, the self-proclaimed Doctor Savant came up with an inventive procedure to give skin and soul to skeletons and give them some sorely needed cinder protection.

[Hivers](/hive) and [Shek](/shek) seeking to join the Skin Bandits will be disappointed to find out that Savant only "operates" on skeletons, while [humans](/human) who manage to sneak all the way to Savant and talk to him will leave him baffled at their stupidity/insanity, and cause him to call on his [flesh brothers](/skin-bandit) for fresh skin.
