If [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) are all [dead or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are alive and free, [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will be [taken over](/town-overrides) by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

This override can switch to [Destroyed](/holy-mines/destroyed), [Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom) or [Kral's Chosen](/holy-mines/krals-chosen) overrides if conditions are achieved.

Assuming the conditions for achieving this override remains the same, it will switch to the [Destroyed](/holy-mines/destroyed) override immediately upon 2nd entry _unless_ [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and/or [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is released from prison and kept alive. If neither [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) nor [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) are alive and free, it could also switch to [Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom) if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is released from prison and kept alive, or alternatively [Kral's Chosen](/holy-mines/krals-chosen) if [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is released and kept alive while Esata is either dead or imprisoned.
