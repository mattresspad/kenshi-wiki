**Diplomatic Status** is given to a person with high importance in a [faction](/faction). Attacking or disturbing such a person can lead to a considerable amount of trouble. In practice, generally, harming them or imprisoning them in any way will severely damage their faction's relationship with your own, but healing them if they're injured is a quick way to get back to being neutral with them. This also applies to other factions and can lead to conflict if both are present in the same place after such an incident.

Contrary to description, the Diplomatic Status doesn't actually mean that a character who is harmed/healed will get a large reputation penalty/boost as a result. It just happens so that characters with Diplomatic Statuses also tend to have extremely high "Faction Importance" inside [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) of 100 (e.g. [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem)), but this is not always the case, as characters such as [Moll](/moll) have only a "Faction Importance" of 1, making any difference in relation change by harming or healing rather insignificant.

All faction's bounty chiefs treat enemy factions with diplomatic statuses as a bounty without a bounty reward assigned. For example, [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) [Inquisitors](/inquisitor) will accept characters with diplomatic statuses such as [Arc](/arc), [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain), [and](/lady-sanda) [all](/lord-nagata) [the](/lord-inaba) [nobles](/lord-yoshinaga) [throughout](/lord-ohta) [the](/lady-tsugi) [entirety](/lord-shiro) [of](/lady-merin) UC (including [Noble Hunters](/noble-hunter)), as well as the [Pacifiers](/pacifiers) of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) and [United Cities](/united-cities) as viable bounty targets, even though they don't have any bounty allocated on top of them.

Such cases also happen with other factions and their pacifiers too, as well as any new enemies that the faction gains by running relation to the point of being hostile. What this means is that you can hand in HN pacifier to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), as well as UC's pacifier and all their [Noble Circles](/noble-circle) if you manage to run their relations with the Empire to the point of being hostile (through actions such as allying with them and have their bodyguards fight UC forces, which will run down the relations between SK and UC).

When you hand in such bounties to their respective chiefs, you will get a reputation boost (if conditions allow), but no bounty rewards, and their respective chiefs would bug out and usually just carry the body instead of allocating them inside cages.

This is possibly a case of an incomplete feature where the developer simply wrote the game in such a way that simply treats any enemy faction's character with high importance as a VIP to be captured instead of assigning bounties to each individually.

Characters with crossbows/turrets will ignore commands to shoot at Diplomatic Status targets, unless that target attacked you first either via a dialog effect or having aggressive AI goals. Even when they are the aggressor and the immunity doesn't apply, ranged weapons will refuse to target them until no other targets are available. A way to get around this is to line up your shot to fire at unit behind the Diplomatic status target.

Only characters with Diplomatic Status can be spoken to if a faction is hostile towards the player. Diplomatic Status allows the player to speak with characters such as [Pacifiers](/pacifiers) to reset their faction relations.

Because of how high in faction importance some of the characters are, healing them can neutralize any hostile actions you've done to their factions and bring it back to neutral, even if said action was hostile to the diplomatic person in question. This could lead to some (rather hillarious) scenarios whereby you could capture an important leader of a faction such as [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), skin them in a [peeler machine](/peeler-machine) or [burn them in Venge](/venge) before patching them up, and they will forgive the transgressions as if nothing had ever happened.

You could still kill them off through methods such as [Acid Rain](/weather-effects) (with the obvious exception of acid-immune leaders) without incurring penalties in relations to yourself if you wish to achieve [World States](/world-states) that requires them dead without antagonizing them.
