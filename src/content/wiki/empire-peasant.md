**Empire Peasants** are characters that belong to the faction of the [same name](/empire-peasants), and can be found in [United Cities](/united-cities) territory. They either reside in UC towns, or in small encampments in the desert. They are the underclass of the Empire, a notable number of them being [Hivers](/hive).

-   An Empire Peasant in the [Old Village](/old-village) may approach the player and offer to join them. They will change their mind after being chastised by another peasant, however.
-   Hiver Worker Drones and Hiver Soldier Drones make up the majority of the Empire Peasants, Worker Drones in particular are 4 times more likely to spawn as one when compared to your average [Greenlander](/greenlander).
