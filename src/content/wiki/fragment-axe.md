A **Fragment Axe** is a [Heavy Weapon](/heavy-weapons) and as a result it takes a long time to swing and block if you lack the strength to properly use it. Very few men can stand up to such a mighty weapon.

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x10,** meaning it can only be used as a primary weapon. Like other heavy weapons, this item will not fit into standard inventories. It must either be equipped or carried inside a backpack.

[Edgewalker](/edgewalkers) and above variants of the weapon are so absurdly heavy that it is impossible for character without robotic limbs to wield them without penalties. Even with robotics, a very high level of strength is needed in order to use the higher quality variants efficiently. The Cross variant requires a character to have 144 STR to use without their combat speed being reduced, which cannot be achieved without [Robotic Limbs](/industrial-lifter-arm-right).

This weapon is primarily found in [shops](/shek-weapon-shop) in the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). A unique version of the [Fragment Axe](/bull-horn-axe) (Which has Indoors -4 instead of -6) is wielded by the leader of Kral's Chosen.

A [Meitou](/meitou) quality of this weapon can be found on [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) in [Admag](/admag) or on [Mukai the Mountain](/mukai-the-mountain) in [Squin](/squin) after she has been killed.
