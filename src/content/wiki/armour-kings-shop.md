**Armour King's Shop** is a hidden unique [armour shop](/armour-clothing-shops) found in [Drowned Ruins](/drowned-ruins) in the river [Wend](/wend). It carries some of the highest quality [armour](/armour) in the game, for those that can afford it.

This shop also rents out beds upstairs for free if the player is in need of healing.

### Residents

-   [Armour King](/armour-king)
-   [Armour King's Thrall](/armour-kings-thrall) (4)
-   [Security Spider II](/security-spider-ii)

This location has a gear [Artifacts](/artifacts) value of c.150,000, meaning it will spawn armour and weapons from the Artifacts system.

## Artifacts spawned

-   [Desert Sabre](/desert-sabre) \[Model # Edge Type 1\]
-   [Guardless Katana](/guardless-katana) \[Model # Mk I\]
-   [Topper](/topper) \[Model # Mk III\]
