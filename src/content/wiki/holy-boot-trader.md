Holy **Boot Traders** are unnamed shopkeepers of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They can be found manning [Holy Boot Shops](/holy-boot-shop).

Like all holy nation shopkeepers, they won't sell to races that aren't human.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
