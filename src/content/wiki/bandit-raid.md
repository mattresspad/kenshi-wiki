**Bandit Raid** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the player lives within 4,500 meters of the [Tower Of Ninjas](/tower-of-ninjas). It has a 20% chance to trigger each day if within 3,800 meters, this chance scales down with distance until it hits a 0% chance at 4,500 meters. It cannot trigger if [Dimak](/dimak) has been killed or captured.

Once the conditions are fulfilled, the Black Dragon Ninjas will send an assault to the player's base. This assault will consist of two squads, each made up of 8-10 [Black Dragon Genin](/black-dragon-genin) and led by an additional [Black Dragon Genin](/black-dragon-genin).

When it arrives, the War Leader will give a short speech, then order their forces to attack the base. Breaking down gates and attacking characters. If the assault forces are victorious, they are supposed to loot food and free prisoners, then leave. However this raid is bugged in vanilla, and the forces will stay indefinitely until removed, usually running between buildings or getting stuck in doorways.
