The **Head of Agriculture** is a member of the [Second Empire](/second-empire) faction. They can be found in [Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i) with many [Legion Thralls](/legion-thrall). This character carries a [Meitou](/meitou) Short-Cleaver. They also carry a unique CPU: [CPU of the Head of Agriculture](/cpu-of-the-head-of-agriculture).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (5% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).
