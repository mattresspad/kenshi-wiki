The **Foreign Sabre** is an excellent weapon, despite the apparent social stigma against it. It provides a huge bonus to [Melee Defense](/melee-defence) with only moderate penalties.

The [Meitou](/meitou) version of this weapon can be found on [Bugmaster](/bugmaster).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x8**, meaning it can only be used as a primary weapon.

Foreign Sabre is one of the best options for [Money Making](/money-making-strategies) through crafting [weapons](/weapon-smith).
