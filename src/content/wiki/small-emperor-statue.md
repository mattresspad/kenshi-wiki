**Small Emperor Statue** is a useless [junk](/junk) Item: no purpose other than trading and only worth a small amount. This item can be found in Residential Buildings. When starting out, stealing this item along with other junk can help players increase their [Thievery](/thievery) stat with little risk (if the building is empty) and reward.
