**Hiveless Gunner** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a ranged combat specialist. They can be hired for c.4,000.

They have a chance of spawning in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), including when the location is "malnourished". They can be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps.

They have a random [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package.
