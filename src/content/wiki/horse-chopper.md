The **Horse Chopper** is the basic sabre. It is a decent weapon, and very common. It is often the preferred weapon for [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), [Red Sabres](/red-sabres), and many other [Bandit Factions](/hostile-factions) across the world.

The [Meitou](/meitou) version of this weapon can be found on the [Red Sabre Boss](/red-sabre-boss) in the [Red Sabre Hideout](/red-sabre-hideout).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x8,** meaning it can only be used as a primary weapon.
