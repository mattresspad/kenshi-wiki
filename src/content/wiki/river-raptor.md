**River Raptors** are passive, bipedal reptilian creatures with round bodies and large jaws. Though they do not hunt, they are ravenous omnivorous scavengers and will attempt to eat crops growing in any farms that they come across, as well as any corpses. This means they can be both a blessing and a curse to any settlements they encounter. Although peaceful by nature, they will fight back if attacked and will not be dissuaded from eating your crops except through violence. Their nests and roaming squads can mostly be found in the crop-heavy area of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), though they can also be found waddling through northern coastal zones, looking to make free meals of dead [cannibals](/cannibals) or the fields of [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).

A stronger version called [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor) can be found primarily in and near [The Swamp](/the-swamp). There is also a rare species called the [Megaraptor](/megaraptor) which is noticeably larger and stronger than both types of Raptors.

[Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) soldiers patrolling [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) often cull River Raptors in order to protect the [Holy Farms](/holy-farm) along the river. This is an easy way to obtain free [Animal Skins](/animal-skin).

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. River Raptors can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 18 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 5 piercing damage which River Raptors have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.77961008 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0039) + Strength (0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.83928 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0052) + Strength (0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.89894992 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt** **\=** Martial Arts (0.0065) + Strength (0.0195)
