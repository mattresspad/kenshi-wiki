As [Skeletons](/skeletons) get constantly damaged and repaired, their bodies start to wear down. When this wear becomes too much, it can be repaired at a **Skeleton Repair Bed**.

This wear also occurs on skeleton limbs worn by non-skeleton characters and can be repaired by a repair bed. These beds can also patch up organic body parts in the same way bandages do but does not multiply the rate at which they heal. Robotic body parts heal 8x faster, and blood/oil twice as fast when resting in a Skeleton Repair Bed.

## Technical Info

- **Healing:** about 1.25 HP per second per limb at normal game speed. Wear (lost maximum HP) is repaired first, with HP rising alongside it; then untreated damage heals at the same rate until the limb is full. No repair kit is needed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
- **Power:** the bed draws 10 power.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> A town's bed works only when the town has a generator, such as Black Desert City, Burn's Tower and Skinhouse HQ. In the Island Lab (batteries only) and the Reprogramming Workshop (no power at all) it refuses with "This bed needs power to operate." The owner faction makes no difference.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
