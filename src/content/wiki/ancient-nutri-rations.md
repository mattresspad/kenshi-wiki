**Ancient Nutri-Rations** are a unique type of [Food](/food) which exclusively spawns in [Old Empire Supply Outposts](/old-empire-supply-outpost), "controlled" by the [Second Empire](/second-empire).

Despite being [Skeleton](/skeleton)\-dominated, the Second Empire was still capable of producing some of the most nutritious food still available for humans and their subspecies.

A more common equivalent to this item is the [Ration Pack](/ration-pack).

## Trivia

-   Ancient Nutri-Rations are not included in the, "\_all food" VENDOR\_LIST, therefor they have no mark up applied to them.
    -   This means that the value/nut will never change.
