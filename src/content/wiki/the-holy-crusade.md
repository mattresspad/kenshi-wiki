**The Holy Crusade** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item is 'unique' to [Temples](/temple) in The Holy Nation. However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in other Holy Nation vendors.

It is a text with verses from the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) calling upon the nation to brace for the upcoming campaign against [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) who have been raiding and plaguing their nation<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> for centuries<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> — in order to completely eradicate their raids and threats and bring peace to their lands<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

A closely related book item, [King Shager's Reign](/king-shagers-reign), is likely a continuation or development of the conflict from the Shek perspective.
