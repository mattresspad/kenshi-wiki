**Reva** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She is a former [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) citizen who was trained by the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) as an apprentice but never made it to become a 'full-fledged Paladin basher' because of her anger issues. She refuses to be recruited by a male human but will join females and any other race not of "Pure Blood". She can be hired for c.6,000.

She can be found in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) or anywhere controlled by the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).

She has an Honorable [Personality](/personality) and after recruitment will have the "Player flotsam" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

Reva shares several lines with her fellow flotsam [Digna](/digna), most of them verbally attacking [Griffin](/griffin), mostly over his past. She has three lines of dialogue exclusive to her, triggered when Griffin first enters Heng. She also shares dialogue with the other Flotsam recruits, though mostly information on the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains).
