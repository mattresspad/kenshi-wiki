**Quin** is a member of the [Skeletons](/skeletons) faction. He is located in the [Scraphouse](/scraphouse): the big ancient factory next to [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city). He has a lot of highly valuable goods for sale such as hard to get [blueprints](/blueprints), high quality [weapons](/weapons), high quality [crossbows](/crossbow-class), [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) and the rare [AI Cores](/ai-core) (which do not respawn).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## Interactions

When Quin interacts with a [Hiver](/hive) he will say his usual line, but with a twist: "Welcome, dear huma-uh... welcome dear. Please excuse the mess. Anything shiny, Dack hoards. A simpleton really. I'm sure his mind was fried when he got hit on the head back in the cha- uh... back some time ago...".

When Quin interacts with a [Skeleton](/skeleton) he will also say his usual line but refer back to the collapse of the First Empire: "Welcome, and please excuse the mess. Anything shiny, Dack hoards. A simpleton, really. I'm sure his mind was fried when he got hit on the head back during the chaos age. Trying times I'm sure you remember..."
