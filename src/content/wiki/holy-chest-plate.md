The **Holy Chest Plate** is a Heavy Armour that is equipped in the Body Armour slot. This item can be purchased from [Holy Armour Shop](/holy-armour-shop). Several options of colouring of this armour exists, depending on which [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) character owns them. A white (blank) version of this armour can be [dyed](/colour-scheme) by the player.

It is the basic uniform of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), as such it is worn by its soldiers and leaders: [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), [High Overseers](/high-overseer), [High Paladins](/high-paladin), [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel), [Inquisitors](/inquisitor), [Paladins](/paladin), [Paladin Guardians](/paladin-guardian), [Protector of the Flame](/protector-of-the-flame), and [Strayed Paladins](/strayed-paladin). It is also worn by the [Cannibal Grand Wizard](/cannibal-grand-wizard), likely as loot from a fallen Paladin.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

## Appearance

The Holy Chest Plate is made up of several sheets of metal overlapping each other. The armour covers the entire stomach and chest but leaves the arms from the shoulder down entirely exposed. There are metal gauntlets that cover the character's forearms and hands. Two additional overlapping metal leg guards hang from the waist and cover the upper thighs of the character, with an ornamental circle on top of the plates.

The appearance can differ in terms of color, as different Holy Nation ranks have different colored plates.
