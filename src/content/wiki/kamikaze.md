**Kamikazes** are members of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) faction. They can only be found if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) has been [killed or kidnapped](/world-states).

They appear to be the remnants of [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> from [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> such as [Admag](/admag)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that enthusiastically went on a suicide-attack upon the rest of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) lands after [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) was killed/imprisoned. Some of them are under the command of [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain).

They are likely to have a Maniacal, Brave or Traitorous [Personality](/personality).

They can be found in squads of 5 ~ 10 in: [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) (if [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) is alive).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (15% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) or [Ancient](/ancient).

## Trivia

-   Unlike the similar [Strayed Paladins](/strayed-paladin), [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) and [Hive Bandits](/hive-bandit), Kamikaze do not appear to be solely a case of a breakdown in organization and military structures causing them to appear, as there are some of them that are directly used in [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) command under [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain).
-   As the above squads are not directly tied with the status of Esata, one could infer that the command structure within Shek Kingdom is loosely organized and each leader only controls one portion of the whole forces.
    -   This extends to [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) too, whose status only affects the Kamikaze forces inside [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) and nowhere else.
-   Kamikaze numbers demonstrate how underpopulated Shek Kingdom actually is compared to The Holy Nation, as this means:
    -   Their entire population mobilized for total war are smaller than the presence of even the outcasts such as [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) in every region.
    -   This is further supported in [King Shager's Reign](/king-shagers-reign) and [Shager's Overthrow](/shagers-overthrow), where both [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) accounts show [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) to be the one with "overwhelming numbers".
    -   In [Border Zone](/border-zone) and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam) squad spawns, their numbers are outnumbered by Holy Nation's military alone by ~9.3x/8.3x, and by ~19.3x/15.6x when compared to all HN-origin (i.e. [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits), [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws)) Human squads.
        -   This only includes the patrols that could be directly "seen" (i.e. with weights and thereby in homeless spawns) and not those that are also heavily hinted to be in the same area<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but not simulated, which would swing the estimated numbers further against Shek Kingdom.
    -   They could be launching assaults even after the latter's military command is [significantly disrupted](/strayed-paladin), [frontier](/stack/destroyed) [settlements](/bad-teeth/destroyed) [abandoned/in disrepair](/holy-mines/destroyed), and they would still be unable to raze any [Holy Farms](/holy-farms)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
        -   Following the evidence above, this would also suggest that even when HN's command is in disarray, the remaining soldiers that haven't [lost their way](/strayed-paladin) are still more than enough to fight off all the forces of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
-   Like their name suggests, Kamikaze are suicide-attack squads whose continuous losses are unsustainable, and they would (lore-wise) eventually run themselves into extinction<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
    -   However, since this effect is never achieved in-game, it could be assumed that their lingering could go on for a significant period of time before being fully eradicated<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
    -   Another piece of evidence to suggest this claim is that all Kamikaze seen have the stats of [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian), suggesting the much more numerous and less-skilled [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) have already been decimated.
-   Very few (less than ~2% of total) standard [Shek Warrior](/shek-warrior) patrols can still be found in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) and [Spider Plains](/spider-plains), suggesting that there are a few negligible number of Sheks that haven't went Kamikaze.
