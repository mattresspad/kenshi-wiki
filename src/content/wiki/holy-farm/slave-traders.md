Holy Farms will only be [overridden](/town-overrides) and captured by the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), functionally turning it into a slave farm, if [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), and either [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) or [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) have been killed/kidnapped, as long as [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are still alive and free.

They are assigned the same overrides asthe [Holy Mines](/holy-mines), it might flip to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), or a destroyed version shortly after going to the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), if the appropriate conditions are fulfilled. Possibly preventing the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) override from ever appearing for the player.

There are dialog changes to farmers if the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is dead, and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive and free, though this is not an override, and the farms will remain in [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) control.

## Trivia

-   Once this override is achieved, the status of [Longen](/longen) and [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) cease to matter for futher overrides, conversely the status and death of [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) will switch this place into destroyed variant. As a result, it could be presumed that [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) still has significant influence or potential influence over this territory.
