**Old Empire Police Captains** are members of the [Second Empire](/second-empire) faction. They can be found alongside [General Hat-12](/general-hat-12) in [Ashland Dome IV](/ashland-dome-iv).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (25% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths) or [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
