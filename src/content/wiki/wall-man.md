**Wall Man** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). This guy absolutely loves walls and wants to build 'em for you. They can be hired for 5,000 Cats.

They can be found in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), as well as [Eyesocket](/eyesocket), [Shark](/shark), [Slave Markets](/slave-markets), [Waystations](/waystation), [World's End](/worlds-end), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), and [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon).

This character's only unique dialogue, aside from recruitment, is clapping at the end of [Bard](/bard)'s song about [Harmothoe](/harmothoe). That dialogue is shared with many other recruits. They have the "Player TOUGH Merc" dialogue package. They have a Maniacal [Personality](/personality). They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
