An unnamed member of the [Vagrants](/vagrants) faction. They are a variant of [Bar Thugs](/bar-thug) which can be found in bars in [The Hub](/the-hub), [Waystation](/waystation), or in [Shark](/shark).

This thug has unique dialogue lines including an offer to sell organs and limbs. There is no way to accept this offer: instead, the player can choose multiple measures to refuse.

They have an 8% chance of having a bounty from c.250 to c.750.
