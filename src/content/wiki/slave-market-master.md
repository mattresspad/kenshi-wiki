**Slave Market Master** is a noble member of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). He can be found at [Slave Markets](/slave-markets) in [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands).

As with the other TG Nobles, Slave Market Master lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house) and is guarded by 4 [Empire Noble Guard Hooded](/empire-noble-guard-hooded) characters and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

He has Smart [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## [World States](/world-states)

If the Slave Market Master is killed, Slave Markets will be destroyed. [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) patrols will decrease in frequency at [The Hook](/the-hook).

His death will also cause [Clownsteady](/clownsteady) to become malnourished. Furthermore, if [Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) has also been killed or imprisoned then [Clownsteady is instead handed over to](/clownsteady/empire-peasants) [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

If [Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) is also killed, [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south) will fall under the control of [Free Traders](/free-traders), while [Manhunter Base](/manhunter-base) will be taken over by [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild). [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) patrol will also cease to spawn in [The Hook](/the-hook).

If [Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) and [Merin](/lady-merin) are also killed, [Samurai](/samurai) patrols will cease to spawn in [The Hook](/the-hook).
