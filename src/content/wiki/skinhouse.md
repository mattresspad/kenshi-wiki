**Skinhouse** is a location controlled by the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits). These locations include [Peeler Machines](/peeler-machine).

## Buildings

### FCS Values (Priority)

-   5x Skin House (1) (8 ~ 12 [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits))

## Town overrides

**Skinhouses** can be affected by multiple [World States](/world-states) to produce the following [Town Overrides](/town-overrides):

-   [Southern Hive](/skinhouse/southern-hive)
-   [Destroyed](/skinhouse/destroyed)

  

## Trivia

-   The "destroyed" overrides of **Skinhouses** and the [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq) are the only [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) items of type "Town" in use which are not assigned to any faction. In practice, though, the game treats them as if they still belonged to the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).
