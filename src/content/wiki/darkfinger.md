**Darkfinger** is a peninsula west of [Sinkuun](/sinkuun). It is inhabited primarily by [Cannibals](/cannibals).

Players can raid the multiple Cannibal Villages here for [Bounties](/bounty) to turn in as well as [Raw Meat](/raw-meat) (if they aren't concerned about its [origin](/human)). The ['Unknown' Cannibal Leader](/meat-lord) is found in the northern tip of the peninsula, in [First Village](/first-village).

## Inhabitants

## Trivia

-   Considering that there is a location called First Village in Darkfinger as well as the sheer number of Cannibals inhabiting the zone, it is speculated that they may have originated from here.
    -   This, however, seems to be disproven by a dialogue line connected to any [Skeleton](/skeleton) player character upon entering the Cannibal Plains, who calls the cannibals "northwesterners" while Darkfinger is not in the north-western quadrant of the continent.
