**Crafting Tech** is one of the [Technology](/technology) branches. This tech category primarily involves the creation of [Fabrics](/fabrics) and [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs).

A lot of research which the player might consider to be crafting ([Splints](/splints-tech), [Clothing Manufacture](/clothing-manufacture-tech), etc.) are actually contained in the [Smithing Tech](/smithing-tech) branch.
