**Gohan** (ご飯, "cooked rice" in Japanese) is a [food](/food) item purchased from relevant [traders](/traders) or created in a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove). It has the same nutritional value as a [Foodcube](/foodcube). This food is easiest to find in [The Swamp](/the-swamp), where [Riceweed](/riceweed) is abundant. It will also be sold at a very cheap markup in places which use the "swamper" Trade Culture, which causes it have the best nutritional/cat value.

Thanks to this being the only crop-based recipe that does not require water, it's the only food that can be grown and made in [The Deadlands](/deadlands) without importing water by placing respective [Hydroponics](/hydroponics) grow-beds on the roofs of structures.

Gohan uses approximately 1.49 water/unit to grow indoors or non-rainy but suitable environments, and none to cook. All water logistics are eliminated entirely if grown in rainy environments like The Swamp, The Deadlands or [Vain](/vain), as rain will automatically water the Greenfruit and Riceweed, making it, situationally, the single most water-efficient food item in the game.
