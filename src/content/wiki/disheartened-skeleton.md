**Disheartened Skeleton** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They are a mercenary. They can be hired for c.6,000.

They can be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps. Please note that they are one of many potential recruits which can spawn. They are not guaranteed to appear.

They have the "Player SKELETON" Dialogue Package.
