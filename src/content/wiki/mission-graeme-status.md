**Mission Graeme: Status** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) which can only be found in the [Mongrel](/mongrel) faction HQ. This item is related to the history of Mongrel. The rest of Mongrel's early history is told in [Maril's Diary Series](/marils-diary-series).

Players have speculated that Greycloud is being imprisoned by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) in nearby [Rebirth](/rebirth).
