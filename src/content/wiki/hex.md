**Hex** is an [Outlaw](/outlaw) being held prisoner in [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault). She has a c.25,000 [Bounty](/bounty). She has the Brave [Personality](/personality).

If you break her out of prison, she'll run away to check on her family at the farm. This gives her the job "Wandering Trader" and has her run around the United Cities forever, as there is no actual farm to return to.
