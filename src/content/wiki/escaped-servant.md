**Escaped Servants** are members of the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws). As their name and faction implies, they are escaped criminals such as [Lower Servants](/lower-servant) and [Rebirth Slaves](/rebirth-slaves) who originated from the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

They spawn in groups of 1 to 8. They can infrequently to rarely spawn in the [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), [Dreg](/dreg), [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), and [Vain](/vain) as homeless spawns. The Hub's is controlled by the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws), so they may also spawn as a patrol squad from [The Hub](/the-hub) and patrol near it.

An Escaped Servant will likely attack any nearby squad, although less likely compared to a [Hungry Bandit](/hungry-bandit). If they find unconscious characters, they search them and loot any [food](/food) items. As well as butcher any animal.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
