**Hive Attack**, not to be confused with the [Western Hive Variant](/hive-attack-western-hive), is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has kidnapped (_**not** killed_) the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south). It has a 100% chance to trigger if the player has a [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) within 50,000 meters of a [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) location but given that distance is larger than the map of Kenshi and the event ignores No-Go zones, it will trigger anywhere in the map with the same frequency.

Once the conditions have been fulfilled, the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) will send an assault to the player's base. Depending on if it is the first trigger of the event or if it is a repeat visit, there are different squad make ups.

1.  The first visit variant is made up of 7 squads, each made up of 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard), 1 [War Gorillo](/war-gorillo), and led by a [Prince](/prince).
2.  The second and later visits are made up of 6 squads, each made up of 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard), 1 [War Gorillo](/war-gorillo), and led by a [Prince](/prince). With one squad being accompanied by [King](/king), though due to a bug this variant of King is a weaker one than the one found at [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location).

When the assault arrives, the War Leader will shout about the queen, then order the attack, attacking gate and characters, and liberating captives. If the assault is defeated, another **Hive Attack** will trigger in 20-32 hours if it was the first visit, or in 12-24 hours if it was a later visit.
