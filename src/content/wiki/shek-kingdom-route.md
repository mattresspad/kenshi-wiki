Shek Kingdom Route is a [World State Route](/world-state-routes) where the player has allied relationship with [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and initiated dialogue with [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) that opens up the questline for SK to change the state of [Kenshi](/kenshi) that advances SK's interests.

The SK route is diametrically opposed to [The Holy Nation Route](/the-holy-nation-route).

The goal of this questline mainly revolves around destroying The Holy Nation by getting rid of all 3 of their leaders.

In order to initiate dialogue with _Esata_, the player should be either allied (+50) relations, or be at least above (+35) relations, and having killed/imprisoned either [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) or [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).

Once that is done, the following lines will be stated by Esata, depending on what condition the player has achieved.

-   (Player Name), you did what I could not... The Bugmaster/Phoenix mutt is finally dead/defeated by your sword. Reverence. To the Shek, you are no longer an outsider... You are our kin.
    -   (+60 relations with SK)
-   This day, I name you a warrior of Kral, an honorary Invincible...
-   (Player Name) The Battle Born!
    -   (+10 relations with SK)
-   Honor to (Player Name)! (Shek rally for applause)
-   Honor to the Battle Born!
-   \*Battlecries\*
-   Let it be known! An enemy of the Invincible Battle Born is an enemy of the Shek!
    -   +20 relations with SK, -15 relations with HN

She would also give you [Seto](/seto).

-   Favour, Battle Born... Take my daughter, Seto. Teach her.
-   My blood is hers. She'll prove her worth to you. Trust.

-   "Seto. Well met, (Player Name)." - Seto
    -   Seto Joins Squad

After the initial opening lines are spoken, the player will unlock further dialogues with the Esata. To do so, the player simply has to reinitiate dialogue with Esata, and tell her that you wish to destroy [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This will give +20 relations with Shek Kingdom and unlock the dialogue with Esata on HN.
