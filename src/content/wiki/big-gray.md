**Big Gray** is the leader of one of the criminal gangs of [the Swamp](/the-swamp), the [Grayflayers](/grayflayers). Despite being a leader, he does not have [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status). He can be found in [Grayflayer Village](/grayflayer-village).

He has an Honorable [Personality](/personality).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Big Gray is killed or kidnapped, it will cause the Grayflayer Village to be taken over by the Swampers. If Big Gray, [Big Grim](/big-grim) and [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) are all dead, [Rot](/rot) will be overrun by [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider).

## Trivia

-   In [Letters From A Swamper V](/letters-from-a-swamper), it is revealed that Big Gray's decisions may not always be popular with his gang. Apparently, he has angered some of them by refusing to seek vengeance for the death of a clan member, possibly an in-universe result of his Honorable personality.
-   If a player whose [faction](/factions) is allied to the [Hounds](/hounds) kidnaps **Big Gray** and takes them to the [Hounds](/hounds)' [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) in [Shark](/shark), [Big Grim](/big-grim) will initially taunt Big Gray, referring to him as "Li'l Gray" among other insults. Following this, [Big Grim](/big-grim) will ridicule the player for bringing her "a chew toy." Afterward, the other [Hounds](/hounds) in the headquarters will join in, shaming the player with accusations such as "Shaking up the equilibrium."
