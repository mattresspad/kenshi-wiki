**Skeleton Legs** is [Crafting Tech](/crafting-tech) which unlocks the ability to craft the [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs) named "Skeleton Leg". This research is needed to unlock the rest of the craftable legs, except for Economy Legs which are unlocked in the prerequisite.
