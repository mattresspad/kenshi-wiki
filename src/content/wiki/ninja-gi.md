**Ninja Gi** is a [clothing](/clothing) [item](/items) that offers no protection and can be equipped in the [Body armour](/body-armour) slot of a character's inventory. They can be worn in a set together with the [Ninja Pants](/ninja-pants) and a [Ninja Mask](/ninja-mask). A white version of this item is simply called a [Gi](/gi).

[World's End](/worlds-end) and [Mongrel](/mongrel) characters equip the Ninja Gi.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

## Appearance

The Ninja Gi is identical in appearance to the [Gi](/gi) except that it is black. It looks similar to the uniform top of several martial arts styles like (Judo and Karate) except the sleeves are removed. The top is held shut by a black belt that is tied at the character's right hip.
