**Outlaw Swordsman Captains** are skilled fighters and field leaders in the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) faction.

They can be found acting as [Boss Simion's](/boss-simion) personal bodyguards in [Fort Simion](/fort-simion). On patrols, they usually lead patrols of [Outlaw Heavies](/outlaw-heavy) and [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman).

They are the strongest characters in the Rebel Farmers faction besides [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) himself. They spawn with c.2,500-c.3,500 [bounties](/bounty) on their heads.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
