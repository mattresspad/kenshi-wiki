The **Dust King** is the leader of the [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) faction. He lives in the [Dust King Tower](/dust-king-tower).

Aside from having an army of [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandit) protecting him, there are also quite a few [Dust Bosses](/dust-boss) with standard or better equipment.

## Tips

-   The player can take the Dust King's bounty early on by hiring [Mercenaries](/mercenary-captain) as bodyguards from the nearby [Waystation](/waystation-border-zone). The Mercenaries can defeat the King and his guards ([lockpicking](/lockpicking) the gate helps limit the amount of time Mercenaries are being shot by the turrets) within the one-day contract, and the player can collect his bounty for profit and strong early-game equipment. It is suggested to do this in the morning to give plenty of time before Dust Bandit patrols return and reinforce the tower.
-   On occasion, the bandit forces inside the tower won't be aggroed by your party when fighting the guards outside. It's possible to fight just a part of the group, retreat to heal, rinse and repeat. As none of the bandits carry bandages, cutting weapons are extremely useful.

## Trivia

-   The Dust King was able to spawn as a [Shek](/shek) in previous versions of the game.
-   Dust King has an exceptional World State condition whereby it requires him to be killed, and ONLY if he is killed to work. Unlike many other characters, which could also be achieved if they are imprisoned. This implies that his faction is strong and/or loyal or somehow decentralized enough to function normally without its leader.
