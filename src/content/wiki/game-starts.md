_**This article is a Work in Progress. It may contain out of date or incomplete information.**_

Known simply as "_**Game Starts**_", players begin the game by selecting one of the 13 Beginnings which change how the player starts their game. Each start can drastically change the equipment, skills, currency, and amount of party members that the player starts out with making the game easier or harder depending on the player's chosen start. If the default choices are no longer interesting, it is always possible to [create your own game start](/creating-a-new-game-start) through modding.

Full details on the characters, including both their stats and their equipment can be found on their page, accessed by clicking their name. Malnourished: True means that all characters in the squad will start with 170/300 hunger. Limitations are if there are only certain races which can do a start. Starts marked with an asterisk (\*) limit the player's choice for a starting [race](/races).

## Starts

### **Wanderer**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>You are just a lone wanderer with nothing but a few coins, a pair of pants and a rusty sword, ready to venture out into the world. This is the way the game is intended to be played.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [The Hub](/the-hub)
-   **Money:** c.1000
-   **Relations**: Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad info:** Single character with 1 in all [Stats](/statistics). Equipped with an [Unknown](/unknown) quality [Iron Club](/iron-club) and a [Rag Loincloth](/rag-loincloth).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **_Playstyle_:** RPG (Difficulty: Default)
-   **Limitations:** None

### **The Wandering Trader**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Seeking to make your fortune, you spent the last of your savings on a backpack full of trade goods and a pack animal, and are about to start a new life as a trader.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge)
-   **Money:** c.0
-   **Relations:** (75) with the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad info:** A character with 1 in all [Stats](/statistics), wearing a [Trader's Wooden Backpack](/traders-wooden-backpack), [Wooden Sandals](/wooden-sandals), \[Shoddy\] (5% chance of \[Standard\]) quality [Trader's Leathers](/traders-leathers) and [Samurai Clothpants](/samurai-clothpants). In their inventory is 5 [Luxury Goods](/luxury-goods), 6 [Hacksaws](/hacksaw), 2 [Gohan](/gohan) as well as 2 [Standard first aid kits](/standard-first-aid-kit). They are accompanied by a teen [Pack Bull](/wild-bull) who has 25 in their combat stats (Randomized by 3) equipped with a [Bull Backpack](/bull-backpack).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   _**Playstyle:**_ Trading RPG (Difficulty: Easy)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** If playing with default settings you can find [Riddly](/riddly) inside one of the two bars in Heng as a free recruit. For a nice ~c.40,000 to c.50,000 early, due to a weird interaction provided your non-animal characters are on passive and not set to defend your allies you can attack [Harmothoe](/harmothoe) at the start with the bull you control and run to the Traders Guild guards nearby for assistance. You can then pick up your Bull, loot Harmothoe and swap back to the bull to take her stuff to avoid getting a bounty. Do the same with her Garru along with the items they hold.

### **The Holy Sword**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>A down and out and wanted criminal, scavenging a battlefield you find something interesting. A legendary sword said to be the work of the gods themselves. Someone's going to be missing this, that's for sure. But who cares? It's YOUR legendary sword now.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [The Hub](/the-hub)
-   **Money:** c.100
-   **Relations:** (-100) with both [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Imperial Lords](/imperial-lords). (Imperial Lords are not actually used in game)
-   **Bounty:** c.20,000 Wanted by The Holy Nation and United Cities.
-   **Squad Info:** A character with 1 in all stats with 3 stat randomization. They are equipped with an [Edgewalker](/edgewalkers) (Equal chance for Edge 1, 2 and 3) [Nodachi](/nodachi), [Black Cloth Shirt](/black-cloth-shirt), [Ninja Pants](/ninja-pants) and \[Shoddy\] (5% Chance of \[Standard\]) [Ninja Rags](/ninja-rags). In their inventory is a single [Standard first aid kit](/standard-first-aid-kit).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **_Playstyl_e:** Wanted Criminal (Difficulty: Harder)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** Only your starting character has a bounty and due to the fact that the Imperial Lords are not used in game you can still enter United City towns without the fear of being attacked for your bounty by the guards.

### **The Cannibal Hunters**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>"I seriously regret coming into cannibal territory. This was a stupid idea."<p>WARNING: You will be attacked immediately upon game start. Not recommended for a first time play.</p></blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [Cannibal Village](/cannibal-village)
-   **Money:** c.9
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** Two characters. The first character will start with 5 in [Combat Skills](/combat-skills) and a [Standard first aid kit](/standard-first-aid-kit), while the second will have 2 in Combat Skills and 20 in [Thievery Skills](/thievery-skills) and a [Standard first aid kit](/standard-first-aid-kit) as well as a [Splint kit](/splint-kit).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **Playstyle:** Action RPG (Difficulty: Dodgy)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** As you start in a Cannibal Village you'll need to escape quickly, or risk being captured and promptly killed. Taking off any armour which reduces your athletics or is encumbering you and sprinting to the nearest pool of water is your best chance at survival.

### **The Hive Exile\***

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>You have been exiled from your hive for not loving the queen enough, sent out into the wilds with no food or equipment. If you don't die from pheremone withdrawl or beak things, you'll be faced with finding your place among the insane human civilisations.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [Hive Village](/hive-village)
-   **Money:** c.0
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** A single character with 1 in all stats.
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Hard)
-   **Limitations:** Can only make a Western [Hive Prince](/hive-prince), [Soldier Drone](/soldier-drone) or [Worker Drone](/hive-worker-drone).
-   **Advice:** As a Hiveless (A Hiver who was exiled from their hive) you are unable to buy or sell from the traders in the villages. The best chance at survival for newer players is to leave Vain as soon as possible so you can get your hands on some first aid kits so you do not die from a random injury you receive. Or you could try your luck at looting some eggs from a Beak Thing nest at night and hope that you encounter a Hive Caravan which will trade with you even if you are Hiveless.

### **Son of a Captain**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Your father was a respected Captain in the United Cities army. He was killed in action, and after burning through your meagre savings looking for work, you find yourself penniless and hungry, with nothing left but your father's trusty katana and the shirt on your back. The United Cities are still your allies, but you will need them if you run into any troops from the Holy Empire. Those guys really hate you.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [The Hub](/the-hub)
-   **Money:** c.0
-   **Relations:** (-100) relations with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), and (100) relations with the [United Cities](/united-cities)
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** A single character with 1 in all stats. Equipped with a \[Shoddy\] (5% chance of \[Standard\]) [Leather Shirt](/leather-shirt), some [Samurai Clothpants](/samurai-clothpants), a pair of [Drifter's Boots](/drifters-boots), a (Equal chance of Catun 1, 2 and 3) [Catun](/catun-scrapmaster-manufacturer) quality [Katana](/katana), some [Gohan](/gohan), and a [Standard first aid kit](/standard-first-aid-kit).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Normal)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** The Standard first aid kit has a random quality when you create the character. If it has a very small amount of charges you can sell it to a shop, close the shop, and then reopen it to buy it back at full charges. (Of course, you'll need some money to afford the charges they refilled for you)

### **The Slaves\***

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>You are just another slave, working in the Holy Rebirth pits to build a pointless statue. You dream of the rumored free lands to the south, and hear tales of rebels to the north.<p>HINT: Get into a cage straight away to avoid a beating</p></blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [Rebirth](/rebirth)
-   **Money:** c.0
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** 2 Characters (Affected by Squad Size Multiplier) with 1 in all stats. Equipped with [Dyed Trousers](/dyed-trousers), [Dyed Robes](/dyed-robes) and locked [Prisoner Shackles](/prisoner-shackles).
-   **Malnourished:** True
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Hard)
-   **Limitations:** You cannot start as a Skeleton with this start.
-   **Advice:** Allow your characters to work until night. Once put into your cage (Or pole) sneak into a building if you wish to get a bite to eat and then sneak your way out of Rebirth by heading east. If you want to use some other slaves as distractions to help you get away in case you are noticed then free the ones on the poles outside as they are less likely to be watched by guards. Avoid the south and west exits as they can be guarded by units on Mounted Crossbows. After you escape you can get a lot of food by making your way to the [Flotsam Safehouse](/flotsam-safehouse) (Marked as Lone Shack from far away) by looping around mountain range or swimming through Wend.  
    

### **Rock Bottom**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>You've hit absolute rock bottom. You are alone, unarmed, naked and hungry, lost in the middle of a harsh desert teeming with slavers, and you just lost an arm. Good luck.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) east of [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai)
-   **Money:** c.0
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** A single 1 in all stats character missing their left arm.
-   **Malnourished:** True
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Very Hard)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** As it is against the law to be starving in United Cities territory, attempting to enter a town may cause a Gate Sargeant to initiate dialogue with you and then (depending on their personality) attack you. To avoid this, you can head east to the Old Village and find a nice amount of loot to sell. Once looted you can either wander around the desert and trail behind wild Garru, Skimmers or Rebel Farmers (Bonedog) until they are knocked out to get meat to cook on a campfire, hope to find a Hive Caravan patrolling around or go north west to the [Distant Hive Villages](/distant-hive-village) to sell your loot and purchase food.

### **Empire Citizen**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>A simple citizen of the United Cities, you lost your job and your house at the whim of a noble lord. Faced with poverty and starvation, which is a crime in this place, you decide to head out of town and find some form of self-employment before you starve to death.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** Sho-Battai
-   **Money:** c.750
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** A single character with 1 in all stats. They come equipped with a [Rag Shirt](/rag-shirt), [Halfpants (ragged)](/halfpants-ragged) and [Wooden Sandals](/wooden-sandals).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Default)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** The [United City Heros League](/united-heroes-league) group which spawns in the town can be attacked right away and the guards will assist you as they are classified as bandits. This can get you a nice head start on cats to help you afford a Crossbow, a backpack, bandages or whatever else you might want. If you find a Sand Ninja Jonin patrolling near the town and lure them to the guards they have a c.6,000 to c.10,000 bounty along with 2x Catun Scrapmaster grade Guardless Katanas totaling an extra c.4,722 to c.7,194 from their weapons alone. A single Jonin will allow you to afford a small shack in town, the research materials to learn how to craft Bandanas and the materials to get to a profitable level of Armoursmithing. (20+ from Bandanas)

### **Holy Nation Citizen\***

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>A simple citizen of the Holy Nation, you live in relative safety as long as you show obeisance to Okran and his priests. The world outside is said to be barbaric and violent and full of monstrosities, but you decide to venture out and decide for yourself.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) or [Stack](/stack)
-   **Money:** c.200
-   **Relations:** (15) [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** A single 1 all stats character. Equipped with an Unknown quality Iron Club, wearing a \[Shoddy\] (5% chance of \[Standard\]) Stout Hessian, Cloth Shirt and \[Shoddy\] (5% chance of \[Standard\]) Drifter's Boots. In your inventory will be a single [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame).
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Default)
-   **Limitations:** Only able to create a [Human](/human) character.
-   **Advice:** If you create a female character and are questioned by a Priest resist the urge to insult him or it will lead to an arrest followed by [Rebirth](/rebirth). Do NOT attempt to bribe any guards or mention Narko to them. Suggested to sell your low quality armour to the boot store if you start in Stack and purchase some Wooden Sandals so you can outrun any hostiles in the area.

### **Guy with a Dog**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>A homeless and hungry drifter rescues an abandoned puppy. It seems pretty useless. Maybe he will just eat it.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [Bast](/bast)
-   **Money:** c.13
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** Two characters. One is a humanoid who has 1 in all stats, a [Basic First Aid Kit](/basic-first-aid-kit), wearing a [Rag Loincloth](/rag-loincloth) and wielding an [Unknown](/unknown) quality [Iron Stick](/iron-stick). The other character is a baby Bonedog who has 20 (randomized by 5) in their combat [Stats](/statistics), however due to the penalties from being a baby its [Stats](/statistics) will seem as if they are zero. Over time the dog will age and the penalty will lessen, and eventually become a bonus.
-   **Malnourished:** True
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Normal)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** Picking up your Bonedog may be a good idea early as they are incredibly slow early on. There are plenty of Bonedog nests nearby which are usually attacked by the Holy Nation and United City troops. If food is still difficult to come by you can enter [Drin](/drin) from the sides (Avoid the gate) and sell off items to the bar to purchase some cheap Dried Meat. The beds in the bar are also free to use. Funnily enough putting your Bonedog and starting character in the cages in Bast will actually fill up their hunger until it reaches 200. Just beware of cannibal patrols which can spawn in the area!

### **The Freedom Seekers**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Fed up with stifled city life and taxes, you gather a band to head out and found your own home base. You have assembled a group of 5 other like-minded people, and you've gathered a good supply of building materials to get started with.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** Random [Waystation](/waystation)
-   **Money:** c.4,000
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** Old Soldier - c.20,000 Wanted by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)
-   **Squad Info:** 6 characters with a total of 5 [Wooden Backpacks](/wooden-backpack), 20 [Building Materials](/building-materials), 20 [Iron Plates](/iron-plates), 4 [Fabrics](/fabrics), 10 [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw), 17 [Gohan](/gohan), 9 [Basic First Aid Kits](/basic-first-aid-kit), a single [Standard First Aid Kit](/standard-first-aid-kit), a blueprint for [Wheatstraw Farming](/wheatstraw-farming-tech). Five of the members have a 1 in all [Stats](/statistics), along with [Halfpants (Ragged)](/halfpants-ragged) and [Unknown](/unknown-manufacturer) quality [Wakizashis](/wakizashi). The remaining character among them is an "Old Soldier" who has around 20-25 average stats. For a more in depth list of the unit's stats check Trivia below. The Old Solider is armed with an [Ancient](/ancient-manufacturer) quality [Horse Chopper](/horse-chopper) and is wearing \[Shoddy\] (5% chance of \[Standard\]) [Leather Shirt](/leather-shirt), [Samurai Clothpants](/samurai-clothpants) and [Drifter's Boots](/drifters-boots). On top of that [Storm House](/storm-house), [Storage: Wheatstraw](/storage-wheatstraw) come already researched.
-   **Malnourished:** False
-   **Playstyle:** Real-Time Strategy (Difficulty: Easy / Hard Combination)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** There is perhaps no consequence of settling in The Holy Nation despite the Old Soldier's bounty, as nobody will recognize and attempt to cash in on it. Additionally, the player may use another character to hand in the bounty and then the Old Soldier can escape, for a one-off c.20,000.

### **Nobodies**

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>A group of five nobodies, with nothing to their name, ready to go out and do something about it.</blockquote></figure>

-   **Location:** [The Hub](/the-hub) or one the two [Waystation Ruins](/waystation-ruins)
-   **Money:** c.0
-   **Relations:** Default
-   **Bounty:** None
-   **Squad Info:** 5 characters with 1 in all stats. Each of them are armed with an Unknown quality [Iron Stick](/iron-stick) and are wearing [Cargopants](/cargopants). They also have 2 [Basic First Aid Kit](/basic-first-aid-kit) in their inventory.
-   **Malnourished:** True
-   **Playstyle:** RPG (Difficulty: Easy)
-   **Limitations:** None
-   **Advice:** If you start in the Waystation Ruins in Venge quickly loot the two [Skeleton Repair Kits](/skeleton-repair-kit) found there and either rush west to [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar) to sell them for food or (A bit more risky) north east to the Grey Desert [Waystation](/waystation-grey-desert). If you are placed at the Waystation Ruins in Stobe's Garden do the same as with the other ruins but this time, go straight north (Avoid the west (Unwanted Zone) as it is populated with Beak Thing nests) until you see a water pool. Once you reach the water head north west to arrive at Black Scratch.

## Trivia

-   The Guy With a Dog start seems to be a reference to the Fallout series (The player starts with 13 cats, referencing Vault 13 and the player could also obtain a pet called Dogmeat in Fallout games 1, 2, 3 and 4). It could also be a reference to "A boy and his dog" a 1975 post-apocalyptic indie movie, or "Mad Max 2" (aka _The Road Warrior_) from 1981. Since "A boy and his dog" together with "Mad Max 2" inspired the creation of Fallout's Dogmeat the reference is hard to trace.

-   The Guy With a Dog used to start with a baby Mountain Dog, but this was later changed to a less powerful Bonedog.

-   The Beginning "_Son of a Captain_" used to be called "_The Retired Captain_".
-   The stats which the Retired Soldier has in the Freedom Seekers start are, 25 Strength/Toughness/Attack/Defense/Athletics, 20 Dexterity/Labouring/Blunt/Hackers/Heavy Weapons/Sabres, 15 Turrets, and 10 Unarmed/Medic/Katana.
