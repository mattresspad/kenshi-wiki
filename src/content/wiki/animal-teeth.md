**Animal Teeth** are an Animal product and [junk item](/junk) [Trade Good](/trade-goods) in Kenshi. They are obtained from unconscious or dead [Bonedogs](/bonedog) or [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider). Removing this item from the creature it belongs to will result in the creature's death.

Animal teeth are likely bought as trophies for decoration by those who obtain these items through trade.
