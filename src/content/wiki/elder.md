**The Elder** is the leader of the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) faction. Talking to him at [Iron HQ](/iron-hq) allows you to join his faction regardless of race with the right dialogue choices.

He is also notable for his [Meitou](/meitou)\-grade [Ringed Sabre](/ringed-sabre) as well as a valuable [AI Core](/ai-core), however, removing this core causes him to die.

## [World States](/world-states)

If **The Elder** is killed or imprisoned, it will cause the Iron HQ to be taken over by the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive). Furthermore, the patrols of Skeleton Bandits in the areas surrounding [The Crags](/the-crags) that once consisted of an [Upgraded Skeleton Bandit](/upgraded-skeleton-bandit) and 10-15 [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandit) become broken, turning to a small squad of 1-5 [Broken Skeleton Bandits](/broken-skeleton-bandit).

If [Savant](/savant) is alive and free while **The Elder** is killed/imprisoned, it will cause [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) patrols of 8-20 individuals to start patrolling in [The Pits](/the-pits) and [The Crags](/the-crags).

The smaller Skeleton Bandit presence in the area also causes patrols of Southern Hive consisting of a [Prince](/prince) and 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard) to start appearing in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden) and [The Crags](/the-crags).

## Tips

-   Looting the [AI Core](/ai-core) from Elder's inventory will immediately kill him.

## Trivia

-   Elder is the only NPC who has an AI Core as a death item that is counted as "stolen" when looted.
    -   You can place the AI Core into a Crossbow Locker to remove the "stolen" tag.
-   As Elder has his stealth category level set to 10, his Dexterity is only 10 as well. If he had no stealth stat set, his Dexterity would be 75.
