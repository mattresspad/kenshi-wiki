**Holy Construction Traders** are unnamed shopkeepers of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They can be found in [Holy Mechanical Shops](/holy-mechanical-shop).

Like their [Ronin counterparts](/ronin-construction-trader) they sell the player various construction goods and crops to kickstart research. Unlike the Ronins however, they are much more subdued in answering questions related to [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book), as the items in question are illegal within Holy Nation territory.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).
