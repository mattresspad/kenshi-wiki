**Guardless Katanas** have a large bonus to the [Melee Attack](/melee-attack) skill, while also having a hefty penalty to the [Melee Defence](/melee-defence) skill. These two combined will cause a character to attack more often in comparison to normal. Though they may end up being defeated more easily if outnumbered due to the animations Katanas use.

The [Meitou](/meitou) version of this weapon can be found on [General Jang](/general-jang) in [Ashland Dome III](/ashland-dome-iii).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **1x7**. It can be equipped in both the primary and secondary weapon slots.
