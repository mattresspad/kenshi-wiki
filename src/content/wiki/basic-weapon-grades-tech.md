**Basic Weapon Grades** is [Smithing Tech](/smithing-tech) which unlocks better craftable grades. After researching this, these weapon models are automatically craftable to any characters with the required levels.

Weapon Smithing is the only type of crafting which requires additional research to improve output.

## Basic Weapon Grades (2)

## Details

### **Estimated Time**

2 Hours

### **Tech Level**

2

### **Costs**

3 [Books](/book)

### **Requirements**

Basic Weapon Grades, [Basic Weapon Smithing](/basic-weapon-smithing-tech)

## Results

### **Improvements**

Allow Crafting Weapon Model:

-   Mid-Grade Salvage

### **Required For**

Basic Weapon Grades (3)
