The town of **Eyesocket** is located on a small hill, in the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) region. [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande) is in charge of this location. You can find it by exploration or by acquiring and reading the **[Map](/maps) of the United Cities (Central Territories)**.

This Slave Camp provides supply lines to [Brink](/brink), [Heng](/heng) and [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge).

The [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) [Headshot](/headshot), [Ray](/ray), named [Scarred Adventurer](/scarred-adventurer), named [Soldier Drone Swordslinger](/soldier-drone-swordslinger) and named [Wall Man](/wall-man) can spawn here, as well as in the [Slave Markets](/slave-markets). The unique slave trader [Garret](/garret) can also spawn at this location.

## Trivia

-   [Eyesocket](/eyesocket-alpha) used to be a town in the [Alpha](/old-world) version of the game.
-   The naming of this area could be as a result of being on the edge of the neighboring zone, [The Eye](/the-eye).
-   Eyesocket could be considered to be the 4th most impactful labor camps of all 6, as it supplies both [Heng](/heng) and [Brink](/brink), neccessary for taking over [Cult Village](/cult-village), and blocks [Anti-Slaver](/anti-slaver) presence at [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and [Heng](/heng-zone).
