**Bloodrum** is a commodity which is made from combining [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum) with [Hashish](/hashish). It cannot be crafted by the player.

It is considered a contraband item by [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

Large quantities can be looted from Smugglers (Grayflayers) in the Grey Desert [Waystation](/waystation-grey-desert) area. Each Smuggler carries two Bloodrum and ten units of [Hashish](/hashish).

## Trivia

-   Many [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) nobles have Bloodrum in their inventory, despite it being illegal in their country.
    -   The head of the Traders Guild, [Longen](/longen) openly drinks Bloodrum and even offers the player some when first meeting him.
-   Although this alcohol is made out of illegal **Hashish**, it appears that this drink is legal both in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).
