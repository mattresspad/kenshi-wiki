**Rhinobot** is a [Skeleton](/skeleton) who was a notable member of the (now defunct) [Second Empire](/second-empire). He can be found in [Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii) alongside 7 or 8 [Screamer Units](/screamer-unit). He carries a [Meitou](/meitou) Topper.

Due to a lack of moral processing, Rhinobot was at one point heavily involved in myriad atrocities of the Second Empire such as child prisons, although his exact role is unclear.

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (5% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).
