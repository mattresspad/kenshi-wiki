**Lord Nagata** is a noble of the [United Cities](/united-cities). He can be found in [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) inside the [Noble House](/noble-house), accompanied by 4 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/hooded-empire-noble-guard). Nagata is probably the noble lord responsible for the player's ruination in the backstory for the [Empire Citizen](/game-starts) start, where he deprived them of employment and sequestered their property.

He appears to be the governor of **Sho-Battai** and also has influence over [Port North](/port-north). He is a Scorchlander with unusually light skin.

He has the Fearful [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Nagata is killed, Sho-Battai to become half-destroyed. If [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is also killed or imprisoned, then Sho-Battai falls under the control of [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

Alternatively, if Nagata, Tengu and [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) have all been killed or imprisoned then [Sho-Battai is overrun by Cannibals](/sho-battai/cannibals).

If Nagata, Tengu and [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) are all killed or imprisoned while [Simion](/boss-simion) is alive, then Port North becomes controlled by **Empire Peasants**.

Samurais will decrease frequency in [Heng](/heng-zone) (77%).

If he is killed or imprisoned alongside with [Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga), [Sanda](/lady-sanda) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) patrols of 4 to 13 [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) start spawning in the following regions: [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

## Trivia

-   Nagata, [Sanda](/lady-sanda) and [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) all use the same army squads.
-   Nagata, Inaba and Yoshinaga all use [Wakizashi](/wakizashi) as their choice of weapon.
-   Lord Nagata and [Lord Shiro](/lord-shiro) are the only nobles in UC that has 4 Hooded Empire Noble Guards instead of 5.
-   The half-destroyed override of Sho-Battai won't be triggered if just Tengu being dead while Nagata is alive, but if Kana is killed as well, it will switch to half-destroyed and malnourished.
-   Out of all Nobles of the United Cities, **Lord Nagata** has the weakest combat skills.
