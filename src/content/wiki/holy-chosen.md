**Holy Chosen** are fighters for the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) that can be found on the front lines of [Bast](/bast-zone) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) against the [United Cities](/united-cities).

If certain World States are achieved, they can also be found counter-attacking the [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) at [Border Zone](/border-zone) and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

Holy Chosen can be found patrolling in large numbers making up the bulk of the Holy Army Force alongside some more experienced [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel) and [Paladins](/paladin).

Based on their appearance and weapons, Holy Chosen appear to be [Holy Servants](/holy-servant) that have been "chosen" to serve on the front lines. They possibly also include volunteering crusaders of [townsfolk](/holy-citizen) and [peasants](/holy-farmer). The name "chosen" may also imply a sense of voluntary participation — either they volunteered and were picked, or they were specially selected for their qualities such as exceptional zealotry.

Holy Chosen may be on the path to become [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel), taking this period to familiarize themselves with the Combat Cleaver, a Sentinel's weapon of choice<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It is possible that some of the sentinels in their squad were former chosen who had the skill and luck neccessary to survive and learn from the combat<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown) (80%) and [Ancient](/ancient) (20%).

## Trivia

-   The other similarly called "chosen" would be the [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), who are similarly zealous combatant followers.
-   Holy Chosen's name could be put in contrast with the [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript) of [United Cities](/united-cities), where the latter case would suggest forced or mandated service instead of voluntary servitude. It also suggests that United Cities are conscripting a larger portion of their populace for combat compared to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
-   The survivability of a chosen could be estimated to be ~20% given the sentinel - chosen ratio in their squads<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
