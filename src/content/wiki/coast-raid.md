**Coast Raid** is an [Event](/events) that is supposed to trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has a base within 5,000 meters of a [Fishmen](/fishmen) owned location. It has a 15% chance to trigger each day if within 3,500 meters, this chance scales down with distance until it reaches 0% at 5,000 meters. The event cannot trigger if the [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler) is killed or captured.

Once the conditions are fulfilled, the [Fishmen](/fishmen) will send an assault to the player's base. This assault will consist of 2-4 squads, each consisting of 20-40 [Gurglers](/gurgler) and 0-3 [Alpha Gurglers](/alpha-gurgler).

Once it arrives, the War Leader will shout a few times before the assault begins, breaking down gates and attacking characters.

**Note:** As of 1.0.64, this event is currently broken and cannot trigger without a mod fixing it, due to the faction having a "not real" faction flag.
