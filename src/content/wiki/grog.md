**Grog** is a [trade good](/trade-goods). Grog has no markup due to its rarity in any faction. Grog is made in a [Grog Machine](/grog-machine) using [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) and [Water](/water) via the [Cooking](/cooking) skill. It is stored in its own unique storage container, [Grog Barrels](/grog-barrels). It cannot be used as [food](/food).

In the description for [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw), grog is referred to as a "rum that makes you go blind".

## Tips

-   Grog is small, stacks easily, and will not be taken by Hungry Bandits if they attack your trade caravan. It is also simple to produce, requiring only unprocessed [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) and Water.
-   The average cost to make a single Grog is c.825, resulting in c.330 of profit. (Not including Markups. If you purchase Wheatstraw from Slavers (-80% cost) the cost to make a single Grog is only c.165, resulting in c.990 of profit.)

## History

Grog and [Empty Grog Can](/empty-grog-can) initially both had rusty metallic icons. They have since been switched to cleaner looking bottle icons.

## Trivia

Despite being an alcoholic drink, many Skeletons appear to have the ability to drink Grog noted by several Grog seen in [Skeleton Bar](/skeleton-bar) and [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq) and other Skeleton places.
