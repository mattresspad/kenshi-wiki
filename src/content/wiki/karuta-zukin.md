The **Karuta Zukin** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot. It can be sold by Ronin, United Cities, and Holy Nation hat vendors. It cannot be worn by [Deadhive Workers](/deadhive-worker), [Hive Worker Drones](/hive-worker-drone), and [Southern Hive Worker Drones](/southern-hive-worker-drone).

It has the exact same stats as [Kusari Zukin](/kusari-zukin) and is worth slightly more.

It can be randomly equipped by [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter), [Hooded Empire Noble Guard](/empire-noble-guard-hooded), [Moon Guardians](/moon-guardian), and [The Preacher](/the-preacher).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3.**

## Appearance

A simple chainmail coif with leather pieces for trim. The armour is reinforced with steel plates along the side of the hood.
