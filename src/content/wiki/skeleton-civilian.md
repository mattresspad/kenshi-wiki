**Skeleton Civilians** are members of the [Skeletons](/skeletons) faction. 5-6 can be found in the [Robotics Shop](/robotics-shop) in [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city).

One of them serves as the shopkeeper of the Robotic Shop.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths) or [Ancient](/ancient).
