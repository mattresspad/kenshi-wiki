**Shek Medic** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a medic looking for work. They can be hired for c.6,000.

They can be found in any town controlled by the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), including [takeovers](/town-overrides) of [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) and [Stack](/stack). They can also be recruited from Bad Teeth, [Squin](/squin), and Stack when those locations are controlled by [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen).

They have a Fearful [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player SHEK Warrior" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
