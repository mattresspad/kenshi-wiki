A shorter and lighter weapon, the **Combat Cleaver** unlike most [Hackers](/hacker-class) has no stat penalties. It is the preferred weapon of the [Sentinels](/holy-sentinel) and [Chosen](/holy-chosen) of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), as well as members of the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) and [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders). The [Meitou](/meitou) version can be found on the [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler) in the [Island Lab](/island-lab).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x8**, meaning it can only be used as a primary weapon.
