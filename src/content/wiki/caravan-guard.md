**Caravan Guards** are guards belonging to either the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) or the [Free Traders.](/free-traders) They can be found protecting [wandering traders](/caravan-trader-boss) as they move from city to city.
