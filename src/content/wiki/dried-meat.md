**Dried Meat** is a [food](/food) item purchased from relevant [traders](/traders), typically Bars. Often, players can find dried meat through looting campfires found in the world.

Dried Meat is made from [Raw Meat](/raw-meat) at either a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove) or a [Campfire](/campfire).

It is the most affordable vendor-bought food that grants the highest Nu-per-Cats value. This makes it a money-conscious pick-me-up when traveling, if caught out without food or before player farming is established.
