**Luquin** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) imprisoned within [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault). He has a c.30,000 [Bounty](/bounty). He has a Brave personality.

There are 2 books that can be found titled "​[Legend of Luquin I](/legend-of-luquin-series)" and "[Legend of Luquin II](/legend-of-luquin-series)" that seem to refer to him, and after he was imprisoned for murdering the Noble Lord Kurusaga and for sending death threats to the others, he was forced to write "[Beautiful Tengu: A Tribute](/beautiful-tengu-a-tribute)" about the Emperor. He was paraded through the streets of [Heng](/heng) upon his capture. By the time of the game, he's been in the Vault for 15 years. If freed, he will join the player's faction.
