**Tinfist** is the infamous leader of the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) and resides at their city [Spring](/spring) located southwest in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble).

He is a [Skeleton](/skeleton) who possesses very high combat skills in general but focuses on using [Martial Arts](/martial-arts). Speaking to him, he mentions his mentor was the founder of modern Martial Arts, [Gatwood the Soft](/gatwood-the-soft), who never took a life despite being undefeated.

He has a Brave [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by years of training and 'Justice!'.

The [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), [United Cities](/united-cities) and [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) want Tinfist captured or dead due to their stance on [slavery](/slavery) and as a result, he is considered a terrorist by pro-[slavery](/slavery) characters. [Bar](/bar) patrons in towns controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities) can be overheard mentioning raids led by Tinfist. It is unclear whether or not this is propaganda made by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) or truly the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) terrorism.

## Background

Tinfist's background is a mystery, almost all of his past is unknown or speculation.

It is known that he existed during the [Second Empire](/second-empire) and likely held a military, administrative, or political role based upon his (former) friendship with [Mad Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon). However, after the decline of the Second Empire, it is assumed that Tinfist fought against Cat-Lon or did not support him, as their friendship likely ended during this period of turmoil. It is unknown if Tinfist learned martial arts before, during, or after the Second Empire's fall.

For the next millennia to the events of Kenshi, it is known that he engaged in warfare and (possibly) raids on the [United Cities](/united-cities), this being due to their rampant participation in mass slavery. It is unknown if Tinfist has always warred with the United Cities, or if it is a recent development.

Tinfist now leads an organized group of [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers). The organization currently resides at the mountain city of [Spring](/spring) and is considered an extreme terrorist group by the United Cities.

## [World States](/world-states)

(Requires Tinfist to be imprisoned/dead)

When Tinfist is killed, several world states will trigger, mostly related with the expansion and strengthening of slavers who could do their operations much more efficiently without hindrance, dominating over several small and insignificant factions.

-   If [Longen](/longen) is alive, [Settled Nomads](/settled-nomads) will [become empty](/settled-nomads/slave-traders).
    -   If [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is also alive, [Fishing Village](/fishing-village) will be taken over by the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), turning it similar to a [Slave Farm](/slave-farm).
    -   If [Grande](/slave-master-grande) is also alive, [Cult Village](/cult-village) will be taken over by [Slave Traders](/cult-village/slave-traders), turning it similar to a [Slave Farm](/slave-farm).

The [Distant Hive Villages](/distant-hive-village) will become trashed ruins if [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) is alive and free.

if [Longen](/longen) is alive and free:

-   [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) will start spawning in [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Shem](/shem), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

-   [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) will increase in frequency in [Heng](/heng-zone), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert).

A '[Jonin Assault](/jonin-assault)' will be launched comprised of 20 [Anti-Slaver Jonins](/anti-slaver-jonin), with special leaders being either [Jaegar](/jaegar), [Bo](/bo), or [Grey](/grey) (all of them might not appear if the player has killed/imprisoned them beforehand).

(Requires Tinfist to be alive and free)

If Tengu and Longen are both killed:

-   [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) will fall under the control of [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers), or to the [Empire Peasants](/traders-edge/empire-peasants) otherwise if Tinfist's killed.
-   [Heng](/heng) will have the override that requires **Tinfist** alive.
-   [Free Settlement](/free-settlement) will also turn into [The Free City](/the-free-city).

If [Longen](/longen) is killed, Anti-Slavers will patrol [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [Heng](/heng-zone).

If [Kana](/lady-kana) is killed, Anti-Slavers will patrol [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

If [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) and [Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) are killed, Anti-Slavers will patrol [Heng](/heng-zone).

If [Grande](/slave-master-grande) is killed, Anti-Slavers will patrol [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and [Heng](/heng-zone).

**Interactions**

When first talking to Tinfist, the player can ask for advice in travelling to the Ashlands, collecting his bounty, or asking if they could join Anti-Slavers, the latter part will have Tinfist redirecting you to talk to [Grey](/grey) instead.

When fighting against the [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) if you go to spring you can talk to him and get comments about your actions against the camps or cities.

## Bounty

If Tinfist is turned into the following factions it will grant the following benefits/penalties:

### United Cities

-   \-75 relations with Anti-Slavers
-   \-50 relations with [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)
-   +75 relations with United Cities
-   c.100,000 bounty reward

### The Holy Nation

_Note: Unlike many other high bounty characters (i.e. [Moll](/moll), [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem), etc.) turning in Tinfist does NOT grant you a speech with [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). This is likely due to a bug or error, because the Inquisitor states that you could talk with Phoenix after turning in Tinfist. This is caused by the fact that the relation gain in of itself isn't big enough to unlock the condition of talking with Phoenix (+70) from neutral, unless the player already has at least +15 relations with The Holy Nation prior._

-   \-75 relations with Anti-Slavers
-   +55 relations with The Holy Nation
-   The [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) is supposed to give bounty money, however, since Tinfist doesn't actually have a bounty assigned from The Holy Nation in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set), this doesn't happen.

If Tinfist is imprisoned/killed, the player can speak to [Longen](/longen) of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) and receive the following benefits:

-   +70 relations with Traders Guild
-   +70 relations with Slave Traders
-   Receive 2 [Leviathan Pearls](/leviathan-pearl)
-   Gain recruit [Yamdu](/yamdu)

## Tips

-   At an [Assassination](/assassination) skill of 100, Tinfist has a 50% chance to be knocked out, have this combined with [Assassin's Rags](/assassins-rags) (1.3x), Tinfist will have 100% chance to be knocked out.
-   If you kill enough Anti-Slavers from his squad and knock Tinfist out be sure to pick him up immediately. Do not put him inside one of his cages. If you do so and then unlock the cage, or he gets back up after knocked unconscious he can spawn multiple Anti-Slavers to assist him. (Common with many faction leaders, but his squad is arguably the most dangerous)
-   Although he does not use a weapon normally, if enslaved (or caged) he will loot weapons from nearby and use them. He also has a very high skill with weapons (other than Sabre and Katanas) and has a habit of using the Harpoons around Spring if he is pulled outside.
-   If Tinfist does man one of the Harpoons, TAKE CARE. He will shoot you and assuming you are close to him he may attempt to attack you with his martial arts right after. As you will be staggered from the harpoon this could lead to a lot of damage or even death.

## Trivia

-   Judging by the above quote (spoken by a skeleton in Black Desert City) Tinfist was very likely a good friend of Cat-Lon before he went mad and was possibly involved in the management of the Second Empire.
-   Tinfist possesses a unique stat line in the Forgotten Construction Set, giving him a Martial Arts skill of 110. Due to this he is the hardest hitting unit in game. He is also capable of leveling up to the mid 500s provided he has a steady supply of units to train on.
-   Tinfist also is one of two NPCs in the game to actually have a set robotics level. [Bo](/bo) is the other character.
