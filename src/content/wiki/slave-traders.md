The **Slave Traders** are a faction composed of the practitioners of [Slavery](/slavery), they typically engage in trading and capture of slaves as well as in working the slaves on farms and mines. They also have close ties to most factions that engage in its practice, including [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and the [United Cities](/united-cities).

The [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) wields significant influence over this faction. Considering all 'Slave Masters' and 'Slave Mistresses' have membership in the guild, one might say the Slave Traders are simply an extension of the former. Meanwhile, the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) lead by [Tinfist](/tinfist) are the mortal enemies of this faction and its allies. Some smaller independent factions exist directly to support their commerce, such as the [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) The [Samurai Police](/samurai-police) will also allow United Cities [police stations](/police-station) [prisoners](/bounty) be handed over to the Slave Traders.

The Slave Traders run several outposts of their own in the world, ranging from markets, to farms and mines. The player can sell captives to the slavers found in certain places in order to make some extra money. Many of their goods can also be bought at a significantly cheaper price than usual. Certain items are banned inside the Slave Trader-controlled territory and will incur penalties for possession ([Bloodrum](/bloodrum), [Hashish](/hashish), [Hemp](/hemp)).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   You can gain relations from this faction by purchasing slaves. It is possible to become allied with the Slave Traders by purchasing a large number of slaves with purchase improving the relations by 1 point.
-   Donating money to [Koin](/koin) will grant you +1/+3/+10/+25/+50 by donating 5k/10k/20k/50k/100k.
-   Talking to [Koin](/koin) about influence and having at least a level 2 [Outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) and paying 10k will grant you +60 relations.
-   Purchasing Traders Guild membership from [Yamdu](/yamdu) will give +75 relations with Slave Traders.
-   Joining [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) by talking to [Simion](/boss-simion) will grant -25 relations.
-   Joining [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) by talking to [Grey](/grey) will grant -30 relations.

## Trivia

-   Slave Traders while being closely related to United Cities and Traders Guild are an entirely different faction. This can be seen in:
    -   **Slave Traders** [Pacifier](/pacifiers) asking for non-enemy relations with [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) to be smoothed over, and accounts the payment for their relations separately.
    -   [Anti-Slaver](/anti-slaver) [Pacifier](/pacifiers) refusing service to those with allied relations with [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), but not those allied with **Slave Traders**.
-   Slave Traders are hinted to be at least somewhat related to The Holy Nation and Western Hive, given their relation list that acknowledges their existence and town overrides of their settlements.
    -   In particular, they share the same opinion of [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction) as The Holy Nation does.
