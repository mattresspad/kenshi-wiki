<figure class="quote"><blockquote>My family taught me everything, lessons those bastards could never take away. But they left me alive, and that was their mistake. You fight bandits, yeah? Kill 'em dead? They tried to ruin me with fear, but I'll show them what fear really is.</blockquote></figure>

**Recruit Seeking Revenge** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She wants revenge for her family's deaths. She will join the player after they give her c.3,000 to pay her [Flophouse](/flophouse) bill.

She can be found in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), Waystation and Waystation Four, and [World's End](/worlds-end). She also has a chance of spawning in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), including when the location is "malnourished".

Her race is randomly determined by her faction ([Drifters](/drifters)). She has a Brave [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
