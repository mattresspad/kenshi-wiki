The **Leather Armour Crafting Bench** is a crafting [building](/buildings-list) type introduced in patch 0.50. This crafting station allows characters to craft both leather-based armour and clothing by hand. Prior to version 0.67.2 the Leather Armour Crafting Bench used [Fabric](/fabrics) rather than [Leather](/leather) to make armour.
