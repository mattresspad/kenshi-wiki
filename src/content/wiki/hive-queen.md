The **Hive Queens** are a [subrace](/races) of the [Hives](/hive). There are only two characters which belong to this race: [The Queen of the West](/the-queen) and the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south).

Unlike the other Hiver breeds, Queens have an artificial metal tank, their dispenser unit, <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> connected to their torso, and circular metal plates throughout their spines. The Queens also have six antennae, instead of the single one most Hivers possess.

Despite the queen appearing as an organic creature, she counts as a machine. She cannot be healed with bandages. She does however need to eat even though her hunger meter is hidden and will die of starvation if kept in a player owned cage without food.

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race are immune to acid and will not be harmed by these environmental effects:

-   [Acid Ground](/acid-ground)
-   Acid Rain
-   [Swimming](/swimming) in acid

_Characters of this race are not immune to these types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which this weather can occur._

-   Burning
-   Duststorms
-   Poison Gas

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Field Medic](/field-medic)
-   [Science](/science)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

## Trivia

-   Queens are the slowest breed of Hivers.
-   While they are part of the [Hive](/hive) race in terms of lore, they are not recognized as hivers by the game internally.
-   Hive Queens are the only robotic race which actually swims in water.
