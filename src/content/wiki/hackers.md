**Hackers** are a class of [weapons](/weapons) that specialize in a balance between cutting and blunt damage. These weapons all deal additional damage to [Robots](/robots) and have some of the highest Armour Penetration values in game, at the expense of slightly lower damage against [Animals](/fauna).

The Hackers skill determines damage with weapons classified as such. Only the Short-Cleaver can be equipped in the secondary weapon slot.

## Hacker Class Weapons

All hackers feature:

-   **Damage vs animals**: -10%
-   A varying degree of **Damage vs Robots** bonuses
-   Armour penetration of +30% (with the exception of [Short-Cleaver](/short-cleaver), which has +10%)

## Locations

Hackers are a relatively uncommon weapon type and rarely show up in shops (with the exception of Long Cleavers, which are commonly sold by Sheks). Low quality Flesh Cleavers can be found on [Cannibals](/cannibals) and [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits). [Paladin's Cross](/paladins-cross) is wielded by [Paladins](/paladin), who tend to travel in large groups and have decent stats, making this an unlikely place to get hackers from. [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) frequently wield Rusted Blade quality Combat Cleavers, making them a good source of hackers in the early game. Various medium-high quality hackers spawn via the [Artifacts](/artifacts) system.

## Meitou Weapon Locations

_Main article: [Meitou](/meitou)_

There are four Meitou weapons which belong to the Hacker Class:

-   Combat Cleaver can be found on [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler) in [Island Lab](/island-lab)
-   Moon Cleaver can be found on [The Preacher](/the-preacher) in [Cult Village](/cult-village)
-   Paladin's Cross can be found on [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   Short-Cleaver can be found on [Head of Agriculture](/head-of-agriculture) in [Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i)

There are no Flesh Cleavers or Long Cleavers of Meitou quality.
