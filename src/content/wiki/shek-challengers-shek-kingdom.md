**Shek Challengers** is an event that can trigger if you defeat any of the following events:

-   [Enemy Hunt](/enemy-hunt) (30%, 72 ~ 150 hours) (~4.6 days)
-   [Tribute Round](/tribute-round) (100%, 6 ~ 12 hours) (~0.4 days)
-   [Return For Blood](/return-for-blood) (100%, 120 ~ 180 hours) (~6.3 days)
-   [Return For Tribute](/return-for-tribute) (100%, 48 ~ 72 hours) (~2.5 days)
-   [Shek Revenge](/shek-revenge) (60%, 200 ~ 272 hours) (~9.8 days)

It also requires that [Bayan](/bayan), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Seto](/seto), [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) to all not be killed or captured and be _not_ allied to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

Once the conditions are met, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will launch an assault composed of 3 ~ 4 squads (an average of 20 individuals) composed of:

-   6 ~ 9 [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)
-   0 ~ 1 [Shek Scout](/shek-scout)

Once the assault arrives, the war leader will make a short speech about how they want to fight you, before launching their assault.

If victorious, the assault will leave shortly after, and a [Return For Tribute](/return-for-tribute) event will trigger almost instantly. If defeated, the [Shek Revenge](/shek-revenge) event will trigger in 72-150 hours.

## Trivia

-   This event is known as "Shek Assault (loss trigger)" in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
