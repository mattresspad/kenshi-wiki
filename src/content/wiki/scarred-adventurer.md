**Scarred Adventurer** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They are a freelance hitman who almost lost an eye while attempting to open a can with a knife. They can be hired for c.6,000.

This character can be found in [Eyesocket](/eyesocket) and [Slave Markets](/slave-markets). They also have a chance of spawning in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), including when the location is "malnourished".

Their race is randomly determined by their faction ([Drifters](/drifters)). They have a Traitorous [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
