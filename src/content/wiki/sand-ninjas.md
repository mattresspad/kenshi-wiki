The **Sand Ninjas** are a [Faction](/factions) of bandits led by [Sand Ninja Oni](/sand-ninja-oni).

They are based mostly in and around [Spine Canyon](/spine-canyon), as well as [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [The Eye](/the-eye) that are adjacent to them.

The Sand Ninjas are a cult of highly skilled highwaymen, who attack any passersby they deem weak. Some ninjas must go through cult challenges to prove their worthiness as lieutenants: such as surviving the badlands alone. These challenges might involve getting limbs broken as Sand Ninja Jounins are among the few characters in game which carry [splint kits](/splint-kit). However, they lack the AI goal to actually use the kits.

Sand Ninjas will no longer [raid](/ninja-raid) the player's [outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) if their [leader](/sand-ninja-oni) is [kidnapped or killed](/world-states), or if the player is allied with the [United Cities](/united-cities) or the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
