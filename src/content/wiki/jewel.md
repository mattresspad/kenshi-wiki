**Jewel** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She is a cook who used to own a bar, but Shek barbarians took it over.

She can be found in any town controlled by [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). She can also be found in [Town Overrides](/town-overrides) of these towns: [Bark](/bark), [Brink](/brink), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady), [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), and [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai).

She has a Smart [Personality](/personality) and after recruitment will have the "Player TOUGH Merc" dialogue package. She has a single line of unique dialogue; however, it is also shared with [Digna](/digna), [Izumi](/izumi), and [Reva](/reva).

She is also noteworthy for being one of the few [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander) who use a Greenlander .bod (resulting in a lighter tone of skin).
