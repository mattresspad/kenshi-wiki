**Bayan** is an advisor to the [Stone Golem](/stone-golem) of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). He can be found in the Faction HQ in [Admag](/admag), unless World States have moved the Shek HQ.

He has a Fearful [Personality](/personality).

Bayan is supposedly the one who influenced Queen Esata's decision to make peace with [United Cities](/united-cities) in order to reverse the slow decline of the [Shek](/shek) population.

## Interactions

**Bayan** can be talked to in order to propose an alliance with the Shek. He will only agree if the player is an enemy of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) or has slain the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) or [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). Additionally, he will claim that the player has already earned their camaraderie if they have more than 50 relations with the Shek. You cannot be 50 or more with the following factions in order to ally with the Shek: Holy Nation, Slave Traders, Trader's Guild, or United Cities

He can be given the Bugmaster, Phoenix, [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) for their bounties and relations with the Shek.

## [World States](/world-states)

**Bayan's** only world-state is when he is killed or kidnapped by the player. This will cause a large assault on the player with [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) herself at the lead and a few of [The Five Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles) in tow along with a large number of [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian).

## World States

### Base Events

**Bayan's** death/imprisonment prevents [Tribute Round](/tribute-round), [Return For Blood](/return-for-blood), [Return for Tribute](/return-for-tribute), [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) from being launched.

It will also allow [Stone Golem's Revenge](/stone-golems-revenge) to be launched provided that the player isn't allied to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

## Tips

-   If your goal is to eliminate (or simply defeat) Esata and her Invincibles, be sure to focus on downing Bayan first. His high level of Strength and Martial Arts should not be taken lightly. Thankfully he has a pitiful level of Toughness.
