Homeland of the [Shek](/shek)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and headed by queen [Esata "the Stone Golem"](/stone-golem), the Shek Kingdom is a very sparsely populated and tribalistic [faction](/factions) that resides almost entirely<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> within the ranges of [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), centered in their capital, [Admag](/admag).

They are a nation composed of battlehungry and stubborn tribal raiders who were unified under [Kral](/way-of-the-warrior-the-book-of-kral), a legendary Shek warrior who consolidated the warring Shek tribes into a centralized nation hundreds of years ago<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and have continued their raids and wars against their neighbors ever since<sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Their entire culture revolves around the teachings of Kral, which emphasizes sacrificing their own life for the prospect of glory<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This pursuit of glory almost led to the destruction of the Kingdom under the reign of Shager, who had extensively depopulated his own kingdom in total war against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) in large scale futile assaults that were defeated due to being far outnumbered in the field<sup id="cite_ref-:9_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Because of these strong beliefs in ‘honor through death in battle’ etc, the Shek make a formidable enemy, persistent and fearless in combat. But their warrior attitude also disadvantages the Shek, as their suicidal mindset means that their numbers are diminishing and weakening them as a nation.

They aim to assert their supremacy above all other nations and races (i.e. [Humans](/human) and [Hivers](/hive)) that they consider to be weaker. In pursuit of this ideal, they have taken an aggressive stance with regards to the outside nations, and they are no strangers to warring, raiding, killing and [demanding tribute](/tribute-round) from their neighbours<sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> to assert their dominance.

However, their recent assaults and total wars against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) (and to a lesser extent [United Cities](/united-cities)) has crippled their population demographics and driven their country to the brink of extinction, and they were forced to relent on their aggressive policies or face certain early destruction.

As a result, they had to sign a truce with UC<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and also forbade the attacking of outsiders in their cities to focus their efforts on plaguing The Holy Nation<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They also opened up their cities to outside trade until they manage to recover themselves to a sufficient degree<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their society has suffered a great fracture in cohesion, and a significant portion of<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> of their Sheks have already deserted the main kingdom itself to join [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) under [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), with the rest on the smaller ones like [Berserkers](/berserkers)<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones)<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They love to hate [Okranites](/the-holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and will often start unprovoked assaults<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> in an attempt to kill any that they see<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their nation often conducts raids to terrorize their nation<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:42_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:42-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The goal of their leadership revolves entirely around [weakening The Holy Nation's military command to gain foothold in their territory](/shek-kingdom-route)<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Fighting prowess are valued above all else in their society<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, to such a degree that they have sidelined all intellectual pursuits, and vast majority of Sheks are illiterate as a result<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## History

While the exact date that the Shek Kingdom was founded is unclear, it ranges anywhere between 100 ~ 1000 years ago<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, some time after they started sprouting horns<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and some time before [United Cities](/united-cities) and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) were founded<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

For most of history that anyone could remember, the Shek were scattered, primitive tribes living in the wilderness, divided and warring regularly amongst each other<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and their neighbors<sup id="cite_ref-:0_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This all changed after a great warrior named Kral unified the tribes together into a powerful kingdom, and led them into a march of conquest<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Eventually he was slain in battle by forces that is rumored to have outnumbered him a hundredfold in a last stand<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and attained a godlike status amongst his followers, who seek to follow in his heroic example. As a result, they have been engaged in raiding, pillaging, and other barbaric practices with their neighbors like [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) ever since<sup id="cite_ref-:0_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The frequent raids of their neighbors have contributed to [The Red Rebellion](/the-red-rebellion) of [United Cities](/united-cities), as their raiders have blockaded the routes and took the supplies for themselves<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

In more recent history however, they have suffered from significant military drawbacks against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) (who by contrast, has [now militarized themselves substantially and start beating back](/the-holy-crusade)), and their kingdom's population demographics crippled under the sustained losses in simultaneous wars against both [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities).

In the final stages of their conflict against The Holy Nation where it became very clear that they were losing<sup id="cite_ref-:6_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Shager in his fury proposed a [suicide-attack](/kamikaze) with the remaining against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) which [almost caused an utter annihilation of their country](/king-shagers-reign) if it weren't for [Bayan's](/bayan) objection that was supported by [Esata](/esata). Shager and Esata dueled with each other, to which the latter won and executed Shager on the spot<sup id="cite_ref-:6_37-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Under Esata now, their kingdom was forced to relent, and they have called off their main [assaults](/kamikaze) against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and resorted to low-intensity raids instead<sup id="cite_ref-:7_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They also signed a truce with the [United Cities](/united-cities), forbade the attacking of outsiders and opened up their cities for trade (albeit begrudgingly)<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This has caused an internal strife within the society, with many Sheks denouncing Esata and breaking off from the Kingdom, such as the [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen).

For these reasons, the present-day Shek Kingdom finds itself in a state of instability, with discontent rising as many warmongers grow impatient yearning for the day of bloodshed.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

## Society

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Around here, a Shek's standing is measured in bravery and battle skill, as in the same footsteps of Kral. Foreigners are only tolerated in this town for their trade, under the Stone Golem's orders. Every Shek in this city is itching for a fight since she came to power, especially against an honourless foreigner like you. I suggest you keep your head down if you want to leave the city in one piece.</blockquote><figcaption><a href="/shek-warrior">Shek Warrior</a></figcaption></figure>

Intellect and advanced technology have no place in Shek society. The Shek earn their respect amongst each other through strength and bravery alone<sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The outsiders are only tolerated for the trade they bring by Esata's orders, and only for now<sup id="cite_ref-47" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-47"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Kral taught them that dying in battle is the only way to find prosperity and honor, and by dying in battle they can pass to Gateway, a spiritual realm where all their great heroes reside. This suicidal mindset makes for some of the strongest warriors on the continent, but greatly decreases their population.

Shek society is divided into two castes. The upper class of warriors, and the lower class of servants. They base this upon the belief that the weak deserve to be subjugated by the strong<sup id="cite_ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, where the weak make, and the strong take<sup id="cite_ref-49" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-49"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The title of the warriors are based upon their rough measure<sup id="cite_ref-:5_50-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-50"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> of fighting skills, the most standard of them are the [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) and [Shek Scouts](/shek-scout). The Shek frequently train in combat and duel with each other to determine their rank in society, with [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) being the top 100 elites of their society<sup id="cite_ref-51" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-51"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> below only the strongest of [The Five Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles), one of the 5 being [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) herself.

The servants of the nation are composed of deserters, cowards and those that are knocked out in combat and branded as such<sup id="cite_ref-52" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-52"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> these individuals have their horns cut, are denied from combat<sup id="cite_ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and are subjected to a life similar to indentured servitude where they owe their life to the lord they serve<sup id="cite_ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This is implied to be insufferable for many, causing the formation of [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) where most of their members (most notably their leader [Tora](/tora-the-fearless)<sup id="cite_ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>) have deserted the kingdom to find freedom to fight for themselves.

The Shek love to have an excuse to fight<sup id="cite_ref-56" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-56"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-57" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-57"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>57<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and have a tendency to impulsively duel and kill others<sup id="cite_ref-58" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-58"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>58<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-59" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-59"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are also known for being very demanding and antagonistic towards others. Should their victim resist, they'd often frame it as an act of aggression on the victim's part and seek 'vengeance' for resisting their demands and assaults<sup id="cite_ref-60" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-60"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-61" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-61"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>61<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Their society is currently suffering from an ever increasingly impatient populace who wish to launch assaults upon other nations to sate their thirst for bloodshed and battle<sup id="cite_ref-:3_42-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and a growing number of them are deserting the kingdom to join [Berserkers](/berserkers) or [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) as a result<sup id="cite_ref-62" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-62"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>62<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It's likely only a matter of time before the kingdom completely crumbles from the pent-up anger and frustration.

The Shek loathe the races that they consider weaker and often target and harass Humans and Hivers that are travelling through their cities as a result<sup id="cite_ref-63" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-63"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>63<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-64" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-64"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>64<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Hashish](/hashish) is outlawed in the Shek Kingdom and characters will be searched by the gate guards to stop its contraband to the Shek cities.

Being the only faction to live in a barren land with neither any noticeable farms nor mines to support themselves, it could be inferred that their source of income comes from the tributes from the neighboring [Western Hive](/western-hive) given their [practice of demanding tributes from neighbors](/tribute-round) and the presence of Hive Trader Caravans that occasionally travel through [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)<sup id="cite_ref-:8_65-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-65"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>65<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## Military

The Shek Kingdom has an extremely small but skilled force that functions less as disciplined military but more as roaming warbands grouped together based on their rough measure of skills<sup id="cite_ref-:5_50-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-50"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The patrols of Shek Kingdom include:

-   Shek Patrol ([Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [Spider Plains](/spider-plains)) (pot. [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride))
    -   1 ~ 2 Hundred Guardians
    -   4 ~ 8 Shek Warriors

-   Shek Scout Squad ([Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [Spider Plains](/spider-plains))
    -   4 ~ 7 Shek Scouts

-   Shek Wandering Duelist ([Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert))
    -   1 Shek Fighter

-   Shek Warrior Bar ([Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert))
    -   1 Shek Warrior

When settling in the vicinity of Shek Kingdom's territory, they will send squads to [demand all the food from your outpost as tribute](/tribute-round) or [face consequences](/return-for-blood).

## Disposition

Shek Kingdom has a list of relations with other factions both inside and outside their country.

### [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

Shek Kingdom is presently at conflict with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), but the frontlines have remained in a fairly cold stalemate as their forces were called off from assaulting Holy Nation under [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem)<sup id="cite_ref-66" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-66"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>66<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> since their assaults would be blocked off by the forces of [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)<sup id="cite_ref-67" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-67"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>67<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The Shek Kingdom have raided their neighbors for food and tribute for as long as they've existed, and continue to do so till this day<sup id="cite_ref-:0_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_38-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The Holy Nation has long been terrorized by them for centuries<sup id="cite_ref-68" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-68"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>68<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_38-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

In more recent times, however, the current [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) has called for [a Holy Crusade](/the-holy-crusade) aimed at eradicating the Shek raids to bring peace to their lands once and for all. The Shek leader Shager, enraged on having being forced to held back from their raids responded<sup id="cite_ref-69" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-69"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>69<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> to this action by mobilizing and sending in a large portion of their population in an attempt to destroy Holy Nation towns and slaughter their populace<sup id="cite_ref-:1_70-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-70"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>70<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-71" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-71"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>71<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their all-out assaults earned them little success in the end, as even the Sheks themselves admit they were overwhelmed by the forces of Holy Nation, who far outnumbered them<sup id="cite_ref-:9_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Shager intended to throw all his remaining weakened warriors away in a [suicide-attack](/kamikaze) against The Holy Nation, which almost caused a complete destruction of their kingdom, but this was opposed by [Bayan](/bayan), who was supported by [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem). Esata dueled Shager to the death and usurped his position. Now under Esata, the main Shek Kingdom force is retained inside her country until an opportunity presents itself<sup id="cite_ref-72" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-72"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>72<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The Shek Kingdom wishes to crush The Holy Nation, conquer their towns<sup id="cite_ref-73" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-73"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>73<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, slaughter their people<sup id="cite_ref-:1_70-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-70"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>70<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and then raid and dominate the rest of the farmers as servants for handing in all their food as tributes<sup id="cite_ref-74" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-74"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>74<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-75" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-75"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>75<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [United Cities](/united-cities)

Much like with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), the [United Cities](/united-cities) were targets for harassment, wars, and raids by the **Shek Kingdom**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_33-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which earned their wariness and ire<sup id="cite_ref-77" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-77"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>77<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

This has recently changed under Esata, out of a necessity to sign truce with United Cities to focus their war efforts against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). Although they move on to target and harass United Cities immediately after [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) is weakened<sup id="cite_ref-78" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-78"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>78<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen)

The Kral's Chosen are a group of rogue Sheks lead by [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), who is ostensibly a Shager loyalist<sup id="cite_ref-79" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-79"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>79<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that have broken off from the kingdom. Their leader holds considerable respect among the general Shek populace<sup id="cite_ref-80" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-80"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>80<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-81" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-81"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>81<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Despite being similar to a rebel faction, they often live in coexistence with those of the Shek Kingdom. Some of their squads can even spawn inside the territory they own<sup id="cite_ref-82" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-82"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>82<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their roaming patrols will often harass and attack outsiders.

### [Berserkers](/berserkers)

Rogue Sheks who have deserted from the kingdom to find excitement in seeking fights and challenges with others, they will often attack outsiders unprovoked.

The Shek Kingdom leadership doesn't really care in eradicating them, stating that they should just be allowed to "Do what they will"<sup id="cite_ref-83" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-83"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>83<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones)

Generally composed of deserted Shek Retainers and servants who are fed up with the life of servantry and have refused to give up the sword and resort to banditry. They are generally found in the areas including and south of [The Swamp](/the-swamp). Members tend to be hornless and malnourished.

### [Western Hive](/western-hive)

Neighbors and tributary state to to the **Shek Kingdom** given the [Shek's practice of demanding tribute from their neighbors](/tribute-round)<sup id="cite_ref-84" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-84"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>84<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, with some commodities as evidence<sup id="cite_ref-85" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-85"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>85<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-86" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-86"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>86<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:8_65-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-65"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>65<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Caravans of this faction will often spawn inside [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).

### [Traders Guild](/traders-guild)

Traders that engage in various trading activities such as weapon-sales of [Catun](/catun-scrapmaster-manufacturer) quality<sup id="cite_ref-87" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-87"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>87<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-88" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-88"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>88<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Caravans of this faction will often spawn inside [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).

## Tips

-   [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) and [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) homeless spawn squads are rare examples of squads that will start unprovoked fights if you are allied with a faction ([Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)), so one should expect combat when coming close to their territory even if peaceful and neutral.

## Trivia

-   Out of all non-Shek races, [Skeletons](/skeletons) are the only ones which Shek Kingdom does not heavily despise<sup id="cite_ref-89" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-89"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>89<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, likely because their sturdy body structure and lack of fear fits greatly within their concept of being a warrior.
-   Shek Kingdom has an _extremely low_ population of Sheks relative to the Humans that neighbor them; all [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) (the vast majority of Sheks emptying out of cities like [Admag](/admag/berserkers) and [Squin](/squin/berserkers)) spawns are outnumbered significantly in every area.
    -   Compared to HN homeless spawns, Shek are outnumbered 9.3x ([Border Zone](/border-zone)), 8.3x ([Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam)), 7.9x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)), 3.6x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf))
    -   Adding in [other](/starving-bandits) [human](/holy-nation-outlaws) [factions](/factions) too, Shek are outnumbered 17x ([Border Zone](/border-zone)), 15.6x ([Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam)), 10.9x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)), 10.3x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf))
    -   Even the token HN force in core SK territory of [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) sent by [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)<sup id="cite_ref-90" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-90"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>90<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> still makes up 0.37x ~ 0.47x as much as the entire SK spawns there.
-   Shek Kingdom is the only major faction to have a tattered banner as their faction symbol; [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) banners both look comparatively more refined.
-   The number of Five Invincibles assaulting a player's outpost can on occasion exceed five due to random nature of the assault's composition. While Hundred Guardians (especially during events) that can altogether exceed a hundred. Especially more if squad multipliers are tuned up.
-   By default, the Shek have zero crossbows, fuel generators, farms and ore refineries.
    -   Once certain world states are triggered they will gain access to mounted crossbows and have no issues with using them.
-   In both [Admag](/admag) and [Squin](/squin) their towns have unusual Tier-2 [Wind Generators](/wind-generator) that's only seen in [Tech Hunter](/tech-hunters) settlements like [World's End](/worlds-end) and Waystations ([Stenn Desert](/waystation-stenn-desert), [The Hook](/waystation-the-hook)). Indicative that the Tech Hunters have settled up these infrastructures inside their towns. The complete lack of Tier-1 [Wind Generators](/wind-generator) as well as [Fuel Generators](/fuel-generator-theory-tech) that are found in both [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) would suggest that they made zero use of electricity prior to [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) coming in to build it for them.
-   Forbidding the attacking of outsiders and opening up cities for trade appears to not be a policy specific to [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), as even towns under [Mukai](/squin/mukai) and [Flying Bull](/squin/krals-chosen) still have towns opened up to outsiders. This could indicate that the Shek have been weakened to such a degree that even the less restrained leaders have to temporarily open up their cities for trade just like Esata did.
    -   However, the town overrides also have plenty of bugs and discrepancies<sup id="cite_ref-91" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-91"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>91<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-92" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-92"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>92<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and it's unclear how much of it is actually intended.
-   The Shek have a specific food storage building set to spawn inside their towns that include all manners of food from all regions, ranging from raw matter ([Riceweed](/riceweed), [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw), [Cactus](/cactus), [Greenfruit](/greenfruit)) to a large variety of processed food ([Dustwich](/dustwich), [Meatwrap](/meatwrap), [Gohan](/gohan), [Ration Pack](/ration-pack), [Dried Fish](/dried-fish)). Indicative that they rely on [extorting their neighbors for tributes](/tribute-round) and hoard their food rather than farming it themselves.
    -   [Holy Farmers](/holy-farmer) in particular would live in misery if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is killed while [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive, with lines indicating that they have all their food robbed away from them.
