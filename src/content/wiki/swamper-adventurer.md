**Swamper Adventurer** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They have a scar from getting cut in a fight. They can be hired for c.6,000.

They can be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps.

They have a Maniacal [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
