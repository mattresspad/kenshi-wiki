**Venge** consists of heated valleys and mountains. The most distinctive feature of Venge are the laser beams that damage anyone who is not wearing 100% [Burn resistance](/weather-effects#burning) armor (except the [Skeletons](/skeletons)) with cut damage.

Player characters may also comment that Venge is "where the gutters come to hunt for spiders." Although [Beak Things](/beak-thing) and [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider) as well as [Iron Spiders](/iron-spider) can spawn in this area, the primary dangers are large squads of [Weak Thralls](/weak-thrall) (likely originating from [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

This place is also a biome where 3 control towers as well as a [Ruined Waystation](/waystation-ruins) can be found.

Skeleton player characters will remark that this zone's burning beams from above are a result of "The other Eye" which is "still running", likely referring to the one in [The Eye](/the-eye) where it's largely harmless.

## Tips

-   If you do not want to risk the lasers, it is best to venture this zone at night since the lasers only come on during the day. The best time to head into Venge would be 23:00, and the best time to find cover would be 05:00.
-   When a character is inside of a building, they will not take damage from the beams. This includes being on the top floor. Care should be taken, though, when loading a save, as if a beam happens to spawn on top of a character, they **will** take damage for a short time until the game registers that the unit is indoors.
    -   This will deal a few hit points at most. It should not be lethal.
-   When building an outpost, you are not safe from dangers tied with other factions - Holy Nation and Reavers can summon a raid from the moment you build first shack. Sometimes beams will leave enemies unharmed - so it is advised to have at least one good athlete and evacuate the others in case of a raid. Bait enemies by running around in circles, while waiting for laser beams to appear and do the job of defending the base.

## Trivia
