**Silvershade** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). He's a hiveless prince hanging out in a noisy bar to quell the sense of loneliness he has from being away from his hive. He's currently worked up a debt with the owner of a casino, who has threatened to break his legs if he doesn't pay up. He'll join you if you pay his debt of c.3,000.

Silvershade should be found in [Mud Town](/mud-town), but he also has a chance of spawning in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), [Waystations](/waystation), and [World's End](/worlds-end).

He has increased [stats](/statistics) all around, not just combat stats like most recruits. He's very likely to have an [Honorable](/personality) or [Brave](/personality) [personality](/personality) and rarely will have the [Traitorous](/personality), [Maniacal](/personality), or [Fearful](/personality) [personalities](/personality). After recruitment will have the "Player HIVER Ronin" dialogue package.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

Silvershade has 2 unique dialogue lines outside of his recruitment dialogue. Neither are exclusive to him.
