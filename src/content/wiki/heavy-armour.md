**Heavy Armour** offers a lot of protection in exchange for maneuverability. Using heavy armour can slow your traveling speed but will also serve as good protection for tank characters which have Taunt mode on and anticipate heavy damage. While using heavy armour and heavy weapons, a character's strength may increase, making it easier for that character to use these items.

The following values assume \[Standard\] quality.

## Headgear

There are six Heavy Armour Headgear options. The Bucket Zukin and the Paladins Heavy Hachigane are commonly worn by members of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s military. The Samurai Helmet is worn by [United Cities](/united-cities)' high level Samurai. Spiked Helmets are worn by [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) and other types of bandits. Finally, the Tin Can is associated with various groups native to the United Cities.

## Body Armour

There are seven Heavy Body Armour options. Ancient Samurai Armour is worn by characters in some way related to the [Second Empire](/second-empire) ([Exile](/second-empire-exile)). Crab Armour is worn by the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders). Empire Samurai Armour is worn by some military characters of the Empire. The Holy Plate Chest is commonly worn by members of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s military. Samurai Armour is worn by [United Cities](/united-cities)' Samurai and by the [Mercenary Guild](/mercenary-guild). Mercenary Plates are worn commonly by [Manhunters](/manhunters) and the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders)' Guards and other types of guards. Unholy Chest Plate is a version of Holy Chest plate for people who don't want to be connected with the Holy Nation.

All Heavy Body Armours except Crab Armour are worth the same amount of [cats](/cats) for corresponding quality types. This is due to Crab Armour having a 0.8x relative price modifier.
