**Storage Boxes** is [Industry Tech](/industry-tech) which allows the player to build two new item-specific [Storage](/storage) buildings.

These buildings store [Raw Stone](/raw-stone) and [Building Materials](/building-materials). This research pairs well with research into [Stone Mine](/stone-mine) and [Stone Processor](/stone-processor) buildings.
