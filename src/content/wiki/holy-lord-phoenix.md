**Holy Lord Phoenix** resides in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) in his throne room.

He has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status) and is surrounded by his entourage that consists of 14 [High Paladins](/high-paladin) and his personal bodyguard, the [Protector of the Flame](/protector-of-the-flame).

He has a c.100,000 bounty with the [United Cities](/united-cities) and the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

He is the author of [The Holy Crusade](/the-holy-crusade) and [Holy Edict](/holy-edict).

His home contains several of the copies of important letters ([Letter To Priest Ton](/letter-to-priest-ton), [Letter To A Priest](/letter-to-a-priest), [Letter From Bad Teeth](/letter-from-bad-teeth)). These items are not unique, indicating that they were copied especially for Lord Phoenix.

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.

## Dialogue

Main Article: [The Holy Nation Route](/the-holy-nation-route)

The Holy Lord Phoenix (blessed be his name) will not talk to you if you have not proven your worth in the eyes of [Okran](/okran).

When playing as a human man, the player will get a 'speech bubble' icon when they mouse over the Holy Lord. However, if the player's relation with The Holy Nation is not high enough, when the player goes to talk with him, he will only respond "..." and one of his High Paladins will warn: "This, is The Lord incarnate. You will not approach without summon, my brother. Step away from the Holy Lord Phoenix Himself, blessed be his name." The player must have a [relation](/guide-to-faction-relations) of 75 or higher with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) to be able to speak with him in the Throne Room. Once you reach 75 relations the Lord Phoenix will give you the [Chalice Of Fire](/chalice-of-fire) when spoken to.

If the player releases Phoenix after imprisoning him, Phoenix will mark that this is a declaration of war and would have relation set immediately to be hostile with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

Conversely, if the player releases Phoenix after being imprisoned by others and doesn't equip any robotic limbs, Phoenix would mark that The Holy Nation will never forget this act of savior and will have relation be set to ally with The Holy Nation. One thing particularly noteworthy for HN is that this dialogue of saving him and having an alliance with HN can happen despite being a non-Human or a woman. If the player releases Phoenix while having robotic limbs in this scenario, there will be no relation changes.

## History

**Phoenix LXII**, blessed be his name, is the leader of the [Holy Nation](/holy-nation), the 62nd Phoenix in an [ancient lineage](/lineage-of-phoenixes) of reincarnated Lawgivers. The Lawgiver is said to be the messenger of [Okran](/okran), the chosen savior who once unshackled men from a savage life to a holy purpose under the law of God. On Phoenix I’s deathbed he announced that he would be reborn again into the body of the next first born male, and his descendants have directly enforced ‘Okran’s will’ ever since.

In particular, the Holy Lord Phoenix LXII, is one of the harshest in the line of the Phoenixes. Brought up and indoctrinated in isolation during the whole of his childhood, he was burdened with the responsibility of judging his own family at the young age of 16. He sentenced them to be purged in the Divine Flame and has unrelentingly cast the judgement of Okran ever since.

He has made it his [life mission](/the-holy-crusade) to completely eradicate [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) to end the war between them once and for all<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## [World States](/world-states)

The death of the **Phoenix** causes several different changes, mostly related to mass panic<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, demoralization<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and chaos inside The Holy Nation<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> (partially since he would be considered to be passing too early<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, before [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)/[Kamikaze](/kamikaze) are fully dealt with<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>), most of these are also asking for the world states of [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) for greater effect.

Blister Hill will become destroyed, or if both Seta and Valtena are also dead/imprisoned, will be handed over to the Flotsam Ninjas if [Moll](/moll) is alive, or to the [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) otherwise. [Rebirth](/rebirth) follows the same pattern (except for the destroyed override).

The Phoenix's death causes some of the HN patrols to diminish in frequency at [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) (83%), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) (50%), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) (44%), and [Bast](/bast-zone) (10%) (see their respective zones' homeless spawns for more information).

[Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) spawns will be in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) in greater frequency provided [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed too while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive, they will also invade [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) if [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is also killed.

His death will also cause [Shek Kingdom's](/shek-kingdom) invasion spawns at [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) become visible if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive.

[Holy Military Bases](/holy-military-base) will become destroyed and turn into [Deadhive Overruns](/deadhive-overrun). [Fogmen Nest Invasion](/fog-deathyard) will also start in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf).

[Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist) will become [half-destroyed](/okrans-fist/half-destroyed) after his death, or [overrun by Cannibals](/okrans-fist/cannibals) if [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is also killed.

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is also killed while [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive, [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will change ownership to [Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom), or to the [Kral's Chosen](/holy-mines/krals-chosen) if Esata is also killed while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive, or to the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) if Flying Bull is also killed while [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are alive (but it will switched to destroyed shortly after).

[Holy Farms](/holy-farms) will remain under HN control. They could potentially have dialogue changes from [Holy Farmers](/holy-farmer) indicating that they are hounded by raids<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and for [tributes](/tribute-round) if Esata is alive or change ownership to [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) if [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is also killed while [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are alive. If _both_ Phoenix and Seta are killed _after_ achieving Slave Traders override, it will be destroyed.

If [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) is under HN control, it will become half-destroyed after his death/imprisonment. If he and [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) are all killed, both [Stack](/stack) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) will fall into ruins.

[Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) spawns will be in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) in greater frequency provided [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed too while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive, they will also invade [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) if [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is also killed.

If a player has an [outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost), the death or kidnapping of the Phoenix will prompt several raids to begin, primarily a [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god). Berserkers will also start raiding the "holy-dog slayer". The [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) [Raid](/raid) on the player outpost will, however, stop.

If Phoenix, Seta and Valtena are all killed, the typical holy army character spawns will cease to exist (the ones in raid events will [nonetheless](/retribution-of-god) [persist](/wrath-of-god)), and instead be replaced by [Strayed Paladins](/strayed-paladin).

##### (Requires Phoenix to be alive)

If Phoenix is alive while [Seto](/seto) is killed, Holy patrols will start spawning mostly in [Border Zone](/border-zone) and slightly in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).

If Phoenix is alive when [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is killed, Holy Army Forces and Patrols will start spawning in [Border Zone](/border-zone) and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

## Bounty

If Holy Lord Phoenix is turned in to the following factions it will grant the following benefits/penalties:

### Shek Kingdom

-   Lord Phoenix can be turned in by talking to [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) in [Admag](/admag) for:
    -   \-75 relations with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
    -   +70 relations with [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).
    -   c.100,000 bounty reward.
    -   Gain recruit [Seto](/seto) if relations are over 35 with [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

Note: Lord Phoenix cannot be turned in if the player is allied with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [United Cities](/united-cities), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) or [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

### United Cities

-   Lord Phoenix can be turned in by talking to [Police Chief](/police-chief) in [Heft](/heft) for:
    -   \-75 relations with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
    -   +60 relations with [United Cities](/united-cities).
    -   c.100,000 bounty reward.

### Flotsam Ninjas

-   Lord Phoenix can be turned in by talking to in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) for:
    -   +75 relations with [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).
