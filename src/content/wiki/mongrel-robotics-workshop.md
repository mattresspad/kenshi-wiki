A **Robotics Workshop** is a store dealing in [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs) and other necessities for [Skeletons](/skeleton).

Robotics Workshops are located in [Small Shacks](/small-shack). Only a [Skeleton Doctor](/skeleton-doctor) works in this shop. There is a Robotic Bed that will restore the "wear" damage that caps a Skeleton's max condition. This bed is also usable by any humanoid with Robotic Limbs, and it will restore max condition to those limbs as well. In addition to restoring max condition, it increases the heal speed of Skeletons and Robotic Limbs just as sleeping in a normal bed helps organic parts.

There is a similar, larger shop in [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city) called [Robotics Shop](/robotics-shop). There is an almost identical shop found [Robotics Workshop](/robotics-workshop) in many other locations.
