**Legion Thralls** are the main force of the [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion). They can be found patrolling the [Ashlands](/ashlands) and in [Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i).

Although not having a head, this character appears to have an Old CPU Unit in it's inventory, indicating that either the CPU Units isn't stored in Skeleton's heads (or at least for this particular model). Or that it's simply a developer oversight.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (15% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) and [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
