The **Grayflayers** (also referred to as the Grayflayer Clan as seen in [Letters From A Swamper V](/letters-from-a-swamper)) are one of the five gangs within [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

They can be found in the city of [Shark](/shark) and within their village called [Grayflayer Village](/grayflayer-village). Their faction is run by [Big Gray](/big-gray) and, like the other gangs, they deal in [hashish](/hashish), and their smugglers can often times be seen at the [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), as well as at [Grey Desert](/grey-desert).

They could be considered to be the smallest gang relative to [Hounds](/hounds) and [Blackshifters](/blackshifters)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## Trivia

-   Most Grayflayer characters have the "Honorable" personality, which may suggest that they, unlike other gangs have some form of moral value or code. This would also support the Swamper Barman's dialogue with regards to Grayflayers "only steal from the 'bad'".
-   Grayflayers have the exact same racial proportion as [Stone Rats](/stone-rats).
