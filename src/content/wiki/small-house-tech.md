**Small House** is [Core Tech](/core-tech) which unlocks the ability for players to construct a [Storm House](/storm-house). This research is required to research and unlock Tech Level 2.

Storm Houses are required for Tech Level 2 because they are large enough to fit a [Research Bench II](/research-bench) inside of them. They have a roof which is accessible to the player, allowing for four times the building space compared to the [Small Shack](/small-shack).
