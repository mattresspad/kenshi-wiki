**Cannibal Skav** is a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. This race is exclusively used for [Scrawny Cannibal](/scrawny-cannibal) characters.

## Health Statistics

-   Max Speed: 27 mph
-   Min Speed: 16 mph
-   Hunger rate: 0.6
-   Vision Range: 1
-   Combat move speed multi: 1

### Health Stats

-   Blood: 75 - 150
-   Bleed rate: 1
-   Heal rate: 1

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race are immune to acid and will not be harmed by these environmental effects:

-   [Acid Ground](/acid-ground)
-   Acid Rain
-   [Swimming](/swimming) in acid

_Characters of this race are not immune to these types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which this weather can occur._

-   Burning
-   Duststorms
-   Poison Gas

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Cooking](/cooking)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Farming](/farming)
