**Small Backpack** is a lightweight [backpack](/backpacks) that is unable to stack [trade goods](/trade-goods) and has comparatively little space, but hinders combatants only slightly.

Small Backpacks have an inventory size of **3x3**, making it the smallest of all backpacks. When equipped in the BACKPACK slot, this backpack adds a backpack inventory with a size of **8x8**.

Can be bought commonly from [Item Traders](/item-trader) or sometimes looted from [Wandering Traders](/wandering-trader) or [Slaver Caravan Guards](/slaver-caravan-guard).
