**Leviathan Pearls** are an [item](/items) found in the inventory of [Leviathans](/leviathan). Looting the pearl from their body will cause them to die. Leviathans can be found on the [Leviathan Coast](/leviathan-coast), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon-zone), [Stobes Gamble](/stobes-gamble), [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley) and the [Bonefields](/bonefields).

Pearls will drop from both adult leviathans and their pups, with no difference in price. Leviathan pups are significantly easier to hunt compared to their parents, assuming you can separate them. Making pearl farming a lucrative business when done correctly.

Pearls can also be stolen from the Bank in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge).

As a status symbol for the rich, leviathan pearls are likely carved into junk items put on display in wealthy houses. The items made of pearl are [Pearl Cup](/pearl-cup), [Pearl Sword Holder](/pearl-sword-holder), [Pearl Urn](/pearl-urn), and [Pearl Vase](/pearl-vase).
