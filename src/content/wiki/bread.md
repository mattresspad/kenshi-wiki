**Bread** is a [food](/food) item purchased from [bars and bakeries](/bars) or created in a [Bread Oven](/bread-oven). The item can be stored in [Bread Baskets](/bread-basket) in high quantities.

  
Bread is one of the most water-efficient one-ingredient food in the game, tied with [Chewsticks](/chewsticks). 1 water = 20 Wheatstraw = 2 Wheatflour, meaning .5=10=1, each Bread requires .25 water to cook, making each unit, 30nu cost .75 water.
