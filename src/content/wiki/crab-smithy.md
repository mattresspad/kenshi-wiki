**Crab Smithy** is a vendor for the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) found in [Crab Town](/crab-town) and [Crab Village](/crab-village). The shopkeeper is a [Crabsmith](/crabsmith). A [Crab Raider](/crab-raider) and a [Crab Raider Civilian](/crab-raider-civilian) can also be found in this shop.

This store only exists as long as the [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) has not been killed or imprisoned.
