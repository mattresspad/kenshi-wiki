1824-gamedata.base **Boot Shop** is a shop which sells [Footwear](/footwear) and travel items. This location can potentially sell the blueprint for [Drifter's Boots](/drifters-boots).

-   [Mongrel](/mongrel)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might be found in:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Stoat](/stoat)
