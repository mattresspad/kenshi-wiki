**Rebel Farmers Reinforcements** is an [Event](/events) that will trigger in response to the [Eyegore's Assault](/eyegores-assault), [Noble's Wrath](/nobles-wrath), or [Noble's Wrath Assault](/nobles-wrath-assault) events as long as the [Player](/nameless) is allied to the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) faction, the player has not killed or captured [Boss Simion](/boss-simion), and [Yabuta of the Sands](/yabuta-of-the-sands) is either killed or still imprisoned.

Once triggered, the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) will send reinforcements from [Fort Simion](/fort-simion) or a camp in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) or [Heng](/heng-zone), whatever is closest. While they will move faster than most hostile events, they can still arrive too late if the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) is closer to the hostile event's spawn location than to the origin of the reinforcements. These reinforcements will consist of 1-2 squads, each made up of 0-2 [Bonedogs](/bonedog), 3-7 [Outlaw Heavies](/outlaw-heavy), 0-3 [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman), led by an [Outlaw Swordsman Captain](/outlaw-swordsman-captain).

Once the reinforcements arrive, they will give a short speech, and then start patrolling the player's base, attacking hostiles, defending the player's characters, and healing wounded allies.
