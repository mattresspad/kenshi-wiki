**Greenfruit** is an inedible raw ingredient used in the crafting of [food](/food) at a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove). It can also be turned into [fuel](/fuel) at [Biofuel Distillery](/biofuel-distillery-greenfruit).

It is grown at a [Vegetable Farm](/vegetable-farm) or [Hydroponic farm](/hydroponic-vegetables) and can be stored in [barrels](/storage-greenfruit) 250 at a time. Both type of farms requires the same amount of water per crop.

### Locations

Greenfuit can be bought from Trade Goods Traders or Holy Farm Leaders at various locations. [Mongrel](/mongrel) has them in their Shops. The [Rebel Base](/rebel-base) also has a few available for purchase as well as a Bar at [The Hub](/the-hub), both of which are [Black Market Bar](/black-market-bar) vendors.

-   [Farm Shop](/farm-shop)

### History

Greenfruit initially had a nutritional value and could be eaten raw.
