**Lady Tsugi** is one of the diplomatic leaders of the [United Cities](/united-cities). She can be found in the city of [Brink](/brink) with 5 [Hooded Empire Noble Guards](/hooded-empire-noble-guard). She lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house).

She has a Traitorous [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

The death or Imprisonment of **Lady Tsugi** will cause town override states to be applied to **Brink.**

If **Lady Tsugi** is killed or imprisoned, then **Brink** is taken over by [Reavers](/reavers). Alternatively, if [Valamon](/valamon) is also dead or imprisoned then **Brink** falls to ruins.

Her death will decrease frequency of Samurai patrols in the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) (60%).

Her death/imprisonment would cause Reavers patrols to start spawning at [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) and [The Eye](/the-eye) if Valamon is alive and free, as well as [Heng](/heng-zone) if Yoshinaga is also killed.

Alternatively, if Lady Tsugi and Valamon are both either killed/imprisoned while [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) is alive and free then [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) would start spawning at Stormgap Coast, as well as [Heng](/heng-zone) if Yoshinaga is also killed.
