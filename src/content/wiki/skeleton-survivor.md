**Skeleton Survivor** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They are a skeleton who can "distinguish the underdogs from the survivors". They can be hired for 2,500 Cats.

They can be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps.

They have the "Player SKELETON" Dialogue Package.
