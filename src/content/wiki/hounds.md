The **Hounds** are the most powerful gang in [The Swamp](/the-swamp). They are the continent's primary growers of [Hemp](/hemp), which is the main ingredient of [Hashish](/hashish), a narcotic substance illegal in all three major nations. Their leader [Big Grim](/big-grim) is purportedly "crazy," but also clever enough to maneuver her gang to the top of the swamp hierarchy. The Hounds have a [Faction HQ](/faction-hq) building located within [Shark](/shark) and a village within [The Swamp](/the-swamp) named [Rot](/rot).

## Joining the Hounds

If you build a base inside [The Swamp](/the-swamp), a pack of [Hounds](/hound) may come to visit you and tell you that, if you want their protection, you should talk to Ears.

Ignoring this request has no consequences and frees you from having to pay "Big Grim's cut" or the Hounds raiding you if you fail to do so.

### Ears

[Ears](/ears) can be found in the Dancing Skeleton bar in [Shark](/shark). Ears says that he can help your faction "get noticed" by [Big Grim](/big-grim) for a price. After paying Ears c.2,000, you can now speak to Big Grim.

**Note:** You don't have to build a base within [The Swamp](/the-swamp) in order to join the Hounds. You only need to to find Ears roaming within Shark.

### Smuggling Hashish

[Big Grim](/big-grim) can be found in the Hounds faction headquarters (the other [swamp dome](/swamp-dome) next to the casino). Grim says you can join her gang if you do a task for her. If the player accepts, they will receive [Hashish](/hashish) to smuggle into the [United Cities](/united-cities). (Notably, this is one of the very few tasks in the game that resembles a traditional video game quest.)

The closest United Cities city is [Clownsteady](/clownsteady). In order to sell illegal goods ([Hashish](/hashish)) to a vendor in the city, characters must find a [Thief Fence](/thief-fence) inside of a [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) tower or there is a chance to find one in a local bar. To trade in a Thieves Tower, players must be allied with the Shinobi Thieves.

Players don't need to actually sell the Hashish in order to complete this task. Players simply need to return with the money.

After a day or two, return to [Big Grim](/big-grim) and pay her the money which you received (or would have received) for selling the [hashish](/hashish). You are given the option to pay the full amount of c.4,000 or come up short with only c.3,000. Grim is not forgiving when players come up short. After this payment, you are allied with the Hounds and village called [Rot](/rot) becomes green on your map.

### Farming Hemp

If the player speaks to Big Grim again, Grim will give them the task of creating a base out in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) to farm more [Hemp](/hemp) for their operation. Grim will give the player up to 19 [Building Materials](/building-materials) to start them out and 1 unit of Hashish as thanks. Some of this reward may appear in the player characters' inventory if there is enough available space; anything that wont fit in their inventory will be deposited on the surrounding floor as un-owned items which you are free to collect. Note: if the items get deposited on the floor, one or more of them may glitch into the floor or another object, making it impossible to pick up. Attempting to fix the glitched item/s with the debug menu may cause a crash to desktop.

NB: From Big Grims' dialogue & previous "timed quest" to sell Hashish in the United Cities, it is implied that setting up a base in the swamp is mandatory in order to progress the "quest" and there may be repercussions if you don't. There are however, no repercussions if you choose not to set up a new base. The "quest" is effectively complete at this time and you're free to walk away & forget about it. If you already have a base inside the Hounds zone of control, the following raid events will apply to it but any existing & future bases outside of their zone will not be affected.

After picking any location in The Swamp, set up your base. [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor) will come to eat your [hemp](/hemp) farms so you should build walls and other defenses.

The [Red Sabres](/red-sabre) will launch a [Base Assault](/base-assault), but the Hounds will also immediately send reinforcements to help with this. When reinforcements arrive, they will patrol your base for an entire day and then leave. The Hounds will easily defeat the [Red Sabre](/red-sabre) Assault, as the generic NPC Hounds typically have twice the combat stats of the Red Sabres.

The Hounds will also send [groups to check in](/hound-check-in) with your base. These groups will warn you about the [Stone Rats](/stone-rats) and collect [Big Grim](/big-grim)'s cut of c.2,000. Reinforcements and check-ins will be made up of [Hound](/hound) NPCs exclusively.
