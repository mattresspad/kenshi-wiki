The **Bonefields** are a large dry plain easily distinguished by the many bones that litter the area.

This zone is relatively sparsely populated, but is home to many hostile wildlife such as [Beak Things](/beak-thing) who hunt for [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) and the unique [Boneyard Wolves](/boneyard-wolf), as well as the similarly hostile [Gorrillo Bandits](/the-gorrillo-bandits).

## Inhabitants

The Bonefields do not spawn any Camps.
