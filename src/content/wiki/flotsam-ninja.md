**Flotsam Ninjas** are the rank-and-file soldiers of [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) who reside within [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) and [Scout Posts](/scout-post).

They are fast runners that are skilled in katana, martial arts and attacking, but are rather pathetic in terms of defending.

They spawn in groups of 4 ~ 10 in [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest) and [Northern Coast](/northern-coast). And when both [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are dead while [Moll](/moll) is alive, spawn in groups of 10 ~ 15 in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (20% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) and [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

## Interactions

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Stop right there, 'brother'... My comrades have you surrounded and they're ready to kill, you're heavily outnumbered, so I suggest you co-operate. Tell me who you are.</blockquote></figure>

The first time a player enters the Flotsam Village they will be interrogated by a Flotsam Ninja, usually the leader of the gate guard. Being aggressive, uncooperative or claiming you are an Okranite pilgrim will cause them to attack.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>... Straight into the wolf den, huh? Alright, lead the way. Today we take down the Holy Nation.</blockquote></figure>

Patrols of **Flotsam Ninjas** can be talked to if one has a [Note From Moll](/note-from-moll) in their inventory. If they have a squad size greater than 4, they will then bodyguard the player for 72 hours.

## Trivia

-   Flotsam Ninja characters can rarely spawn as a bar squatter in [World's End](/worlds-end), [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Waystations](/waystation). In such cases, their racial proportion is assigned by the [Drifter](/drifter) faction, allowing them to spawn rarely as [Hive Princes](/hive-prince) or [P4 Units](/p4-unit).
