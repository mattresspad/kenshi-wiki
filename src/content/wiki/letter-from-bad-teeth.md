**Letter From Bad Teeth** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) relating to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item is 'unique' to [Temples](/temple). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in the home of [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).

This letter tells a story of a [Paladin](/paladin) killed during a [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) sneak attack. It also details of HN authorities beginning to carry on the policies of discouraging defectors with greater fervor and rigidness after this incident.
