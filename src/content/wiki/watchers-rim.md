**Watcher's Rim** is the mountain range surrounding [Arach](/arach), home of the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster). Watcher's Rim is filled with adventurers such as [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunters) and [Mercenaries](/mercenary) alike.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns), [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts), or Ancient Locations in Watcher's Rim. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Homeless Spawns.

### Camps

_These are temporary locations which can spawn randomly. They usually spawn with [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) which are free to use._

-   Bounty Hunter Camp
-   [Mercenary Camp](/mercenary-camp)
