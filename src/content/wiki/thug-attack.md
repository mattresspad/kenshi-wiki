**Thug Attack** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger as a result of failing or refusing to pay the fees in the [Traders Guild Visit](/traders-guild-visit) event. It cannot trigger if the player is allied to the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

When the conditions have been fulfilled, the [United Heroes League](/united-heroes-league) will send one of two variants of assault to the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost).

1.  The first variant will be sent if it is the initial attack after refusing to pay the [Traders Guild's](/traders-guild) fees, or if the previous **Thug Attack** event was victorious. It consists of 3-6 squads, each made up of 3-7 [City Heroes](/city-hero), and led by a [Hero Boss](/hero-boss). If victorious, the first variant has an 80% chance to trigger again in 90-150 hours. If defeated, the second variant has a 90% chance to trigger in 50-120 hours.
2.  The second variant will be sent if the previous **Thug Attack** event was defeated, and as long as [Lord Mirage](/lord-mirage) is alive and free. It consists of 4-6 squads, the first being made up of 6-8 [City Heroes](/city-hero) led by [Lord Mirage](/lord-mirage), and the rest being made up of 3-7 [City Heroes](/city-hero) led by a [Hero Boss](/hero-boss). If victorious, the first variant has an 80% chance to trigger again instantly. If defeated, the second variant has a 75% chance to trigger in 50-120 hours.
