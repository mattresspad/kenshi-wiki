**Red** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She can be found in [Bark](/bark), wandering around the city. She has a Brave personality. Beneath her face-obscuring hat and rags, she has bright red hair, quite possibly the source of her nickname.

If the player has more than c.200, and is in a squad smaller than 5, Red has a chance to pickpocket them when in talking range. The player can immediately talk to Red to demand their money back. Choosing non-aggressive dialogue will eventually lead to asking Red to join the player. \[She gives the money back after joining the player.\]

Red will not join if the player has more than c.100,000 and more than one backpack, instead running away.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
