**Southern Hive Soldier Drones** are a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. They are a subrace of the Southern [Hive](/hive).

When compared to their [Western Cousins](/hive-soldier-drone), Southern Hive Soldier Drones don't have a -20% XP penalty on [perception](/perception) skill.

Characters of this race can become [Playable Characters](/playable-characters) when bought or freed from [Slavery](/slavery) provided they are not from the Southern Hive faction. This is because the Southern Hive Faction Solder Drones are either [Droneguard](/droneguard) or [Elite Droneguard](/elite-droneguard) or [Armoury Droneguards](/armoury-droneguard) all of which are tagged as military characters meaning they cannot pass the '[is recruitable](/playable-characters#is-recruitable-tag)' check. Further the Dialogue package 'npc HIVER Southern' does not contain any dialogue that allows recruitment when bought or freed from slavery or prison.

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Melee Attack](/melee-attack)
-   [Toughness](/toughness)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Armour Smith](/armour-smith)
-   [Cooking](/cooking)
-   [Engineer](/engineer)
-   [Farming](/farming)
-   [Field Medic](/field-medic)
-   [Robotics](/robotics)
-   [Science](/science)
-   [Weapon Smith](/weapon-smith)

There are no [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) which can spawn as a member of this race.

## Trivia

-   [Slaves](/slave) have a very low chance of spawning as a member of this race, which can be recruited if it decides to join you.
-   It is not possible to recruit Soldiers of the Southern Hive faction in vanilla due to the fact that they will always spawn with stats too high and are tagged as military characters. The southern hive dialogue package also contains no chances for recruitment, and they do not use or inherit any other dialogue package if they came from the southern hive.
