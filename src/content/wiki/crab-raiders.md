**Crab Raiders** are a [bandit](/bandits) faction of [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander) who worship [Crabs](/crab). They are primarily located in [The Pits East](/the-pits-east), the location of their capital [Crab Town](/crab-town), but also have [villages](/crab-village) in [Greenbeach](/greenbeach) and [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden). They are led by the [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) and like other "pit gang" factions, they often engage in border raids and skirmishes with their rivals and share hostility towards [United Cities](/united-cities). They will also [raid](/crabbed-ones) the [player outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost), and attack neutral players unless allied.

Their strong [Crab Armour](/crab-armour), high combat stats and the usage of pet [Crab](/crab) often allows them to win most skirmishes with their rival gangs.

They will host a "[Crab Tournament](/crab-tournament)" at a player outpost, seeing you as a rival crab trainer, in order to determine if you are worthy enough to own crabs as well and become crab master.

Note: Player can buy a crab at a [Crab shop](/crab-shop) without being allied to them or doing a tournament. The player cannot, however, buy their [Crab Armour](/crab-armour) without being allied.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Relations

###  Faction Relations

_The factions this faction has special relations towards. For all others, it will use this faction's default (0)._

-   [Crabs](/crabs) (100)
-   [Reavers](/reavers) (-100)
-   [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) (-100)
-   [United Cities](/united-cities) (-100)

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Harming [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) will seriously damage your relations with this faction.
-   Allying with the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) or the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) will decrease relations with the Crab Raiders by 75.
-   Winning a Crab Tournament will increase relations with the Crab Raiders by 200.
-   Talking to the Crab Queen, and stating your love for Crabs allows you to join increasing your relations by 75.

## Trivia

-   Wearing dyed Crab Raider faction armor will make all wild [Crab](/crab) your allies due to it acting as a [Disguise](/disguise).
-   Crab Raider buildings can spawn [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) memorabilia such as [Small Emperor Statues](/small-emperor-statue), which could imply they are followers of a form of the Okranite faith.
    -   This may explain the lack of [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander) living within the Holy Nation, as they may be the descendants of exiles due to their disdain for authority.
    -   It could also imply that the Raiders have been living on the south-eastern coast for longer than their rivals such as the [Reavers](/reavers) or the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits).
-   Winning the [Crab Tournament](/crab-tournament) is the only way to increase reputation with the Crab Raiders without incurring penalties with the [Reavers](/reavers), [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) and the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) however it is rather buggy and not the ideal method.
    -   Winning the Crab Tournament is also the only way to ally with both the Crab Raiders and one of two joinable pit gangs. To do this, join one of the two rival pit gangs first and then start the Crab Tournament.
-   Allying does not decrease your reputation with the [United Cities](/united-cities), despite them being hostile to the Crab Raiders.
-   You can have squads of Crab Raiders follow you like [Mercenaries](/mercenary-guild) in bodyguard work when allied
