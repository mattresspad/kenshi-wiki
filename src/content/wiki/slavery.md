Kenshi is a harsh and unforgiving world, where desperation festers and banditry clings to every shadow, in these lawless frontiers, trust is scarce, and survival is law. In these places, money, food and even individuals are free-for-all to grab.

Therefore, Slavery is a huge industry in Kenshi, functioning both as punishment and a form of commerce, as slavers and mercenary outfits constantly scour the wastes for criminals, vagrants, bandits and various unconscious bodies to that are often patched up and chained into servitude for money and to feed the ever-hungry engines of industrial prison labor.

The ones who interact with these mechanics mostly are the [United Cities](/united-cities), [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), [Manhunters](/manhunters), [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [Reavers](/reavers).

### Enslaving others

Various characters can be enslaved for a decent c.400. In particular, [Starving Vagrants](/starving-vagrant), [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers), [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas), [Bloodraiders](/bloodraiders) and other types of bandits commonly found within the desert can all be handed over to [Slaver Bosses](/slaver-boss) once you capture them.

[Rebirth](/rebirth) and the [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits) and [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) around their territory can be handed in for c.500, or +1 relations with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

Characters will always be enslaved by slavers if they are put into prison cages (including their own people, rather ludicrously). However, the player will not receive any reward other than the bounty that was assigned to the slave (if any). If characters are assigned a [prisoner shackle](/prisoner-shackles) (and their [no-leg equivalent](/hive-prisoner-shackles)), they would get the "Is a slave" status.

In [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set), only characters that are assigned the class of "OT\_Bandits", "OT\_Slaves", "OT\_Adventurer", "OT\_Civilian" can be turned in and enslaved.

What this means is that most types of bandits, escaped-slaves, civilians (e.g. [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants)), and adventurers (e.g. [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters)) can be turned in by talking to the [Slaver Boss](/slaver-boss) or [Paladin](/paladin) in [Rebirth](/rebirth).

However, military-classes<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> ([Paladins](/paladins-cross), [Samurais](/samurai), [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)) and [diplomats](/diplomatic-status) ([Pacifiers](/pacifiers)) can't be turned into the slaver camps.

### Purchasing Slaves

Players cannot own their own slaves. Slaves purchased from slavers will become allies and will either join the player as a [Slave Recruit](/slave-recruits) or escape at the first chance they get. The player can still re-capture the escaped slaves and re-sell them back into slavery to make up for a portion of their lost money.

Characters who were sold into slavery by the player will likely remember that fact and will not be grateful to be purchased or freed by the player. However, if you were to pick up an unconscious character (Not kidnap) and drop them on the ground near a slaver they would have no idea that you were responsible for their capture.

Purchasing slaves gives +1 relations with [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), and +4 relations with the faction of the purchased slave.

### Being Enslaved

Players who have been enslaved will first have their [weapons](/weapons) confiscated and have some of their armour replaced with [Prisoner Shackles](/prisoner-shackles). If the character is unable to wear shoes, they will be given [Hive Prisoner Shackles](/hive-prisoner-shackles). If they regain consciousness, their captors may allow them to follow. Otherwise, slaves are carried by a member of the slaver squad. Players will have the rest of their [Equipment](/equipment) confiscated as well, but depending on the faction, that may not occur until they reach a town or slave camp.

Players might also be enslaved if they have been imprisoned in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) or [United Cities](/united-cities).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> If players desire to be enslaved, they can most readily achieve this state by getting in a [Prisoner Cage](/prisoner-cage) in a slave shop.

Being enslaved could be potentially a safe way of training various combat stats, primarily because slavers will always patch you up when you are knocked out to keep you alive. It's also prime for training [Strength](/strength) and [Martial Arts](/martial-arts), as the food meter will always be kept at a minimum of 100 (malnourished but not starving), which is enough to penalize stats significantly without killing you or knocking you unconcious.

When enslaved, player characters have everything on their [jobs](/jobs) list replaced with a single "Obedient Slave" job. Removing this job will result in that character being labeled an "escaped slave," although this may not be immediate. The Obedient Slave job causes characters to follow their master's orders, whether they are to farm, labour, or to fight in their defense.

There are at times however, that this job may cause the enslaved character to attempt and steal any weapon that is nearby either inside a nearby unlocked chest or at a downed character, which risks them being attacked for thievery if discovered attempting to do so.

### Escaping Slavery

Escaping slavery can be as easy as finding the right moment or as hard as having to stage an elaborate plan.

If you have the legal means and connections, you can buy the slave out of slavery (in cases of [Slave Shops](/slave-shop)). If you are allied with the slaver faction, you can simply picklock your own shackles to free yourself from slavery (the slaver faction wouldn't react or object to this action).

If you do not have such luxury, however, the important skills to aid in your escape will be Stealth, Assassination, and Thievery. Lockpicking is important too, but it's not usually time-sensitive, so you can keep trying until you get it. To start, unlock your shackles. Keep them on while the guards are around, even in your cage, or they'll immediately attack you, but when you need to sneak, fight, or run, take them off as they lower your stealth and movement skills. The guards won't re-lock them unless you're caught, so it's good to do that as soon as possible.

Secondly, if you think you'll have to fight your way out, plan ahead. Knock guards out with Assassination, and take their weapon and drop it, and take off all their armor- they won't pick them up or put them back on. This is easiest to do while they're sleeping, of course, but even if you're out working, don't hesitate to attack a lone guard. As long as the knockout is successful, and none of the other guards saw it, you won't get attacked. This is easiest at Rebirth because of how spread out they are, but at smaller outposts, guards tend to run around pretty quickly, so nighttime is your only friend there.

Smaller slave sites like Holy Mines have plenty of gaps in security, and if bandits attack, most of the guards will run off to fight them. It is not recommended to try and escape while you are in-transit unless there's an attack or something to distract the guards. Better to be patient and plan carefully to avoid a slow and painful death out in the desert.

Even if you make a clean getaway without alerting the guards, you'll be branded an Ex-Slave for a while, causing hostility to any pro-slavery faction characters who notice you.

Keep an eye on your "Looks like a Slave" percentage in your inventory window to see how likely someone is to identify you.

Finally, your greatest tool for making your escape will be the disguise. Steal a guard's armor and equip all of it (keep the shackles, until you're ready to run). They won't take it or give you new slave clothes unless you're caught without the shackles on. Once things quiet down and guards stop running around, make for the exit. Keep to the side and stay as far from other guards' line of sight as possible. Sneak, if you can, it doesn't negatively affect your disguise, but with the guards' armor, you will be slow, and your stealth rating might be zero anyway. At the gate, they usually have two rows of guards: one in the back and 2-4 in the front. Knock out the one in the back and try to leave out the side.

If you're having trouble, especially if you're in a big city, it might be necessary to cause a distraction. Have another character fight the guards at the gate or bring a hostile creature or release the other slaves and escape while the guards chase them down (be warned- they go down quickly).

If you are allied with the slaver faction, you can simply picklock your own shackles to free yourself from slavery, the slaver faction wouldn't react or object to this action.

<div class="notice loading-tip"><span class="loading-tip-label">Loading screen tip:</span> Escaped slaves should avoid most other towns and factions until the heat dies down.</div>

  

After you escape, you'll need to lay low for several game-days before the faction stops being hostile (unless you have an already low rating or bounty with them), so change your clothes and hair until the "Looks like a Slave" stat in the inventory window reads 1%.

Alternatively, if you are not on bad terms with [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers), [Grey](/grey) can immediately clear your status as a slave upon talking to him. However, unless you have a high level in toughness and athletics, it is usually unfeasible to look for him.

### [Slave Recruits](/slave-recruits)

Depending on their personality, [slaves](/slave) will either welcome the chance to escape or fearfully insist on remaining in their cage. Freed slaves will likely ally with the player for a time, following them around. Allies are visible as a green dot on the map. After leading an ally far enough away from their masters, the rescued slave may choose to join the player or separate and run away.

A slave usually will not join the player if the slave has combined stats from [Strength](/strength), [Dexterity](/dexterity) and [Toughness](/toughness) of over 75. (Some rare cases may allow some NPCs to skip this tag)

## Factions

These [Factions](/factions) will enslave characters.

### Manhunters

> The [Manhunters](/manhunters) are a minor faction which patrol United Cities territory in search of escaped slaves. If they encounter characters which they think are weaker than them regardless of how they look (unless they are surrounded by hired mercenary or nearby United Cities/Traders Guild enforcers), they will likely attempt to down and capture them as well. They sell their slaves to the Slave Traders. They will not target players if the players squad is allied to UC or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), of if the player has more characters in their squad than they do.

### Reavers

> The [Reavers](/reavers) are a minor faction found in [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [The Crags](/the-crags), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands). They can be found patrolling the surrounding [zones](/category/zones) as well. Reavers have armed slaves called [Reaver Slave](/reaver-slave) which are taken on patrols. However, Reavers still disarm player characters while enslaving them.

### Slave Hunters

> [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) are a minor faction also found in United Cities territory. They're essentially just a weaker version of the Manhunters. Unlike Manhunters, they disregard if the characters are surrounded by hired mercenary and will attack the group if they decided the characters are weaker than them. They will not target players if the players squad is allied to UC or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), of if the player has more characters in their squad than they do.

### Slave Traders

> [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) is the slave owning branch of the United Cities. They control slave farms and camps where they capture, transfer, and work the slaves. If players are on good or neutral terms with the Slave Traders, they can purchase slaves from slave shops in those locations as well as in slave markets.

### The Holy Nation

> [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) enslaves characters whom they consider in need of redemption. These characters are criminals or characters of organic non-[human](/human) races. These slaves are brought to [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) and [Rebirth](/rebirth), where they will work as a [Lower Servant](/lower-servant) or [Rebirth Slave](/rebirth-slave). Because this slavery is for religious purposes and not economic, The Holy Nation will not trade slaves.
> 
> It helps to have a [Human](/human) escort with [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame) to pacify HN patrols demanding non-humans, males have a higher chance of successfully convincing when choosing the right option.
> 
> Players can carry and sell characters to Rebirth to make a small sum of money. (Note: The guards in rebirth have no repair kits so any Skeleton unfortunate enough to be enslaved will need to be careful not to take damage. Lore-wise they were not supposed to be captured.)

### United Cities

> The economy of the [United Cities](/united-cities) relies heavily on slave labour. Players in a United Cities cage can be enslaved by the Slave Traders and taken out of town to a slave camp.

## Slave Traders

Slave Traders are NPCs. They will not attack the player as long as the organization they're a part of isn't currently hostile towards the player. However, if a player-controlled unit goes unconscious close to them, they can heal them and take them to any nearby slave-based outpost, where the unit will be forced to work. If the unit is unconscious, it will be taken first to a cage while healing. Then a slaver will come to the unit and take them to work.

Slave Traders will then watch the unit move and work and will taunt it and remind it of its duty. At night, the slave will be taken back to a cage, where he can try to unlock or destroy his shackles.

From time to time, some slavers will come to the camp and buy or rent slaves, including the player's unit, which will now follow the caravan for a while, then sometimes returning to the cage.

Slave Traders caravans can also bring new slaves, like bandits, and warn everyone that non-workers will be brought to the furnace.

If the player tries to run away, he won't be able to run at full speed because of [shackles](/prisoner-shackles), will be called a runner and marked as an escaped slave, and will be attacked by everyone.

If the character talks to the slave monger in a slave trader group, it is possible to buy slaves from them, but the slaves will not do any work other than to follow the player (unless they don't decide to join immediately upon purchase). If they do follow the player (marked as green dots, "allies") former slaves might join a player at a later time or leave them altogether.
