**Samurai Clothpants** are Light Armour that are equipped in the Legwear slot. They are sold by [Mongrel](/mongrel-armor-shop), [Ronin](/ronin-armor-shop), [Shek](/shek-armor-shop), and [UC](/empire-armor-shop) [Armour Vendors](/equipment-shops) as well as by [Clothing Vendors](/equipment-shops).

They are equipped (randomly or otherwise) by [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slaver), [Anti-Slaver Jonins](/anti-slaver-jonin), [Anti-Slavers (unarmed)](/anti-slaver-unarmed), [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter), [Drifters](/drifter), [Elite Hunters](/elite-hunter), [Flotsam Jonins](/flotsam-jonin), [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninja), [Grayflayers](/grayflayer), [Human Mercenaries](/human-mercenary), [Jonin Elites](/jonin-elite), [Manhunters](/manhunter), [Mercenaries](/mercenary), [Mercenary Captains](/mercenary-captain), [Moon Guardians](/moon-guardian), [Ninja Guards](/ninja-guard), [Police Chiefs](/police-chief), [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript), [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue), [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninja), [Sand Ninja Jounins](/sand-ninja-jounin), [Shek Retainers](/shek-retainer), [Shop Guards](/shop-guard), [Shop Guards (holy)](/shop-guard-holy), [Skeleton Doctors](/skeleton-doctor), [Skeleton Drifters](/skeleton-drifter), [Slavemongers](/slavemonger), [Slavers](/slaver), [Slaver Caravan Bosses](/slaver-caravan-boss), [Slaver Caravan Guards](/slaver-caravan-guard), [Stone Rats](/stone-rat), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard), [Tech Hunter Guards](/tech-hunter-guard), and [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x4**.

## Appearance

Samurai Clothpants are gray in color and loose fitting except near the shins where they bunch and fit tight to the leg. The waist is held up and/or reinforced by a leather belt that is wrapped around the character numerous times. There are also two leather strips that wrap around the character just below the hip bone.

If worn in addition to certain clothings such as the [Dustcoat](/dustcoat), the clothpants will appear to be tightened in order to fit in the aforementioned clothing.
