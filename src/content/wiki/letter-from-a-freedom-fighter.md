**Letter From A Freedom Fighter** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item can be found in the inventories of [Holy Citizens](/holy-citizen).

While this letter is about a female resistance against the Holy Nation, it is unclear whether it is related to the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).

It is possible that this note comes as a result of increased investigations and crackdowns on rebel activities from The Holy Nation after the events of [Letter From Bad Teeth](/letter-from-bad-teeth).

From this item, players can learn that the punishment is death for being a woman without an escort at night in The Holy Nation. The Holy Nation likely has many other laws in order to discourage secret 'meetings'.
