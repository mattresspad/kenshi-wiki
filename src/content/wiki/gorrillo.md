**Gorrillo** is the leader of [The Gorrillo Bandits](/the-gorrillo-bandits). He can be found in [The Old Prison](/the-old-prison). Upon seeing a player character, he will exclaim his dislike of "tiny people" breaking into his home trying to hunt him down.

Not to be confused with the animal [Gorillo](/gorillo), where he might get his name from. Certainly not to his face.

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (35% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Gorrillo is killed, Gorrillo Bandits will cease to spawn in [Bonefields](/bonefields) and [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon).

## Tips

-   Gorrillo will sometimes personally kick out downed enemies from his Fortress. This can lead to an easy way to separate him from his fairly large gang. When he carries enemies out, you can just ambush him, retrieve your comrade and get the bounty.

## Trivia

-   His name has 1 more 'r' than the animal [Gorillo](/gorillo) (so spelling is important here).
