The **Bandana** is a Light Armour that is equipped on the Headgear slot. The Bandana has two other variants: the [Black Bandana](/black-bandana) and the [Red Bandana](/red-bandana). This "blank" version of bandana is [dyeable](/colour-scheme).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

The Bandana is a white cloth tied around the character's face like a headband. It leaves the majority of the face and head exposed.

Crafting Bandanas is one of the more profitable options for a beginning smith (tailor).
