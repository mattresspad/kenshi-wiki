**The Holy Nation** (sometimes also referred to as **Holy Empire<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>) is a very large theocratic [Greenlander](/greenlander)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> state ruled by a [Phoenix](/birth-of-the-phoenix) whom they believe to be a reincarnation of their first ruler<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and is rumored to be over 5000 years old<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their denizens are concentrated mostly in and around [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but could be found on over a quarter of the [entire continent](/world-of-kenshi)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are the largest faction in terms of population, outnumbering others by several dozen times<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:9_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:12_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:12-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They are a zealous farming society whose religion encompasses all aspects of their life. Their religion, Okranism, is based around the dualism of the [Okran](/okran), Lord Of Light, and [Narko](/narko), Demoness of Darkness<sup id="cite_ref-:0_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Holy Nation citizens are often interchangeably referred to as Okranites<sup id="cite_ref-:5_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They live their life based on the doctrines [set forth by the first Phoenix](/scripture-of-radiance-series), which elevates men as the virtuous role model<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that assumes the role of stewardship over women<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Prior to the game start they were a nation constantly plagued by [Sheks](/shek-kingdom) who have been raiding and warring against them for centuries<sup id="cite_ref-:1_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:6_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, this all changed under the rising of their [62nd Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), a harsh and ambitious leader who saw the futility of merely defending against the [Shek](/shek) raids. Recognizing that peace would never come from a passive posture alone, he launched a bold and decisive military campaign - [The Holy Crusade](/the-holy-crusade) - an initiative that sought to rid the [Kingdom's](/shek-kingdom) raids once and for all.

The Okranites - bolstered by the initiative of their leader - fought tooth and nail against the raiders of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) whose leader Shager [launched all-out offensives of their own in response](/king-shagers-reign). In a war of attrition with heavy casualties on both sides, the forces of HN prevailed as a result of their overwhelming numbers and [struck a decisive blow to the Shek's leadership](/shagers-overthrow), decimating their forces and nearly driving them into extinction.

At the final phases of the conflict, they have managed to secure their borders as the Shek no longer had the strength to raid their nation effectively<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. But the relative peace is a fragile one, as the few remaining warriors were preserved by their [new leadership](/esata-the-stone-golem) bide their time and raid at the edges of their territory, and their threat isn't entirely gone.

On another front, in recent times after [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) came into power, simmering tensions between them and [United Cities](/united-cities) have broken out into open conflict at the border of [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) - [Bast](/bast-zone). The forces of HN have successfully overwhelmed and pushed UC back to [Bast](/bast-zone) in a single day and razed the entire region in the process, the borders have since become conflict zones with constant skirmishes between the 2 giants<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, where HN has been fairly content simply keeping UC at bay<sup id="cite_ref-:10_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

As a result of their experiences and religious beliefs, [Hivers](/hive) and [Sheks](/shek) coming to their domain are considered to be dangerous criminal elements<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> by their society, and would require a [Human](/human) escort to be trusted to keep them in order<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Those coming to their domain without an escort would be considered to be trespassing their borders and imprisoned as a result<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, though they care little for the non-humans living outside their areas. Their society also harbors a deep-rooted fear of [robots](/skeleton) (and [their enhancements](/robot-limbs)) where they will often react to them in fear-induced outbursts of violence. [Ancient tech](/ancient-science-book) and [narcotics](/hashish) are banned in their society.

## History

The origins of the Holy Nation are unclear; however, texts associated with Okranism itself are some of the oldest legible texts available. <sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is speculated that the Okranite creation myth dates back to the time of the First Extinction, around the fall of the First Empire.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

There are two known accounts of how [Okran](/okran) and [Narko](/narko) came to be. According to Holy Nation dogma, [Narko](/narko) was out of the "darkest depth of the world," and "sought to erase all that is good." [Okran](/okran) appeared and fought [Narko](/narko) for millennia until He was able to restrain and absorb [Narko](/narko). During their battle, servants of [Narko](/narko) ravaged the world, attacking mankind. At some undetermined point later, [Okran](/okran) and [Narko](/narko) separated again. [Okran](/okran) then sent the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) as a champion to assist [Humanity](/human) in defeating [Narko's](/narko) minions, with [Narko](/narko) being "banished deep under the earth."<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

The other account, considered heretical by the Holy Nation, is that [Okran](/okran) and [Narko](/narko) are the fractured halves of another God, [Chitrin](/chitrin), whose worship had declined prior to some great cataclysm where "the lands began to swallow themselves." Humanity plead for help and Chitrin "cleansed the planet of it's sickness." This act caused [Chitrin](/chitrin) to split in two from the strain, creating [Okran](/okran), God of day, warmth and renewal; and [Narko](/narko), god of night, cold and destruction, to exist in equilibrium.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

At some point during the [Second Empire](/second-empire), a cult formed that would eventually grow into Okranism. [General Hat-12](/general-hat-12), the Chief of Empire Police lead a crackdown on the new cult. This heavy-handed approach "Fueled the blame and confusion further," apparently escalating existing tensions within the [Second Empire](/second-empire) between [Humans](/human) and [Skeletons](/skeleton). <sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

While exactly when the Holy Nation split off from the [Second Empire](/second-empire) and the details of the split are unknown, the timing can be approximated to likely predate the collapse of the [Second Empire](/second-empire) itself, as the Second Empire did not collapse all at once, and instead "just sort of rotted and fell apart."<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

## Society

The Holy Nation is a stratified and hierarchical society that is very protective of their way of life, this has manifested itself in a form of a structured and organized society with intense xenophobia and scrutiny<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They see the faithless as a dangerous rotting influence to their society<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but are eager to accept foreigners who live by their religion and customs through conversion<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The top of their hierarchy is the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) (their spiritual leader), with [priesthoods](/holy-priest)<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [military](/high-inquisitor-seta)<sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> assisting him in governing and ruling over the territory, the most important of which are the 2 High Inquisitors, [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), who manage the defense of the country on entire fronts. There are also inter-ranks within the human males<sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that differentiate their role based on their skills.

Their country views itself as a shining beacon of light amidst a world of darkness and deceit - a safe haven for all [Humans](/human) who are willing to embrace their faith through conversion. Where through discipline, devotion and hard work do they nurture a semblance of civilization and unity against the evils of outside forces.

Evil lurks in all shadows of the land but Okran watches over you here, brother, where you belong as a pure blood.</blockquote><figcaption><a href="/high-paladin">High Paladin</a></figcaption></figure>

Their society adopts a warm, welcoming attitude toward men, but remains noticeably reserved when it comes to women<sup id="cite_ref-47" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-47"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Men are seen as the virtuous leaders and stewards of their society, serving as the role models it strives to uphold. Women on the other hand are seen as a potentially corrupting influence - one that could deteriorate if not controlled<sup id="cite_ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> - yet they believe proper guidance and discipline can redirect them toward the correct path<sup id="cite_ref-49" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-49"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-50" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-50"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Men are expected to live pious lives and volunteer under the command of military or priesthood service<sup id="cite_ref-51" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-51"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> (often times in a combined role<sup id="cite_ref-52" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-52"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>), and to guide and govern women and non-humans into a more sanctified life<sup id="cite_ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Women of HN on the other hand are generally expected to submit to their husbands, child-bear<sup id="cite_ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and farm<sup id="cite_ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

While men have most types of freedom within their society, the women are allowed a more restricted degree of autonomy<sup id="cite_ref-56" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-56"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which includes the right to trade and keep [Hivers](/hive) and [Sheks](/shek) under control, but would require a male escort when travelling at night<sup id="cite_ref-57" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-57"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>57<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, wearing jewelry<sup id="cite_ref-58" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-58"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>58<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, handing in criminals<sup id="cite_ref-59" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-59"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-60" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-60"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and setting up outposts<sup id="cite_ref-61" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-61"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>61<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. As it appears, many women are also allowed to freely enter or leave the country<sup id="cite_ref-62" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-62"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>62<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-63" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-63"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>63<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Social norms discourage women from approaching men directly<sup id="cite_ref-64" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-64"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>64<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, reflecting the society’s standards of decency. Consequently, most marriages are arranged by men<sup id="cite_ref-65" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-65"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>65<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Modesty<sup id="cite_ref-66" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-66"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>66<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, decency<sup id="cite_ref-67" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-67"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>67<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and loyalty<sup id="cite_ref-68" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-68"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>68<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> are heavily emphasized throughout their society, especially towards women<sup id="cite_ref-69" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-69"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>69<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-70" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-70"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>70<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. As a result, narcotics such as [Hashish](/hashish) is banned, alcoholic drinks are permitted but frowned upon<sup id="cite_ref-71" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-71"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>71<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Polyamory is implied to be practiced by The Holy Nation but discouraged from "taking too many women" <sup id="cite_ref-72" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-72"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>72<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Those who overstep such boundaries are often subject to swift and harsh disciplinary measures<sup id="cite_ref-73" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-73"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>73<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-74" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-74"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>74<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-75" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-75"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>75<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but can also be shown mercy if the offender is repentant<sup id="cite_ref-76" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-76"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>76<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Non-humans are seen as beastly and darkened in nature<sup id="cite_ref-77" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-77"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>77<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, though the reasons for this perception vary between races.

[Sheks](/shek) — a bony, horned race known for their violent and aggressive tendencies<sup id="cite_ref-78" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-78"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>78<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-79" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-79"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>79<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-80" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-80"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>80<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> — are viewed as wild beasts, embodying the savagery of monsters<sup id="cite_ref-81" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-81"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>81<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Hivers](/hive), an insectoid race bred for servitude and expendability within the Hive<sup id="cite_ref-82" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-82"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>82<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, live in huts made from vomit<sup id="cite_ref-:8_83-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-83"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>83<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, eat foul food<sup id="cite_ref-84" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-84"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>84<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, 'animal-like' hygiene and way of life<sup id="cite_ref-85" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-85"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>85<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and are notorious for scamming and stealing<sup id="cite_ref-:8_83-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-83"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>83<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-86" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-86"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>86<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-87" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-87"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>87<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> are seen as hollow and selfish beings born to a life of misery<sup id="cite_ref-88" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-88"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>88<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They are seen as darkened by HN for committing atrocities such as [warmongery](/shek-kingdom), [slavery](/western-hive), [extortion](/tribute-round) and [cannibalism](/fogmen)<sup id="cite_ref-89" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-89"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>89<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and fear that the humans will be influenced by them<sup id="cite_ref-90" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-90"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>90<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The general treatment of these non-humans would be to have them sent to [Rebirth](/rebirth), where they would be subject to a life of servitude and teaching of Okranite scripture in the belief that they could be reincarnated into a [Human](/human) in the next life.

However, it can be allowed for non-humans to serve outside of Rebirth in certain circumstances, particularly under a trustworthy [Human](/human) escort<sup id="cite_ref-91" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-91"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>91<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The economy of their nation ostensibly relies in large part on the donation/taxes on the various free labour at [Holy Farms](/holy-farms) that grant them food, and the prison labour at [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) that grants them material for construction and smitheries, with the latter likely having their workforce sourced<sup id="cite_ref-92" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-92"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>92<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> from the various [criminals](/holy-nation-outlaws)<sup id="cite_ref-93" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-93"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>93<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [bandits](/starving-bandits) that are scattered throughout their territory<sup id="cite_ref-94" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-94"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>94<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-95" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-95"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>95<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Skeletons are currently seen as Agents of Darkness that have wrought upon The First Extinction, and the fear of them is widespread within HN society<sup id="cite_ref-96" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-96"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>96<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-97" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-97"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>97<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-98" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-98"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>98<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-99" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-99"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>99<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Interestingly, Skeleton dialogue on Okran's Pride<sup id="cite_ref-100" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-100"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>100<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> indicate that plenty of Skeletons lived in that area until they were destroyed by fear that culminated into the crusade of one of the previous Phoenixes. Those with [robotic limbs](/robot-limbs) are presumed to be Skeletons-in-disguise, and ancient-tech is likely banned for being presumed to have something to do with Skeletons.

Despite the mostly low-tech aspects of their society<sup id="cite_ref-101" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-101"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>101<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-102" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-102"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>102<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, they possess advanced medical equipment for their [Doctors](/doctor), [Surgeons](/surgeon) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) himself<sup id="cite_ref-103" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-103"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>103<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

While dissent<sup id="cite_ref-104" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-104"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>104<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and disillusionment<sup id="cite_ref-105" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-105"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>105<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> persist in peripheral circles, they remain a minor undercurrent<sup id="cite_ref-106" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-106"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>106<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> in a society largely unified under Phoenix's leadership. His rule, vision and doctrine resonates profoundly with the vast majority of the populace<sup id="cite_ref-107" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-107"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>107<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-108" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-108"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>108<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> who are content<sup id="cite_ref-109" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-109"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>109<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and supportive of the regime. For the vast majority he is not a tyrant, but a protector<sup id="cite_ref-110" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-110"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>110<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> — an unyielding shield against chaos, foreign subversion<sup id="cite_ref-111" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-111"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>111<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and ideological decay. Those who express sympathies for the enemies of the state are met with swift societal condemnation<sup id="cite_ref-112" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-112"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>112<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. At best they are seen as deluded or out-of-touch<sup id="cite_ref-113" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-113"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>113<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, at worst they are branded as heretics and traitors<sup id="cite_ref-114" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-114"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>114<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The population amount and concentration inside The Holy Nation lands can be presumed to be very high, especially given their ability to field large numbers of patrols with multiple people<sup id="cite_ref-115" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-115"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>115<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> across multiple fronts using only males<sup id="cite_ref-116" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-116"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>116<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, alongside having an unusually high residency amounts<sup id="cite_ref-117" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-117"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>117<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It is likely that their abundant population is a result of both [having a fertile food source](/okrans-pride), and having a heavy societal focus in child-bearing.

Literacy rates can be presumed to be high within the Holy Nation, given the law to requiring all citizen to carry a copy of [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame), and the abundance of other religious texts available throughout the Holy Nation's cities. The women generally have less access to education<sup id="cite_ref-118" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-118"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>118<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but could benefit from the men's literacy by extension<sup id="cite_ref-119" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-119"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>119<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Quality of life within the Holy Nation can be considered to be relatively high by comparison to the rest of the continent, especially in [Okran's Pride.](/okrans-pride) Law abiding citizens can travel in relative safety as frequent patrols of [paladins](/paladin) keep the roads clear of bandits. Fertile soil and plentiful water allow for an abundance of food and Okran's Pride is considered to be one of the most stable regions in the game.

## Disposition

The Holy Nation, being one of the major factions, has a long list of relations with other factions and groups both inside and outside their countries.

### [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)

The Shek are a warlike group of people who warred within themselves and everyone else neighboring them for as long as they've existed<sup id="cite_ref-147" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-147"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>147<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:6_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-148" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-148"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>148<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-149" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-149"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>149<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and have plagued Holy Nation for centuries till this very day<sup id="cite_ref-:7_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, which fueled their hatred of the Shek and the image of them as savage beasts<sup id="cite_ref-150" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-150"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>150<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

But while Sheks have warred and launched raids against their neighboring Holy Nation territory<sup id="cite_ref-:1_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, it was not to the level of destruction that the current war has wrought<sup id="cite_ref-151" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-151"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>151<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This has been changed drastically in recent decades, during the reign of the current Phoenix (and the late Shek King Shager). Accounts on the war would suggest that Phoenix launched a military campaign called [The Holy Crusade](/the-holy-crusade) to eradicate the Sheks that have been invading their borders for good and bring peace to their lands<sup id="cite_ref-152" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-152"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>152<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, to which Shager responded by ordering a large portion of his own forces to assault and "bring blood and doom to" Holy Nation<sup id="cite_ref-153" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-153"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>153<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-154" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-154"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>154<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

In the midst of the war, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) launched all-out assaults to inflict extensive casualties on the Holy Nation, razing [The Hub](/the-hub) and its inhabitants, as well as several smaller<sup id="cite_ref-155" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-155"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>155<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> settlements, but these reckless assaults has crippled their own demographics, causing the depopulation of multiple Shek settlements<sup id="cite_ref-156" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-156"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>156<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> while the Holy Nation prevailed as a result of their overwhelming numerical superiority<sup id="cite_ref-157" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-157"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>157<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, allowed HN to secure their borders<sup id="cite_ref-158" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-158"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>158<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and captured large portions of the invading Sheks for [Rebirth](/rebirth)<sup id="cite_ref-159" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-159"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>159<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-160" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-160"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>160<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

King Shager, the former ruler of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) intended to hold a suicidal last stand in a [final charge](/kamikaze) against the Holy Nation with whatever remaining forces he has left. This was opposed by [Bayan](/bayan), who [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) supported and fought Shager to the death. [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), now leading the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), was forced to compromise and called off her main forces from the frontlines, signed a peace treaty with the [United Cities](/united-cities), reluctantly opened the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) cities to outside trade temporarily, and is attempting to rebuild the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)'s strength while biding her time, waiting for the opportunity to destroy The Holy Nation.

[Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) plays a critical role in reinforcing both the Holy Nation’s city defenses<sup id="cite_ref-:11_161-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:11-161"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>161<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and its frontlines<sup id="cite_ref-162" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-162"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>162<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, ensuring that Shek offensives cannot break through or destabilize the border<sup id="cite_ref-:11_161-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:11-161"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>161<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the current time, the Sheks have temporarily halted their largest attacks since [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) has called off her forces from assaulting HN until at least one of their leaders ([Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena)) is taken out<sup id="cite_ref-165" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-165"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>165<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The lingering raids and existential threat they pose was a cause for great concern by [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)<sup id="cite_ref-167" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-167"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>167<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and HN's populace in general<sup id="cite_ref-168" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-168"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>168<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Phoenix plans to eradicate the Shek raids and threats by firstly weakening the Shek Kingdom by taking out [Seto](/seto) to resume Sheks assaults unto HN to [weaken Admag](/admag/weakened) before moving onto taking out [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and have Sheks [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) themselves into inevitable extinction and bring peace to their lands<sup id="cite_ref-:2_128-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-128"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>128<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [United Cities](/united-cities)

Sometime after [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) (a man noted for his cruelty, impulsivity, and lack of common sense)<sup id="cite_ref-169" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-169"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>169<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> was chosen<sup id="cite_ref-170" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-170"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>170<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Simmering tensions between United Cities and The Holy Nation have broken out into open border conflict primarily in [and](/skimsands) [around](/okrans-valley) the [Bast](/bast-zone) region, and has been going on for at least 15 years<sup id="cite_ref-171" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-171"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>171<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Though it is unclear whether this action marked the start of hostilities or was merely a part of an ongoing conflict<sup id="cite_ref-172" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-172"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>172<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Bast](/bast-zone) was once an area filled with many farmlands and towns for trading<sup id="cite_ref-173" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-173"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>173<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> by [United Cities](/united-cities)<sup id="cite_ref-174" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-174"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>174<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and is implied to have been colonized and developed in recent times<sup id="cite_ref-175" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-175"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>175<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, which came close to [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley)<sup id="cite_ref-176" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-176"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>176<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It eventually ended up into a border war, with devastation for both sides<sup id="cite_ref-177" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-177"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>177<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

It is said that HN's forces prevailed against the UC's forces in an offensive that razed the namesake city/region in a single day, burning its head [Noble](/noble-circle) alive, and sent the remaining youngsters in the devastated region to Rebirth<sup id="cite_ref-178" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-178"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>178<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Both giants continuously skirmish with one another in the region as neither side establishes a lasting foothold<sup id="cite_ref-179" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-179"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>179<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The HN has a very negative viewpoint of UC's culture, seeing their materialistic focuses as decadent and corrupt that goes contrary to their spiritual values<sup id="cite_ref-180" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-180"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>180<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and abhor their practice of using slavery<sup id="cite_ref-181" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-181"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>181<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This opinion, however, isn't extended towards those who practice or organize slavery<sup id="cite_ref-182" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-182"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>182<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-183" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-183"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>183<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-184" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-184"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>184<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Implying that their issue is with the case of forced labor being used to serve personal gains like the [nobility](/noble-circle), rather than forced labor itself, as they themselves practice it in [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) and [Rebirth](/rebirth) and see it as justified to maintain order against criminals. [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) considers [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) as a "rotten tyrant"<sup id="cite_ref-185" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-185"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>185<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [Inquisitors](/inquisitor) see him as "One of the biggest threats in all the land"<sup id="cite_ref-186" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-186"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>186<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) commands a smaller contingent at the front<sup id="cite_ref-187" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-187"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>187<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, functioning primarily in an auxiliary capacity and serving as the Holy Nation’s second line of defense<sup id="cite_ref-188" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-188"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>188<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), by contrast, bears the brunt of the fighting in [Bast](/bast-zone), effectively managing the frontline operations almost single-handedly<sup id="cite_ref-189" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-189"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>189<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, preventing UC and TG's [expansion](/okrans-shield/united-cities) and [enslavement](/holy-farm/slave-traders) of their own populace. [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield) and [Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist) serve as both a defensive hardpoint and a place for organizing much of the squads<sup id="cite_ref-190" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-190"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>190<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The threat that UC poses is only seen as a secondary issue by the Phoenix<sup id="cite_ref-:10_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and HN army is seemingly content with stationing just enough forces at the border to prevent UC's penetration into their land<sup id="cite_ref-191" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-191"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>191<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> rather than conquering UC outright even if they clearly had the military might to<sup id="cite_ref-192" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-192"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>192<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-193" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-193"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>193<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas)

The Flotsam Ninjas are a tiny rebel group lead by [Moll](/moll) based inside [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest) primarily made up of a few disgruntled women and former [slaves](/slaves) who defected from **The Holy Nation**, with significant denizens from [Deadcat](/deadcat)<sup id="cite_ref-194" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-194"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>194<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They engage in various subversive activities inside the Holy Nation such as influencing and smuggling dissidents,<sup id="cite_ref-195" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-195"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>195<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-196" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-196"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>196<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> assassinations,<sup id="cite_ref-197" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-197"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>197<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, spreading heresies<sup id="cite_ref-198" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-198"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>198<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, fermenting unrest and riots<sup id="cite_ref-199" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-199"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>199<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-200" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-200"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>200<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and advocating for armed resistance to the Holy Nation in general,<sup id="cite_ref-201" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-201"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>201<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-202" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-202"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>202<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but is implied to have been largely unsuccessful<sup id="cite_ref-203" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-203"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>203<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Their various subversive and violent actions have inspired more zealous crackdowns and purges from HN authorities<sup id="cite_ref-204" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-204"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>204<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-205" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-205"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>205<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-206" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-206"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>206<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are seen as traitors<sup id="cite_ref-207" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-207"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>207<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, terrorists<sup id="cite_ref-208" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-208"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>208<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and a constant source of annoyance by the general populace both men and women alike<sup id="cite_ref-209" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-209"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>209<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-210" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-210"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>210<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-211" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-211"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>211<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction)

Faction of a tiny, isolated hideout based in the middle of [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), composed of [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) and [outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) of The Holy Nation. They have mutually hostile relations with HN.

### [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws)

Escaped criminals of The Holy Nation. Typically found in small groups wandering the wilds and engages in banditry across HN areas.

### [Starving Bandits](/starving-bandits)

Destitutes, beggars and thugs who have banded up in gangs to pressure and rob others for food. Commonly found in the wilds with sizes varying from small groups to gigantic ones. Engages in banditry across HN areas.

### [Black Dragon Ninjas](/black-dragon-ninjas)

Bandit ninjas whose leaders are known to engage in murder, arson, kidnap and assault and [Ninja Raids](/ninja-raid). Their leaders ([Buzan](/buzan), [Dimak](/dimak)) have a bounty with The Holy Nation.

### [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits)

Bandits who engage in assault, murder and looting and engage in [extortion](/bandit-demands). Their leader ([Dust King](/dust-king)) has a bounty with The Holy Nation.

### [Berserkers](/berserkers)

Sheks led by [Ghost](/ghost), who engage in assault, murder and looting. Their raids are blocked off as a result of [Seta's](/high-inquisitor-seta) defenses<sup id="cite_ref-212" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-212"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>212<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen)

Sheks led by [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) who engage in assault, murder and looting. Their raids are blocked off as a result of [Seta's](/high-inquisitor-seta) defenses<sup id="cite_ref-213" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-213"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>213<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Western Hive](/western-hive)

Neighbors of The Holy Nation forbidden from coming into The Holy Nation lands. [Lost Drones](/lost-drone) occasionally found inside HN territory originates from this faction.

### [Skeletons](/skeletons)

[Holy Nation Robots](/holy-nation-robot) wander around [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and engages in assault and murder on denizens of The Holy Nation. Occasionally has bounty put upon them.

### [Fogmen](/fogmen)

Insane and cannibalistic [Hivers](/hive) that will eat their victims alive. Their intrusions are blocked off as a result of [Phoenix's](/holy-lord-phoenix) defenses<sup id="cite_ref-:3_121-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-121"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>121<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Cannibals](/cannibals)

A threat rarely spotted through [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Bast](/bast-zone), whose overrun is blocked off as a result of [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and [Valtena's](/high-inquisitor-valtena) defenses<sup id="cite_ref-:4_122-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-122"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>122<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## Tips

-   It is recommended to have Holy Nation ally you and gain their trust as soon as possible if one wishes to go in their territory without trouble, doing so requires at least a single human male to hand in bounties to [Inquisitors](/inquisitor) 10 times without asking for payment. This can be easily achieved by luring [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) such as the [Escaped Servants](/escaped-servant) into their towns, where upon being attacked will have [bounty](/bounty) assigned unto them and be ripe for handing in. The other alternative is to hand in various bandits into [Rebirth](/rebirth) 50 times (without payment) The benefits of allying with them are numerous, including:
    -   Skeletons and Robotic-limbed players can also have access to Holy lands without being attacked.
        -   However, they cannot speak to officials.
    -   Non-humans no longer require a human escort to travel into the city.
    -   Wearing their [Faction Uniform](/faction-uniform) without repercussions.
    -   Free usage of beds, training dummies, and entrance into private facilities (most notably [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap)).
    -   [Chalice Of Fire](/chalice-of-fire) and [Holy Seal](/holy-seal) (Holy Seal can be used to call upon bodyguards, or sell for c.15,000), and [Holy Nation Reinforcements](/holy-nation-reinforcements).
    -   Note: [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) characters and squads will proceed to attack unprovoked if they are in your vicinity even if you hadn't doing anything to antagonize them prior. Avoid that area or be prepared for conflict.
-   While [losing limbs](/severed-limbs) and [replacements](/robot-limbs) is generally a non-issue since they get hit rarely (for [Greenlanders](/greenlander), Chest is 1.75x more likely to be hit than all limbs except right arm, which is 3.5x more likely instead) and requires it to reach -100% HP for it to be severed, it nonetheless helps to be wary not to take undue risks against high-damaging enemies to not accidentally lose them.
    -   [Splint Kits](/splint-kit) and [Advanced Splint Kits](/advanced-splint-kit) help prevent the loss of limbs.
        -   [Camp Beds](/camp-bed) can be used for some speedy recovery if the wilderness is safe.
    -   [Toughness](/toughness) helps slow down wound degeneration and [First Aid Kits](/standard-first-aid-kit) help stop it.
    -   If one wishes to procure advanced [Robotic Limbs](/robot-limbs) for the benefits for all of their squad members and still be able to hand in bounties, hire and keep a single human male who at least has their natural left arm and legs intact for the sake of doing so. This can be easily done by hiring [Holy Nation Male Recruits](/holy-nation-male-recruit).
-   Another easy way to achieve an alliance with the Holy Nation is by using assassination to capture [Seto](/seto) and bringing her to the Holy Nation.
-   Holy Nation patrols won't attack your [Hive](/hive) and [Shek](/shek) characters as long as they are closely accompanied by a human character, as the guards will assume that they are their servants.
    -   Ensure that the characters are in the same squad.
-   Roughly 2 in every 5 patrols to seize the player for interrogation if they see [Hivers](/hive) and [Sheks](/shek) accompanying them if _not_ allied.
    -   Occasionally the guards will demand the non-humans during interrogations, with the right dialogue choices and [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame), this has a 91%/50% chance of convincing the guards to keep [Hivers](/hive) and [Sheks](/shek) in your squad as a ([Human](/human)) Male/Female, respectively.
-   When [smuggling](/hashish), human males with [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame) make the best choice if travelling without non-humans, as they get searched the least often and have a chance of convincing the gate guards to not search based on the latter's personality.
-   [Gate Sentinels](/holy-sentinel) will never demand the arrest of [Hivers](/hive) and [Sheks](/shek) if they are presented to the gate sentinel first, and the humans (accompanied closely) later.
-   Unless allied with them, they will attack [Skeletons](/skeleton) and characters that wear replacement limbs, as they are mechanical and resemble Skeleton limbs.
-   Holy Nation NPCs will generally ignore anyone being carried by a PC. You can safely transport [Skeletons](/skeleton) and Robotic-limbed characters through Holy Nation territories by having your non-robotic characters simply pick them up. You can also carry escaped slaves to safety this way if you fully equip them with weapons and armor, and no one witnesses you picking them up.
-   Keeping a copy of "[The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame)" (their sacred bible) can allow humans to pacify Holy Nation patrols during interrogation with 100% success. Without one, there is a chance to fail some dialogue checks which lead to being attacked.
    -   If one does not have it, swear your loyalty to Okran/Phoenix if the goal is to avoid conflict. And choose the most loyal responses for the safest measures (the ones that include bowing or excessive chants), since there is a rare chance that the interrogator in question would find something suspicious in the lack of devotion and attack nonetheless.
-   Passing patrols may heal injured human player characters or provide food if starving. Take care if knocked out nearby a slave camp however, as they have no problem with enslaving unconscious characters. Some activities within the Holy Nation are limited to male [humans](/human), such as turning in bounties, or interacting with certain NPCs.
-   Some items are [illegal](/bounty) in the Holy Nation, take care not to bring any of them near their gate guards ([AI Core](/ai-core), [Ancient Science Book](/ancient-science-book), [Cultural Groupings and Evolution Series](/cultural-groupings-and-evolution-series), [Forbidden Sympathy](/forbidden-sympathy), [Hashish](/hashish), [Letter To Moll](/letter-to-moll), [Moll's Speech](/molls-speech)).

## Trivia

-   Following their ascetic culture and religious lifestyle, Holy Nation towns will only have ceiling lamps and torch posts as light fixtures and buildings interiors have no carpets
    -   However, a [Well III](/well) spawns in some locations (Automated version) and materials that require ancient tech to be fabricated (such as [Steel Bars](/steel-bars)) can be found in their towns. A (working) [Hybrid Stone Mine](/hybrid-stone-mine-tech) can also be found in the north of the Holy Mine Ruin in Skinners Roam which is affiliated with the Holy Nation.
-   While [HN's Trade Culture](/the-holy-nation-trade-culture) is supposed to make any technological level above (and including) IV illegal, they have multiple instances that goes above these inside their own country.
    -   Advanced Kits (Phoenix, Doctor, Surgeons) (Tech IV)
    -   [Chainmail Sheets](/chainmail-sheets) (Tech IV)
    -   A (working) [Hybrid Stone Mine](/hybrid-stone-mine-tech) in northern [Holy Mine Ruin](/holy-mine-ruin) (Tech V)
    -   HN's [Strong Defensive Walls](/strong-defensive-walls-tech) in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) (Tech VI)
-   Holy Nation has a generally lower technological level, especially when compared to [United Cities](/united-cities) as training dummies and mounted crossbows that spawn are replaced by MkI versions. This could be owing to the fact that they have a larger population, and each person has on average less resources as a result.
-   Their [trade culture](/the-holy-nation-trade-culture) has some discrepancies/anomalies:
    -   [Cactus](/cactus) and it's related items are forbidden from being spawn inside HN, yet the northern [Holy Farm](/holy-farms) in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) does farm cactuses.
    -   [Copper](/copper) doesn't spawn, but [Electrical Components](/electrical-components) (which uses copper) can spawn.
