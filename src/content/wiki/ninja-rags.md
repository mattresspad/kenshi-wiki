**Ninja Rags** are Light Armour that is equipped in the Body Armour slot. It can be found sold by Clothing Traders and Swamp Traders, as well as from Shinobi Thieves Traders.

It can be found equipped by ninjas and bandits such as [Black Dragon Genins](/black-dragon-genin), [Cloud Ninjas](/cloud-ninja), [Grayflayers](/grayflayer), [Ninjas](/ninja), [](/skeleton-grayflayer)[Skeleton](/skeleton) Grayflayers, [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninja), [Sand Ninja Jounin](/sand-ninja-jounin), and [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen). It is randomly worn by [Ninja Guards](/ninja-guard) and [Shinobi Citizens](/shinobi-citizen).

## Appearance

The Ninja Rags appear like a loose rag shirt covering the torso with a shawl made from tightly wrapped rags covering the bottom half of the face and shoulders. Strips of leather or cloth are wrapped around the forearms and slightly above the elbow.
