**Shager's Overthrow** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). This item can be found in a couple locations alongside other books about Shek.

This book tells the story of [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) defending [Bayan](/bayan) and overthrowing her predecessor, thereby preventing Sheks continuing the path of [Kamikazing](/kamikaze) themselves into extinction as proposed by Shager.

Places which might stock this item:

-   [The Great Library](/the-great-library)
-   Shek residential
-   Shek storage

There's another book on King Shager called "[King Shager's Reign](/king-shagers-reign)".
