**Engineering Research** is a [Research Artifact](/research-artifacts) needed for researching various [Technologies](/technology).

It is near guaranteed to spawn at [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop), [Workshop Complex](/workshop-complex), and [Deadland Workshop](/deadland-workshop). It can also be found in various locations using the [Artifacts](/artifacts) System such as [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs).

Shops that have a chance of selling them are [Travel and Repairs Shop](/travel-and-repairs-shop) and [Scraphouse](/scraphouse).

You need a total of 19 Engineering Research to research everything.
