If [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are all [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), **Okran's Shield** will [fall to ruins](/town-overrides) under [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) rule.

This override can come as a result of the initial state or as a result of flipping from [United Cities](/okrans-shield/united-cities), [Shek Kingdom](/okrans-shield/shek-kingdom), [Reavers](/okrans-shield/reavers) version.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

All buildings are abandoned and destroyed in this override.
