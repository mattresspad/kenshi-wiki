If [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states) while [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive, [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield) will [fall under the control](/town-overrides) of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) – but only if the player is allied with that faction.

The override can flip to become the [destroyed variant](/okrans-shield/destroyed).

## Trivia

-   Despite lacking traversing routes to take over Okran's Shield, this override can still happen even if both [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) are alive, which prevents much of Shek Kingdom to attack through southern HN territory.
-   Once this override is achieved, Esata's status no longer matters. Conversely, if Tengu is killed it will switch to the destroyed variant instead.
