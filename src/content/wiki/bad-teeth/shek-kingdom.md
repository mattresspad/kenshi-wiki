**Bad teeth, Shek Kingdom** is a city controlled by [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). It belonged to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) until [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) was kidnapped or killed, while [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) remained alive and free. The town itself is guarding a mountain pass between the fertile valley of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and hostile wilderness of the [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

The chain of command for defense in The Holy Nation's southern front fractured after Seta was defeated, allowing Esata's forces to slaughter and conquer the town with ease<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

This override can switch to [Destroyed](/bad-teeth/destroyed), [Kral's Chosen](/bad-teeth/krals-chosen) or [United Cities](/bad-teeth/united-cities) override.

It appears to be a town where most buildings and original inhabitants are destroyed and massacred in the midst of the conflict, with a few of the buildings moved in and Shek's HQ relocated in this area.

Be aware that [Shek Kingdom Trade Culture](/shek-kingdom-trade-culture) applies here.
