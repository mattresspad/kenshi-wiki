**The Queen** is leader of the [Western Hive](/western-hive) and has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status). She rules her domain from the "Hill Hut" in the [Western Hive](/western-hive-location), located on an island west of [Vain](/vain).

Upon entering the Hill Hut, multiple [Royal Soldier Drones](/royal-soldier-drone) will gasp and walk over to you and demand you leave. They do not become hostile if you refuse. The Hive Queen can be seen sitting atop a strange throne and only communicates through screeches.

They wear no armour.

## [World States](/world-states)

The world states affected by the Queen are linked to her being either dead or kidnapped.

[Lost Hivers](/lost-hiver) can be found in cities talking about how they miss The Queen. Also, the [Hive Villages](/hive-village) and [Distant Hive Villages](/distant-hive-village) will be [overtaken](/town-overrides) by [Fogmen](/fogmen) and become [Deadhive Overruns](/deadhive-overrun).

[Hive Attack](/hive-attack-western-hive) will commence on the players base not long after in order to avenge the queen.

[Fogman Deathyard](/fog-deathyard) will start spawning in [Dreg](/dreg), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Vain](/vain).

[Hive Bandits](/hive-bandit) and [Lost Princes](/lost-prince) will spawn in [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

[Lost Hivers](/lost-hiver) will start to spawn in [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [The Swamp](/the-swamp), [Purple Sands](/purple-sands).

[Farm Trader](/farm-trader) and the [Materials Trader](/materials-trader) events will no longer occur and a [Hive Attack](/hive-attack-western-hive) event will trigger.

If [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south) is alive and free, then Southern Hive patrols will spawn in the [Bonefields](/bonefields), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [The Hook](/the-hook), [The Swamp](/the-swamp) and in [Vain](/vain).

## Trivia

-   Despite being the leader of the Western Hive, who are heavily disliked by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), she does not appear to have a bounty by [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) or the [Inquisitors](/inquisitor). It is especially strange because her existence is known to the Holy Nation as referenced in the book [Guiding Light VI](/the-guiding-light-series).
-   **The Queen** has the exact same stats of their Southern Cousin Queen, [Queen of the South.](/queen-of-the-south)
