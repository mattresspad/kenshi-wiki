The **Police Helmet** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot. It can be purchased from armour shops in UC and the Swamp.

It is equipped by the [Vigilante Chief](/vigilante-chief). It has a chance of being equipped on [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter).

It can be crafted in the [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy) after researching the blueprint.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Lore

The police helmet and armor is most likely the remnant armor of the [Second Empire](/second-empire) [Skeleton](/skeleton) Police Force, as evident with the appearance of [Old Empire Police Captain](/old-empire-police-captain) within the [Ashlands](/ashlands).

## Appearance

The Police Helmet is an open-faced helmet with a metal mesh attached to cover the face of the wearer. With the addition of the faceguard, the helmet provides full protection for the wearer's head.
