**Lord Yoshinaga** is one of the diplomatic leaders of the [United Cities](/united-cities). He has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status) and can be found in [Heng](/heng). Entering his home is considered trespassing and his 5 [Hooded Empire Noble Guard](/hooded-empire-noble-guard) will attack.

He is responsible for raising armies to patrol the [Skimsands](/skimsands) and is possibly '[Lord Mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_mayor)' of Heng.

He has a Fearful [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

Lord Yoshinaga's death/imprisonment would cease [Samurai](/samurai) patrols spawning in [Skimsands](/skimsands).

If [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) and **Lord Yoshinaga** are both dead/imprisoned while [Valamon](/valamon) is alive and free, [Reavers](/reavers) patrols will start spawning in [Heng](/heng-zone).

Alternatively, if **Lady Tsugi**, **Lord Yoshinaga** and **Valamon** are all either killed/imprisoned while [Crab Queen](/crab-queen) is alive and free, [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) patrols will start spawning in [Heng](/heng-zone).

If Lord Yoshinaga is killed/imprisoned alongside with [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata) and [Lady Sanda](/lady-sanda) it would cause patrols of [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) consisting of 4 to 13 to start spawning in the following regions: [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

## Trivia

-   Yoshinaga, [Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Sanda](/lady-sanda) all use the same army squads.
-   **Lord Yoshinaga**, [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) and [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata) all use [Wakizashi](/wakizashi) as their choice of weapon.
-   Intentional or not, if Heng is half-destroyed or handed over to [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants), **Lord Yoshinaga** will despawn. However, him despawning that way will NOT cause any of the World States mentioned up there being triggered. The mod, **[Yoshinaga Fix](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2725254347)**, should also resolve this issue.
-   Out of all Nobles of UC, (excluding [Lord Ohta](/lord-ohta)) Lord Yoshinaga is the only Noble without World States aligned with their hometown.
-   One could potentially still have Yoshinaga's status controlling the various homeless patrol spawns tied to his states even when Heng is no longer under United Cities control, meaning that he shouldn't even have a town to govern or exert his power over the forces. As Heng's status is tied with Longen and Tengu instead.
