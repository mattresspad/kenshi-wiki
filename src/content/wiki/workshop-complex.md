**Workshop Complex** is an [Ancient Location](/ancient-locations) in The Grid. This complex is comprised of three completed workshop buildings and two uncompleted/submerged. Workshop buildings are large, hangar-like structures. This location is one of the few reliable places to obtain [Engineering Research](/engineering-research). Similar locations are the [Dead Workshop](/dead-workshop), [Deadland Workshops](/deadland-workshop), and [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop). All of them can be marked by reading the **Engineers [Map](/maps)**.

The workshop located near the icon on the map is empty, while the hangar southwest of the icon on the map and northeast of the icon both have the containers with item spawns. The amount of [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) and [Engineering Research](/engineering-research) spawning is between 4-9 for each item. The fourth workshop, located westmost along the same line, was probably never completed and consists of the hanger shell only. Fifth workshop, if ever completed, is now completely submerged and is located to the south-east of the main line of workshops. There are also several iron nodes nearby which could explain placement of the Complex in this area.

## Dangers

The zone which is closely located to [The Crater](/the-crater), which is a zone with a high number of [Beak Things](/beak-thing). Sometimes small groups wander into the Grid.

## Item Spawns

This location's storage containers will be locked with container lock levels ranging from levels 25 to 75.
