If [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Inaba](/lord-inaba) are killed/imprisoned while [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is alive and free, [Drin](/drin) will become controlled by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

This override can switch to [Destroyed](/drin/destroyed) version.

After the forces of the UC completely scattered from the area as a result of key leaders taken out, the Holy Nation's forces under Valtena moved in and made this place into their own barracks.

## Trivia

-   Likely as a result of override removals being incomplete, [Samurai Gate Guards](/samurai-gate-guard) will spawn in this area. Despite it being extremely unlikely that HN has managed to move in all their barracks and amenities into this region before eradicating the remaining parts of UC forces.
