**Hemp-Based Fabric** is [Crafting Tech](/crafting-tech) which unlocks a [Fabric Loom](/fabric-looms) that converts [Hemp](/hemp) into [Fabrics](/fabrics). This technology is not required for [Fabric Manufacture](/fabric-manufacture-tech), which unlocks the Cotton version of this building.

Players planning on making hemp-based fabrics should construct their base in a Swamp [Environment](/environment) or invest in [Hydroponic Hemp](/hydroponic-hemp) farming technology.

While hemp is faster at generating fabrics, hemp is also an illegal item in many factions. Players should be aware of that before constructing a hemp farm in territory controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities).
