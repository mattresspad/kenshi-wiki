The **Heavy Armour Smithy** is a crafting [building](/buildings-list) type introduced in patch 0.50. This crafting station allows players to [craft](/armour-smith) metal plate-based armour by hand. It is unlocked by finishing the [Plate Armour Crafting (Tech)](/plate-armour-crafting-tech) research.
