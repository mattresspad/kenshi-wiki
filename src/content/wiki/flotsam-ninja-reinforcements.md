**Flotsam Ninja Reinforcements** is a bugged [Event](/events) that is supposed to trigger in response to the [Holy Assault](/holy-assault), [Holy Nation Assault (Major)](/holy-nation-assault-major), [Retribution of God](/retribution-of-god), and [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god) events, as long as the [Player](/nameless) is allied to the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) and not the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), while [Moll](/moll) and [Yayoi](/yayoi) are both alive and free.

Once triggered, the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) will send reinforcements from the [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) or a [Scout Post](/scout-post). While they will move faster than most hostile events, they can still arrive too late if the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) is closer to the hostile event's spawn location than to the origin of the reinforcements. These reinforcements will consist of 1-3 squads, made up of 4-10 [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninja), and led by [Yayoi](/yayoi).

Once the reinforcements arrive, they will give a short speech, and then start patrolling the player's base, attacking hostiles, defending the player's characters, and healing wounded allies.

As implied earlier, this event however is bugged and will not trigger in vanilla due to [Yayoi](/yayoi) missing a squad template.

## Additional outside info

-   There are mods to fix this event to allow it to work, such as the mod [Yayoi Appears](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2841885683).
