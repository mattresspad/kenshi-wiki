**Dust Bosses** are squad leaders of the [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), who can be found leading groups of their fellow outlaws in their territory. They are stronger than their comrades, and may approach player characters with their group in tow to mug them. The player may either pay a fair sum of [Cats](/cats) and be left alone, or refuse the offer, which initiates a fight.

They lead squads with 5-10 [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandit) which are sometimes accompanied by up to 4 [Dust Bandit Bowmen](/dust-bandit-bowman). These squads can be found roaming [Shem](/shem) or the [Border Zone](/border-zone).

They also populate [Dust Bandit Camps](/dust-bandit-camp), which spawn in the aforementioned biomes, with the addition of [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam). These camps contain 3 Dust Bandit squads, which are slightly larger than their roaming counterparts.

They can also be found in the [Dust King Tower](/dust-king-tower) as guards, in a large group of 5-6.

Finally, they lead the [Bandit Demands](/bandit-demands) event squad which is sent to the player's outpost if it lies within the Dust Bandits' zone of control.

They have a 25% chance to have a bounty between 1000 and 3000 cats for the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Holy Nation](/holy-nation), and [United Cities](/united-cities).
