**Harmothoe** is a unique member of the [Free Traders](/free-traders). She can be found leading a caravan, which can trade goods with the player. She can be found in either [Heng](/heng) or [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge). Her caravan includes 2 [Pack Beasts](/pack-beast) and 8 to 10 [Caravan Guards](/caravan-guard).

If she is killed, a [Caravan Trader Boss](/caravan-trader-boss) (Free Traders) will replace her position.

Her character is based upon a fanfiction written by a supporter of the game, you can find the [full original backstory here](https://steamcommunity.com/app/233860/discussions/4/611704531888528365/#c350532536102110190). In it, she was born into a wealthy family of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), her parents were killed by [Skeletons](/skeletons) during a night journey from [Stack](/stack) to [Blister Hill](/blister-hill). When she was found by a passing [Paladin](/paladin) patrol, she was promptly put to [slavery](/slavery) by an [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) for penance. She spent years plotting an escape, and her hatred of men intensified as more abuses and attention were given to her as she came of age.

One night, when everyone is deep asleep, she succeeded in escaping the slave camp by taking her makeshift weapon from a secret stash she had been keeping and stabbing everyone inside to death. The patrols who were spared for being outside the camp returned to see the mutilated corpse of their commander with said weapon buried in his eyesocket, above his deceased body was Harmothoe's name written in his blood.

[Bard](/bard) also sings a song about her, which alleges she was originally a slave in [Rebirth](/rebirth) that escaped by killing the guards, including a [High Paladin](/high-paladin) that ravished her.

She is warmly receptive to female characters and is extremely rude to human males. She vehemently hates and outright refuses to deal with [Skeletons](/skeleton) and will trigger combat with any skeletons who speak to her after she threatens them once.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

## Ballad of Harmothoe

"_Harmothoe, Harmothoe, she lived a live of anguish!_

_For chained in Rebirth she was condemned to languish,_

'_Til one day she'd been ravished by the high paladin most vile!_

_And she swore her dark vengeance with a murderous smile..._

_She stashed a long nail, forged to a blade..._

_And one by one the guards were slayed..._

_Now the high paladin lies dead, a nail in the eyeball..._

_With it the name Harmothoe, in blood on the high wall!"_

## Trivia

-   Harmothoe is the name of an Amazon in Greek mythology, and the likely inspiration for the name.
-   If the player takes the helmet off her head, the player can notice that she has an unusual red hair.
-   According to the dialogue, Harmothoe names her blade "Nail" and has a tendency to stash it into the eyes of her enemies.
-   Harmothoe claims that [Moll](/moll) is her leader.
-   Inside the FCS, Harmothoe has a body shape file named "HaTsUnE NeKo.bod2", which shares a similar name as someone listed in the [Credits](/credits) under Special Thanks section.
