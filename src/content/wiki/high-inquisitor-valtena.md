**High Inquisitor Valtena** is a member of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and its [Inquisition](/inquisitor), who occupies as a high command for the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). He is located in [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield) but can also lead an attack against the player in an event called the [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god). He can be captured and turned over to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) for a sizable Bounty of c.40,000.

He also functions as a regular [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) and players can hand in [Bounty](/bounty) to him.

Valtena is the talented commander of the Holy Army's northern and eastern forces<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and almost entirely<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> responsible for the campaign in the frontlines of [Bast](/bast-zone) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) against the [United Cities](/united-cities). The forces that he commands is tremendous, bigger than that of [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) combined<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and likely [Nagata](/lord-nagata) and [Sanda's](/lady-sanda) of the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) as well<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. He aims to take [Drin](/drin) after Empire scatters away from that area<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction. The color used is "Holy Nation Level 3".

## [World States](/world-states)

If High Inquisitor Valtena is killed there are several World States that will trigger, mostly related with the collapse and abandonement of HN's northeastern front that he commands as the chain of command breaks down, causing demoralization, chaos, and panic amongst the HN's army and populace<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

His death causes the Holy army patrols to decrease in frequency in [Bast](/bast) (90%), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) (56%), and completely cease in [Skimsands](/skimsands).

[Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield) would be turned over to United Cities control if Valtena is killed, or to the Shek Kingdom if the player is allied with them (because of a quirk in the system, this cannot happen unless [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed as well). If Okran's Shield is under [United Cities](/united-cities) control it can be turned over to the [Reavers](/reavers) if [Lady Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) is killed while [Valamon](/valamon) is alive and free or fall into ruins if he is killed too.

[Okran's Fist](/okrans-fist) would become [half-destroyed](/okrans-fist/half-destroyed) or overrun by [Cannibals](/okrans-fist/cannibals) control if Holy Lord Phoenix is also killed.

If Valtena and either Phoenix and/or Seta is killed, while [Longen](/longen) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are alive, [Holy Farms](/holy-farms) will be taken over by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

If Valtena, Phoenix and Seta are all killed while [Moll](/moll) is alive and free, [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) will take over [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) and [Rebirth](/rebirth). If Moll is also dead, both towns will be part of [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws).

If Valtena, Phoenix and Seta are all killed while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive and free, [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) patrols will start spawning at [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Skimsands](/skimsands).

If Valtena and Seta are both killed while [Moll](/moll) is alive and free, [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) patrols would start spawning in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) region.

If Valtena, Phoenix and Seta are all killed/imprisoned, [Strayed Paladin](/strayed-paladin) patrol consisted of 4 to 10 people patrolling the following zones: [Bast](/bast), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

(Requires Valtena to be alive)

If [Inaba](/lord-inaba) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are dead, [Drin](/drin) will be taken by [The Holy Nation](/drin/the-holy-nation).

## Trivia

-   **High Inquisitor Valtena** is the only character in HN to wield an [Edgewalker](/edgewalkers)\-grade weapon, which is also notable for having a better quality than the other High Inquisitor who holds a [Catun](/catun)\-grade weapon, **Seta**.
-   It is heavily hinted that if he is dead or captured, Holy Army forces will make a retreat closer to the core territories, thereby abandoning their frontier positions<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as seen by how army forces cease to spawn in many of the border regions and the abandonment of military settlements.
-   Valtena has a significantly higher (50) faction importance in FCS than the other High Inquisitor, (4) Seta.
-   Valtena has more forces under his command than the Empire's combined force of [Inaba](/lord-inaba) and [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga), as seen in squads at [Bast](/bast-zone) and [Skimsands](/skimsands).
-   Valtena has significant amount of army force acting as patrols tied under his command, roughly 30% of all HN army patrols, compared to 10% of [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and 60% of [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).
