The **Stenn Desert**: the barren, sparsely-populated, inhospitable home of the [Shek](/shek). Surrounded to the West, North, and North-East by [Vain](/vain); and to the East by the [Border Zone](/border-zone). This place is rife with banditry of the notorious [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) and [Berserkers](/berserkers), as well as the [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) roaming through the area, likely migrated from the neighboring [Spider Plains](/spider-plains) (which then likely originated from [Arach](/arach)).

The player can find the capital of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) ([Admag](/admag)), as well as the headquarters of [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) ([New Kralia](/new-kralia)) and [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) ([Exile Camp](/exile-camp)) in this area.

Occasionally, trade caravans of [Western Hive](/western-hive), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) and (rarely) [Nomads](/nomads) traverse through [Admag](/admag).

True to name, it never rains in the Stenn Desert. The desert is filled with low quality (10%) copper mines.

Packs of [bonedogs](/bonedog) can be seen roaming in the desert. Also, it is possible to encounter individual or pairs of [skin spiders](/skin-spider) that hail from the southern Spider Plains.
