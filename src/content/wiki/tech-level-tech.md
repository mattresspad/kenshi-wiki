**Tech Levels** serve as a gating mechanism to other [Technologies](/technology). They ensure that a player must research in tiers before reaching late game technological wonders such as [Hydroponics](/hydroponics-tech) or [Automated Mining Tech](/automated-mining-tech-tech).

Players are required to begin their research with a [Small Research Bench](/small-research-bench) at Tech Level I. Progression to Tech Level II unlocks [Research Bench II](/research-bench), and so forth. These advancements are made by researching the next tier and having the appropriate materials. [Books](/book) are the only requirement initially for many forms of research. Greater levels require rarer items such as [Ancient Science Books](/ancient-science-book) or [AI Cores](/ai-core).
