**Animal Claws** are an Animal product and [Trade Good](/trade-goods) in Kenshi. They are obtained from unconscious or dead [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) and [Skimmers](/skimmer). They can be sold for c.90.

It's likely bought as trophies for decoration by those who obtain these items through trade.
