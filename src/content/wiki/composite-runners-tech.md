## Bugs

-   This research is currently bugged and will not add an additional 5% damage to Mounted Crossbows or Mounted Crossbow MkII if done after [Crossbow Turret Strings (Tech)](/crossbow-turret-strings-tech). If done prior to the 10% research, it will be overwritten by it later. If not after the Crossbow Turret Strings research, it will override the 10% bonus with the 5% one until you save/load the game at which point the Crossbow Turret Strings (Tech) multiplier of 1.1x will be used again making this research useless.
    -   Attempts at modding this research to give 1.15x instead of 1.05x will result in the same overwrite of 1.1x after reloading.
