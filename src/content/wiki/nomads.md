**Nomads** are a faction of [Human](/human) wanderers and animal herders. Neutral and generally content to just tend to their animals.

The region that they are most present in can be roughly divided into two areas:

-   Southwestern areas of [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Shem](/shem), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Dreg](/dreg).
-   Northeastern areas of [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert).

They own several camps, which are free for the player to use, usually containing shelter from rain, a [campfire](/campfire), and a [Camp bed](/camp-bed). They travel with a small pack of [Goats](/goat) and a [Pack Beast](/pack-beast) which provides light.

If your character is starving and inside an NPC town, they might rarely give you some [Cooked Vegetables](/cooked-vegetables).
