The **Protector of the Flame** is an elite Paladin of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) tasked with protecting the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) in his throne room at [Blister Hill](/blister-hill). He can be differentiated from the Phoenix's [High Paladin](/high-paladin) entourage by his buffer look caused by his high stats.

Care should be taken when engaging the protector in combat, as he is one of the highest stat characters in the game. Despite being set as Unique, he also has a unique replacement spawn of himself, so if you manage to slay him, another Protector will eventually take his place, and raising the squad size multiplier can cause multiple Protectors to spawn.

They wear \[High\] armour (10% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.

## Trivia

-   Due to the stat randomization, it's possible for this character to achieve over 100 [Strength](/strength) stats.
-   The Protector has very high combat stats of 90, a strength of 100 but an unarmed stat of 50. This puts his Dexterity, Unarmed and Dodge at level 50. Due to this it is suggested to not remove his weapon if you happen to defeat him as he will be a larger threat when unarmed.
