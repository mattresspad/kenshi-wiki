**Old-Eye Blades** is a weapon manufacturer company.

## Overview

Old-Eye Blades is a mid-grade weapon manufacturer. Their quality is above average, as is their price and damage. They perform better than [Metal Purity](/metal-purity) and [Unknown](/unknown), and only slightly worse than [Catan Scrapmaster](/catan-scrapmaster). Old-Eye Blades does not produce [Flesh Cleaver](/flesh-cleaver), [Heavy Iron Club](/heavy-iron-club), or [Iron Club](/iron-club) type weapons.
