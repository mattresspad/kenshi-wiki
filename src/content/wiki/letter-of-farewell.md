Letter Of Farewell is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item is 'unique' to Flotsam HQ in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in the inventories of [Holy Citizens](/holy-citizen).

This item is ostensibly a last note from a woman to her husband before she is put to death for rebelling against the state of women in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

As there are multiple copies of this note, it is possible that the woman who wrote this made it in preparation in the future scenario where she happens to be found and executed, or to give an impression of such. It is possible that she herself may still be alive and joined the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), given that the copy of the note is present in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village).
