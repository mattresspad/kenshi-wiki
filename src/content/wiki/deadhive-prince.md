**Deadhive Prince** is a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. Deadhive Prince is a subrace of [Hive](/hive). This race is used exclusively for [Fog Prince](/fog-prince) characters.

Compared to [Western Hive Princes](/hive-prince), the Deadhive Prince is slightly more delicate in all of their bodyparts, with a 5 HP decrease of all their body parts resulting in 75 HP each instead of 80 HP. They also have a slightly slower bleed rate of 0.2 instead of 0.3, they also have a larger hunger rate of 1 instead of 0.5.

When comparing experience multipliers, Deadhive Prince lacks the [Field Medic](/field-medic), [Lockpicking](/lockpicking), [Perception](/perception), [Science](/science) and [Toughness](/toughness) boosts, instead having boosts in [Engineering](/engineer), [Farming](/farming), [Labouring](/labouring) and [Melee Attack](/melee-attack). They also do not have the [Labouring](/labouring) penalty, but having it as a boost instead, in direct opposite to both [Western](/hive-prince) and [Southern](/southern-hive-prince)\-equivalent princes.

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race have no special immunity to any types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which hazardous weather can occur.

  

### Equipment Slots

_Characters of this race can wear any type of equipment in these slots._

-   [Headgear](/headgear)
-   [Body Armour](/body-armour)
-   [Legwear](/legwear)

_Characters of this race cannot wear any equipment in these slots._

-   [Shirts](/shirts)
-   [Footwear](/footwear)

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Athletics](/athletics)
-   [Dexterity](/dexterity)
-   [Engineer](/engineer)
-   [Farming](/farming)
-   [Melee Attack](/melee-attack)
-   [Stealth](/stealth)
-   [Thievery](/thievery)
-   [Labouring](/labouring)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Strength](/strength)
