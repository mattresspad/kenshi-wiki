Assassination is a [stat](/statistics) that affects the player's ability to successfully knock out non-player characters. It is useful when players want to get rid of a person or a group of people without fighting or alarming the guards. Players can attempt to stealth knock-out any character in the world. Stealth knockouts are not possible on animal characters. Aside from the KO time of an assassinated character, it also affects the time that a character is knocked out for after being picked up and put back on the ground<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Players earn experience in this stat for successful and unsuccessful attempts. Failed attempts grant 3x more experience than successes. The [Toughness](/toughness), Assassination, [Thievery](/thievery) or [Defense](/melee-defense) skill of the target decreases your chances of knocking them out, whichever impacts it the most. Toughness and Assassination affect the chances 2x more than the other stats (so a target with 30 Toughness/Assassination would be harder to knock out than a unit with 59 in defense/thievery as an example).

A knocked-out person can be looted the same way as if they were asleep.

## Training

You can train Assassination at [The Hub](/the-hub) or any other city with a Thieves tower after joining the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves). They have Assassination Dummies on the first floor of their tower. Player Characters can also build their own [Assassination Dummies](/assassination-dummy) after researching [Assassin Training](/assassin-training-tech).

## Tips

-   It is recommended that players avoid knocking out shopkeepers because of a bug which causes the shop's residents to despawn. The shop's items will not restock if this occurs. This can be fixed through [importing saved games](/main-menu).
-   Players can also attempt stealth knockouts on any eating or worshiping [Fogmen](/fogmen) in the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands). The Fogmen will be too distracted in their task and will likely not initiate combat after a failed attempt.
-   An easy way to train this is to stealth knock-out characters within [Prisoner Cages](/prisoner-cage). This can be done easily against any enslaved characters in places such as [Rebirth](/rebirth) and [Reaver Camps](/reaver-camp).
-   Right clicking on one of your characters that is sleeping in a bed you don't own will allow you to perform a stealth knock-out on your own character and get assassination experience.
