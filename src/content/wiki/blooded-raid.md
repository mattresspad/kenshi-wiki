**Blooded Raid** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has a base within 8,000 meters of a location owned by the [Reavers](/reavers). It has a 15% chance to trigger each day if within 2,500 meters, this chance scales down with distance until it reaches 0% chance at 8,000 meters. It cannot trigger if [Valamon](/valamon) is killed or captured, or if the player is allied with the [Reavers](/reavers) (which is not possible in vanilla without exploits).

Once the conditions are fulfilled the [Reavers](/reavers) will send an assault to the player's base. This assault will consist of three to five squads, each made up of 4-7 [Reaver Blooded](/reaver-blooded) and 1-6 [Reaver Slaves](/reaver-slave), led by a [Reaver Ironclad](/reaver-ironclad).

When it arrives, the leader will give a short speech and then order the attack. Breaking down gates, attacking characters, and enslaving any downed characters. Captured characters will be take to a [Reaver](/reavers) owned location and imprisoned there.
