**Flotsam Armory** are weapon, armour, and supply shops of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas). Most supplies from the Flotsam can be bought from here, aside from food.

### Possible Locations

-   [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)
-   [Rebirth](/rebirth)

### Residents

-   [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja) (3)
-   [Flotsam Refugee](/flotsam-refugee) (3)
