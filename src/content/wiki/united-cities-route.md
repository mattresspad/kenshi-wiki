**United Cities Route** is a [World State Route](/world-state-routes) where the player has allied relationship with [United Cities](/united-cities) and having initiated dialogue with [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) or [Koin](/koin) that opens up the questline for UC.

The UC route is diametrically opposed to Rebel Farmers Route, Anti-Slavers Route. And comes into conflict with [The Holy Nation Route](/the-holy-nation-route), [Shek Kingdom Route](/shek-kingdom-route). It has elements of overlap with Traders Guild Route.

The goal of this questline mainly revolves around crushing [banditry](/rebel-farmers) and [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) activity, while nudging HN forces out of their [eastern](/bast-zone) [border](/skimsands) but still preserving them enough so that they can counter the Shek.

The player can decide to speak with Tengu about quest matters, however, it will usually be re-directed to Koin instead, who will tell you which targets should be taken down.

## Primary Targets

### [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Bah! Way to sour my relaxation time, (player name). Talk to Koin about desert cleaning duties, I don't want my apetite spoiled thinking about those filthy dregs.</blockquote><figcaption>Emperor Tengu, when asked about destorying the rebels</figcaption></figure>

Since Emperor Tengu is dismissive when talking about rebels, you will be redirected to Koin when talking about rebels, who will in turn state this.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>The rebel farmers, what an annoyance! They've not been tracked down yet, rumours suggest they run their little base of operations up north among the stick men and the cannibals.</blockquote><figcaption>Koin, when asked about destroying rebel farmers</figcaption></figure>

The [Stickmen](/western-hive) and the [Cannibals](/cannibals) refer to [Sinkuun](/sinkuun) and [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), respectively. [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) (Rebel Farmer's leader) is located in [Fort Simion](/fort-simion), which is located in Sinkuun. Take him out and the rebel farmers' organization will cripple.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Glad you got those rebel farmers out of our hair, (player name). Certainly makes my job a little easier. Now what can I do for you?</blockquote><figcaption>Koin, Boss Simion defeated</figcaption></figure>

Many of Rebel Farmers squads will cease to spawn, with only the weaker ones remaining, representing a lower amount of banditry inside the UC desert areas.

### [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>I can't give you much information on the Anti Slavers I'm afraid. Tinfist hides south in the Forbidden Zone, not the kind of unknown place you want to sacrifice too many soldiers to explore. His lackeys were sighted in Flats Lagoon and the ruins of the crags. Hiding is what they do best unfortunately. Cowards.</blockquote><figcaption>Koin, when asked about destroying Anti-Slavers</figcaption></figure>

"Forbidden Zone" here refers to generally the entire southeast portion of the Kenshi map, which is too hazardous and wide to travel through, [Tinfist](/tinfist) himself is located specifically in [Spring](/spring), at [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble). Once Tinfist is down, the empire will expand their influence significantly, together with Traders Guild (see Tinfist's world state section for more info).

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Good work with Tinfist, (player name).</blockquote><figcaption>Koin, after Tinfist is defeated</figcaption></figure>

### [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>The Holy Nation? Kill them all! Pain in the ass god chanters, they need to learn where they stand!-[...][He looks around the room at his expectant guards and sinks back into his throne][...] But bah! A fool I would be to involve myself in that petty little threeway between tribal bone warriors, flame worshipping kooks and yet another clan of ninja bandits... You think I want to deal with more ninjas after I've just cleaned my hands of Tinfist? Leave the Holy Nation where they are, they act as a nice little peaceable buffer between us and those bone loonies. Take down that inquisitor Vlahahlena and push them back at Okran's Shield, remind them who rules the world and take that lovely little defensive position of theirs. I'm sure they'll start sticking to themselves again and then we don't have to deal with those bloddthirsty boneheads knocking at our gates.</blockquote><figcaption>Tengu, when asked about destroying THe Holy Nation after Tinfist is defeated</figcaption></figure>

Tengu mispronounces the name of [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) as Vlahahlena, which is promptly corrected by Koin. If you talk with Koin, he will tell you this:

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Well please don't goad the Emperor with such ideas. We need to be smart, (player name). If the western balance shifts too much in favour of the Shek then we'll have barbarian warlords pillaging in our desert in no time. The Holy Nation make a good distraction for the Shek... You want to keep Lord Tengu happy, give him Okran's Shield and he will be grateful to you. Once we push the Holy Nation back and take that defensive border position I'm sure they'll start sticking to themselves. Last thing they want are foreigners stepping foot in their precious holy plains anyway.</blockquote><figcaption>Koin, when asked about destroying HN</figcaption></figure>

High Inquisitor Valtena is located in Okran's Shield and is responsible largely for the military planning and organization at Bast and Skimsands. Once he is down, United Cities will be able to nudge the HN forces outside of those regions, capture Okran's Shield, and gain a more stable territory hold there.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Nice work with Okran's Shield, (player name). He may not show it but, I think the Emperor likes you... well, a little anyway...</blockquote><figcaption>Koin, Valtena defeated</figcaption></figure>

Much or all of the HN forces at Bast and Skimsands will be reduced, representing the scattering of HN forces after Valtena was knocked out, giving UC the greater advantage.
