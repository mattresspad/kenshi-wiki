The **Starving Bandits** are a frequently seen hostile [faction](/factions) found roaming primarily in and around the wilds and peripheries of [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) lands, they are similar to the [Starving Vagrants](/starving-vagrant) that are found in [United Cities](/united-cities) lands but are somewhat more organized since they are always found in groups (sometimes gigantic ones) and have leaders.

They are made up of countless nobodies, destitutes, thugs and beggars who, instead of working or begging inside settlements, have ganged up and resorted to banditry out in the wilds to feed themselves instead. They tend to roam around zones in gangs of 8, 13 or 42 and look for any wanderers (usually [the player](/nameless)) to prey upon. Due to their fragile states, many are weak and untrained for combat, but they have strength in numbers.

They are found to be especially numerous in frontier places like [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Border Zone](/border-zone) and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam) where the reach of law is weak, as their members have a clear tendency to congregate outside of core Holy Nation regions<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, likely as a result of the lack of regular patrols and policing allows for these bandit gangs to rob with relative impunity.

This faction doesn't have any named characters, bases, or an overall faction leader. They do have [leaders](/hungry-bandit-leader) for their individual squads, however. The various zones they have large presence in would suggest that they may form a loose crime network where the members group up with each other<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The population they occupy relative to the rest of the society can be considered to be small as they are never mentioned to be a significant problem anywhere<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, nor do they have an influential leader leading them<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

They will often attack neutral targets unprovoked or, more rarely, beg them for money on occasion. They will loot any unconscious characters they come across for food, as well as butcher any animals they see. If their weapon has been taken, they may loot the weapon from another character as well.

It is likely that many of these bandits end up as [Lower Servants](/lower-servant) in the [Holy Mines](/holy-mines), as they have a tendency to attack those areas as well as any [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) guard patrol they come across<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, who will promptly [shackle](/slavery) them and put them to better use. It is also hinted that these same bandits ended up as part of [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) as [Escaped Servants](/escaped-servant) if they managed to escape<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

Members of this faction never have the Honorable and rarely have Smart [Personality](/personality)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. When recruited, they will have "Player THUG" dialogue package.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Relations

### Faction Relations

This faction has no special relations with any factions. Their default relation is -10.

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   If the relations with the player are not hostile, they will occasionally come up to speak with you and ask for either c.50, c.500 or c.1,500. Giving them c.50 doesn't change alignment, giving them c.500 raises it by +2, and giving them c.1,500 raises it by +5.

## Tips

-   If you wish to avoid having your food stolen after being knocked out by Starving Bandits, you can put your party member's food inside of a backpack and store it in your inventory rather than equipped or drop it on the ground and wait for them to leave before recovering it.
-   If you are allied with Starving Bandits, they no longer attack you on sight in any situation, and if you respond to their request for food positively then they will bodyguard the character who spoke to them for 24 hours. However, despite the dialogue implying that you are giving them money, no Cats are subtracted from your total.
-   Giving a group of Starving Bandits enough money to raise the faction's alignment towards you turns that specific group of Starving Bandits into friendly NPCs, meaning that they will defend you from any nearby enemies, although they will not follow you like they will when they are allies.

## Trivia

-   Starving Bandits are a testament to how extraordinarily populated [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) civilization as a whole is compared to everyone of their neighbors.
    -   The population of the bottom destitutes alone outnumbers all the [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and several hundred times compared to [Lost Drones](/lost-drone).
    -   Regardless of how large the societal collapse of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) is (such as destruction of from [enemy razings](/stack/shek-kingdom) to [internal collapse](/blister-hill/destroyed)), the starving bandits (unlike the [similar vagrants](/starving-vagrant)) can never fully die out, they form a persistent underclass constantly renewed and replenished by crime and desperation. It could be assumed that the drop in population is offset by increased bandit activity caused by rampant instability, leaving the total number of bandits unchanged.

-   Starving Bandits have a very large and frequent presence in regions that are at the rims of The Holy Nation territory, indicative that their main operation and bands are grouping up at lawless frontiers.
-   HN army patrols are 0.7x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf)), 1.25x ([Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam)), 1.4x ([Border Zone](/border-zone)), 4.1x ([Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley)), 7.4x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)) as much in individuals as compared to **Starving Bandits**.
-   [HNO](/holy-nation-outlaws) are usually outnumbered by starvers significantly, the zones overlap include: 11.1x ([Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam)), 5.4x ([Border Zone](/border-zone)), 4.6x ([Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf)), 1.8x ([Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride)), 1.5x ([Dreg](/dreg)). 0.9x ([Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley)).
    -   This would suggest starvers are likely at any moment ~4x as much as [escaped servants](/escaped-servant).
