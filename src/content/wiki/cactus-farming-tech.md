**Cactus Farming** is [Farming Tech](/farming-tech) which allows you to build a [cactus farm](/cactus-farm).

This research is very useful for players building outposts in dry environments. The cacti can be used as ingredients for [Dustwiches](/dustwich) or [Chewsticks](/chewsticks). With [Rum Brewing](/rum-brewing-tech) researched players can also make [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum) and sell it for a good profit.
