**Deadhive Worker** is a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. Deadhive Worker is a subrace of [Hive](/hive). This race is used exclusively for [Fogman](/fogman) characters.

Compared to the [Western Hive Worker Drones](/hive-worker-drone), the Deadhive Worker is significantly feeble in all of their body parts. They also bleed slower with a rate of 0.2 instead of 0.3, as well as having twice as much vision and hunger rate of 1 instead of 0.5 and a slightly slower minimum running speed of 16 mph instead of 18 mph.

When comparing experience multipliers, the Deadhive Worker lacks the [Toughness](/toughness) experience boost (same as the [Southern Hive-equivalent](/southern-hive-worker-drone)) but does not have the penalties of [Cooking](/cooking) and [Science](/science).

## Health Statistics

-   Max Speed: 31 mph
-   Min Speed: 16 mph
-   Hunger rate: 1
-   Vision Range: 1
-   Combat move speed multi: 1.2

### Health Stats

-   Blood: 50
-   Bleed rate: 0.2
-   Heal rate: 1

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race have no special immunity to any types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which hazardous weather can occur.

  

### Equipment Slots

_Characters of this race can wear any type of equipment in these slots._

-   [Headgear](/headgear)
-   [Body Armour](/body-armour)
-   [Legwear](/legwear)

_Characters of this race cannot wear any equipment in these slots._

-   [Shirts](/shirts)
-   [Footwear](/footwear)

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Athletics](/athletics)
-   [Dexterity](/dexterity)
-   [Engineer](/engineer)
-   [Farming](/farming)
-   [Labouring](/labouring)
-   [Stealth](/stealth)
-   [Thievery](/thievery)
-   [Turrets](/turrets)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Strength](/strength)
