**Machinists** are members of the [Machinists](/machinists) faction. 10 can be found in the University in [World's End](/worlds-end). They spawn with the Smart [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
