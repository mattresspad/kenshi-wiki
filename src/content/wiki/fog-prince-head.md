**Fog Prince Head** is an [item](/items) in Kenshi. This item is only found on [Fog Princes](/fog-prince) and removing it instantly kills its owner.

Through selling this item to any vendor who can afford it, characters can earn the c.6,000 [bounty](/bounty) on Fog Princes.

### History

Like the [Gurgler Head](/gurgler-head), before this item's own icon was designed, it made use of the icon for [Potatoes](/potatoes). While the icon has been changed, the item's mesh in the game is still of a bag of potatoes.
