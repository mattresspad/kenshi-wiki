AI Cores are needed for researching various [Technologies](/technology), including [Hydroponics](/hydroponics-tech) and [Tech Level 6](/tech-level-tech). You need a total of 32 cores to research everything.

The following research requires AI Cores:

-   [Automated Hybrid Mine](/automated-hybrid-mine-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Automated Mining Tech](/automated-mining-tech-tech) (1 Core)
-   [Double-Barrel Harpoon Turret](/double-barrel-harpoon-turret-tech) (1 Core)
-   [Durable Arms](/durable-arms-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers-tech) (4 Cores)
-   [Extra Durable Legs](/extra-durable-legs-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Extra Strong Arms](/extra-strong-arms-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Fully Automatic Ore Drill](/fully-automatic-ore-drill-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [High-Speed Legs](/high-speed-legs-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Hybrid Stone Mine](/hybrid-stone-mine-tech) (1 Core)
-   [Hydroponics](/hydroponics-tech) (1 Core)
-   [Revolving-Barrel Harpoon Turret](/revolving-barrel-harpoon-turret-tech) (4 Cores)
-   [Skeleton Arms](/skeleton-arms-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Skeleton Legs](/skeleton-legs-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Stealth Legs](/stealth-legs-tech) (2 Cores)
-   [Tech Level 6](/tech-level-tech) (2 Cores)

This item is illegal in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), and thus caution is advised before entering their towns with one.

## Acquisition

AI Cores can be found through the [Artifacts](/artifacts) System or spawn in as "special items" for some residents. The system will allow AI Cores to spawn in ancient buildings such as [Ancient Tech Labs](/ancient-tech-lab), [Island Lab](/island-lab), [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap), factories and the Ashland Domes.

Fixed locations where AI Cores can be found and their amounts are...

-   [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) - 3 AI Cores. May be purchased for c.25,000 each or stolen.
-   [Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i) - 3 AI Cores. (4 If you kill the [Head of Agriculture](/head-of-agriculture) and get his)
-   [Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii) - 0 AI Cores. (1 if you kill [Rhinobot](/rhinobot) and get his)
-   [Ashland Dome III](/ashland-dome-iii) - 5 AI Cores. (6 If you kill the [General Jang](/general-jang) and get his)
-   [Ashland Dome IV](/ashland-dome-iv) - 7 AI Cores. (8 If you kill the [General Hat-12](/general-hat-12) and get his)
-   [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile) - 5 AI Cores. Found in the building containing the [stormthralls](/stormthrall). (6 If you kill the [Mad Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon) and get his)
-   [Island Lab](/island-lab) - 3 AI Cores.
-   [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) - 1 AI Core.
-   [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse) - 3 AI Cores.
-   [Narko's Trap](/narkos-trap) - 3 AI Cores.
-   Ancient Tech Lab in [The Iron Trail](/the-iron-trail) - 4 AI Cores.
-   Ancient Tech Lab in [Shun](/shun) - 4 AI Cores.
-   [Spider Factory](/spider-factory) - 3 AI Cores.
-   [Iron HQ](/iron-hq) - 0 AI Cores. (1 if you kill [Elder](/elder) and get his)

Additionally, some notable [Skeletons](/skeleton) and robotic animals have an AI Core in their inventory which if removed will cause death. These are:

-   [Cleanser Units](/cleanser-unit) (rare homeless spawns in the [Ashlands](/ashlands))
-   [Elder](/elder) ([Iron HQ](/iron-hq))
-   [General Hat-12](/general-hat-12) ([Ashland Dome IV](/ashland-dome-iv))
-   [General Jang](/general-jang) ([Ashland Dome III](/ashland-dome-iii))
-   [Head of Agriculture](/head-of-agriculture) ([Ashland Dome I](/ashland-dome-i))
-   [Mad Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon) ([Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile))
-   [Rhinobot](/rhinobot) ([Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii))

## Trivia

-   **AI Cores** somewhat resemble [Leviathan Pearls](/leviathan-pearl). Similarly, the [Cleanser Units](/cleanser-unit) resemble in some ways [Leviathans](/leviathan).
-   [King](/king) is supposed to have an AI Core as one of his death items, but due to his limited inventory size, this item does not spawn.
