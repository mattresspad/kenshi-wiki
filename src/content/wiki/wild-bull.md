**Wild Bulls** are [herbivorous](/herbivore) [animals](/fauna) which can be found roaming the wilds in herds, especially in [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam). They are stocky, muscular quadrupeds that look similar to real bovines, but with a prominent bony faceplate and back ridge. They are peaceful but will protect themselves if assaulted and make formidable opponents with their charge attack.

There are other types of Bulls, including [Domesticated Bulls](/domesticated-bull) which can be purchased from Holy Farms and [Pack Bulls](/pack-bull) which can be found in Trade Caravans. Pack Bulls and Wild Bulls are somewhat stronger in combat than [Domesticated Bulls](/domesticated-bull) due to their damage formula and do more cutting damage.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Animals can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 23 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 10 piercing damage which Wild Bulls have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 23 + 0.8774025 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.039) + Strength (0.117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 23 + 0.89232 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.052) + Strength (0.156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 23 + 0.9072375 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.065) + Strength (0.195)

## Spawns

### Regions

-   Wild Bulls are predominantly found in [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam) but appear in several other zones as well: [Berserker Country](/berserker-country), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), and [Vain](/vain).

### Spawn Groups

-   Bull Squad (3-6 Adults, 0-3 Pups)
-   Bull Herd (8-16 Adults, 0-7 Pups, Skinner's Roam)

### Other Locations

-   Bulls may also be found with wandering nomads, or in [Settled Nomad Villages](/settled-nomad-village).
