**Slaver Guards** are common soldiers of the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). They are frequently found in slaver-owned towns and act as guards and patrols to the establishments within.

Despite their status as guards, they are no stronger than the typical [Slaver](/slaver) - though they do wear better [armour](/armour).

They have a chance to spawn with a [Ranger](/ranger) crossbow to differentiate them from their [heavy counterparts](/slaver-heavy-guard). They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (20% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
