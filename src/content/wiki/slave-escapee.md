**Slave Escapee** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a member of the [Slaves](/slaves) faction. If found alone, they can be spoken to for a chance to join the player faction.

They can be found in [Bast](/bast-zone) and [The Eye](/the-eye) in squads of 1 to 5 characters.

They commonly have a Smart or Brave [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player CIVILIAN" Dialogue Package.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
