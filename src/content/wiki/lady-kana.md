**Lady Kana** is a noble member of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), as well as an important associate of the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). She can be found at [Port North](/port-north) in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

As with the other TG Nobles, Kana lives in a [Noble House](/noble-house) and is guarded by 4 [Empire Noble Guard Hooded](/empire-noble-guard-hooded) characters and 4 [Samurai Heavy](/samurai-heavy) characters.

In many [United Cities](/united-cities) Bars, characters will talk about being jealous of her skin.

She is the only NPC to wear a [Mask Type III](/mask-type-iii) - Important to note that the blueprint for this item is possible to obtain so you may craft your own. She also wields a [Meitou](/meitou)\-grade [Wakizashi](/wakizashi).

She has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## [World States](/world-states)

If Lady Kana is killed or imprisoned, then town overrides can apply to a number of locations.

[Port North](/port-north) will become destroyed. Furthermore, it will be taken over by [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Nagata](/lord-nagata) are also dead or imprisoned.

[Sho-Battai](/sho-battai) will become malnourished if it's still under [United Cities](/united-cities) rule.

If [Wada](/slave-master-wada) is also killed, [Bark](/bark) will become malnourished if it's under UC rule. Furthermore, [Bark becomes controlled by Empire Peasants](/bark/empire-peasants) if [Sanda](/lady-sanda) is also killed.

[Slave Trader](/slave-traders) and [Manhunter](/manhunter) patrol will decrease in frequency moderately at [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), provided [Haga](/slave-master-haga) wasn't already killed beforehand (which provides the same effect).

If [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive, an [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) patrol will start spawning in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

If Kana and/or [Longen](/longen) are killed, the [Traders Guild Visit](/traders-guild-visit) should no longer trigger.

Lady Kana is considered to be one of the Northern Slave Masters/Mistresses alongside with [Longen](/longen), [Slave Master Haga](/slave-master-haga), [Slave Master Grande](/slave-master-grande), [Slave Master Ruben](/slave-master-ruben), [Slave Master Wada](/slave-master-wada), and [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren). Killing or imprisoning them all will completely remove [Manhunter](/manhunter) and [Slave Hunter](/slave-hunter) in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Skimsands](/skimsands) and [The Eye](/the-eye).

(Requires Lady Kana to be alive and free)

If Kana is alive and free while [Tinfist](/tinfist) is killed/imprisoned, [Distant Hive Villages](/distant-hive-village) will become trashed ruins under Slave Traders.

## Trivia

-   Lady Kana, [Slave Mistress Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) and [Slave Mistress Grace](/slave-mistress-grace) all use [Meitou](/meitou)\-grade [Wakizashi](/wakizashi).
-   Lady Kana is the only Traders Guild noble to use a UC Noble-style title in lieu of Slave Mistress.
-   Lady Kana, by herself, is listed in the FCS as part of the Slave Traders faction. Her noble squad, however, ultimately designates her as a Traders Guild member in game.
