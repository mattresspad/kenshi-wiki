The **Ashlands** is an ashen landscape which was the domain of the [Second Empire](/second-empire). Groups of Skeletons and Iron Spiders of the [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion) patrol this area, alongside the occasional [Cleanser Unit](/cleanser-unit). There are also large clouds of [Poison Gas](/weather-effects#gas) which periodically appear in this area. As such, players are advised to bring [Fog Masks](/fog-mask) (Or one of the other two 100% gas protection helmets) if their characters are vulnerable to this danger.

This Zone is a very good source of [Research Artifacts](/research-artifacts) as well as multiple [Meitou](/meitou) and [Edgewalker](/edgewalkers) grade weapons.

## Tips

-   Either bring a [Fog Mask](/fog-mask) or avoid gas clouds to the best of your ability.
-   If you have a good sneaky character, this location can be an excellent source of income if you are not concerned with using artifacts for research (one can easily come back with hundreds of thousands of cats worth of loot from a single run).
-   One way to get to the Ashlands is through [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble) and then to [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley), then go around the [Skinhouse](/skinhouse) in the northeast. Going through [Cheaters Run](/cheaters-run) and then [Sonorous Dark's](/sonorous-dark) coast has robots if you are curious about entering their shacks.
-   You can safely swim into the Ashlands by entering a bit east of the [Sniper Tower](/sniper-tower), provided you do not look at it.
-   Gas clouds do not spawn inside of [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile) so you can feel free to remove any gas protection when there. You are also immune to gas clouds when inside/on top of a building. This includes the domes themselves.

## Trivia

-   Some brief mentions in-game of "Ashland Drifters" likely refer to the mysterious individuals that ventured through this area. They appear to have [Ashlander Stormgoggles](/ashlander-stormgoggles)/[Ashland Hat](/ashland-hat) and [Dustcoat](/dustcoat)/[Sleeveless Dustcoat](/sleeveless-dustcoat) as their equipment and is mentioned by [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) [bartender](/lagoon-barman) as well as having one interviewed by [Iyo](/iyo) in [Tech Hunter Note's Series](/tech-hunters-notes-series).
