**The Holy Flame** is an [item](/items) that can be found everywhere in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

Having one in your inventory will allow you to get out of many situations involving Paladins and Inquisitors. Holding it in your inventory is a neccessary requirement for fulfilling [Prayer Day](/prayer-day).
