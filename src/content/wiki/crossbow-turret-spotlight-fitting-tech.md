**Crossbow Turret Spotlight Fitting** is [Defense Tech](/defense-tech) which allows the player to construct [Turrets](/turrets) with [Lighting](/lighting).

This research is helpful to players who rely on [Turrets](/turrets) for base defence, as working in the dark can negatively affect ranged combat stats.
