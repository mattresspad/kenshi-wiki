**Legwear** includes any piece of equipment of any armour class that is meant to be in the pants slot.

For damage and resistance calculations, see [Damage](/damage) and [Resistances](/resistances).

The following values assume \[Standard\] quality.

## No Armour Coverage

While these items also offer no armour protection, wearing them may help characters not look like slaves while escaping [slavery](/slavery). [Gi Pants](/gi-pants) give their wearer a +2 [Martial Arts](/martial-arts) bonus, while [Ninja Pants](/ninja-pants) grant a 1.10x bonus to [Stealth](/stealth). The rest offer no other bonuses. Most [Clothing](/clothing) can be crafted at a [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench).
