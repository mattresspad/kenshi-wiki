**Droneguards** are the bulk of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) and are usually found in squads lead by a [Prince](/prince).

They can be found patrolling in [Greyshelf](/greyshelf), [Royal Valley](/royal-valley), and [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble). They are also guards in [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location) and [Southern Hive Village](/southern-hive-village), as well as any locations which are [overtaken](/town-overrides) by the Southern Hive.
