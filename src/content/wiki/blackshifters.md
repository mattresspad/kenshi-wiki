A group of ronin [Hivers](/hive), the **Blackshifters** are one of the 5 gangs from [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

They run the casinos found in [Mud Town](/mud-town) and [Shark](/shark-swamp-town) and can often be found extorting money out of people that owe them. They are led by their boss, [Big Darkbrow](/big-darkbrow) inside Shark. They appear to be smaller than [Hounds](/hounds) but bigger than [Grayflayers](/grayflayers)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They are hinted to often times scam and cheat in their gamblings<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
