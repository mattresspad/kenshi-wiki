**Outpost Guards** are high skilled protectors of the [Free Traders](/free-traders) and the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

This character is not set to spawn anywhere. The two locations controlled by the Free Traders are either guarded by [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard) or [Outlaw Farmers](/outlaw-farmer). [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge), the only location controlled by the Traders Guild, is guarded by [Samurai Gate Guards](/samurai-gate-guard) lead by a [Samurai Gate Sergeant](/samurai-gate-sergeant).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
