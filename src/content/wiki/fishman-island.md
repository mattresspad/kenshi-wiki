**Fishman Island** is a small zone south of the [Bonefields](/bonefields).

Despite being an island, players do not have to worry about needing the [Swimming](/swimming) stat to enter this zone. The lightened area on the map to the north of the island is the location of a bridge, identical to the one found connecting [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city) with [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) and the bridge found crossing the [Wend](/wend) river north of [Blister Hill](/blister-hill).

## Inhabitants

There are no [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in Fishman Island. This zone does not spawn any Camps.

### Major Towns

-   [Fish Isle](/fish-isle)

### Ancient Locations

_These are places which do not have competent inhabitants. Ancient Locations are usually lost outposts or ruins. Beware: sometimes these locations contain territorial protectors such as [Security Spiders](/security-spider)._

-   [Island Lab](/island-lab)

### Nests

[_Nests_](/nest) _are temporary locations which can spawn randomly and typically belong to [Animals](/fauna). This zone has a max number nest of 6._

-   [Gurgler Nest](/gurgler-nest) - if [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler) is alive.

### Homeless Spawn

_These are squads which spawn without being tied to a location, camp, or nest. This list is in order of likelihood. (**0.4x** multiplier)_

-   [Fishmen](/fishmen) - if King Gurgler is alive.
    -   Fishmen Patrol (20 ~ 40 [Gurglers](/gurgler), 0 ~ 3 [Alpha Gurglers](/alpha-gurgler)) {10}
-   [Crab](/crab)
    -   Crab Wander Group {1}
    -   Crab Wander Solo {1}

## Tips

-   Fishman isle has the highest crop yield for hemp due to its environment which has 100 Green and 50 Swamp. However, you cannot harvest more than 1 crop for each plant in a farm so it is a bit overkill. Any zone with 100% yield will do fine as well for hemp.
