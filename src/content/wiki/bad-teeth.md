**Bad Teeth** is a [Major Town](/major-towns) guarding a mountain pass between the fertile valley of [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and hostile wilderness of the [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam). Unlike [Stack](/stack) and [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), there are no buildings which are unique to this location.

Bad Teeth is one of the two locations for the "Holy Nation Citizen" [game start](/game-starts) (the other one is **Stack**). This town can be an advantageous starting location since the Gate Guards frequently kill roaming [River Raptors](/river-raptor) which can easily be looted for profit. It can also be discovered via exploration or by reading the **Glorious Holy [Map](/maps)**.

In [older versions](/old-world) of the game, the city was located on the eastern edge of the map and had a large number of barracks and not a single bar, looking more like a military outpost than an actual city.

[Bard](/bard) and [Griffin](/griffin) could spawn here to be recruited.
