**Book Of Sacrifice** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This is one of the few books which is worth some money. Despite the book itself claiming that it is forbidden, this book can be found inside of [Temples](/temple) in The Holy Nation, possibly for study by the [Priests](/holy-priest).

This book can also be found inside of [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village).

This book details the origins of [Okran](/okran) and [Narko](/narko). Players have speculated that the story of [Chitrin](/chitrin) bears resemblance to another lore figure in the game, [Stobe](/stobe).
