**Stubs Momuso** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). He can be found in [Shark](/shark) or [Mud Town](/mud-town). He is a rare [Scorchlander](/scorchlander) that comes with a Greenlander skin.

He is not very good with words so he often mispronounces things, and is highly aggressive. If the player is rude to him, he may attack, but if the player accuses him of "eyeballing back", he will branch off into a humorous dialogue. He can be recruited for a price no lower than c.5,000. Offering him any less will insult him and make him end the dialogue.

He has increased combat stats, with even higher [Dexterity](/dexterity) and [Athletics](/athletics), but no other skills. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).
