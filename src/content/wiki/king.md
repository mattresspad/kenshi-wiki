**King** is the only [Crimper](/crimper) in the game and a member of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive). He can be found wandering the [Royal Valley](/royal-valley) alongside his [Droneguards](/droneguard). If imprisoned by the Southern Hive, King will appear and begin to eat the character.

If the player has kidnapped the Queen, King will join a [Base Assault](/base-assault) against the player. This base assault squad also contains a [Prince](/prince), a [War Gorillo](/war-gorillo), and 8 [Droneguards](/droneguard).

Prison poles are located northeast of the Southern Hive. If player is unable to locate King, one can place prisoner into the prison pole, King will then approach for a feast.

  

## Stats

When spawned at the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-location), King will spawn with an age multiplier of 2.40x to 2.50x, bringing his effective skills well over 100. Important to note however that when hitting an animal, you gain XP through stronger opponent logic based off their stats when they are age 1.0 leading to very minimal XP. He is very tanky and is impossible to defeat solo without using Crossbows, microing or using [exploited recruited characters](/anti-slaver-jonin). However, when spawned as part of the raid that attempts to rescue the [Queen of the South](/queen-of-the-south), he will only spawn with a multiplier of 0.90x to 1.00x, making him pathetically easy.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Animals can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) Their "weapon" provides them with 5.2 cut damage and 13 blunt damage which is unaffected by stats. King has 3 attacking animations, 2 of which have 100 power and one which has only 70. To find the damage for the 70 power attack simply multiply the results for the variants below by 0.7.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.05844149 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.02925) + Strength (0.08775)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.06739199 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.039) + Strength (0.117)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.07634249 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.04875) + Strength (0.14625)
