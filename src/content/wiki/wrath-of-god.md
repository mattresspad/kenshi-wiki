The **Wrath of God** is a recurring [Holy Nation](/holy-nation) base assault event that is launched once a player takes down [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) or defeated [Retribution of God](/retribution-of-god).

It technically has a max range of 90,000 meters, and a "near" range of 50,000 meters, but both are far larger than the map itself, meaning that the initial event has a 100% of triggering each day no matter where in Kenshi's map you live.

Normally the event will be led by [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), and he is required for the initial event to trigger (Note: There is a variant that is meant to have [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena), which is supposed to trigger after [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) has been killed or captured, but in vanilla this is bugged and will not trigger due to [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) being set as the leader).

Subsequent triggers of the event by defeating it do not require [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) to have survived, though the assaults will cease unless you've defeated [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) before triggering the event (due to the aforementioned bug with his variants not triggering).

This event has three main variants, with the second triggering after defeating the first, and the third triggering after defeating the second, with the third having a high chance to repeat after each defeat. The squads would be summed up to have 80 individuals in total.

-   The first initial variant, will be led by [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), followed by 5 [Paladins](/paladin) and 3 [Inquisitors](/inquisitor).
    -   He will also be accompanied by 3 ~ 5 squads each consisting of: 1 [Inquisitor](/inquisitor), 1-2 [High Paladins](/high-paladin), 10-12 [Paladins](/paladin), and 4-9 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant).
    -   If defeated, the second variant will trigger in 30-60 hours (~1.9 days), assuming [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is still okay, or both he and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) have been killed or captured.
-   The second variant will be led by either [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), or one of the normal [Inquisitors](/inquisitor). Seta will bring his entourage of 5 [Paladins](/paladin) and 3 [Inqusitors](/inquisitor) if he joins the assault.
    -   Otherwise, the assault forces will consist of 3 ~ 4 squads each consisting of 1 [Inquisitor](/inquisitor), 1-2 [High Paladins](/high-paladin), 10-12 [Paladins](/paladin), and 4-9 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant).
    -   If defeated, it has a 95% of triggering the third variant in 36-80 hours (~2.4 days). If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) has been killed or captured, but [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) has not, the third variant will trigger, but will fail when the timer reaches 0.
-   The third and final variant will be led by either [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), or one of the normal [Inquisitors](/inquisitor).
    -   Seta will bring his entourage of 5 [Paladins](/paladin) and 3 [Inqusitors](/inquisitor) if he joins the assault.
    -   Otherwise, the assault forces will consist of 3 ~ 5 squads each consisting of 1 [Inquisitor](/inquisitor), 1-2 [High Paladins](/high-paladin), 10-12 [Paladins](/paladin), and 4-9 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant).
    -   If defeated, and as long as [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) has not been killed or captured, it will have a 100% chance of repeating the third event again in 168-672 hours.
        -   If [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) has been killed or captured, but [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) has not, the event will trigger again but will fail (due to bug) when the timer reaches 0.
        -   If both have been killed or captured, there is a 95% chance of repeating in 168-672 hours (~17.5 days).
