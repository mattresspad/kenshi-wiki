The **Unholy Chest Plate** is a Heavy Armour that is equipped in the Body Armour slot. It can be obtained at the [Bookshop](/bookshop) in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), rarely at a smithy in [Shark](/shark), at a Tech Hunter shop in [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) or World's End, at Shek armor shops in [Admag](/admag) and any HN town that has been taken over by Shek, and the [Mongrel](/mongrel) armor shop.

It can be randomly equipped by [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard).

It can be crafted in the [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy) after researching the appropriate blueprint.

As it is apparent from the description, it is a direct copy of the [Holy Chest Plate](/holy-chest-plate), a [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)'s faction armour.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x6**.

## Appearance

The Unholy Chest Plate is made up of several sheets of metal overlapping each other. The armour covers the entire stomach and chest but leaves the arms from the shoulder down entirely exposed. There are metal gauntlets that cover the character's forearms and hands. Two additional overlapping metal leg guards hang from the waist and cover the upper thighs of the character.
