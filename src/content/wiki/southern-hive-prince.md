**Southern Hive Princes** are a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. They are a subrace of the Southern [Hive](/hive).

Compared to their [Western cousins](/hive-prince), the Southern Hive Prince have a lesser bleeding rate of 0.2 compared to the 0.3 of a [Western Hive](/western-hive) Prince, and also have no bonuses of xp boost from [Lockpicking](/lockpicking), [Perception](/perception) and [Toughness](/toughness) like they do. Southern Hive Princes have slightly more health than their hungrier [Deadhive](/fogmen) [bretheren](/fog-prince) and have xp bonuses in [Field Medic](/field-medic) and [Science](/science).

Characters of this race can become [Playable Characters](/playable-characters) if bought or freed from slavery.

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Athletics](/athletics)
-   [Dexterity](/dexterity)
-   [Field Medic](/field-medic)
-   [Science](/science)
-   [Stealth](/stealth)
-   [Thievery](/thievery)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Labouring](/labouring)
-   [Strength](/strength)

## Trivia

-   Southern Princes are the rarest type of race that can spawn as a slave in Slaver shops, as only 1 in a thousand can spawn at a time. They are so rare that it's possible that only a single Southern Prince slave may spawn per save.
