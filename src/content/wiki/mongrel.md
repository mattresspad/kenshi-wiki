**Mongrel**, the sole settlement of [eponymous faction](/mongrel-faction), is an outlaw [town](/major-towns) located in the middle of the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands). It is an isolated town located in the middle of nowhere made up of various shady individuals lucky enough to safely reach there.

Due to its location, it can be extremely dangerous to attempt to reach it. Players may lose characters to the [Fogmen](/fogmen) on the journey if not careful. However, once you reach the town you will be protected by many powerful gate guards. Purchasing a **[Map](/maps) to Mongrel** prior to journey can help to stay on the right path.

The town itself has good variety of shops, many having goods not available in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). [Beep](/beep), [Crumblejon](/crumblejon), [Infinite Wingwang](/infinite-wingwang), and [Shryke](/shryke) are [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) found in Mongrel, either in one of the [bars](/bar) or wandering the town.

The town guards are in constant conflict with waves of Fogmen patrols attempting to breach the gates and carry off prisoners (potentially including the player). While the guards are powerful foes, it nevertheless pays to be vigilant while visiting, as Fogmen sometimes slip through the defenses.

Many [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> try to make their way to Mongrel, although they are often killed and devoured by the surrounding horde of Fogmen.

## Lore

The lore of Mongrel, much like the outlaw town itself, is shady and shrouded in a thick fog of mystery.

The founding of the town can be estimated to be decades ago as prescribed in [Mission Graeme: Status](/mission-graeme-status) and [Maril's Diary Series](/marils-diary-series).

In the texts it is detailed that after an unknown failed operation led to their target being deceased, it is hinted that one of their ninjas in the mission was captured by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) authorities and sentenced to [Rebirth](/rebirth). A team of at least 5 ninjas were dispatched to Rebirth under the cover of night to mount a rescue operation for their teammate.

After escaping Rebirth into the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands), the ninjas eventually lost their pursuing [sentinels](/holy-sentinel) who were swarmed by the [Fogmen](/fogmen). They found a fitting spot to set up their base, which has likely since become known as the town **Mongrel**.

In the current time, the town itself is an outlaw haven composed of various criminals such as [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves), and a few [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), the vast majority of which are stranded on the isolated island. The town has been hinted to run low on food supplies<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Arc](/arc), a legendary swordsman, can be found inside this town.

## Tips

Care should be taken with regards to sleeping squad members as certain Fogmen could slip through the defenses and simply pick up the resting squad member and run off, characters cannot resist while being carried so it would require another nearby member to take down the said Fogmen kidnapper.

## Trivia

-   One definition of 'mongrel' is that it is "Used to describe something of mixed origins", this is fittingly named for a town that has people from all walks of life and races, be they [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), [Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws) or [shady ninjas](/shinobi-thieves).
