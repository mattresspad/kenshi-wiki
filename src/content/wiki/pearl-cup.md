**Pearl Cups** can be found in Residential Buildings or in Hive village shops. When starting out, stealing this item along with other junk can help players increase their [Thievery](/thievery) stat with little risk (if the building is empty) and reward.

It is quite possible that this item is made from [Leviathan Pearls](/leviathan-pearl), alongside other pearl-related things such as [Pearl Vase](/pearl-vase), [Pearl Sword Holder](/pearl-sword-holder) and [Pearl Urn](/pearl-urn).

This [item](/items) is also used to build a [Thief Training Box III](/thief-training-boxes). As many other Pearl items, it can be bought from the [Hive Trader](/hive-trader) in Hive villages.

## Uses

-   1 Pearl Cup is required to construct a [Thief Training Box III](/thief-training-boxes)
