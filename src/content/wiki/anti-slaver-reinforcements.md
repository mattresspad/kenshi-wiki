**Anti-Slaver Reinforcements** is an [Event](/events) that will trigger in response to the [Eyegore's Assault](/eyegores-assault), [Noble's Wrath](/nobles-wrath), and [Noble's Wrath Assault](/nobles-wrath-assault) events triggering, as long as the [Player](/nameless) is allied to the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) faction, and as long as [Tinfist](/tinfist) and [Jaegar](/jaegar) are still alive and free.

Once triggered, the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) will send reinforcements from [Spring](/spring). While they will move faster than most hostile events, they can still arrive too late if the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) is closer to the hostile event's spawn location than to [Spring](/spring). These reinforcements will consist of 2 squads, each made up of 4 [Anti-Slaver Jonin](/anti-slaver-jonin) and will be led by [Jaegar](/jaegar).

Once the reinforcements arrive, they will give a short speech, and then start patrolling the player's base, attacking hostiles, defending the player's characters, and healing wounded allies.
