**Holy Seal** is an item which is given to the player through dialogue from [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). The player must have 75 relations with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) to receive this item. Please note that you must not be a female or have robotic limbs and must not be allied with any of their enemies to speak with the Phoenix. The dialogue you must complete is to agree to destroy the Sheks/Flotsam Ninjas. Simply saying you will try or responding that they will be purged will reward you will the Seal.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Special item.<p>Reads: 'Absolver of the Infernal'</p><p>A seal given to you by the Holy Lord Phoenix, blessed be his name. <a href="/events">Show</a> it to paladins for aid.</p></blockquote></figure>

It has a value of c.30,000 and as such can be sold for c.15,000. The item can be used to interact with Paladin-ranked patrol leaders. (i.e [Paladins](/paladin) and [High Paladins](/high-paladin) at the front of the formation) The item is not consumed upon use and may be stacked in backpacks which permit the stacks of certain item types.

## Trivia

-   The Holy Seal can be obtained an unlimited number of times by speaking to the Phoenix, making it one of the fastest (Exploiting) ways to make money.
-   The troops who bodyguard you are contracted for 36 hours.
-   It is possible to hire Inquisitor patrols if the Inquisitor is knocked out/taken away and a High Paladin takes the lead of the new formation.
