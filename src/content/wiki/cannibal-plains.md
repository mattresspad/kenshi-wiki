Located in the North West of the map, the **Cannibal Plains** are characterized by the large presence of [Cannibals](/cannibals), as well as some [Shrieking Bandits](/shrieking-bandits) and [Berserkers](/berserkers) mixed in the fight. [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters) sometimes come here to hunt the Cannibals, and sometimes get hunted by them instead.

Players can easily be overwhelmed by cannibals as they frequently appear in this region.

## Tips

-   This area can be dangerous to traverse, as being swarmed by 40+ enemies is very possible. If traveling through this regions, players should consider bringing either large squads or well-trained, well-geared characters (or bring those who [can run as fast as possible](/athletics)).
-   Avoid sleeping on camp beds in open area without guards, as Cannibals can easily pick up player characters who are sleeping and they cannot break free.

## Trivia

-   This area likely has been the source of most of the Cannibal spawns from the western part of the continent, including the zones adjacent or close to this region such as [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Purple Sands](/purple-sands), [Berserker Country](/berserker-country) and [Dreg](/dreg).
