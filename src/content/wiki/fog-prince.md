**Fog Princes** are the leaders of the [Fogmen](/fogmen) faction. They each have a head which can be removed from their inventory and sold for a bounty of c.6,000. Fog Princes are never alone in the wild, often surrounded by [Fogmen](/fogman) and [Fog Heavies](/fog-heavy). Seeking the Princes out in the fog, even with several well-equipped combatants, can be very unforgiving.

Fortunately, Fogmen often attempt to attack [Mongrel](/mongrel), including Fog Princes. Mongrel's [Shinobi Guards](/shinobi-guard) are more than capable of defending the town. Before being defeated, Fog Princes usually reach the Mongrel Barracks, however some Princes can be defeated before even entering the city gates. Fog Princes can be difficult to spot in large piles of Fogmen, but their distinct yellow blood and round head shape helps. Another method of spotting them is looking for the [Ninja Blade](/ninja-blade) they wield. After looting their heads from their bodies, the bounties can be earned by simply selling the heads to any vendor.

It is also possible to steal the Fog Prince head from imprisoned Fog Princes in the Watchtower.

Fog Princes can also be found in the [Tower of Fog](/tower-of-fog) and [Deadhive Overruns](/deadhive-overrun). They are often attracted to [Fog Deathyard](/fog-deathyard) prisoners and can be found eating the captive.

They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

## Trivia

-   Fog Princes, if captured and set free, remain non-aggressive unless provoked.
-   Fog Princes (And Heavies) apply -1 Bandaged health for every 1 cut damage they apply when eating a character on a Fog pole. Negative bandaged health will not heal on it's own and needs to be bandaged to get back to normal.
