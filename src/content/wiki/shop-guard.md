**Shop Guards** are guards of the faction the shop they reside in belongs to. The possible factions include the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants), [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), [Free Traders](/free-traders), [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction), [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), and [United Cities](/united-cities). They are normally found residing in bars and shops in the city.

If a character is still inside the shop during closing hours the Guards will remind them to leave before resorting to violence. Intruders found inside the shop after closing hours are immediately assaulted.

There is a [Holy Shop Guard](/holy-shop-guard) variant that are found exclusively in the Holy Nation. Aside from equipping shoddier blunt weapons and slightly weaker stats, their main difference is that the holy variant are always greenlander males. However, some shops will still spawn normal Shop Guards, resulting in a chance for HN shop guards to be greenlander females.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
