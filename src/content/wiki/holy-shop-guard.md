**Holy Shop Guards** are guards of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They can normally be found inside shops in Holy Nation towns.

They are slightly weaker than the normal [Shop Guard](/shop-guard) and wield inferior blunt weapons, they also appear to have better jack of all trades skills on Laboring, Farming and Cooking than the standard Shop Guard. They have a set gender and race as well as holy nation-related dialogue lines to further set them apart. Although unique to holy nation, they can also spawn alongside with the standard **Shop Guard**.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
