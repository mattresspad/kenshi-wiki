The **Cannibals** (AKA the "Painted Tribe") are a group of tribal humanoids native to the [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), [Darkfinger](/darkfinger) and other surrounding zones. While most densely present in the northern areas of Kenshi, these groups are known to migrate and can expand as far away as [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Bast](/bast-zone), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Dreg](/dreg).

Cannibals will attack other factions and carry unconscious organic members of other factions to their [camp](/cannibal-camp) or [Village](/cannibal-village). When the cages start to get full, they will periodically start beating their prisoners to death and throwing them into the cooking pits.

They will not eat [Skeletons](/skeletons) or animals, but they are still hostile to them. Strangely, similar to Skeletons and [Hivers](/hive), all Cannibals are immune to Acid.

## History

According to the [Machinists'](/machinists) book, the [The Cannibal Plains: De-evolution Of Man?](/the-cannibal-plains-de-evolution-of-man), the "Painted Tribe" has existed for thousands of years, and they are suspected to actually be a [race](/cannibal-race) of "devolved" humans.

One theory suggests that the Cannibals are descended from inhabitants of the northwestern [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs), and started degenerating due to environmental toxins, the effects of lab experiments, or simply severe famine.

Another theory suggests that the Cannibals existed in the same time frame as the Ancient Lab inhabitants, with the latter being killed off by migrating Cannibals.

The Cannibals are known to feed on one another as well, since most outsiders avoid the Cannibal Plains.
