**Public Sales** is [Core Tech](/core-tech) which allows players to construct [Shop Counters](/shop-counter).

Players who are interested in making some money through trading may want to complete this research. However, characters will only buy items which are on their [shopping list](/npc-shopping-behavior), making this only a reliable way to sell vices and first aid kits.
