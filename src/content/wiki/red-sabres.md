The **Red Sabres** are a bandit group from [The Swamp](/the-swamp) led by their leader, [Red Sabre Boss](/red-sabre-boss). The **Red Sabres** can be found roaming in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) and the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands). They also reside at their base, [Red Sabre Hideout](/red-sabre-hideout), located southwest in [the Swamp](/the-swamp) with their leader.

True to their name, their choice of clothing is red while their weapon of choice are Sabres. They are constantly in a power struggle with their rivals, the [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Trivia

-   Originally was called "Water Clan", seen in the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
-   It's possible that the name "Red Sabres" is a reference to [The Red Rebellion](/the-red-rebellion). Interestingly, the rogues mentioned in the book and the Red Sabre's both have presence in the [Southern Plains](/south-wetlands).
-   While [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) patrols will increase if the leader of the Red Sabres is down, it is not the same vice-versa.
