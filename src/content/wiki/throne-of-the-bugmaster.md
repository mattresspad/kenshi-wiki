The **Throne of the Bugmaster** is the base of the notorious [Bugmaster](/bugmaster). His base is located in the middle of the crater of [Arach](/arach).

The Bugmaster is surrounded by a giant army of [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) and [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider). It is highly recommended that players avoid encountering Bugmaster until they have a group of high level [stats](/statistics) and mid to endgame gear.

## Loot

This location contains a unique [map](/maps) item named "Bugmaster's Map." When used, this map will reveal the location of [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile). If it has already been discovered the map cannot be used.

## Spawns

These characters can spawn inside this location and engage in "Home-Territory Aggression" behavior when they spot the player.

### Blood Spiders

The Bugmaster can have 6 to 10 pet [Blood Spiders](/blood-spider).

### Skin Spiders

The Bugmaster has 15 pet [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider).
