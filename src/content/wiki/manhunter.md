**Manhunters** are the sole members of the [Manhunters](/manhunters) faction. They roam and patrol around [United Cities](/united-cities) territories in groups of 5-14, hoping to capture any slave they find.

Occasionally, if there are no witnesses, they will attempt to attack and capture freemen to pass them off as escaped slaves even if doing so is clearly a very bad idea. In these situations, fighting back in self-defense will not incur a [bounty](/bounty), however losing may result in some or all of your involved characters being captured as [slaves](/slavery).

They will not target the player if the player is allied with either [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), if there are any police nearby, if they are inside an NPC town, or if they are lesser in number than the player's squad. Although if the character in question IS indeed an escaped slave, they will still try to recapture them, nonetheless.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
