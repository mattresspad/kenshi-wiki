**Flotsam Recruit** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits) and member of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas). She can be hired for c.3,000.

They can be found in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) or any other location controlled by the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).

She commonly has an Honorable, Traitorous, Smart, Dumb, or Brave [Personality](/personality) and after recruitment will have the "Player flotsam" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
