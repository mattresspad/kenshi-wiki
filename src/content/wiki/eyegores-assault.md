**Eyegore's Assault** (also shown in-game as "Eyegore Assault" instead) is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the player has killed or kidnapped [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Longen](/longen), or as a retaliation for defeating the [Elite Hunter Assault](/elite-hunter-assault) event.

In the scenario of a Tengu trigger, it will have a 200% chance to trigger each day anywhere on the map, as its range is more than 3x the size of the map.

For a Longen trigger, it will have a 50% chance to trigger within 5,000 meters, and the chance will scale down with distance until it reaches a 0% chance at 11,000 meters.

Killing or capturing [Eyegore](/eyegore) will stop the events from being triggered. In addition, killing or capturing [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga), [Sanda](/lady-sanda), [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) will stop the Longen or Tengu triggered variants.

Once triggered, the [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) will launch an assault on the player's base. The forces making up the assault will differ drastically depending on the trigger.

-   Tengu triggered assault will consist of 3-9 squads (80 individuals set).
-   Longen triggered assault will consist of 2 squads (20 individuals set)
-   Retaliation trigger will consist of 2-4 squads. (35 individuals set)

The squad composition will have

-   1 [Eyegore](/eyegore), 1 [Samurai Captain](/samurai-captain), 2 [Samurai Sergeants](/samurai-sergeant), 3 [Samurai Heavies](/samurai-heavy), 1 [Samurai Scout](/samurai-scout), 10 [Samurai](/samurai).

The remaining squads have two possible variants they can spawn as. With the 1st one spawning more frequently than the latter.

-   1 [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant), 1-3 [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), 10-15 [Samurai](/samurai), 8-20 [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript).
-   1 [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant), 1-3 [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), 5-8 [Samurai](/samurai), 1-4 [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript).

Once the assault arrives Eyegore and his Samurai will have a short conversation, usually differing depending on the trigger source or faction relations. And then Eyegore and his soldiers will attack.

If victorious, the assault should leave the base shortly afterward. If defeated and it was a Longen or Tengu trigger, there will be a 40% chance to trigger an [Elite Hunter Assault](/elite-hunter-assault) in 24-72 hours. If it was a retaliation trigger, nothing else will trigger.
