**Return for Blood** is an event that can trigger if you refuse to pay tribute during the [Tribute Round](/tribute-round) or [Return for Tribute](/return-for-tribute) events.

It also requires that [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seto](/seto), [Bayan](/bayan) to all not be killed or captured, and that the player is _not_ allied to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

If all the conditions are met, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will trigger an assault on the player's base.

This assault will consist of 1 ~ 2 squads, each made up of (set to 8 individuals):

-   0 ~ 1 [Shek Scouts](/shek-scout) and 6 ~ 9 [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior).

Once the assault arrives the leader will taunt the player in a short speech, then attack the player's base and characters.

If the assault is victorious, they'll leave the player's base shortly after, and [Return for Tribute](/return-for-tribute) will trigger in 72 ~ 150 hours (~4.6 days).

If the assault is defeated, [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) will trigger in 120 ~ 180 hours (~6.25 days)
