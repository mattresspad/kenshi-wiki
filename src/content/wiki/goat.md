**Goats** are small [Herbivores](/herbivore) that roam free in many parts of the world, usually in groups of around 8, and can also be found domesticated, mostly by [Nomads](/nomads). They look much like real-world goats except for their prominent bony faceplates. They can be hunted for their [Raw Meat](/raw-meat) and [Animal Skin](/animal-skin) drops but make tougher melee opponents than their low stats might suggest. A new character who mistakes them for an easy meal will likely get an unpleasant surprise.

Goats do not have [Nests](/nest), although domesticated ones can be found in Nomad [Goat Herder Camps](/goat-herders-camp). They can be purchased from those herders, and also from Nomad animal shops and caravans. A unique goat named [Cornelius](/cornelius) can be purchased in [Settled Nomad Village](/settled-nomad-village). Like all tamed animals in Kenshi (and many real goats), domesticated goats will happily eat any type of food at all.

Goats are a niche addition to a player squad. They cannot be used to haul goods and are not as sturdy or strong as a [Bonedog](/bonedog). But they are relatively fast, not very expensive, mature quickly, and do not eat as much as most other tamed animals, potentially making them useful as expendable scouts/kiters or a low-maintenance choice for early base defense.

20 to 30 hostile Adult Goats can be found in the [Tower of Goats](/tower-of-goats) in [Sinkuun](/sinkuun).

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Goats can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 18 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 5 piercing damage which Goats have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 18 + Martial Arts(0.77961008) + Dexterity(0.77961008)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts(0.0039) + Strength(0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 18 + Martial Arts(0.83928) + Dexterity(0.83928)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts(0.0052) + Strength(0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 18 + Martial Arts(0.89894992) + Dexterity(0.89894992)

**Blunt** **\=** Martial Arts(0.0065) + Strength(0.0195)

## Trivia

-   Goats do the same damage as a Bonedog if they have identical stats.
