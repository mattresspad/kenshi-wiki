**Deadhive Soldier** is a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. Deadhive Soldier is a subrace of [Hive](/hive). This race is used exclusively for [Fog Heavy](/fog-heavy) characters.

Compared to the [Western Hive Soldier Drones](/hive-soldier-drone), the Deadhive Soldier has only half as much HP in the Head body part, making it 100 instead of 200, the same as a [Human](/human). They also bleed slower with a rate of 0.2 instead of 0.3 and have a greater hunger rate of 1 instead of 0.6.

When comparing experience multipliers, Deadhive Soldiers don't have the standard bonus in [Toughness](/toughness), instead having a +10% bonus in [Strength](/strength). They also don't have the penalties in [Perception](/perception), like their [Southern-Hive equivalent](/southern-hive-soldier-drone).

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race have no special immunity to any types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which hazardous weather can occur.

  

### Equipment Slots

_Characters of this race can wear any type of equipment in these slots._

-   [Body Armour](/body-armour)
-   [Legwear](/legwear)

_Characters of this race cannot wear any equipment in these slots._

-   [Headgear](/headgear)
-   [Shirts](/shirts)
-   [Footwear](/footwear)

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Melee Attack](/melee-attack)
-   [Strength](/strength) +10%

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Armour Smith](/armour-smith)
-   [Cooking](/cooking)
-   [Engineer](/engineer)
-   [Farming](/farming)
-   [Field Medic](/field-medic)
-   [Robotics](/robotics)
-   [Science](/science)
-   [Weapon Smith](/weapon-smith)
