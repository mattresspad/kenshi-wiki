**Note From Blue Eyes** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in [The Swamp](/the-swamp). This item can only be found in a Hideout in [Rot](/rot) and [Big Al](/big-al)'s Hideout in [Stone Rat Village](/stone-rat-village).

This note is written by notorious con artist [Blue Eyes](/blue-eyes). Despite the content of the note, Blue Eyes cannot be found in [The Swamp](/the-swamp).
