An unnamed member of the [Vagrants](/vagrants) faction. They are a variant of Bar Thugs which can be found in bars in [The Hub](/the-hub), [Waystation](/waystation), or in [Shark](/shark).

This thug will talk to the player about the Blood Bandits, [Cannibals](/cannibals), or the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) if he is given c. 50 worth of drinks.

They have a 2% chance of having a bounty from 250 to 1750 Cats.
