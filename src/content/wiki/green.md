**Green** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) found in [The Swamp](/the-swamp). Specifically, he can be found in a Bar or [The Dancing Skeleton](/the-dancing-skeleton) in [Shark](/shark) or [Mud Town](/mud-town). Players will need to pay off his bar tab in order to recruit him, meaning he costs c.2,300.

He is an ex-smuggler who has an "Eye for shooting." His group was wiped out by a rival gang (called [Skull Collectors](/krals-chosen)) and now he spends his days as a bar fly, racking up an excessive tab.

He always has a Smart [Personality](/personality). He has 6 unique lines of dialogue, many of which are shared by [Miu](/miu).
