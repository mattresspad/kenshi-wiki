**Band of Bones** are the generic members of the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) faction. They can be found roaming the regions of [Bonefields](/bonefields), [High Bonefields](/high-bonefields), [Shem](/shem), [Shun](/shun), [Spider Plains](/spider-plains), [The Hook](/the-hook), and [The Swamp](/the-swamp) in groups of 4 to 8. If [Tora the Fearless](/tora-the-fearless) is not alive however, these groups are reduced to 2 to 4 members.

They live in the [Exile Camp](/exile-camp) but if [Big Gray](/big-gray) and [Big Grim](/big-grim) are not alive while Tora is, they take over [Grayflayer Village](/grayflayer-village) and may make that their new headquarters.

They will raid the player outpost if it is in range with a sizeable force.

## Interactions

If a **Band of Bones** warrior is found in a non-shek biome, they may challenge the player to a battle, which the player can decline. Accepting or insulting them will cause a normal fight.
