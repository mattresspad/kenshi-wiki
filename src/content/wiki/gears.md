**Gears** is an item which can often be found on the shelves of [Trade Goods](/trade-goods-trader) shops across Kenshi. They have no function outside trade and due to their low value are considered a [junk](/junk) item.

Given their abundance in Kenshi's world, one could speculate that they are used by civilians and certain specialized engineers to fix broken down manual/auto machineries.
