**Biofuel** is [Electrics Tech](/electrics-tech) which unlocks the ability to construct two types of [Biofuel Distilleries](/biofuel-distillery). Research into the third type of distillery is available after completion of this research.

In areas where [Power](/power) cannot be adequately generated from the wind, [Generators](/generator-ii) are necessary. Since large quantities of [Fuel](/fuel) are generally not available in shops, players should research the generation of biofuel.
