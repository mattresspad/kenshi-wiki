**Inquisitors** are elite law enforcers and commanding generals of the [Holy Nation](/holy-nation). They run the Holy Nation's [Inquisitoriums](/police-station).

Inquisitors are charged with seeking out heretics of [Okran](/okran), as well as acting as the chief leaders of the Holy Army. They are also responsible for the day-to-day operation of town police stations, known as Inquisitoriums. Their duties include locking up prisoners, handling bails, and re-selling confiscated equipment.

Inquisitors are powerful warriors, even stronger than [High Paladins](/high-paladin). They have authority over High Paladins as well, often leading them on patrols. Inquisitors are therefore among the highest ranks in the Holy Nation, especially High Inquisitors, who serve as the [right](/high-inquisitor-seta) [hand](/high-inquisitor-valtena) men of the [Holy Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).

They wear \[High\] armour (10% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction. The color used is "Holy Nation Level 3".

## Player Interactions

Inquisitors are dismissive of female and non-human characters, so only human males can turn in bounties by speaking to them. However, a female/non-human character can still drop a bounty in a cage to automatically get the money.

If [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is killed or imprisoned, the dialogue with Inquisitors will be locked off.

One can bail out their party members by paying a sum of money, as well as buying some of the equipment and confiscated goods inside the inquisitorium by talking to an Inquisitor.

When you turn in a bounty, Inquisitors don't assume you expect payment unless you ask for it. If you turn in a bounty to them without asking for payment, you will get a decent (+5) reputation boost with the Holy Nation.

Upon request, they will give bounty money, at the cost of a smaller (+2) reputation boost and a disapproving lecture.

Allying with [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) or [United Cities](/united-cities) and then turning in bounties will only give the bounty reward but no relation boost and a lecture. Allying with [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) or [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) then speaking to an Inquisitor will prompt an immediate attack.

If the player turns in "high-level" bounties such as [Moll](/moll) or [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) as a female or non-human, the Inquisitor will be suspicious of the player character and attempt to arrest them as well, this can be solved by having the bounty be handed in by a human male character in the squad. If one doesn't have a human male, they can see for recruits in nearby bars.

Uniquely, while Inquisitors are tolerant of most other bounty characters being not handed in, they will demand [Tinfist](/tinfist) be handed over to them if he is presented in front of them. Refusal to do so will result in both the bounty, and the interacting player character to be attempted to be arrested by the Inquisitor.

They are seen leading the [Holy Nation Assault (Major)](/holy-nation-assault-major) and the [Holy Nation Assault (Inquisitor)](/holy-nation-assault-inquisitor) events against a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) as War Leaders. Inquisitors will also lead the [Retribution of God](/retribution-of-god) event in case the [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is not available. In that case they will command one or two remaining squads. They will also lead the [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god) event under the same condition.

  
Inquisitors also lead patrol squads, mainly consisting of Paladins and High Paladins, roaming around Holy Nation territories.
