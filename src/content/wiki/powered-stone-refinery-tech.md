**Powered Stone Refinery** is [Industry Tech](/industry-tech) which allows players to construct [Stone Processor II](/stone-processor).

This research is a stepping stone to further building upgrades and eventually [Hybrid Stone Mine](/hybrid-stone-mine-tech). The building this research unlocks is beneficial to players who frequently create [Building Materials](/building-materials).
