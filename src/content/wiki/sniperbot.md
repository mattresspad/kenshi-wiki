**Sniperbots** are members of the [Old Machines](/old-machines) which can be found patrolling in the southeast. Specifically, they can spawn in the zones [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley), [Sonorous Dark](/sonorous-dark), [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble), [The Crags](/the-crags), and [The Pits](/the-pits).

[Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley) is likely to be exclusively named after them.

They spawn alone and can be difficult to spot, allowing them chances for a free shot using their crossbow, which is almost a guaranteed hit due to their high ranged skills.

They wear \[High\] armour (20% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
