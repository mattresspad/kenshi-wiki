**Noble's Wrath** (not to be confused with [Noble's Wrath Assault](/nobles-wrath-assault)) is an [event](/events) that will occur when the player pisses off someone in the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) by doing actions such as disrespecting them, punching them in the guts (or other body parts) or stealing in a [Noble House](/noble-house).

After the requirements up there are fulfilled, the [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) will launch an assault on the players outpost in 2-100 hours, depending on the action committed. This assault consisting of one squad, led by an [Elite Hunter](/elite-hunter), accompanied by 3-5 additional [Elite Hunters](/elite-hunter).

Upon arriving at the designated player outpost, the War Leader will give a short speech and proceed to attack. If victorious, the assault will leave shortly afterward. If defeated, an [Elite Hunter Assault](/elite-hunter-assault) event will trigger in 72-100 hours.
