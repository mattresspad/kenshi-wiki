**Automatic Loom (Hemp)** is [Crafting Tech](/crafting-tech) research which allows the player to construct a Hemp Auto-Loom. This is the [Hemp](/hemp) equivalent of [Automatic Loom (Cotton)](/automatic-loom-cotton-tech).

This research can be helpful for players who craft a lot of armour and create [Fabrics](/fabrics) from Hemp. Though caution should be taken as **Hemp** is considered to be Illegal by the [United Cities](/united-cities).
