**Foul Raw Meat** is a [food](/food) item which can be gathered from many [Animals](/fauna) and the humanoid Fishmen. It is edible for [Hive](/hive) characters (except for Hive [Princes](/hive-prince)) and [Domesticated Animals](/fauna#domesticated). It can also be added to the [Animal Feeder](/animal-feeder). While the nutritional value of 10 is considered low, high amounts can be harvested from many creatures.

  

## Gathered From

-   [Beak Thing](/beak-thing) - 2
-   [Crab](/crab) - 1
-   [Fishmen](/fishmen) - 4
-   [Megacrab](/megacrab) - 20
-   [Barnabus](/barnabus) - 20
-   [Landbat](/landbat) - 3
-   [River Raptor](/river-raptor) - 4
-   [Swamp Raptor](/swamp-raptor) - 6
-   [Skimmer](/skimmer) - 8

A good way to gain a large amount of them is by fighting river raptors in the Lands of Okran. They are attacked by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) troops on sight.

## Trivia

-   Players could previously cook this item into [Dried Gristle Flaps](/dried-gristle-flaps), a food item which has been removed from the game.
