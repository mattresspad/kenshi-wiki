The **UC Weapons Traders** are unnamed shopkeepers of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). They can be found manning [Small Empire Weapon Shop](/small-empire-weapon-shop) and [Empire Weapon Shop](/empire-weapon-shop) in [United Cities](/united-cities) territory.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
