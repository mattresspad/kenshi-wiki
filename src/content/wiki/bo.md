**Bo** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) who is part of the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers). She can be found in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), accompanied by an [Anti-Slaver Jonin](/anti-slaver-jonin).

She can be found sitting down in either the Tech Hunter Bar or the Waystation.

Despite her small stature, she is called 'Big' Bo by members of the Anti-Slavers.

She is also capable of asking a squad of Anti-Slavers you encounter to bodyguard your squad for three days. Strangely, this seems to work on [Tinfist's](/tinfist) squad, so one can potentially get Tinfist and [Grey](/grey) to follow them around. Be very careful if followed by Tinfist as if he happens to be KOed or bugs out he may register as "not okay" for his world state and be treated as dead.

She appears to always spawn wearing a Masterwork [Dark Leather Shirt](/dark-leather-shirt) if the squad size multiplier is at 1.0 (default game settings).

## Interactions

**Big Bo** can be recruited if the player is an ally of the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) and has killed or imprisoned: [Eyegore](/eyegore), [Lady Kana](/lady-kana), [Longen](/longen), [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), one of the Slave Masters, or one of the nobles. Bo will also join the player if the player is an enemy of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), [United Cities](/united-cities), or [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), but they will also need to capture a noble.

If the player captures Bo, then she can be recruited as long as the player is not allied with the [United Cities](/united-cities), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), or the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

## World States

If the player has killed or imprisoned Bo alongside [Grey](/grey) and [Jaegar](/jaegar) while [Tinfist](/tinfist) is still alive, it will cause an [anti-slaver raid](/jonin-assault) on a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost).

If recruited, Bo can sometimes hail Anti-Slaver squads to follow the player for three days.

## Trivia/Tips

-   If training defense/dodge on the guards in Black Scratch be sure to avoid Bo's squadmate. The AI of her squad will cause the unit to pick you up when you are knocked unconscious but never drop you. If playing solo this is a hard lock as you are unable to free yourself and they will only move between the two bars in town.
-   Bo is one of two NPCs with an actual set Robotics level. The other character who has a Robotics level set is [Tinfist](/tinfist).
