**Skin Bandits** (AKA the "Skinned Ones") are a faction of [Skeletons](/skeleton) who hunt [humans](/human) for their skin in order to turn them into [clothing](/clothing). They can be found in [Sonorous Dark](/sonorous-dark) and are led by [Savant](/savant).

They will knock organic members of other factions unconscious and bring them to a [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine) which will delimb and eventually kill the prisoner.

## History

The Skin Bandits have been around for an unspecified long time, likely after the collapse of the [Second Empire](/second-empire). Nearly everyone living outside of the Forbidden Zone seems to be unaware of their existence, as their horrific actions have widely been believed to be the work of mythical creatures. However, the [United Cities](/united-cities) are somewhat aware of Savant and the skinless victims that were found, as seen on his wanted poster, but don't have any details on his appearance or the faction he leads.

Upon entering [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast) in a group, one of the player's characters will mention tales of skinless corpses found in [The Pits](/the-pits).

According to the [Tech Hunter's Notes Series](/tech-hunters-notes-series), Stone-Head, the last surviving member of the [Ashland](/ashlands) Drifters, survived a run-in with the Skin Bandits during his group's expedition into and out of the Ashlands. In the interview, he vaguely details the encounter of what they did to his friend Cobby and possibly other members of the group, but starts trailing off, staring into space for ten minutes, and doesn't respond to external stimuli.

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Skin Bandits can be allied with if the player is a [Skeleton](/skeleton).
-   Skin Bandits are neutral towards [Hiver](/hive), Skeleton, and [Shek](/shek) player characters. However, if a skirmish occurs between Skin Bandits and their enemies, they will also attack neutral characters whenever they're seen during the skirmish, regardless of their race.

### Alliances

Players can ally with the Skin Bandits by speaking to [Savant](/savant), although only if the player character is a Skeleton. As with the other warring southeast factions, allying with the Skin Bandits results in the other 3 factions becoming enemies. These are the specific relations changes:

-   [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) -75
-   [Reavers](/reavers) -75
-   [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) -75
-   Skin Bandits +75

## Base Assault

The Skin Bandits will assault your base if you are within relative range to their location, provided you are not in a "no-go" zone.

### The Big Skin Harvest

This assault consists of around 2 squads or ~36 units that will approach your base and say upon arrival

> "A Human Village . . ." "Full of Fresh Skins . . ." "And Lots of Human Food For Our Hungry Bellies . . ."

They then will proceed to attack.

## Trivia

-   Both [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) and **Skin Bandits** have neutral relations with each other however they will still fight. They also share the same (100) opinion for [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion). However, the Legion still tends to attack both factions.
    -   The Skin Bandits will eventually fight back against the Legion when their relations with them reach lower than (50). This can happen faster if the Legion keeps knocking members of their patrols unconscious in the Skinhouse HQ.
-   The Skin Bandits in Skinhouse HQ have an AI Goal to kill a random prisoner. Getting put inside a [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine) can lead to a quick death because of this.
    -   The Skin Bandits in the North and South Skinhouses lack this AI Goal, however they will attack and put a character back into a Peeler Machine if they break out.
    -   The Skin Bandits inside of the [Horrible Ruin](/horrible-ruin) lack either of the AI Goals previously mentioned. However, they will still place certain races into the Peelers if they are unconscious nearby. (Or Hostile)
-   The Skin Bandits have unused dialogue for looting unconscious characters. Because they lack the AI Goal to loot unconscious characters and share the same AI packages as the [Cannibals](/cannibals), they can never be seen in the vanilla game.
-   If you're allied with the Skin Bandits, there's multiple traders available at the HQ (inside Skinhouse buildings particularly). You'll have to meticulously hover your mouse over each NPC until you find the bubble icon. They have around 3k cats for trade, you can also train up your assassination skill and only lose -1 relations with them (from 0-88 you could lose up to -6 total).
-   The Skin Bandits may have been inspired by the Type-3 Screamer Robots in the 1995 sci-fi movie Screamers. In the movie, these self-replicating Screamer Robots were once used by the Earth-centric faction called the Alliance in a war against a rogue corporate-state, but have suddenly and mysteriously gained sentience and began to steal the body-parts of fallen Human soldiers in order to create the Type-3 Screamer, a rogue sentient android that uses a human skin-suit to infiltrate human society.
