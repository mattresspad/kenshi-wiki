**Human Skin Suit** is a [Body Armour](/body-armour) worn by [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).

It is the perfect non-disguise, but somehow the [fleshies](/human) can tell that you are a skeleton.

Because for every good thing there has to be another good thing, the Skin Bandits also store another type of [Human Skin](/human-skin) clothing.

## Appearance

Just humans being humans, doing normal human things.

We are not sure why it's a separate item, all humans have one so they can do normal human things like breathing through their nostrils, singing human songs, and eating human fue- food.

If anyone can shine light on this mystery please contribute.
