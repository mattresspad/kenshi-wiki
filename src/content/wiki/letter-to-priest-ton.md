**Letter To Priest Ton** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) relating to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item is 'unique' to [Temples](/temple). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in the home of [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).

It reveals that members of the Holy Army are responsible for buying their own gear.
