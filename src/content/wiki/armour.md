**Armour** is a selection of equipment worn on various parts of the body to mitigate damage. It can be worn on five different parts of the body. Armour can be made by the player through the skill [Armour Smith](/armour-smith), purchased from [Traders](/armour-clothing-shops), or looted from characters. All types of armour are most efficiently stored in [Armour Storage](/armour-storage) (but can also be stored in general storage).

For the Armour **category** please see [this page](/category/armour).

## Types of Armour

Kenshi categorizes Armour into four different categories, depending on damage resistance and skill multipliers.

### Clothing

[Clothing](/clothing) is equipment which does not have armour coverage. Clothing is generally worn by civilians. Wearing clothing can be advantageous for keeping a character's "looks like a slave" rating low when escaping from [Slavery](/slavery). Some clothing has bonuses specifically for [Martial Arts](/martial-arts).

### Light Armour

[Light Armour](/light-armour) is likely to have bonuses to Combat [Stats](/statistics). Light armour is preferred by players who want to prioritize bonus attack, defense, dodge, and attack/block speeds of their characters. After all, who needs protection when your enemy doesn't get a chance to attack?

### Medium Armour

[Medium Armour](/medium-armour) offers more coverage than light armour and less resistances than heavy armour, but without the latter's severe skill penalties.

### Heavy Armour

[Heavy Armour](/heavy-armour) is armour which weighs a lot. Heavy Armour offers the most damage resistances but also has negative effects on stats. The penalties caused by this type of armour make battles more difficult and therefore allow characters to level up faster in combat stats. (Attack, Defense, Dodge, Martial Arts, Dexterity)

This armour is favored by high level military members and tanks. Because heavier armour is likely to have penalties to [Dexterity](/dexterity), characters using cutting weapons should avoid it unless training.

### [Colour Scheme](/colour-scheme)

Some types of armour can have a colour variance, mostly dependent on the faction that wears it. Armour with these colour variations can be obtained through looting characters from these factions.

You can remove the color of dyed armour by selling it to a vendor, closing the shop and purchasing it back. Please note that Armour sells for 0.25x of its value so by doing so you will be paying 0.75x of the price to simply remove the dye.

## [Faction uniforms](/faction-uniform)

A Faction Uniform is armour that some [Factions](/factions) members may wear. An **armour** is considered a Faction Uniform if the clothing is tagged as being a uniform of a set faction. Player Characters wearing Faction Uniforms will be considered "[in Disguise](/disguise)" and may be arrested by police for [Uniform Theft](/bounty).

Your characters may be allowed to wear a Faction Uniform if you are allied with that particular faction. Note that factions which are hostile to the faction you are wearing the uniform of may also attack you, mistaking you for their enemy.

You can remove a faction uniform by knocking out (Assassination) a recruitable character like a Drifter and making them wear the uniform. They MUST be wearing it. After that wait for them to wake back up and recruit them. This will remove the Faction Uniform tag. (If the character is hostile with you as you were caught knocking them out, wait around 5 game hours and they should return to neutral or leave and come back later.)
