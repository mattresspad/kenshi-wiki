**Fighter Recruit** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a person looking for work. They can be hired for c.7,500.

This character can be found accompanying [Crumblejon](/crumblejon) and other recruits in [Mongrel](/mongrel). They can also be found in any [Waystation](/waystation), and, depending on certain [World States](/world-states), in [Bark](/bark), [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady), or [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai). They can also be found in locations controlled by [Deadcat](/deadcat) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). They have a chance of spawning in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), including when the location is "malnourished". They can be found in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), any [Waystation](/waystation), and [World's End](/worlds-end).

They have a random [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package. They wear no armour and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
