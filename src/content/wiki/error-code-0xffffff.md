**Error Code 0xFFFFFF** are skeletons found exclusively in the [Reprogramming Workshop](/reprogramming-workshop). They are members of the [Broken Ones](/broken-ones) faction. They will become allies with the player upon discovery.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown) and [Ancient](/ancient).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## Interactions

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Master!</blockquote></figure>

Once the Error Code bots see the player, they will immediately follow the character around as bodyguards almost permanently. They will indiscriminately attack most targets they see, be they enemies or neutral factions. (Excluding many neutral wildlife)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Error.</blockquote></figure>

Talking to any Error Code bot will be given a single line of dialogue, along with the bot turning towards the character.

## Tips

-   Once they are allied, the bots will allow themselves to be picked up by player characters after a short time. This will bring two advantages that makes these characters invaluable. First, they allow themselves to be placed in [Prisoner Cages](/prisoner-cage), creating a "jack-in-the-box" kind of reserve military force. Being caged also keeps them away from neutral characters that these bots could otherwise attack. Second, once picked up, the bots can be equipped with new armour, weapons, and repair kits significantly extending their survivability and usefulness.
-   You need to save and then load your save after giving one a repair kit in order for them to be used (if you are playing an organic character be sure to also give them first aid kits to help patch you up when injured).
-   If the character they are following is sneaking, they (mostly) don't attack everything around you. This can be extremely helpful for traveling longer distances to avoid losing many of your Error Codes.
-   As NPCs do not consume charges you can equip them with crossbows and a single bolt to create a massive firing squad.
-   They will instantly get off of a Skeleton Repair Bed if placed in one. So, if you wish to heal the wear damage of an Error Code you may pick one up and drop them on the ground. This will KO the Error Code for a short time. Simply pick the unit back up and place in a Skeleton Repair bed. Repeat if needed until the wear damage is healed.
-   If enslaved/jailed by an NPC, their AI goals may change to retreating fast and will despawn once they travel far enough away from you.

## Trivia

-   0xFFFFFF is the Hex Code for the color white.
    -   It is also the maximum value of a 64-bit unsigned integer in binary notation. This might imply that the bots' odd behavior is the result of an integer overflow error possibly caused by remaining active for hundreds of years without resetting.
