**Stone Golem's Revenge** is an [Event](/events) that occurs [Seto](/seto) or [Bayan](/bayan) has been killed or captured, as long as the player is not an ally of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) herself not been killed or captured.

It has an 80% chance to trigger each day if within 3,000 meters of a [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) owned location. This scales down with distance until it hits 0% chance at 11,000 meters.

Once the conditions are met, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will launch an assault on the player's base. This assault will consist of 4-6 squads. The first squad being led by [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem), joined by a [Five Invincible](/the-five-invincibles) and possibly [Seto](/seto) or [Bayan](/bayan) if one or the other is still okay.

The other squads will each be made up of (up to 20 individuals in total for the assault):

-   1 [The Five Invincible](/the-five-invincibles)
-   3 ~ 5 [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian)

Amusingly, this can lead to more than five members of the [Invincible Five](/the-five-invincibles) assaulting your base at a time.

Once the assault arrives at your outpost, [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) will give a speech referencing which of the two were killed or captured and then attack the player's characters and gates. If victorious, the assault's forces will leave the base shortly after.

If defeated, there is a 30% chance to trigger the [Shek Revenge](/shek-revenge) event in 72-150 hours.

## Trivia

-   Ironically, despite being the highest-tier level of assaults that Shek Kingdom could field, this assault is notably downsized by around half as compared to [Shek Revenge](/shek-revenge).
-   This assault composition is almost the exact same as [Invincible Vengeance](/invincible-vengeance).
-   This event is known as "Shek Esata seeks revenge - seto" or "Shek Esata seeks revenge - bayan" in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
