**Hack Stopper Pants** are Medium Armour that are equipped on the Legwear slot. They can not be bought from shops.

They are worn by the [Reavers](/reavers). This includes [Reaver Bloodeds](/reaver-blooded), [Reaver Ironclads](/reaver-ironclad), [Reaver Neophytes](/reaver-neophyte), [Reaver Slaves](/reaver-slave), [Reaver Whelps](/reaver-whelp), and [Valamon](/valamon) himself. It is often worn together with a matching body armour called the [Hack Stopper Jacket](/hack-stopper-jacket)**.** The pants are also worn by [Blood Raiders](/blood-raider).

## Appearance

**Hack Stopper Pants** appear to be short brown shorts with metal plates attached to the belt covering the groin, the hips, and the backside, along with a pair of circular cages wrapped around the thighs. Kneeguards and another, longer circular cages that are wrapped around the rest of the legs complete the set.
