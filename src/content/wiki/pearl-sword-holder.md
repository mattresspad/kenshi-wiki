**Pearl Sword Holder** is a useless [junk](/junk) Item: no purpose other than trading and only worth a small amount. This item can be found in Residential Buildings. When starting out, stealing this item along with other junk can help players increase their [Thievery](/thievery) stat with little risk (if the building is empty) and reward.

It is quite possible that this item is made from [Leviathan Pearls](/leviathan-pearl), alongside other pearl-related items such as [Pearl Urn](/pearl-urn), [Pearl Cup](/pearl-cup) and [Pearl Vase](/pearl-vase).

In the middle of this icon there appears to be a helmet with 2 antennas that resembles of a [Samurai Helmet](/samurai-helmet).
