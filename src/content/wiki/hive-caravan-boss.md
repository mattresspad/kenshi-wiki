**Hive Caravan Bosses** lead caravans for the [Western Hive](/western-hive) throughout the world, looking to trade goods. Their inventory typically consist of cheap [skeleton limbs](/skeleton), materials, and food.

Typically, the ranking of quantity of commodities from these 3 vendors from many to little would be:

Food, First Aid & Repair Kits: Survival Items > Mats > Crops

Building Materials: Mats > Survival Items > Crops

Farming Materials: Crops

Robotic Limbs: Survival Items > Mats

A **Hive Caravan Boss** has three vendors lists:

-   Hiver Trader Caravan (survival items): a lot of [food](/food) items, aid kit, repair kit and some [economy robot limbs](/robot-limbs), [building materials](/building-materials) and [iron plates](/iron-plates).
-   Hiver Trader Caravan (mats): a lot of [building materials](/building-materials) and [iron plates](/iron-plates), some food items, aid kit and repair kit and few economy robot limbs.
-   Hiver Trader Caravan (crops): a lot of [wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) and [cactus](/cactus), some [hemp](/hemp) and few [cotton](/cotton). Few food items, aid kit, repair kit, building materials and iron plate.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
