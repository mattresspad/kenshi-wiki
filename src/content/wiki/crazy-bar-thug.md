An unnamed member of the [Vagrants](/vagrants) faction. They are a variant of Bar Thugs which can be found in bars in [The Hub](/the-hub), [Waystation](/waystation), or in [Shark](/shark).

This thug has dialogue but cannot be spoken to. He will tell the user to "Get lost." and other variations to the same intent.

They have a 7% chance of having a bounty from 250 to 1750 Cats.
