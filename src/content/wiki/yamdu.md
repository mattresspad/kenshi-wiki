**Yamdu** is Noble Squire Supreme under his master [Longen](/longen). He has a Fearful [Personality](/personality).

If you talk to him he will give an offer to make an alliance with the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) for c.100,000. If you have an outpost the price will be at a discount for c. 75,000. Players can also talk to Yamdu about which war will best profit the Traders Guild.

If the player is enemies with [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), or allies with [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers), or killed either [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) or **Longen**, the dialogue with **Yamdu** will be locked.

After defeating [Tinfist](/tinfist), players can talk to Longen. Longen will then tell Yamdu to "take good care" of the player character, after which Yamdu will join the player faction.

Because Yamdu is not marked as a Unique character, Yamdu will respawn if the player leaves [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) and returns. This results in the possibility of controlling an army of Yamdus.

Yamdu does not have any special dialogue as a player character. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).
