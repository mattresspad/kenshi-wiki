**Ells** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). He is a fat, alcoholic, simple-minded [Empire](/united-cities) citizen with a small [bounty](/bounty) on his head for stealing alcohol from distilleries, which can expire like all small bounties. He wanders around town, begging for food. When recruited, he has 3 bottles of [cactus rum](/cactus-rum) in his inventory.

He can be found in [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai), no matter what [World States](/world-states) are affecting it. He will join the player after they give him c.300. If you can't find Ells, check by the [outhouses](/outhouse).

He has a Dumb [Personality](/personality). He doesn't have any lines of unique dialogue in zone entries, but he does have a unique dialogue package with a large number of exclusive lines.

## Trivia

-   He was also called both "Pickles" and "Squint" at some point in Kenshi's development, which can be found in his dialogue package in the FCS. In rare instances, he will even incorrectly refer to himself as Squint in game.
-   Due to the small amount of bounty that Ells is assigned with, it's likely for the player to notice him AFTER the bounty has expired.
    -   If noticed by a law official he may be attacked. (Very rarely this can occur)
-   Due to how cheap he is, and the items he has in his possession, you actually turn a profit by recruiting Ells.
