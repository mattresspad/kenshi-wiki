**Splint Kits** are [medical](/medical-items) [items](/items) used to improve functionality of injured limbs. Once applied, they help limping characters run normally, except in most extreme cases of limb damage. Players can learn how to craft this item by researching [Splints](/splints-tech). After researching, these kits can be crafted on any [Medical Workbench](/medical-workbench). These kits can be stored in a [Medicrate](/medicrate).

They can be found on [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), who are knowledgeable about exploring the old ruins and, curiously, on [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) who might carry them for training reasons. Traveling Gear Shops are also one of the buildings which sells them in the United Cities.

## Trivia

-   If you play with limbloss on frequent, then wound degeneration/limb damage via weather (Acid water/ground and beams) will still cause limbloss once below -100% health even if the limb is splinted.
-   No NPCs actually have the AI goal by default to use a splint kit, even if they carry one.
-   The UI for rigged health is shown as if it is a percent instead of a flat value. For instance, if you were to splint a Leviathans leg for 26 health (The most you can splint using a normal Splint Kit) the white bar which shows the rigged health would take up around 26% of the UI on their leg, although only 26 health was rigged, not 26% health.
