**Harpoon Turrets** is [Defense Tech](/defense-tech) which allows players to construct basic [turrets](/turrets). There are many related research options which improve accuracy and speed.

Turrets are not reliant on [power](/power) or ammunition, so players can build them entirely standalone. Players may find them to be useful weapons against to use against large enemies like [Leviathans](/leviathan) or to fend off [base raids](/base-assault). This is especially true when paired with other defenses, such as walls and gates. Turrets can be built mounted on walls and the roofs of many building types.
