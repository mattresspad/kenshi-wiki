**Raw Meat** is an ingredient used primarily in the crafting of [food](/food) at a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove). It can be bought from relevant [Traders](/traders) including those in [Hive Villages](/hive-village) or gathered from most [Fauna](/fauna).

It is only edible to [hivers](/hive), [scorchlanders](/human) and [animals](/fauna). Despite its raw status, it retains the same nutritional value as [Dried Meat](/dried-meat). It does not spoil over time.

### Locations

Meat can be gathered from the following creatures.

-   [Beak Things](/beak-thing), 5 meat per corpse (not counting 12 [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat)).
-   [Bonedogs](/bonedog), 3 meat per corpse.
-   [Boneyard Wolves](/boneyard-wolf), 3 meat per corpse.
-   [Crabs](/crab), 6 meat per corpse (not counting 1 [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat)).
-   [Garru](/garru), 10 meat per corpse.
-   [Goats](/goat), 7 meat per corpse.
-   [Landbats](/landbat), 2 meat per corpse (not counting 3 [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat)).
-   [Swamp Turtles](/swamp-turtle), 14 meat per corpse.
-   [Skimmers](/skimmer), 2 meat per corpse (not counting 8 [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat)).
-   [Wild Bulls](/wild-bull), 10 meat per corpse.
-   [Domesticated Bulls](/domesticated-bull), 20 meat per corpse.

The safest way to acquire meat is by hunting swamp turtles in the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands), though weaker unit should be escorted by 30s characters to deal with the other dangers. Swamp turtles can also be found in [The Swamp](/the-swamp). Garrus and Goats can be found nearly anywhere in the world, but since they do not have nests, they are hard to track down.

Battle-hardened adventurer groups can also hunt for creatures in the Bonefields, [Flats Lagoon (Zone)](/flats-lagoon-zone) or [High Bonefields](/high-bonefields), where many of the more dangerous animals roam in large packs. Beak things can also be hunted in many other areas, but requires groups with 40-50 stats to do some until a decent amounts can be acquired.

If players can ignore potential lore ramifications, raw meat can be looted in large quantities from [Cannibal Villages](/cannibal-village), especially [First Village](/first-village).
