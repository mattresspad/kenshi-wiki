**Fabrics** are a versatile item used in the creation of [Armour](/armour), [Clothing](/clothing), [Medical Items](/medical-items), and [Weapons](/weapons).

Fabrics can be produced (using either [Hemp](/hemp) or [Cotton](/cotton)) at a [Fabric Loom](/fabric-looms) and stored in a [Fabric Chest](/fabric-chest). The average cost (Assuming purchasing the materials to make Fabrics) is c.450 if using Cotton and c.57 if using Hemp.
