**Imprisonment** is a [Defense Tech](/defense-tech) which allows the player to construct their own [Prisoner Cages](/prisoner-cage).

This research is important to players who like to capture characters and use them for [combat training](/guide-to-training-statistics), or to stockpile multiple bounties to turn them in at a later date.
