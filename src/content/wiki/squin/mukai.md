If [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states), [Mukai the Mountain](/mukai-the-mountain) replaces her in [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) headquarters in [Squin](/squin) as the new faction leader.

There is little change inside this town, with the only notable difference being a new headquarters with Mukai in it.

## Trivia

The town override with Mukai has a few weird instances and could be considered to be poorly done, these include:

-   When handing in [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) or [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), they will still refer you to [Bayan](/bayan), even though Bayan should be dead or missing in this scenario.
