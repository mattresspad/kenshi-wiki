## [World States](/world-states)

If **Yabuta of the Sands** is freed from Tengu's Vault and successfully escaped, several World States will trigger.

His release will cause the [Yabuta Outlaws](/yabuta-outlaws) faction to appear.

If [Fort Simion](/fort-simion) is still under [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) control it will be turned into [Fort Yabuta](/fort-yabuta) provided the player is not allied with **Rebel Farmers.**

[Stoat](/stoat) will also fall under **Yabuta Outlaws** control if it is under the control of [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters).

[Cannibals](/cannibals) patrols in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) will cease to spawn after Yabuta is freed if the conditions for them to spawn there are already achieved beforehand.

His release will also cause Rebel Farmers patrols in [Heng](/heng-zone) to become less frequent.

**Yabuta Outlaws** will start patrolling in [Bast](/bast-zone), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Hook](/the-hook).

## Trivia

-   The name Yabuta is a derivative of Yabu (藪) and Ta (田) which means "a field of bushes" which is also has a same phrases as Yabutta (破った) which mean "broke" as in "breaking the promise"
