**Copper** is a material found across the land used to make anything electrical and much more. Presently it can only be mined from copper outcrops by using the [Labouring](/labouring) skill. Large deposits are also found [underground](/prospecting), but it is impossible to reach them.

## Used In

-   [Arrow Making Bench](/arrow-making-bench) - to make [Spring Steel](/spring-steel) (by combining it with [Raw Iron](/raw-iron))
-   [Construction](/construction) - used to construct [Small Generator](/small-generator), [Skeleton Repair Bed](/skeleton-repair-bed), [Wind Generator](/wind-generator)
-   [Copper Alloy Bench](/copper-alloy-bench) - to make [Copper Alloy Plates](/copper-alloy-plates) (by combining it with [Iron Plates](/iron-plates))
-   [Electrical Workbench](/electrical-workbench) - to make [Electrical Components](/electrical-components)

## History

This item was previously named "Raw Copper" in the same way as [Raw Iron](/raw-iron) is.
