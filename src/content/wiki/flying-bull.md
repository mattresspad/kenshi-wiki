**Flying Bull** (also named Ida) is a huge [Shek](/shek) warrior and the leader of [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen). He resides in [New Kralia](/new-kralia), carrying around a unique [Fragment Axe](/fragment-axe) named [Bull Horn Axe](/bull-horn-axe) gifted by [King Shager](/king-shagers-reign).

Soon after Shager's overthrow, Ida, better known as Flying Bull, a devout follower of [King Shager](/king-shagers-reign), rebelled against [The Stone Golem's](/esata-the-stone-golem) new rule, instead cementing himself as a rebel following [Shager's](/king-shagers-reign) doctrine of [Shek](/shek) supremacy, claiming the [Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) has proven herself a coward bowing to the [Flatskins](/flatskins) by not continuing to fight against the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) until the end. He recruits many of [Shager's](/shagers-overthrow) supporter, and departs from [Admag](/admag), establishing the rebel outpost of [New Kralia](/new-kralia) and the [Krals Chosen](/krals-chosen).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (15% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## [World States](/world-states)

(Requires Flying Bull to be imprisoned/dead)

New Kralia becomes ruined. Patrols of 5 ~ 8 [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen-character) in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) and [Spider Plains](/spider-plains) disappear and are replaced by frequency-halved and broken patrols of 3-6 Kral's Chosen.

(Requires Flying Bull to be alive and free)

If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) are killed, [Squin](/squin) will fall under [Kral's Chosen rule](/squin/krals-chosen), or to the [Berserkers](/berserkers) if both of them are killed/imprisoned.

If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are killed, [Stack](/stack) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) alongside with [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will fall under Kral's Chosen rule, or else become ruins under [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed, patrols will spawn in [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) (further increase in frequencies in the latter 2 regions if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is killed as well).

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), **Holy Lord Phoenix** and [High Inquisitor Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are all killed/imprisoned while **Flying Bull** is alive and free will cause **Kral's Chosen** patrols to patrol in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Skimsands](/skimsands).
