**Jittes**, along with their larger counterpart the [Heavy Jitte](/heavy-jitte), are [Blunt weapons](/blunt-weapons) that inflict [Stun Damage](/stun-damage) and an extremely minor amount of cut damage/blood loss classifying them as a non-lethal weapon. This allows you to subdue and capture bandit leaders without the risk of them dying from wound degeneration or blood loss.

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **1x7**. It can be equipped in both the primary and secondary weapon slots.

The [Cross](/cross)\-version of this weapon can be found on [The Vault Warden](/the-vault-warden) in [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault).
