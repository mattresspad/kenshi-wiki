**Ludin's Reports** are three [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) patrol records about [The Hub](/the-hub). To find them, look for the one [Small Shack](/small-shack) that is still intact among the ruins. All three reports and both priests' notes are inside.

## Report I

Commander Nix sends High Paladin Ludin to investigate The Hub. The patrol finds burned buildings on the verge of collapse and gaps in the town walls. There are no enemy soldiers or bodies in sight. Ludin thinks the Shek occupied the town for some time, but he cannot explain why they left. In his judgment, the Holy Nation could take possession of the ruins easily. Making the town habitable again would be the harder task.

## Report II

At dusk, Paladin Lusac's hound spots figures on the hill, breaks free and chases them into The Hub. By the time the patrol reaches the ruins, the figures and the hound have disappeared. Open doors and objects moved from their places convince Ludin that someone has been there. He suspects Shek scouts and recommends sending patrols farther south. He also asks that Lusac be given leave after losing his hound.

## Report III

On a night patrol, Ludin hears voices outside the walls. They fall silent as his squad passes through the gate. While Ludin searches the bar, he hears Sentinel Amphetio scream. The patrol finds neither Amphetio nor any sign of a struggle and withdraws. Ludin now calls the figures wraiths and urges the Holy Nation to burn what remains of The Hub. Who the patrol heard and what happened to Amphetio remain open questions.

## The priests' notes

[Priest Varloo's Note](/priest-varloos-note) blames Narko for the Holy Nation's losses and argues that it cannot hold The Hub. [Priest Tyluig's Note](/priest-tyluigs-note) agrees about abandoning the town but puts the blame on the Holy Nation's own people for losing their faith.
