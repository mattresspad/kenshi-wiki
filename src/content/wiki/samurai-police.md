**Samurai** **Police** are law enforcers for the [United Cities](/united-cities), [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants), and the [Free Traders](/free-traders). This branch of Samurai operates out of [Police Stations](/police-station) and are mainly responsible for protecting urban centers, while standard [Samurai](/samurai) perform military duties such as patrolling.

As law enforcement, Samurai Police carry blunt Jittes instead of the typical katanas.

Inside of Police Stations, a [Police Chief](/police-chief) resides, giving bounty notices, offering bails, and acting as a merchant for reselling confiscated items.

Despite the loosening of the United Cities' grip on the southern territories after certain [World States](/world-states) are achieved, they still seem to assume the role of law enforcement in the cities. This may be due to the lack of [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) presence in the south, leaving security to be handled by the Police and [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter-gate-guard).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
