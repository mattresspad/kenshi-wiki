If [Seto](/seto) is killed, [Admag](/admag) will have a notable lack of military population to reflect the increased [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)'s patrols around [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride). Indicating that they've weakened themselves after throwing out most of their warriors against HN forces.

Notably, these are the most significant changes

-   Shek HQ will only have 1 [Five Invincible](/the-five-invincibles) now instead of 4 (decrease of 75%).
-   [Police Stations](/police-station) will only have 2 ~ 3 [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) instead of 5 ~ 7 (decrease of ~60%).

**Note**: Some bugs may cause the town's overall population to _increase_ in the town interface icon, contrary to the lore.
