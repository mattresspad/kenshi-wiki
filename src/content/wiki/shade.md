**Shade** is the leader of the [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) and resides at the [Swamp Ninja Base](/swamp-ninja-base).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

## [World States](/world-states)

Killing or imprisoning Shade will cease [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) patrols inside [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands), and decrease their numbers by 60% in [The Swamp](/the-swamp).

(Requires Shade to be alive)

Killing [Red Sabre Boss](/red-sabre-boss) results in Swamp Ninja Squads roughly doubling in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) with a squad composed of (0 ~ 1 [Swamp Ninja Jonin](/swamp-ninja-jonin), 5 ~ 7 [Swamp Ninja Chunins](/swamp-ninja-chunin), 4 ~ 5 [Swamp Ninja Genins](/swamp-ninja-genin)).

[Stone Rat Village](/stone-rat-village) can be controlled by the Swamp Ninjas if [Big Grim](/big-grim) and [Big Al](/big-al) are dead and Shade is still alive.
