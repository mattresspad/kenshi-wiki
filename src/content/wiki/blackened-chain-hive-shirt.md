**Blackened Chain Hive Shirt** is a Medium Armour equipped in the Shirt slot. This shirt is one of the four shirt options available for [Hive](/hive) characters to wear in the shirt slot.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x2**.

There are [standard](/hiver-chain-shirt) and [rusted](/rusted-hive-shirt) versions of this shirt. This is also the Hiver-equivalent of [Blackened Chain Shirt](/blackened-chain-shirt) and [Blackened Chainmail](/blackened-chainmail).

## Appearance

The **Blackened Chain Hive Shirt** appears to be a black sleeveless chain shirt that fits the upper part of a hiver's chest. A semi-circular hole is open to match the hiver's torso, which is instead covered by a metal plate fastened by four leather belts. The stomach is covered by a metal cage attached to a thick leather border.
