The blueprint for this weapon can be found at the [The Great Library](/the-great-library).

After unlocking the ability to craft through use of a blueprint, players can craft this crossbow at a [Crossbow Crafting Bench](/crossbow-crafting-bench). A single Ranger is made up of 6 [Steel Bars](/steel-bars) and 1 [Crossbow Parts](/crossbow-parts).
