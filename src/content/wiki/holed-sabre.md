The **Holed Sabre** is essentially a [Horse Chopper](/horse-chopper) with a hole cut out of it. This makes it slightly lighter and presumably gives it an extra bonus to [Melee Defence](/melee-defence). Regularly used by [Mercenaries](/mercenary-guild).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x8**, meaning it can only be used as a primary weapon.

The [Meitou](/meitou) version of this weapon can be found on [Ponk](/ponk) in the [Tower of Abuse](/tower-of-abuse).
