**Sinkuun** is a large peninsula located north-west of the [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert). The peninsula is infamous for being where the outcasts and bandits of the [United Cities](/united-cities) live.

It is also where a relatively isolated group of [Western Hivers](/western-hive) have settled at.

Despite bordering on the Cannibals territory of [Darkfinger](/darkfinger), the noticable presence of [Cannibal Hunters](/cannibal-hunters) in this region prevents their penetration into this area. The Cannibals instead go through [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Bast](/bast-zone), and ultimately [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).
