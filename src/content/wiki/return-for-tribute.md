**Return for Tribute** is an event that triggers if you paid tribute in the [Tribute Round](/tribute-round) or was defeated in [Return For Blood](/return-for-blood) or [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) events.

It also requires that [Bayan](/bayan), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Seto](/seto), [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) to all not be killed, and be _not_ allied with [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) to trigger.

When viable, [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will send 1 squad composed of:

-   0 ~ 1 [Shek Scouts](/shek-scout)
-   6 ~ 9 [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)

After arrival, the leader will give a short speech based on whether you were previously defeated by them or not.

If you refuse or ignore their demands, the [Return for Blood](/return-for-blood) event will trigger in 24-30 hours (hadn't refused tribute). If you had previously refused, it will instead trigger earlier in 12-24 hours.

If tribute is paid, the assault's forces will wander around the base, looting food from any food storage containers they can find. Eventually they will leave, and another **Return for Tribute** event will trigger in 100-150 hours.

Like most tax Events, if the campaign is defeated by a third party faction like bandits or wild animals on the way to the player's outpost, it will prevent any future tax visits from the faction until the player's next import. This is due to the long repeat limit timer, and the fact that it doesn't consider being defeated by a third party as the player killing them. This can be exploited by purposefully luring hostiles to the event's forces while they en route to the player's base. Though in the case of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) here, it is possible to reactivate the tribute events by failing to defeat a [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) event.

## Tips

-   An exploit: It is possible to pick up all your food before paying tribute, and the assault's forces will not react to it. Even if they find nothing, they will leave satisfied.
