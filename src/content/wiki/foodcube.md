A **Foodcube** is a [food](/food) item, purchased from relevant [traders](/traders) or created in a [Cooking Stove](/cooking-stove). They make good travelling rations and can be very easy to mass produce. Making Foodcubes for profit is not recommended however as food sells for half of their value unless sold in a [counter](/shop-counter). Foodcubes have the same nutritional value as [Gohan](/gohan) but are much more expensive.

Foodcubes are essentially a nutritious block made from a vegetable-grain mixture. It is similar in concept and appearance to the real-world [nutraloaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutraloaf).

Foodcubes are listed as an ingredient for [Ration Packs](/ration-pack), however they are not actually craftable.

The ideal ratio of same sized farm beds is 5 Wheat to 4 Greenfruit.

Foodcubes are the second most water-efficient cultivated foods in the game, costing 1.477 water per unit from seed-to-table, narrowly beating the 1.49 of Gohan. The best water-efficient food is the [Dustwich](/dustwich) at 1 water per 70nu.
