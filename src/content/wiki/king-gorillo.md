The **King Gorillo** is a giant version of a [Black Gorillo](/black-gorillo). It is found in the [Crumbling Labs](/crumbling-labs) in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble).

Unlike the [Great White Gorillo](/great-white-gorillo), there is no extra reward for killing the King Gorillo besides the loot inside the Crumbling Lab.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Animals can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) Their "weapon" provides them with 5.2 cut damage and 13 blunt damage which is unaffected by stats. King Gorillo has 3 attacking animations, 2 of which have 100 power and one which has only 70. To find the damage for the 70 power attack simply multiply the results for the variants below by 0.7.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.05844149 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.02925) + Strength (0.08775)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.06739199 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.039) + Strength (0.117)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 5.2 + 0.07634249 (Martial Arts + Dexterity)

**Blunt =** 13 + Martial Arts (0.04875) + Strength (0.14625)
