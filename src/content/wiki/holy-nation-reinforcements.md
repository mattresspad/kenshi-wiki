**Holy Nation Reinforcements** is an [Event](/events) that will trigger in response to the [Enemy Hunt](/enemy-hunt), [Invincible Vengeance](/invincible-vengeance), and [Stone Golem's Revenge](/stone-golems-revenge) events, as long as the [Player](/nameless) is allied to the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is alive and free.

Once triggered the [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) will send reinforcements from the location they own closest to the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). While they will move faster than most hostile events, they can still arrive too late if the player's base is closer to the hostile event's spawn location than to the origin of the reinforcements. These reinforcements will consist of 4-9 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant), 10-12 [Paladins](/paladin), and 1-2 [High Paladins](/high-paladin), led by an [Inquisitor](/inquisitor).

Once the reinforcements arrive, they will give a short speech, and then start patrolling the player's base, attacking hostiles, defending the player's characters, and healing wounded allies.
