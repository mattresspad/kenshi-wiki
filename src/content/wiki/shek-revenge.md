The **Shek Revenge** is a recurring base assault [event](/events) that can occur if you defeat any of the following and is _not_ an ally of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom):

-   **Shek Revenge** (40%/60%, 72 ~ 200 hours) (~5.7 days)
-   [Enemy Hunt](/enemy-hunt) (50%, 72 ~ 150 hours) (~9.25 days)
-   [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) (100%, 72 ~ 150 hours) (~9.25 days)
-   [Invincible Vengeance](/invincible-vengeance) (20%/60%, 72 ~ 150 hours) (~9.25 days)
-   [Stone Golem's Revenge](/stone-golems-revenge) (30%, 72 ~ 150 hours) (~9.25 days)

Once the conditions are met, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will launch an assault on the player's base. This assault will consist of 5 ~ 9 squads each composed of (up to 40 individuals):

-   3 ~ 5 [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)
-   2 ~ 4 [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian),

Once the squads arrive, the leader will make a short speech before proceeding their attack. If victorious, the assault forces will leave shortly after.

If defeated, there is a 60% chance for the [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) event to trigger in 72-200 hours. And if possible, another 40% chance to trigger another **Shek Revenge** event in the same amount of time.

If the conditions for [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom) are not valid, then the **Shek Revenge** event will have a 60% chance of restarting instead.

## Trivia

-   This event is known as "Shek Assault Big (loss trigger)" in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
    -   Of all assaults that Shek Kingdom launches towards the player, this raid is the most powerful of a standard assault that can be launched without taking down leaders.
-   This assault is roughly 5x the size of [Enemy Hunt](/enemy-hunt).
