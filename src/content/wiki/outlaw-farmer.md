**Outlaw Farmers** are members of the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers). If UC towns get half-destroyed, they have a chance to spawn inside towns fighting with [Samurai](/samurai) forces as part of the [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) faction instead.

They can be found patrolling several biomes, usually in the UC territories: [Grey Desert](/grey-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Hook](/the-hook). They patrol these areas in groups of 3-10, usually led by groups of the stronger [Outlaw Swordsmen](/outlaw-swordsman), so long as [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) is alive. A smaller squad consisting of 3-7 Outlaw Farmers can spawn from [Raider Camps](/raider-camp) that dot Heng or The Great Desert.

There are at least 7-10 that spawn in the HQ of the Rebel Farmers - [Fort Simion](/fort-simion).

They can spawn as squatters in [Bast](/bast), [Endin](/endin), [Heft](/heft) (empire peasant), [Stoat](/stoat) (tech hunter), and [Manhunter Base](/manhunter-base) (merc guild).

If UC cities devolve into civil war due to the loss of the resident noble or [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), they can be seen fighting for control on the streets.

Additionally, they serve as gate guards for Fort Simion, and small towns the peasants have taken over: [Port North](/port-north), [Port South](/port-south), [Slave Farm South](/slave-farm-south), and [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) (if [Tinfist](/tinfist) is dead).

They have a 10% chance to spawn with a bounty ranging from c.300 to c.1,300. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
