Fishing Village, Slave Traders is a town controlled by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). It [belonged](/fishing-village) to [Deadcat](/deadcat) until [Tinfist](/tinfist) was imprisoned or killed while [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) remained alive and free. [Empire Southwest Trade Culture](/empire-southwest-trade-culture) and [Slavers Trade Culture](/slavers-trade-culture) apply here.
