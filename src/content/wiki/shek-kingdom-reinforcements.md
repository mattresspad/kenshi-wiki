**Shek Kingdom Reinforcements** is an Event that will trigger in response to the [Eyegore's Assault](/eyegores-assault), [Holy Assault](/holy-assault), [Holy Nation Assault (Inquisitor)](/holy-nation-assault-inquisitor), [Noble's Wrath](/nobles-wrath), [Noble's Wrath Assault](/nobles-wrath-assault), [Retribution Of God](/retribution-of-god), and [Wrath Of God](/wrath-of-god) events, as long as the player is allied to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) has not been killed by the [Player](/nameless).

Once triggered the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will send reinforcements from the location they own closest to the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). While they will move faster than most hostile events, they can still arrive too late if the player's base is closer to the hostile event's spawn location than to the origin of the reinforcements. These reinforcements will consist of 1 squad, made up of 3-5 [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) led by a [Five Invincible](/the-five-invincibles).

Once the reinforcements arrive, they will give a short speech, and then start patrolling the player's base, attacking hostiles, defending the player's characters, and healing wounded allies.
