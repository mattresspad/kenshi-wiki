The **Swamp Ninjas** are a bandit faction from [The Swamp](/the-swamp). They are the rivals of the [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) and can often be found fighting them. Nothing much is known about their history.

The Swamp Ninjas can also be found in the [Swamp Ninja Base](/swamp-ninja-base) which is in the [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands). This is where their boss [Shade](/shade) can be found.

The Swamp Ninja uniform includes Dyed [Rag Shirts](/rag-shirt), [Turtlenecks](/turtleneck), [Cargopants](/cargopants), and [Swamp Ninja Masks](/swamp-ninja-mask). They are armed with [Ninja Blades](/ninja-blade), [Guardless Katanas](/guardless-katana) and sometimes [Tooth Picks](/tooth-pick).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
