**Holy Nation Assault (Inquisitor)**, simply referred to as 'Holy Nation Assault' in-game, is an [event](/events) that can occur if the player has defeated the [Holy Nation Assault (Major)](/holy-nation-assault-major) event. It is closely related to, but not to be confused with, two other events of the same name, [Holy Nation Assault (Minor)](/holy-nation-assault-minor), and [Holy Nation Assault (Major)](/holy-nation-assault-major).

The event cannot trigger if [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed or captured. (Note: The raid is supposed to be able to trigger with [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) as the leader instead, in the scenario that Seta has been killed or captured, but the game is not actually capable of triggering it despite this.)

If all the above requirements are met, [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) will launch an assault on the player's outpost in 72-200 hours (~5.7 days).

The assault will generally consist of two squads with on average around 25 individuals in total.

This 1st and 2nd are made up each of

-   [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) leading 3 [Inquisitors](/inquisitor) and 5 [Paladins](/paladin)
-   and 1 [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) 10-12 [Paladins](/paladin), 1-2 [High Paladins](/high-paladin), 4-9 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant)
-   Very rarely (roughly 3% chance) the squad will be accompanied by a copy of the 2nd.

Once the assault arrives, the War Leader will give a short speech that changes based on your past interaction with him or the faction as a whole, then he will order his men to attack the outpost and the player characters inside it.

If the raid is victorious, they will occupy the player's base, patrolling around it, using the furniture, freeing captives, and imprisoning your characters.

There are no special events if it is defeated.
