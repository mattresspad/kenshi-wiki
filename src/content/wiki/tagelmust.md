The **Tagelmust** is a Light Armour Class Hat item. It can be made by using a [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench). This armour can be [dyed](/colour-scheme).

It also has a chainmail [blackened](/blackened-chainmail-tagelmust) and [rusted](/rusty-chainmail-tagelmust) version.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

Made from what looks like one sheet of cloth, the Tagelmust is wrapped around the character's head in a manner that leaves the face exposed while covering the neck, forehead and the top of the head.

There is a bug/feature found in v1.0.51. When [Cat](/cat) wears an ordinary Tagelmust it becomes black while its name and all the stats remain the same.
