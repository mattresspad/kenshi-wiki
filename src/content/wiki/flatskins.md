**Flatskin** is a derogatory term used by characters in the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) to describe [Humans](/human). It is used to describe the lack of horns that play a significant part in [Shek](/shek) culture and/or the lack of rough boneplates that cover most of Shek's skin.

Flatskin is a Word Swap used in Kenshi dialogue. As a result, different words would be used in place of flatskin depending on certain conditions.

## Trivia

-   As this word is a word swap in Kenshi dialogue, it may cause some odd instances (if the player isn't allied with the Shek Kingdom) where Shek Warriors will belittle the player character while using rather glorious titles such as the "slayer of the Bugmaster/Phoenix".
