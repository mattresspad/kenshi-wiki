**Humans** are the most populous race in the world of [Kenshi](/kenshi). Human is also a [race](/races) category which includes [Greenlanders](/greenlander) and [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander).

## Greenlanders

[Greenlanders](/greenlander) are the most similar to humans on Earth. They serve as a reference point for hunger and are generally well-rounded in terms of stats, since they do not have any innate racial skill penalties, and their bonuses only apply to [farming](/farming), [cooking](/cooking), and [science](/science).

## Scorchlanders

[Scorchlanders](/scorchlander) are anatomically similar to Greenlanders but are differentiated by their much darker skin color, glowing eyes, shorter height and silvery hair. They are more specialized compared to Greenlanders, as they have innate racial bonuses in [armor smithing](/armour-smith), [weapon smithing](/weapon-smith), [dexterity](/dexterity), [dodge](/dodge), [stealth](/stealth), and [athletics](/athletics). Compared to a Greenlander they also run faster (Until they reach 100 Athletics at which point they move at the same speed), heal 10% faster and bleed 10% less. The tradeoff is a slight 10% reduction in [strength](/strength) experience gain.

## History

According to theories that can be drawn from clues and text in the world of Kenshi, humanity dates much farther back than the modern age, being alive during a much more technologically advanced [First Empire](/history-of-kenshi). Whether or not humanity predates skeletons is unclear, and this is only one of the mysteries that complicates the history between skeletons and humans. Humanity has been able to endure a number of natural disasters and conflicts that has plagued the world of Kenshi, but many of the humans you meet within Kenshi are apparently unfamiliar with their own history.
