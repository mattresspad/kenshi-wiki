**Skin Spiders** are hostile carnivores found primarily in [Arach](/arach), their neighboring [Shun](/shun) as well as the aptly named [Spider Plains](/spider-plains), with likely migrations to the [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) in the latter case.

They also have a significant presence in southeast section of the world, sprawling throughout [Bonefields](/bonefields) and [High Bonefields](/high-bonefields).

They are large tan insectoid or arachnoid creatures with long, multi-jointed legs and a tiny, creepy head. They hunt anything nearby with high cutting damage attacks from their pointy legs, and they share their [smaller cousins'](/blood-spider) ability to "eat" their prey alive by draining its hunger bar.

Although larger and tougher than Blood Spiders, Skin Spiders are generally less dangerous because they favor open terrain where they are easier to see coming and can fairly easily be outpaced. Like their cousins, they have high bleeding resistance and will wake up quickly should they be knocked out, so it is wise to loot their claws to kill them once downed.

Skin Spiders can be found in enormous swarms of 20-30 in Arach and are rumored to be controlled by that zone's infamous humanoid [resident](/bugmaster). Anyone hoping to claim the Bugmaster's treasure hoard will need to fight (or sneak) through many waves of these guardian spiders.

Sharp [polearms](/polearms) offer a 25–50% damage bonus against animals and can thus be useful when fighting Skin Spiders. The [Desert Sabre](/desert-sabre) also provides a 100% damage bonus against Skin Spiders.

Instead of consuming the limbs of a downed individual like a [Gutter](/beak-thing), the Skin Spider will rapidly drain the prey's hunger, leading to a [Strength](/strength) penalty. This may hinder the victim's movement speed, making it more difficult to escape.
