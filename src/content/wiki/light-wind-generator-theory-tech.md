**Light Wind Generator Theory** is [Electrics Tech](/electrics-tech) which allows the player to construct [Small Wind Generators](/small-wind-generator).

Wind generators rely on weather. Players should [prospect](/prospecting) their surroundings before constructing a wind generator in their [Outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) or on the roof of a [Player-Owned Building in Town](/player-owned-buildings-in-town).
