**City Heroes** are the racist pro-[Empire](/empire-peasants) vigilantes and 'patriots' that make up the [United Heroes League](/united-heroes-league). They can be found in their HQ: [Fort Mirage](/fort-mirage). Squads will also patrol United Cities towns: [Bark](/bark), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai), and [Stoat](/stoat). They will sometimes leave the safety of the walls to patrol [Heng](/heng-zone) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

[United Heroes](/united-hero) are the version that show up at Outposts.
