After researching **Hydroponics**, characters can research how to make hydroponic growing stations for four different types of crops. Only [Hemp](/hemp), [Riceweed](/riceweed), [Greenfruit](/greenfruit), and [Wheatstraw](/wheatstraw) can be grown with hydroponics. Hydroponics Bays are smaller than outdoor farms but can allow for bases to be built in any area without concern for what [Environment](/environment) it is.

Hydroponic farms require [Water](/water) and [Power](/power) to grow. If there is not enough power, farms can pause growth or grow at a decreased rate.

After completing all the research, characters can build Hydroponics with 4 [Iron Plates](/iron-plate), 2 [Electrical Components](/electrical-components), and 10 of their desired crop.
