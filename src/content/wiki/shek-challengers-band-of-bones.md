**Shek Challengers** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has defeated the [Raid](/raid) or **Shek Challengers** events. It cannot trigger if [Tora the Fearless](/tora-the-fearless), [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem), the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), or the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) have been killed or captured.

One the conditions have been fulfilled, the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) will send an assault to the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). This assault will consist of 3-6 squads; each made up of 4-8 [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones-character).

When the assault arrives, the War Leader will give a short speech, then order the attack to begin, attacking gates and characters, and liberating captives. If the assault is victorious, the assault party will occupy the base, inhabiting it until forcibly removed, in addition to the [Raid](/raid) event triggering in 200-300 hours. If the assault is defeated, the **Shek Challengers** event will trigger again in 90-120 hours.
