The **Flotsam Village** is the base of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) and home of [Moll](/moll), their leader. It is located in the [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest), to the North of [Rebirth](/rebirth) and west of [World's End](/worlds-end).

This town contains many residences. Most of the population is composed of refugees and deserters from the Holy Nation. [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) guard this village and, as implied, they are hostile to The Holy Nation. However, most of the townsfolk still believe in and even worship [Okran](/okran), made clear by how they react to seeing skeleton characters.

Many recruits can be found in this village including [Digna](/digna), [Knife](/knife), [Pia](/pia), [Reva](/reva) and the generic randomly named [Flotsam Recruit](/flotsam-recruit).

If [Moll](/moll) is dead or imprisoned **Flotsam Village** will be overrun by [Cannibals](/cannibals). [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) will still appear as part of the "Residence" list despite no longer present. Most of the buildings will be destroyed.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

6 ~ 25 [Cannibals](/cannibals) and 10 ~ 35 [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal) can be found as bar squads here.
