The **King Gurgler** commands the [Fishmen](/fishmen) on an island south of [Catun](/catun) called [Fishman Island](/fishman-island). He can be found in the [Island Lab](/island-lab) on the most south-western tip of the island.

He wields a [Meitou](/meitou) grade [Combat Cleaver](/combat-cleaver) and is a deadly foe.
