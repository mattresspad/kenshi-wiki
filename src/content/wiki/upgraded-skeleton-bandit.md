**Upgraded Skeleton Bandits** are elite member of the [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandits) faction. They can be found leading patrols of [Skeleton Bandits](/skeleton-bandit) in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble), [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [The Pits](/the-pits), and [The Crags](/the-crags). These patrols persist so long as [Elder](/elder) is alive.

They wear \[Standard\] armour (18% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).
