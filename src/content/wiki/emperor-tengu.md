**Emperor Tengu** is the current leader of the [United Cities](/united-cities). He is carrying the strongest [Katana](/katana) ([Meitou](/meitou) grade) in the game.

Emperor Tengu resides in [United Cities](/united-cities) faction HQ in [Heft](/heft), where he often spends time in his throne room. He is usually flanked by his diplomat, [Koin](/koin). The building is dotted by his ever-vigilant personal Scorchlander retinue, composed of 5 [Samurai Elites](/samurai-elite), 8 [Samurai Captains](/samurai-captain), 2 [Samurai Heavies](/samurai-heavy), and 5 [Jonin Elites](/jonin-elite) if **Heft** is _not_ malnourished (the numbers will decrease if it is).

Players wishing to destabilize UC in the desert area and spawn [Samurai Rogues](/samurai-rogue) will have to take down him and [Lady Sanda](/lady-sanda), [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) and [Lord Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga).

Behind his glasses, Tengu has the distinctive glowing eyes of a [Scorchlander](/scorchlander), but he has dark hair and unusually light (by Scorchlander standards) skin, making it easy to confuse him for a [Greenlander](/greenlander), although in a more subtle way than characters like [Stubs Momuso](/stubs-momuso) that outright use the Greenlander skin texture.

He has Smart [Personality](/personality).

## Interactions

If you talk to Emperor Tengu, he will tell you that he needs you to defeat an ancient Grievewrath. He says that first you will need to find [Clunknad Spanner](/clunknad-spanner), the skeleton wizard, so he can brew you the "Elixer of Godly Powers." He will then give you a [sack](/mystery-sack) which will contain Tengu's seal to give to Clunknad to prove that Tengu sent you.

Tengu's "seal" is in fact a chewed-up fruit pit, and none of the other items or characters he mentions exist at all. Upon handing the seal to the player character, he bursts into a fit of disturbing, uncontrollable laughter and mocking with his guards, ending with an abrupt declaration that it is time to return to work.

If the player is allied with United Cities (and not allied to [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers), [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)) the player would be able to speak to Tengu about war and expansion to other nation, though the player will typically just be redirected to speak with [Koin](/koin), a high-ranking diplomat of UC. You can then intimidate him by objecting to being called a servant, if you've managed to make a name for yourself by taking down strong characters like [Bugmaster](/bugmaster), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Tinfist](/tinfist), or [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon).

## History

After the Red Rebellion, a new leader was chosen: Emperor Tengu, a starkly more impulsive man with a lack of common sense in comparison to his [predecessor](/puppet). As is tradition, Tengu has an ‘Inner Circle’ of four equally cruel, powerful, high nobles handpicked by himself. The emperor and his nobles are massively feared, particularly by the lower working class, and there are a huge number of rumors that reinforce this image, in particular stories of Tengu’s mysterious ‘Vault’ where rebels and enemies are sent.

But despite his feared position, Tengu is only an average fighter, having merely above average stats and wearing almost no armour. The noble culture is one of imported luxuries, exotic novelties, and flamboyant fashion. Although most of the high nobles rely on their famed bodyguards for protection and can only swing a sword well enough to buy their guards time against a truly skilled assailant, they nonetheless tend to carry elaborate and expensive swords on them as a symbol of status.

## Trivia

-   In Japanese, 「天狗」_(tengu)_ are a type of supernatural being in Japanese folk religion similar to a goblin. They are known for having long noses, living in mountain caves, and having a mix of human, monkey, and avian characteristics. Tengu is also a euphemism in Japanese for a bragging or conceited person, which could be a reference to Emperor Tengu's ego.
-   "Tengu" can also be found on some randomly named characters as it appears in the [Random Names List](/random-names-list).
-   Although Tengu has an Advanced First Aid Kit he only seems to use it if his legs become crippled, and only on himself.
-   As Tengu is a Noble, if either of his arms are lost in combat he will flee until knocked unconscious.
