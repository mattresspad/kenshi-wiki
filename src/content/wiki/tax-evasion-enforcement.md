**Tax Evasion Enforcement** is an [Event](/events) that can occur when you refuse or fail to pay taxes as part of the [Tax Collector](/tax-collector) or [Tax Man Visit](/tax-man-visit) events. It cannot trigger if [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is killed or captured.

Once triggered, the [United Cities](/united-cities) will launch an assault party. This party will consist of at least two squads, usually each consisting of 10-20 [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript), 2-5 [Samurai](/samurai), and 1-3 [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), led by a [Samurai](/samurai). Rarely one or more of the squads will be substituted by a squad consisting of 1-4 [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript), 5-8 [Samurai](/samurai), and 1-3 [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), led by a [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant).

Once the assault party arrives, the leader will read out the charges the player is being charged with, then orders the attack to begin. If the player interrupts the War Leader with dialog, they can pay a fine of c.6,000 instead and the assault will retreat, while the [Tax Collector](/tax-collector) Event will trigger in 120-140 hours. If the player refuses to pay, or ignores the War Leader, the assault will begin. If the assault is defeated, the [United Cities Assault](/united-cities-assault) event will trigger in 72-144 hours. If the player is defeated, the [Tax Collector](/tax-collector) event will be triggered in 12-48 hours, where you will be expected to pay c.6,000 or face another attack.
