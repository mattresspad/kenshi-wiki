**Sonorous Dark** is the home of the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits). It is the start of the peninsula which makes up the [Ashlands](/ashlands). Organic characters from [The Pits](/the-pits) and [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley) are sometimes kidnapped and [peeled](/peeler-machine) alive by Skin Bandits, due to the occasional skirmishes that occur near the zone's border.

## Tips

-   Don't let your organic characters get knocked out. If one does, prioritize picking them up.
-   Bring enough [Sleeping Bags](/sleeping-bag) for around half of your squad.
-   [Those are not humans, if you couldn't already tell.](/skin-bandits) They are extremely dangerous, do not approach them, and go out of your way to avoid them. Any organic humanoid character without extremely high stats should not attempt to fight them
-   Do not stay in this zone for long.
-   Although Acid protection is practically required, sacrificing conventional protection to attain 100% acid protection is not advised, 60-80% is good enough for those travelling through.
-   The [Crab Helmet](/crab-helmet) is the best helmet for any non-crossbow characters traveling through this zone, take or buy some in preparation.
-   Consider bringing one fabric for a [Mercenary Tent](/mercenary-tent) (one tent will protect ~30 characters from acid).
-   Consider equipping your squad with Hackers (especially the [Paladin's Cross](/paladins-cross)), as they do bonus damage against the most common enemies in this area.
