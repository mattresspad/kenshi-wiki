**Sadneil** is a [Skeleton](/skeleton) [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They are an exceptionally depressed skeleton who will join you for free after you annoy them a bit. Sadneil can be found in the [Skeleton Bar](/skeleton-bar) in [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city). If you can't find them, be sure to check upstairs as well.

They wear no armour.

## Trivia

-   Sadneil and his depressive personality are likely a reference to Marvin the Paranoid Android from the 'Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy' series of novels by Douglas Adams, as well as Neil (Played by Nigel Planer) from 'The Young Ones', a British sitcom by Rik Mayall that he quotes directly at times.
