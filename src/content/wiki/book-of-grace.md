**Book Of Grace** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) found in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This is one of the few books which is worth some money. Despite the book itself claiming that it is forbidden, this book can be found inside of [Temples](/temple) in The Holy Nation.

This book can also be found inside of [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village).
