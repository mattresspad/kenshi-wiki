**Soman** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She is part of a failing family business fishing with her brother.

She can be found in locations controlled by [Deadcat](/deadcat) and [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). Typically, she is found in [Fishing Village](/fishing-village-northern-coast).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).

Aside from her recruitment dialogue, Soman has nothing unique to say.
