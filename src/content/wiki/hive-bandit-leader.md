**Hive Bandit Leaders** are members of the [Western Hive](/western-hive). They start spawning after the [Hive Queen](/hive-queen) is killed or kidnapped. They spawn accompanied by a squad of 4 to 8 [Hive Bandits](/hive-bandit).

They spawn in 2 main regions:

Western regions of [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert), [The Swamp](/the-swamp), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon).

Eastern Regions of [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [The Eye](/the-eye).

This character has a Brave [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (20% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
