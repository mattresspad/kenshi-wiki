The **Crabsmith** is a vendor of the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders). He can be found in [Crab Town](/crab-town) in the [Crab Armour Shop](/crab-smithy).

He will sell the full set of Crab Armour and their blueprints, as well as some fish.

He will only sell to allies of the Crab Raiders. Even if players have a [Crab](/crab), he will call them 'Crabless' if they are not allies.
