**Sand Ninja Jounin** are elite units of the [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas). These mysterious ninja travel by themselves in the desert, attacking anybody who gets near.

They have a bounty ranging from c.6,000 to c.10,000. They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
