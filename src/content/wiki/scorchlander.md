**Scorchlanders** are a [Human](/human) subrace, and a common [Race](/races) in the [world of Kenshi](/world-of-kenshi). Scorchlanders are anatomically similar to [Greenlanders](/greenlander), but are differentiated by their much darker skin color, glowing eyes, and commonly have silvery coloured hair.

Due to their [Crafting](/crafting)\-related experience rate boosts, Scorchlanders make great crafters. As they have a heal rate of 1.1, Scorchlanders are the fastest healing organic playable humanoid race in Kenshi.

Scorchlanders can consume [Raw Meat.](/raw-meat)

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race have no special immunity to any types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which hazardous weather can occur.

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience in at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Armour Smith](/armour-smith)
-   [Athletics](/athletics)
-   [Dexterity](/dexterity) (+10%)
-   [Dodge](/dodge)
-   [Stealth](/stealth)

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Cooking](/cooking)
-   [Farming](/farming)
-   [Labouring](/labouring)
-   [Strength](/strength) (-10%)

## Trivia

-   [Stubs Momuso](/stubs-momuso) and [Jewel](/jewel) are Scorchlanders who look like [Greenlanders](/greenlander).
    -   Scorchlander Gorrillo Bandits also look like Greenlanders, aside from their eyes.
-   Scorchlanders capable of eating Raw Meat could show that they don't tend to care about the food taste or quality, hence their [Cooking](/cooking) skill penalties.
    -   A rare exception to this is [Jewel](/jewel), who appears to be at least half-Greenlander.
-   Scorchlanders are almost never found in farming professions, such as [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) or [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers), this is fitting with their [Farming](/farming) skill penalty.
    -   [Green Finger](/green-finger) is a rare exception to this rule.
-   Scorchlanders have a vision range mult of 1.25 which is equal to that of a Skeleton. Vision range mult impacts how far a unit can see, and most organic races have a mult of 1.
-   The Scorchlanders are likely based on an _Oblivion_ mod by Chris Hunt known as the [Sun Demons race](https://www.nexusmods.com/oblivion/mods/3528), minus the tail. Earlier editions of Kenshi also referred to Scorchlanders as Sundemons in the FCS.
-   Despite the fact that [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) does not discriminate against Scorchlanders they are rare to find in their territory. Likely because of their freedom-seeking nature, most Scorchlanders in Holy Nation owned land are Mercenaries or Thieves. Many may rebel and join the Flotsam Ninjas.
    -   The Holy Nation will treat a Scorchlander the same way they treat a Greenlander as the tag to check if someone is the same race as them uses the "Human" group which Greenlanders and Scorchlanders are in.
-   True to the racial description, Scorchlanders tend to be found in a lot of freedom-seeking, adventurous, rebel or ninja positions.
    -   Individual examples include:
        -   Rebels: [Moll](/moll), [Bo](/bo), [Jaegar](/jaegar), [Luquin](/luquin), [Hex](/hex)
        -   Ninjas: [Oni](/sand-ninja-oni), [Buzan](/buzan), [Shade](/shade), [Gutterhead](/gutterhead)
    -   Group examples include:
        -   Ninjas: [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) (33%), [Black Desert Ninjas](/black-desert-ninjas) (44%), [Swamp Ninjas](/swamp-ninjas) (45%), [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) (50%), [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves) (56%)
        -   Bandits: [Reavers](/reavers) (20%) [Preacher Cult](/preacher-cult) (25%), [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) (33%), [The Gorrillo Bandits](/the-gorrillo-bandits) (33%), [Grass Pirates](/grass-pirates) (45%), [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) (100%)
        -   Guards: [Grayflayers](/grayflayers) (25%), [Stone Rats](/stone-rats) (25%), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) (30%), [United Cities](/united-cities) (39%), [Free Traders](/free-traders) (42%), [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) (43%), [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) (47%)
-   [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders), [Twinblades](/twinblades) and [Emperor Tengu's](/emperor-tengu) [personal](/samurai-elite) [entourage](/jonin-elite) are all specifically set to be Scorchlanders only.
