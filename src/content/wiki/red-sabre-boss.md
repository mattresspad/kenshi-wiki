**Red Sabre Boss**, or "The Red Bandit" is the boss of the [Red Sabres](/red-sabres). He resides in [Red Sabre Hideout](/red-sabre-hideout) surrounded by 22 [Red Sabres](/red-sabre). Though he is named in his bounty letter, he is not given the name 'The Red Bandit' in-game.

They wear \[High\] armour (22% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached to their faction. The colour is also separately attached to this character, although it is the same colour as the faction's colour.

## [World States](/world-states)

The **Red Sabre Boss's** capture or death will result in the roaming [Red Sabre](/red-sabre) patrols in [The Swamp](/the-swamp) and [South Wetlands](/south-wetlands) to decrease in numbers by 90% and 83%.

If [Shade](/shade) is alive, the Boss's capture or death will also cause the roaming [Swamp Ninja](/swamp-ninjas) patrols to increase in strength in [The Swamp](/the-swamp), roughly doubling their frequency and numbers by adding another squad of 5-7 Chunins, 4-6 Genins, and a possible [Swamp Ninja Jonin](/swamp-ninja-jonin).

It will also stop the [Red Sabre Raid](/red-sabre-raid) from triggering.
