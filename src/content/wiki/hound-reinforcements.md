**Hound Reinforcements** is an [Event](/events) that will trigger in response to the [Bandit Raid](/bandit-raid) or [Red Sabre Raid](/red-sabre-raid) events, as long as the [Player](/nameless) is allied to the [Hounds](/hounds), and [Big Grim](/big-grim) is alive and free.

Once triggered the [Hounds](/hounds) will send reinforcements from [Rot](/rot) to the player's [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). While they will move faster than most hostile events, they can still arrive too late if the player's base is closer to the hostile event's spawn location than to the origin of the reinforcements. These reinforcements will consist of 1-2 squads, each made up of 4-7 [Hounds](/hound).

Once the reinforcements arrive, they will give a short speech, and then start patrolling the player's base, attacking hostiles, defending the player's characters, and healing wounded allies.
