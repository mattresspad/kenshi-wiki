**Priest Varloo's Note** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) which can only be found in [The Hub](/the-hub). This item is unique to an abandoned residential building found in The Hub.

In that same residence, there are also four other unique items which combine to give a history of The Hub and why [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) has abandoned it.

Players have speculated that the 'wraiths' mentioned in this note could easily be members of the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves), especially since The Hub is home to a [Thieves Guild](/thieves-guild).
