**Greenlander** is a very common [Race](/races) in Kenshi. It is a subrace of [Human](/human). Greenlanders can be found all across the game but are most abundant in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) in particular have large proportion of Greenlanders.

Greenlanders have no experience rate penalties to any [stats](/statistics). This allows players to play characters of this race in any way they want without having to worry about slower leveling speed.

## Health Statistics

Greenlanders have a blood color of rgb 128 0 0

-   Hunger rate: 1
-   Vision Range mult: 1
-   Blood: 75 - 150
-   Bleed rate: 1
-   Heal rate: 1

### Movement

-   Max Speed: 26.844 mph
-   Min Speed: 15.659 mph
-   Combat move speed multi: 1

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race have no special immunity to any types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which hazardous weather can occur.

  

### Equipment Slots

_Characters of this race can wear any type of equipment in these slots._

-   [Headgear](/headgear)
-   [Shirts](/shirts)
-   [Body Armour](/body-armour)
-   [Legwear](/legwear)
-   [Footwear](/footwear)

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Cooking](/cooking)
-   [Farming](/farming)
-   [Science](/science)

### Decreased Rate

There are no Statistics which Greenlanders are penalized in.

## Trivia

-   In the earliest versions of the game before it was released, this was the only available species.
-   Greenlanders have the highest bleedrate of any playable humanoid race.
-   Greenlanders are the default race when no races are assigned to the Faction or Character in [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
    -   When starting a new game (Provided another race is not "forced") all characters you start with will have their Cooking, Farming and Science multiplied by 1.2x as they start out originally as a Greenlander.
-   Due to how the game saves character data, saving the game while bleeding (<'s over blood) and then loading that save causes the bleeding value of all NPCs to go up by 20%, and then be multiplied by their racial bleedrate. As Greenlanders have a bleedrate of 1.0, this can be repeated to stack up an insane amount of bleeding to kill any Greenlander after multiple save/loads.
