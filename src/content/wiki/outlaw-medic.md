**Outlaw Medic** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a medic looking for work. They can be hired for c.6,000.

They can be found in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), Waystation and Waystation Four, and [World's End](/worlds-end). They can also be found in locations controlled by [Deadcat](/deadcat) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). They can be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps. They can be found in [Crab Town](/crab-town), [Mourn](/mourn), [The Hub](/the-hub), and, depending on certain World States, in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), or [Stoat](/stoat).

They have a Smart [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player THUG" Dialogue Package. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
