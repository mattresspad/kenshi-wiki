**Boneyard Wolves** are creatures found in the [Bonefields](/bonefields). These wolves are noticeably stronger and larger than [Bonedogs](/bonedog) and have brownish fur. Their real-life equivalent would likely be Bears, rather than wolves, due to their size and fur colour.

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Boneyard Wolves can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 18 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 5 piercing damage which Boneyard Wolves have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.77961008 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0039) + Strength (0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.83928 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0052) + Strength (0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 18 + 0.89894992 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt** **\=** Martial Arts (0.0065) + Strength (0.0195)
