The **Chainmail** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Shirt slot. It could be considered the long-sleeved variant of the [Chain Shirt](/chain-shirt) with a higher cut efficiency.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

## Appearance

Similar in appearance to the [Chain Shirt](/chain-shirt), Chainmail is made up of thousands of individual metal rings linked together. Chainmail covers the entire torso, halfway up the character's neck and down to the wrist of both arms.
