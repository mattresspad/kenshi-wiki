**Leather Vest** is a Light Armour that is equipped in the Shirt slot. It is sold by United Cities clothing shops, Holy Nation armour and clothing shops, and Shek Kingdom and Tech Hunter Ronin armour shops.

There are [short-sleeve](/leather-shirt) and [long-sleeve](/leather-turtleneck) versions of this clothing. Additionally, there is also a [Hiver-equivalent](/leather-hive-vest) version of this clothing.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **2x4**.

It can be worn by [Anti-Slaver Jonins](/anti-slaver-jonin), [Bounty Hunters](/bounty-hunter), [Caravan Trader Bosses](/caravan-trader-boss), [Deadcat Fishermans](/deadcat-fisherman), [Drifters](/drifter), [Empire Mercenaries](/empire-mercenary), [Farmers](/farmer-preacher-cult), [Holy Sentinels](/holy-sentinel), [Hounds](/hound), [Mercenaries](/mercenary), [Outlaw Swordsman Captains](/outlaw-swordsman-captain), [Rebel Captains](/rebel-captain), [Samurais](/samurai), [Samurai Gate Guards](/samurai-gate-guard), [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), [Shek Retainers](/shek-retainer), [Slavers](/slaver), [Slaver Bosses](/slaver-boss), [Slaver Caravan Bosses](/slaver-caravan-boss), [Slaver Caravan Guards](/slaver-caravan-guard), [Strayed Paladins](/strayed-paladin), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard), [Tech Hunter Guards](/tech-hunter-guard), [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins), and [Twinblades](/twinblade).

It can be crafted in the [Leather Armour Crafting Bench](/leather-armour-crafting-bench) after researching the blueprint. When compared to [Leather Shirt](/leather-shirt), they both profit the same of resistances at any grade, but the shirt has 100% chest coverage and 30% coverage for both arms.

## Appearance

The Leather Vest is similar in appearance to a Tank Top/Sleeveless Shirt. It is made of a light brown leather that covers the chest but not the arms.
