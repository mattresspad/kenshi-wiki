**Slave Capture** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger when recruiting an [Escaped Slave](/escaped-slave) or [Slave Escapee](/slave-escapee) via dialog, or if a visiting soldier from the [United Cities](/united-cities) spots an [ex-slave or escaped slave status](/slavery) character.

Once triggered the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) will send an assault to the [Player's](/nameless) [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost), this assault will consist of 1-2 squads, each made up of 7-14 [Slaver Caravan Guards](/slaver-caravan-guard), 0-1 [Slaver Heavy guard](/slaver-heavy-guard), led by a [Slaver Caravan Boss](/slaver-caravan-boss).

When the assault arrives, the War Leader will give a short speech before ordering the attack to begin, attacking characters and gates, and kidnapping downed characters. Any kidnapped characters will be enslaved and then delivered to a slave camp.
