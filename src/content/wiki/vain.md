**Vain** is the heartland and inhabited primarily by the [Western Hive](/western-hive), it is a zone that occupies the west coast and red acidic river of the middle section of Kenshi. The Hivers also inhabit the adjacent region of [Dreg](/dreg). This place neighbors [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) to the east in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) to the south in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)

Short of the river in the middle being acidic, the environment of this place is fairly safe without much [weather hazards](/weather-effects) for [Humans](/human) to travel through<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, though one should nonetheless be wary and either avoid or be prepared to fight against the wildlife of [Beak Things](/beak-thing) and [Gorillos](/gorillos) that occassionally roam this area.

It displays incredible biodiversity, and the [faunas](/fauna) that come with it. This location is known for being inhabited by [Beak Things](/beak-thing), [Gorillos](/gorillos) and a few [Bonedogs](/bonedog), [Garrus](/garru) and [Bulls](/wild-bull).

The water is acidic and the terrain in Vain has a red hue, which makes it very ominous and "bloody". Despite this, there appears to be no adverse side effects on non-hiver characters or farming. Vain is a very promising location for crops like [cotton](/cotton) and [vegetables](/vegetable-farm), and is located right beside a desert which can cultivate [wheatstraw](/wheatstraw), [hemp](/hemp), and [cactus](/cactus). The ground type that can be found here is Green/Arid, on the border of the arid neighboring desert.

[Beak Things](/beak-thing) are strong and dangerous creatures against novices, and will eat their prey alive. They are incredibly fast, and even a character with very high [athletics](/athletics) will find it near impossible to outrun them. Vain is host to another creature, the [Gorillo](/gorillo), which is often seen traveling alone. It is recommended to either sneak past them or at least train some level of combat

## Tips

-   Hive Traders won't trade with "Hiveless" characters.
    -   The Caravan Traders will though.
-   The Hive Caravanners will not help the player if they are attempting to escape an attack from Beak Things unless you bait them into striking them. The village guards will defend characters from Beak Thing/Gorillo attacks.
-   Vain could be an overwhelming region for novices, it is recommended to at least have a combat level of 40 to deal with wildlife.
-   Vain is a decent place to get copper as well as make money. [Copper](/copper) deposits are abundant in Vain, and always 100% quality. Some daring players will start off by going to Vain and mining for a living, slowly making money through trade with the Hive.

-   If you are interested in making cats by selling animal skins, Vain is a good place to do it. Beak Things, Gorillos and Garru all wander the area, and all drop a fair amount of skins and raw meats which can be sold to Hive vendors.

-   Establishing towns is much better on the outskirts alongside the desert, where iron, stone, and other crops can be exploited with the same benefits of Vain and copper.

## Trivia

-   Given that this region has an acid river flowing in the middle, and that several [Hive Traders](/hive-trader) sell [Thinfish](/thinfish), [Grand fish](/grand-fish) and [Dried Fish](/dried-fish), it's possible that the river contains a notable amount and a variety of Fish. (though essentially only 2 varieties exist in Kenshi anyway)
-   Hivers are at least 10x more populated that Beak Things in this area, as seen in the nest weights for Fogmen Nests and Beak Things.
    -   One could infer that the Beak Thing density is about as low as Bonedogs at Okran's Gulf, as they are both 10x less than Fogmen (which are of a result of turned Hivers when [The Queen](/the-queen) is dead).
-   Although it is red, the rain isn't acidic (or perhaps, not acidic enough to burn). However, the bodies of water are.
-   Vain is the only one of 2 zones whose [Beak Thing](/beak-thing) spawns have no adjacent regions spawning (indicating migration to or from adjacent regions), with the other one being [The Crater](/the-crater).
-   The internal name for the [biome](/map-biome-areas-region-correlation) designation that covers the Vain zone is _Artery_. The name Vain may be a play on the word _vein_.
