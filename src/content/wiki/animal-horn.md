**Animal Horns** are an Animal product and [Trade Good](/trade-goods) in Kenshi. They are obtained from unconscious [Wild Bulls](/wild-bull) or [Pack Bulls](/pack-bull), killing them in the process.

This item is not found on [Domesticated Bulls](/domesticated-bull).
