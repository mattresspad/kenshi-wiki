**Crumblejon** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) found in [Mongrel](/mongrel). His dialogue consists mostly of you pestering him, with him getting annoyed and refusing to join your squad (unless you pester him enough in which case he will join out of frustration).

According to dialogue, especially between him and [Luquin](/luquin), Crumblejon was a former [Samurai](/samurai) in the [United Cities](/united-cities), famed as the _"White Ripper"_ around the time when Luquin was first captured. He was heavily favored by the nobles, who apparently found him "exotic".

Unfortunately, time has not been kind to the once-feared Samurai. After his master died, he was left as an old Ronin, drifting the lands unwanted and cannot sustain his conjugal duties as in his prime, he decided to retire in Mongrel. He is now known as _"Crumblejon"_ on account of his crumbling bones, and he has become unnaturally stooped and shrunken. However, a trip to the local [Surgeon](/guild-plastic-surgeon) may remedy this...

It is possible to get him to accept your request at no cost. He will also attempt to convince female members of your squad to give him a massage.

Crumblejon will also reveal that he has a wife, although they have been estranged for a substantial period of time.

Crumblejon's exact age is unknown, but he claims to be at least 45 years old<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. He also boasts that he had been killing Ninjas while most characters were in their mothers' wombs<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, sometimes even to [Skeleton](/skeletons) characters, much to their confusion<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

If he dies or is already recruited a generic sword for hire will spawn.
