**The Immortal King** is a character that does not exist in the game but can be found in the [Forgotten Construction Set](/forgotten-construction-set).

He does have unique dialogue lines which all lead to fighting. Not very much is known about him. The developers just scrapped him from the game or made him "cut content". His name might suggest that further development of the concept of this character lead to the introduction of [Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon) in the final release of the game.

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (30% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).
