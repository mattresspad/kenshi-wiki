**Enemy Hunt** is an [event](/events) that occurs when the player is enemy of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), and lives in vicinity of their settlements. Alternatively, it could be launched as a result of defeating [Invincible Vengeance](/invincible-vengeance).

It has a maximum of 50% chance to trigger each day if within 3,000 meters, scaling down to 0% at 9,000 meters away.

When viable, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) will send an assault to the player's base. The assault will consist of 1 squad (average of 5) made up of:

-   3 ~ 5 [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)
-   2 ~ 4 [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian),

When it arrives, the War Leader will call out the player, generally taunting them and calling them a coward. Amusingly, sometimes the War Leader will refer to themself as a member of the [Invincible Five](/the-five-invincibles), despite the fact that they are a [Hundred Guardian](/hundred-guardian).

After the dialogue, the assault will attack the player's base. If they are victorious, they will leave the base shortly.

If they are defeated, there is a 30%/50%/20%to trigger the [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom)/[Shek Revenge](/shek-revenge)/**Enemy Hunt** event in 72-150 hours (~4.6 days). The Enemy Hunt here in particular would only trigger if it's a territorial trigger.

## Trivia

-   Despite clearly not having [Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles) in this assault, this event is still called "Shek 5 Invincible Enemy Hunt" in the [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set).
