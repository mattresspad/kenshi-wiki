_This article is about the Deadland Workshop. For the Dead Workshop in The Pits, see [Dead Workshop](/dead-workshop)._

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Old ruins. You gotta look at a map, go way out into the furthest reaches of the ass end of nowhere, the more dangerous the better, and then look around for old ruins and stuff.</blockquote><figcaption>Ronin Construction Trader</figcaption></figure>

**Deadlands Workshops** are large, hangar-like structures located in the [Deadlands](/deadlands). The structures themselves are composed of two "walkway" areas along either edge of the Workshop with one or two switchback ramps leading up, overlooked by [Mounted Crossbows](/mounted-crossbow) with spotlights. Along the walkways are various machines, tool stations and containers ranging from basic crates through to high-level locked chests.

This location is one of the few reliable places to obtain [Engineering Research](/engineering-research). Similar locations are the [Dead Workshop](/dead-workshop), [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop), and [Workshop Complex](/workshop-complex). All of them can be marked by reading the **[Engineers Map](/maps)**. There are 3 **Deadlands Workshops** in the region in total.

## Dangers

They are protected by potentially a [Broken Skeleton](/broken-skeleton) guarding the center of the site and 1 to 10 [Broken Model 30s](/broken-model-30) manning 2 Double Barrel [Harpoon Turrets](/harpoon-turret). Furthermore, there is nearly constant [Acid Rain](/weather-effects) in the area.

## Item Spawns

This location has a gear artifacts value of c.25,000 and an item artifacts value of c.10,000, meaning it will spawn additional items through the [Artifacts](/artifacts) system.
