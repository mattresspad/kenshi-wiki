**Izumi** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) from the Empire. She is a young scientist and medic. She can be hired for c.2,500.

She can be found in any town controlled by [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). She can also be found in [Town Overrides](/town-overrides) of these towns: [Bark](/bark), [Brink](/brink), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady), [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), and [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai).

She has a Smart [Personality](/personality) and after being recruited will have the "Player CIVILIAN" dialogue package. She has six dialogue lines linked to her, only one of which is exclusive to her.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by Ancient.
