**Shirts** include any piece of [equipment](/equipment) of any armour class that is meant to cover the torso in the shirt slot.

The following values assume \[Standard\] quality.

## Human and Shek Shirts

These shirts can be worn by [Human](/human) and [Shek](/shek) characters. They can be purchased at clothing shops and looted from certain places.

## Hiver Shirts

These shirts are the only options available for [Hive](/hive) characters to wear in the shirt slot. Their [Blueprints](/blueprints) can be purchased at [Hive Villages](/hive-village) as well as some shirts ranging from prototype to high grade.
