**Admag** is the capital of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), this is a place where the overwhelming vast majority of [Shek](/shek) inside their nation reside<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

This [Major Town](/major-towns) is placed on top of a hill and only has one entrance. It is located in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert). Its location can be revealed by reading the **[Map](/maps) of the Shek Kingdom**.

The powerful [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) are the town's main defense, although [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) patrol the desert.

This is the home of the Shek Kingdom's leader: [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem). She resides in the Faction HQ building. Esata is accompanied by [Bayan](/bayan) and [Seto](/seto). The building is also guarded by the kingdom's toughest warriors: [The Five Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles).

There is also a chance for a player to encounter some [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) inside the bar here such as [Oron](/oron), [Ruka](/ruka), [Rane the Giant](/rane-the-giant) and [Kang](/kang). [Seto](/seto) can also be recruited here after a player distinguishes themselves in the eyes of the Shek Kingdom.

If the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) has control over [Stack](/stack) or [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth), the Faction HQ may relocate there.
