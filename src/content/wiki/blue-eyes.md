**Blue Eyes** is a wanted criminal in the UC who can be found in [Mourn](/mourn), likely in a bar. She frequently brags about her exploits, making it easy to notice her. In [The Swamp](/the-swamp), players can find [Note From Blue Eyes](/note-from-blue-eyes) in which she writes about making it to the swamps.

## Trivia

-   She is Greenlander but has very dark skin. This is probably why she is described on the poster as racially ambiguous with the two sub-races "Greenlander/Scorchlander".
-   Although Blue Eyes is supposed to spawn with a [Jitte](/jitte), the quantity setting for the weapon spawn is set to 0, so she is unarmed instead.
