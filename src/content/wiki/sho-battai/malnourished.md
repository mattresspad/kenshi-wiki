**Sho-Battai** will become malnourished if [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) is killed or imprisoned while still under United Cities rule.

The town is plagued by rampant famine and poverty after the sole slave camp of [Port North](/port-north) supplying this town had been [destroyed](/port-north/destroyed) and cut off, and the ensuing chaos has destroyed and killed a part of the town and its inhabitants.

If [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) or [Lord Nagata](/lord-nagata) is killed/imprisoned as well, it will trigger the Half-Destroyed and malnourished version instead, causing squads of [Peasant Farmers](/peasant-farmer) to spawn and fight with the [Samurai](/samurai).
