If **Lord Nagata** AND **Emperor Tengu** are both dead or kidnapped, while [Boss Simion](/boss-simion) is alive and free, [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) will establish control of the city and many [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) and peasants are malnourished. There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

This town appears to be rebuilt after the [Empire's](/united-cities) authority completely fractured away from the area, allowing [Simion's](/boss-simion) forces to come in and re-organize the town. The town is around 20% destroyed.

The gate will be guarded by high level [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) and their [Bonedogs](/bonedog), and shops ran by [free traders](/free-traders) will open. Bar beds in this area will be replaced by camp beds.
