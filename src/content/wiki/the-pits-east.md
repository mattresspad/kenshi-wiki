**The Pits East** is the home of the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders). In [Crab Town](/crab-town), the home of the Queen of the Crab Raiders, players can become "crabbed" and a Unique Recruit named [Lumi](/lumi) can be found.

[Crabs](/crab), as well as Crab Raider patrols, are common in this Zone, and may occasionally fight with [Reaver](/reavers) patrols around here.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) in The Pits East. This zone does not spawn [Nests](/nest) or Camps.
