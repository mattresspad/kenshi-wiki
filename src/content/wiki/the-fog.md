**The Fog** is an [Event](/events) that is supposed to trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has a [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) within 1,600 meters of a [Fogmen](/fogmen) owned location. It has a 20% chance to trigger if within 1,200 meters, this chance scales down with distance until it hits a 0% chance at 1,600 meters.

Once the conditions have been fulfilled, the [Fogmen](/fogmen) will send and assault to the player's base. This assault will consist of 2-3 squads, each made up of 18-25 [Fogmen](/fogman).

Once the assault arrives the War Leader will scream outside the gates, signaling the assault to begin, attacking gate and characters, and kidnapping downed characters. Any characters kidnapped will be taken to a [Fog Deathyard](/fog-deathyard) or [Deadhive Overrun](/deadhive-overrun), or if certain [World States](/world-states) have been triggered they may be taken to certain locations previously owned by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), [Southern Hive](/southern-hive), or [Western Hive](/western-hive). Kidnapped characters will be eaten alive by [Fog Princes](/fog-prince) not long after being imprisoned, unless they are rescued or escape on their own first.

**Note:** As of 1.0.68, this event is currently broken and cannot trigger without a mod fixing it, due to the faction having a "not real" faction flag.
