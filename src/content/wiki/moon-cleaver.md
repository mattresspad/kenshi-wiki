The **Moon Cleaver** is an unusual weapon; it is uniquely shaped unlike any other weapon. It is the preferred weapon of the [Cannibal Grand Wizard](/cannibal-grand-wizard), [Dack](/dack), [Blackshifters](/blackshifters), and the [Preacher Cult](/preacher-cult).

The [Meitou](/meitou)\-grade version of the weapon can be found on [The Preacher](/the-preacher) in the [Cult Village](/cult-village).

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **2x9**, meaning it can only be equipped as a primary weapon.

At Edge Type and above quality, it is the only blunt-majority hacker.
