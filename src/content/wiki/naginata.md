The **Naginata** is a long and fast weapon, often used by the armies of the [United Cities](/united-cities). With its bonus against animals, it is a very potent weapon for wiping out nests.

This weapon has inventory dimensions of **1x10**, meaning it can only be equipped as a primary weapon.

The [Meitou](/meitou)\-version of this weapon can be found on [Crab Queen](/crab-queen), the leader of the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders).
