**Ration Pack** is a uncraftable [Food](/food) item which can be purchased from certain traders and caravans or received as a gift. Ration Packs and [Ancient Nutri-Rations](/ancient-nutri-rations) have the highest nutritional value of any food.

### Locations

These Ration Packs can be purchased from relevant [traders](/traders), usually [bars](/bars) or trade caravans.

There is a possibility that the following characters will give the player a Ration Pack as a gesture of good will.

-   [High Paladin](/high-paladin)
-   [Holy Sentinel](/holy-sentinel)
-   [Paladin](/paladin)
-   [Protector of the Flame](/protector-of-the-flame)
