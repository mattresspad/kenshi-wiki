Flotsam Ninjas Route is a [World State Route](/world-state-routes) where the player has allied relationship with [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) and initiated dialogue with [Moll](/moll) that opens up the questline for HN to change the state of [Kenshi](/kenshi) in a way that advances the FN's interests. To talk with Moll, the player must be allied with FN.

The goal of this questline mainly revolves around taking down [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) leaders and instigating revolutions to topple down the regime of HN.

(This part includes the targets that are directly referenced by the questline)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Seta controls Stack in the south west of the plains. Valtena you'll find in a military outpost, Okran's Shield, on the eastern border. Do not underestimate their hold on the Holy Nation, the two of them are probably smarter than the Phoenix himself - he was born into his role, remember, but those guys earned it with pure Okranite crazy and cunning.</blockquote><figcaption>Moll</figcaption></figure>

While there aren't any strict priorities in which you take down [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) leaders, Flotsam's plan mainly revolves around taking down the 2 High Inquisitors, Seta and Valtena, before they march upon [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) and take down the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). There are a few differences in dialogue depending on whether you take down Phoenix, Seta, or Valtena first.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>The people of Blister Hill couldn't handle the downfall of their 'invincible' god lord. I guess you've seen the ruins, (player name)?</blockquote><figcaption>Moll, Phoenix down first, Seta and/or Valtena alive</figcaption></figure>

Once you take down both Inquisitors, Flotsam Patrols will start spawning in [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), this is when they will be finally strong enough to take down HN.
