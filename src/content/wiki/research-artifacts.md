**Research Artifacts** are [items](/items) in Kenshi which are required to research [Technology](/technology) on a [Research Bench](/research-bench).

Book artifacts are common and can easily be found in [Mechanical Shops](/empire-mechanical-shop) and other similar shops. The other artifact types can also be found in shops such as [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) and [Travel and Repairs Shops](/travel-and-repairs-shop). Although, these items are primarily found in [Ancient Locations](/ancient-locations).
