**General Hat-12** was the Chief of the Second Empire's police force. He is a member of the [Second Empire](/second-empire) and can be found in [Ashland Dome IV](/ashland-dome-iv).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
