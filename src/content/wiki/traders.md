Traders in Kenshi are NPCs who focus on the buying and selling of goods for profit. Traders can be found in [Major Towns](/major-towns) and most [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts).

## Trade Cultures

Trade Cultures dictate whether some items spawn in locations as well as whether an item or building is illegal or not. Trade Cultures are tied to [factions](/factions), meaning that the same building spawned as a United Cities building can have slightly different items than if it was spawned as part of the Holy Nation.

## Trading with an unconscious squad member

An unconscious squad member can still trade while another member carries them. During a trade, click their portrait: their inventory takes the carrier's place beside the trader's, and items can be sold from it and bought back into it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
