**Suchon**, or **'Ghost**' as he is more commonly known, is the leader of the [Berserkers](/berserkers). He can be found in the [Berserker village](/berserker-village) east of [Last Stand](/last-stand) camping with many other [Berserkers](/berserkers).

When turning Ghost in to the police in the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) will talk about his past.

Before his exile, Ghost was a warrior of the Shek Kingdom, but after he presumably lost a battle, he was expelled from the Kingdom in disgrace. Subsequently, he became the leader of the Berserkers - Sheks who did not accept civilization and led a barbaric lifestyle. Now Suchon wanders the world as a Ghost of himself, searching for the peace of an afterlife that he will never achieve.

He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

The [Berserker Village](/berserker-village) is at times attacked by [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) which sometimes leads to Ghost being killed and subsequently eaten.

## [World States](/world-states)

(Requires Ghost to be imprisoned/dead)

If **Ghost** is killed or kidnapped, roaming [Berserker](/berserker) squads in [Berserker Country](/berserker-country), [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains), [Spider Plains](/spider-plains), and [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) have their frequency halved and become broken, having their patrols reduced from 2 ~ 8 members to 2 ~ 5.

(Requires Ghost to be alive and free)

If **Ghost** is alive and free while [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed/imprisoned, Berserker patrols will patrol in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) region.

(Relocation)

If **Ghost** is alive and free while [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem) is killed/imprisoned, he will relocate his position to [Admag](/admag) or [Squin](/squin) if both [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) and [Mukai The Mountain](/mukai-the-mountain) are killed/imprisoned.

## Trivia

-   Ghost appears to be named Suchon, or at least, before he "died" and "became a Ghost", as implied by Hundred Guardian's lines when turning him in.
-   There is a possibility that Ghost fought with Gorrillo Bandits and lost, given that their leader holds an [Meitou](/meitou) [Exile Plank](/exile-plank) that could be part of a former significant disgraced warrior. This is especially likely considering the items description of "The shamed ones walk the land forever as lost ghosts."
