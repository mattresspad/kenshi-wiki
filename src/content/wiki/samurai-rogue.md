**Samurai Rogues** are members of the [United Cities](/united-cities). They appear to be ex-[Samurai Sergeants](/samurai-sergeant) turned to banditry after the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) that employed them was incapacitated.

Alternatively, they may be bandits employed by the government to rob wanderers for emergency provisions needed by the government after the loss of food supplies from now-destroyed slave camps.

They spawn in groups of 4 ~ 13.

Only when specific nobles with the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle) perishes will they start to spawn at specific location:

-   The deaths of [Sanda](/lady-sanda), [Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) cause them to start spawning in the [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Eye](/the-eye), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Outlands](/the-outlands).

The following will trigger if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is still alive:

-   The deaths of [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) and [Haga](/slave-master-haga) cause them to start spawning malnourished in [Bast](/bast-zone) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).
-   The deaths of [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren) and [Ruben](/slave-master-ruben) cause them to start spawning malnourished in [Heng](/heng-zone), [Skimsands](/skimsands) and [The Eye](/the-eye).

They are likely to have a Maniacal, Brave, or Traitorous [Personality](/personality).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (10% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).
