**Tengu's Vault** is located at the south end of rocky [Skimsands](/skimsands) and is guarded by the [Samurai](/samurai) of the [United Cities](/united-cities). The Vault seems to be a military prison presumably for the worst criminals of the empire. [The Vault Warden](/the-vault-warden) oversees the prison.

When approaching, the gate guards will tell you not to approach and are going to attack if you're trespassing anyways. If you allied with the [United Cities](/united-cities) the guards will only react verbally but will not attack the player which allows you to see what is inside.

Currently the inside resembles a police building, floor 0 bed area/training area, floor 1 cooking/eating area, floor 2 prison area.
