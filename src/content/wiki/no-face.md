**No-Face** is the only human character of the [Gutters](/gutters) faction. He can be found at [Strange Camp](/strange-camp) located on an island in [Gut](/gut).

Upon landing on his islands' shores he will attack with his [Oldworld Bow Mkl crossbow](/oldworld-bow-mki) and switch to his sword for close combat.

He has a c.15,000 [Bounty](/bounty) from the [United Cities](/united-cities).

They wear \[High\] armour (5% chance of \[Specialist\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
