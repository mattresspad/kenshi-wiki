The **Rusted Hive Shirt** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Shirt slot. It can be bought from Western Hive Villages. This shirt is one of the four shirt options available for [Hive](/hive) characters to wear in the shirt slot.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x2**.

There are [standard](/hiver-chain-shirt) and [blackened](/blackened-chain-hive-shirt) versions of this clothing. This is also the Hive-equivalent of [Rusty Chain Shirt](/rusty-chain-shirt) and [Rusty Chainmail](/rusty-chainmail), and, as such, has no arm protection.

It can be randomly equipped by [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard) or [Armoury Droneguards](/armoury-droneguard).

## Appearance

The **Rusted Hive Shirt** appears to be a rusty, sleeveless chain shirt that fits the upper part of a hiver's chest. A semi-circular hole is open to match the hiver's torso, which is instead covered by a metal plate fastened by four leather belts. The stomach is covered by a metal cage attached to a thick leather border.
