The **Berserker Village** is a hostile [Berserker](/berserkers) outpost located in the [Spider Plains](/spider-plains). The Berserker leader [Ghost](/ghost) can be found there as well as a couple of other [Berserkers](/berserker). It is a small settlement consisting of mostly ruined buildings and remaining wall sections.

## Buildings

-   [Storm House](/storm-house) (destroyed) x2
-   [Watchtower](/watchtower) (destroyed) x2
-   [Defensive Gates](/defensive-gates) III
-   [Defensive Wall](/defensive-walls) II (section)
-   Old Storm House (Collapsed) (For Sale: c.6,000)

## Lore

The **Beserker Village** was, presumably, a [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) outpost or minor settlement that was [overrun by spiders](/bugmaster), or was abandoned due to depopulation after the kingdom's many wars.

## Town overrides

_Beserker Village is not affected by any [World States](/world-states) and thus, does not produce [Town Overrides](/town-overrides)._

## Tips

-   The campbeds that are placed around the village can be used regardless of your relations to the Berserkers.
