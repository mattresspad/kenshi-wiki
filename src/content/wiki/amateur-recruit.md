**Amateur Recruit** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They are an inexperienced person who anxiously threatens the player for looking at them. They can be hired for c.3,000.

They can be found in [Crab Town](/crab-town), [Mourn](/mourn), [The Hub](/the-hub), and, depending on certain [World States](/world-states), in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), or [Stoat](/stoat). They might be found in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), Waystation and Waystation Four, and [World's End](/worlds-end). They can also be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps.

They have a Fearful [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package.
