**Esata the Stone Golem** is the leader of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) (or potentially [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen)) and has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status).

She can be found in the Faction HQ in [Admag](/admag) along with her daughter [Seto](/seto), [Bayan](/bayan), and 4 of [The Five Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles), unless [World States](/world-states) have moved the Shek HQ.

Esata wields a legendary [Meitou](/meitou) [Fragment Axe](/fragment-axe) but lacks enough Strength (It needs 144) to use it at normal speed. Although her combat speed is very low, she is still a force to be reckoned with. She has a hefty c.50,000 [Bounty](/bounty) from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities).

Players can "join" the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) by reaching +75 relations with it, which can be achieved by returning in diverse bounties or instantly by capturing the [Bugmaster](/bugmaster) or any of the Holy Nation's leaders ([Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) or [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta)) and bringing them to Esata. If players manage to kill or kidnap Esata, the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) leader will become [Mukai The Mountain](/mukai-the-mountain).

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## History<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Esata is the first of the Shek leaders to want to address their kingdom’s problem of a declining population, seeing that they would fight themselves extinct if they continue their assaults against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) without weakening them first. However, this new strategic rationality has caused a divide in its battle hungry citizens.

Fiery and fueled by her anger for her enemies, Esata is a little less conventional than other past leaders in that she places bigger victory on long-term crushing of enemies over short-term glory. She tries to balance prosperity with glory without fighting themselves extinct, while keeping the religious and warmongering aspirations of her people happy. She temporarily tolerates outsiders for the need to recover a bit from the losses of their war.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Flatskins. I despise them, and if it were up to me they'll be chased away at our gates... but Bayan tells me their trade is neccessary to the growth of the Shek... so I'll tolerate them. For now.</blockquote></figure>

The [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) puts little importance on academic education, meaning that most Shek can’t read – Esata is no exception to this and is not particularly intelligent. She does, however, have formidable battle skills and an open mind to rational thought. Her aide on the other hand, [Bayan](/bayan), is an impressive intellect. Although the talent of intellects is normally wasted in the traditional Shek Kingdom, Esata relies on Bayan for his advice, for the good of the nation.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote><a href="/bayan">Bayan</a> is my sheath. without him, I’d have gotten lost in my own ego, a corpse on Holy Nation land long ago. Those Okran scum don’t deserve to be my defeaters. With Bayan at my side, the Shek will crush them once and for all. There will be no better glory than this.</blockquote></figure>

Only the best of the Shek warrior can earn battle names, and Esata earned hers ‘the Stone Golem’ when she joined [The Five Invincibles](/the-five-invincibles), the Kingdom’s highest-ranking fighters. It was during this time that Esata served under the previous king, [Shager](/shagers-overthrow), whom she challenged on his plans for a potential last ditch suicide attack against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). After a hard-hitting battle one day, [Bayan](/bayan) protested against Shager’s plans for a suicide attack against The Holy Nation to regain their honor. In response to Shager’s angry threats against his ‘cowardice’, Esata stepped in and boomed, "the man speaks the truth. You want Bayan silenced, you’ll have to cut me down first”. She fought and defeated Shager, becoming the new leader in his place. _This may be why there are only four of "The Five Invincibles" as Esata was the 5th_.

## [World States](/world-states)

If Esata the Stone Golem is killed/imprisoned, vast majority of the Sheks will go [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

(Requiring Esata to be dead)

[Admag](/admag) would fall under [Berserkers](/berserkers) rule (It will spawn [Ghost](/ghost) in Admag if he hasn't been killed/imprisoned).

[Squin](/squin) will have a town override which would include [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) and still being under SK rule. Or handed over to [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) if [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) is killed while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive. Or to the [Berserkers](/berserkers) if both Mukai and Flying Bull are dead.

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed, [Stack](/stack) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) will be conquered by [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) rule if [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive, [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) will also fall under [Kral's Chosen](/holy-mines/krals-chosen) if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is dead as well.

-   If [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are alive then:
    -   If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are killed, [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth/united-cities) is taken over by [United Cities](/united-cities).
    -   If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) and [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) are killed, [Holy Mines](/holy-mines/slave-traders) are taken over by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).
    -   If [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)/[Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are killed, [Holy Farms](/holy-farms) are taken over by [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

Squads of [Shek](/shek) patrols will cease to spawn inside [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).

[Kamikaze](/kamikaze) patrols will start invading [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam) regions. If [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) is alive and free they can be found patrolling [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) region.

If [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is alive, [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) will start spawning 2 types of counter-attack patrols in [Border Zone](/border-zone) and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam) against the [Kamikaze](/kamikaze).

(Requiring Esata to be alive)

If [Seto](/seto) is killed, Shek Kingdom patrols will start invading [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) while [Admag will have a weakened override](/admag/weakened).

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) is killed, Shek Kingdom patrols will start invading [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) while [Stack](/stack/shek-kingdom) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth/shek-kingdom) will be taken over by them.

If [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is killed, Shek Kingdom patrols will start invading [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), while [Holy Farmers](/holy-farmer) will lament over being extortionately taxed [through their tributes](/tribute-round).

If [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) is killed, [Okran's Shield will be taken over by Shek Kingdom](/okrans-shield/shek-kingdom) if the player is allied with them.

If [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) are killed, [Holy Mines will be controlled by Shek Kingdom](/holy-mines/shek-kingdom).

## Bounty

If Esata the Stone Golem is turned in to the following factions, the following benefits/penalties are granted:

### The Holy Nation

-   \-75 relations with Shek Kingdom
-   +80 relations with The Holy Nation
-   c.50,000 bounty reward

### United Cities

-   \-75 relations with Shek Kingdom
-   +40 relations with United Cities
-   c.50,000 bounty reward

## Trivia

-   Due to racial experience multipliers, Esata's Attack and Toughness can spawn between level 99.6 to 104.4.
-   Her bounty is actually assigned by Trader's Guild, but under the United Cities legal system.
    -   This means she can still be turned in to [Police Chiefs](/police-chief) of UC.
-   Most Shek cannot read, Esata included. <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
-   Although Esata usually prioritizes long-term victory over short-term vengeance, she can still be irrational and disregard such notions if she is sufficiently enraged. Most notably, when Seto is killed or imprisoned for any reason.
-   The defeat of Esata causes the removal of around 76% potential Shek Warrior squads in [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) (among other areas), with the rest ending up as [Kamikaze](/kamikaze), indicative that they de-populated themselves significantly from fighting after sending out all their forces from [Admag](/admag/berserkers).
-   [World States](/world-states) suggests that the Golem's forces are constantly raiding [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), but these raiders are blocked off and 'silenced' by the defenses of [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), making penetrations very rare, and below the level of simulation in homeless spawns<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. This is seen in how when [Seto](/seto) is down, the main army force begins its assault by throwing a large portion of their remaining warriors at [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. If HN's defenses are destroyed at this point, additional raider forces beyond the main forces begin to spawn even if the main assault has already started, revealing that the raids have been happening all along, and that they were indeed previously blocked<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
    -   This is further supported by the mentions of their raidings throughout history<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and [Phoenix's](/holy-lord-phoenix) lines about [Shek Kingdom's forces](/shek-kingdom) still terrorizing their populace<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
-   Esata could potentially be a member of [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) in [Squin](/squin/krals-chosen) if she is kidnapped, imprisoned or otherwise unwell while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive and free, and released after achieving the [Mukai override](/squin/mukai) before taking out [Mukai](/mukai-the-mountain) to achieve the [Kral's Chosen Squin](/squin/krals-chosen).
    -   She could also spawn as one in [Stack](/stack/krals-chosen) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth/shek-kingdom) if the appropriate conditions for those towns are achieved as well, although there is a chance for it to switch back to [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) control in this case.
