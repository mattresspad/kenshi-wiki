**Mongrel Armour Shop** is an armour shop found in [Mongrel](/mongrel).

This shop is unique in the way that it has **Shek Warriors** as their guards rather than the standard [Shop Guard](/shop-guard).

### Possible Locations

-   [Mongrel](/mongrel)

### Residents

-   [Ronin Armour Trader](/ronin-armour-trader)
-   [Shek Warrior](/shek-warrior) (2)

This shop is capable of spawning [Artifacts](/artifacts), as such it may spawn items not in the list. These items, however, will not respawn when the shop restocks.
