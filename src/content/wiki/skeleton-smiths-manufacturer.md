**Skeleton Smiths** weapons are weapons from the [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city) that have found their way across the world.

## Overview

Made by the [Skeletons](/skeleton) from the [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city), these are some of the best weapons around. As such, they are more expensive than normal. These are often wielded by wandering Skeletons and some of the other strongest characters in the game, or found as [Artifacts](/artifacts) in ruins.

## Trivia

The model of the blades named by Mk, is the same as how some Skeleton races are named with examples such as [Screamer MkI](/screamer-mki), and [Skeleton P4MkII](/skeleton-p4mkii).
