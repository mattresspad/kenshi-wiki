**Western Hive Princes** are a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. They are a subrace of the Western [Hive](/hive). They are a playable race from the start of the game.

Free Princes are rare, as they evolved to be more independent from the main Hive and remain separated for long periods of time. They also do not eat [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat), unlike the other Hiver breeds. Of all Hive breeds, they are also the most intelligent. [Dead Hive](/deadhive-prince) and [Southern Hive](/southern-hive-prince) have their own version of the Hive Prince.

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race are immune to acid and will not be harmed by these environmental effects:

-   [Acid Ground](/acid-ground)
-   Acid Rain
-   [Swimming](/swimming) in acid

_Characters of this race are not immune to these types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). It is recommended that they wear weather protecting armour when entering [Zones](/category/zones) in which this weather can occur._

-   Burning
-   Duststorms
-   Poison Gas
