**Southern Hive Worker Drones** are a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. They are a subrace of the Southern [Hive](/hive).

Like all non-Prince Hivers, they can eat [Raw Meat](/raw-meat) and [Foul Raw Meat](/foul-raw-meat).

They also have a slightly higher bleeding rate of 0.4 compared to their [Western Cousins](/hive-worker-drone) of 0.3, and do not have a [Toughness](/toughness) experience boost.

Though not an option as a starting race, they can potentially spawn as [slaves](/slave) in the [United Cities](/united-cities) and therefore have a chance of being recruited via either [lockpicking](/lockpicking) their shackles or buying them from [Slave Traders](/slave-traders). That being said, their spawn rates appear to be extremely low. [Espher](/espher) is the only guaranteed spawn of the Southern Hive Worker Drone recruit.
