If [Moll](/moll) is dead or imprisoned **Flotsam Village** will be overrun by [Cannibals](/cannibals). [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) will still appear as part of the "Residence" list despite no longer present. Most of the buildings will be destroyed.

There are no further possible overrides for this settlement after achieving this version.

6 ~ 25 [Cannibals](/cannibals) and 10 ~ 35 [Scrawny Cannibals](/scrawny-cannibal) can be found as bar squads here.

## Buildings

### FCS values (Priority)

-   Default Resident: Destroyed Building
-   3x Cannibal home occupation (2) (1 [Cannibal Chief](/cannibal-chief), 7 ~ 13 [Cannibals](/cannibals))
