**Eagle's Cross** is a [Crossbow](/crossbow-class) with farthest range and highest damage of any ranged weapon. It can be dangerous in the hands of an unskilled shooter; a single friendly-fire shot could kill any low-toughness allies.

It is the only Crossbow which requires [Long Bolts](/bolts-longs) which only hold 10 shots and weigh 2kg each stack. On long adventures a smaller crossbow may be more suitable due to this. The primary use for an Eagle's Cross is to eliminate targets from far distances and to deal with opponents who wear armour with high harpoon resistance.

## Obtaining

### Looting (Artifacts)

-   Masterwork Quality
    -   1x Found in the [Ashlands](/ashlands) inside of [Ashland Dome II](/ashland-dome-ii).
    -   1x Found in [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon) inside of a shop.
-   Specialist Quality
    -   1x Found in [Greyshelf](/greyshelf) in the Northwestern Hiver-Claimed Armoury.
    -   1x Found in [Sonorous Dark](/sonorous-dark) in the Northern [Skinhouse](/skinhouse).

### Purchasing

Eagle's Cross can be purchased at many shops such as the [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) and [Ranger Shops](/ranger-shop) owned by Tech Hunters.

### Crafting

[Blueprints](/blueprints) for the Eagle's Cross can be found at [The Great Library](/the-great-library), [Scraphouse](/scraphouse), and [Cat-Lon's Exile.](/cat-lons-exile) This blueprint also has a rare chance of spawning at [Barrier Tower](/barrier-tower) and [Sniper Tower](/sniper-tower) or of being sold at [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) [Ranger Shops](/ranger-shop) in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Mongrel](/mongrel), [Spring](/spring), and [World's End](/worlds-end).

After unlocking the ability to craft through use of a blueprint, players can craft Eagle's Crosses at a [Crossbow Crafting Bench](/crossbow-crafting-bench). A single Eagle's Cross is made up of 8 [Steel Bars](/steel-bars) and 4 [Crossbow Parts](/crossbow-parts).

### **Versus the Oldworld Bow MkII**

The Eagle's Cross and the [Oldworld Bow MkII](/oldworld-bow-mkii) are arguably the best two crossbows in the game, but choosing between them requires careful consideration of several factors:

1\. Damage Per Shot

The Eagle's Cross does more damage per shot, making it a superior choice against armoured opponents, as it will overcome the target's harpoon resistance, leading to greater damage. Furthermore, a masterwork-quality bow is capable of one-shotting many enemies, including human enemies up to a moderate degrees of toughness, with light armour. If including height advantage, the Eagle's Cross is capable of doing over 200 damage to low-toughness enemies, one-shotting some [robots](/robots) when shooting from a high cliff, for example. In contrast, the Oldworld Bow MkII is generally limited to one-shotting only weak enemies with no armour.

2\. Rate of Fire and Damage Output

At low crossbow and dexterity levels, the Oldworld Bow MkII has only a slightly better rate of fire, and a significantly inferior damage output than the Eagle's Cross: as noted in game, the Oldworld Bow MkII can be difficult for beginners to reload; this is reflected in-game by its reload time scaling more on user skill than other bows; an Eagle's Cross will double its rate of fire going from minimum to maximum crossbow skill and dexterity, while a Oldworld Bow MkII has its rate of fire increase by 2.87 times.

In effect, this generally makes the Eagle's Cross a better choice for low-level characters; or else the [Oldworld Bow Mark I](/oldworld-bow-mki) may be a better choice for users wishing for a high rate-of-fire bow, which can be more practicable in chaotic melees and can be helpful to stun-lock enemies.

However, at very high skill levels, the Oldworld Bow MkII has superior damage output (against unarmoured foes) and a very rapid rate of fire. The Oldworld Bow reaches damage-per-second parity with the Eagle's Cross at about 75 crossbow skill and dexterity, while with the aforementioned skills at 100 (which can be more practically achieved with with the aid of buffs from [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs) or outfits such as the [Assassin's Rags](/assassins-rags)), the Oldworld Bow MkII has a 16% greater damage per second.

3\. Range and Accuracy

The Eagle's Cross has the longest range and highest accuracy of any bow in the game, shooting farther than even [harpoon turrets](/harpoon-turret), giving it the ability to snipe enemies from extreme ranges in the hands of a skilled marksman. Furthermore, the long range allows for many training opportunities: marksmen can safely rain down bolts upon distant leviathans, or onto denizens of a [cannibal village](/cannibal-village) from the safety of a [watchtower](/watchtower) placed on the outskirts, for example. Furthermore, the Eagle's Cross has a higher bolt velocity, which further increases the hit probability, as it makes it less likely the enemy will deviate from the expected trajectory in the time it takes for the bolt to reach its target.

4\. Weight and Economy

The Eagle's Cross uses [long bolts](/bolts-longs) as opposed to the Oldworld Bow MkII's [regular bolts](/bolts-regulars). Long bolts only contain 10 bolts per item as opposed to 30, weigh 2kg as opposed to 1kg, take up 5 inventory squares as opposed to 4, cost 389 cats as opposed to 243 cats. This effectively makes them, approximately:

-   4 times heavier
-   3.2 times more costly
-   2.5 times more inventory-space consuming

Per point of damage, assuming the Eagle's Cross does 50% more damage per bolt, which is roughly accurate (it depends on the quality level).

The Eagle's Cross itself also weighs 2kg more than the Oldworld Bow MkII. All things considered, wielding the Eagle's Cross and supplying it with bolts is a far greater logistical challenge than the Oldworld Bow MkII — which is far more convenient. A beast of burden is often necessary to carry long bolts if heavy fighting is expected far away from any resupply area, as well as a base with a production line, or regular purchases of bolts from [rangers shops](/ranger-shop).

5\. Friendly Fire

While the Eagle's Cross's higher damage is generally an advantage, it is a detriment in friendly-fire situations. While fighters in heavy armour can generally shrug off many accidental hits from the Oldworld Bow MkII, friendly fire from the Eagle's Cross can sometimes prove catastrophic — knocking out, maiming or even outright killing allies in a single shot. If one wants to provide supporting fire to melee fighters, with the Eagle's Cross, it is recommended to only use high-toughness melee fighters with good quality — and in particular, high coverage — armours, such as [Crab Armour](/crab-armour) with underlying [Chainmail](/chainmail) armour. Players should also consider the effect that height advantage may have on any friendly-fire damage.
