**Holy Nation Bar** is the [bar](/bar) specifically found in [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This variant is used in all Holy Nation towns which have a bar. The most distinct difference between this bar and ones found outside The Holy Nation is that this bar's residents are exclusively [human](/human) races.

These bars comes in generally 2 types of buildings within the Holy Nation, including [L-House](/l-house) (more common) and [Snailhouse](/snailhouse).
