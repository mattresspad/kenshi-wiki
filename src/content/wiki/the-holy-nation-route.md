The Holy Nation Route is a [World State Route](/world-state-routes) where the player has allied relationship with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and initiated dialogue with [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) that opens up the questline for HN to change the state of [Kenshi](/kenshi) in a way that advances the HN's interests.

The HN route is diametrically opposed to [Flotsam Ninjas Route](/flotsam-ninjas-route) and [Shek Kingdom Route](/shek-kingdom-route).

The goal of this questline mainly revolves around purging [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) and eradicating the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) (alongside some of their [influential](/mukai-the-mountain) [figures](/seto)). While there are also other notable characters that are also of interest to HN, they are not included into the main dialogue, they are added into the "Secondary" or "Tertiary" targets based on their percieved importance and how much they align with HN's goals.

In order to initiate the dialogue with the _Phoenix,_ the player must have at least +75 relations with HN, initiate as a speaker who is a human male without robotic limbs.

Once that is done, the following lines will be said by the _Phoenix_:

-   The Lord Of Light recognizes your good deeds, holy brother. The Lord sees all... Virtue is in your destiny, as it is my own. We are united, both, in our paths for greatness, for change and for goodness... Come! \[He motions for you to come closer\]
-   \[He gives you a bear hug and squeezes tight. There's a potent smell of vegetables. He presses a thumb to your forehead\]
-   Bless this one, holy father. Protect him from all that may harm his spirit and body. Bless this one, holy father, my own brother...
-   Here, take my gift with you. The Chalice of Fire, a beacon of light. May it ward you from darkness wherever your journey may take you.
    -   (Receives [Chalice of Fire](/chalice-of-fire), +10 relation with HN)
-   ... Now, go forth, holy one! Continue your path...

## Main Targets

(This part includes the targets that are directly referenced by the questline)

After the initial opening lines are spoken, the player will unlock further dialogues with the Phoenix. To do so, the player simply has to reinitiate dialogue with the Phoenix.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>We have many enemies that plague us. Flotsam's Narko worshipping heretics still roam free and need to be brought to justice. They steal our child bearers and incite riots. And then there's the barbaric warmongering devils... They've terrorized our towns enough with their needless hunger for blood, the war must end.</blockquote><figcaption>Holy Lord Phoenix</figcaption></figure>

Once this is spoken, you could ask for tips on how to destroy the Flotsam or SK, in either case, if you accept the task of destroying them, you will recieve a [Holy Seal](/holy-seal) that allows you to call on [Paladins](/paladin) for help (or sell it for a rather heft sum of 15k [Cats](/cats)). There is, rather ludicruously, no limits to how many times you can get the Holy Seal, and you could potentially get extremely rich just by repeatedly initiating dialogues and selling off Seals.

(Technically speaking, it doesn't really matter if you take down Flotsam Ninjas or Shek Kingdom first, outside of a few dialogue changes but for the sake of simplicity we will assume you will take down [Moll](/moll) first, since she is comparitively much easier to take down than SK.)

### [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas)

Phoenix will tell you the rough location of where Flotsam are located (specifically at [Hidden Forest](/hidden-forest)), and the reason for why he doesn't send army forces to vanquish them.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Moll hides far away north west in the godless lands, the place of demons and monstrosities. Without her, the Flotsam order would fall apart. But where she hides, at home with the demons, I would not burden that journey upon any of my holy brothers.</blockquote><figcaption>Holy Lord Phoenix</figcaption></figure>

Flotsam Ninjas will be defeated once Moll is ridden of, their [settlement](/flotsam-village) will become overrun with [Cannibals](/flotsam-village/cannibals).

### [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)

Once Moll is successfully captured or killed, the attention will move towards the Shek Kingdom.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote><p>The holy plains are a safer place with Narko's spawn, Moll, out of the way... But the devil woman, Esata, she terrorizes us with her barbarian warmongerers. She bides her time, waiting to butcher us all in her centuries-old feud.</p></blockquote><figcaption>Holy Lord Phoenix</figcaption></figure>

You could then ask for tips from Phoenix on how to successfully destroy SK, to which he will advise you to first take down [Seto](/seto) to enrage [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem). Since they are formidable opponents, he advises you to use [Stealth](/stealth) (and probably [Assassination](/assassination)) to get to her.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>The Golem may have demon strength but she has the weakness of an impulsive savage.&nbsp; We can trigger her wrecklessness by taking the thing she cherishes most, Seto, her demon child. Her warriors are erratic and violent, tackling them head on will be risky so you'd have to use stealth.</blockquote><figcaption>Holy Lord Phoenix</figcaption></figure>

Once you capture/kill _Seto_, the following lines will be spoken.

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>It's begun. With the devil child purged, the nest has been riled and the Golem's forces have already started warring in the holy plains. I pray for the southern cities... but the capital's defences are down at least. Our forces are on their way to Stenn. We can destroy the horned barbarians once and for all. The devil war will finally come to an end and we'll see peace again after hundreds of years...<p>The Golem is vulnerable. Now is our time to attack.</p></blockquote><figcaption>Holy Lord Phoenix</figcaption></figure>

This coincides largely with the [World States](/world-states) that happen after Seto's demise. Specifically, vengeful [Hundred Guardian](/hundred-guardian) and [Shek Warrior](/shek-warrior) patrols (with more HG than that of a typical Shek patrol) will start invading [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), at the same time, HN will start exploiting the weakness of SK by sending forces into [Border Zone](/border-zone) and [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert) (see their respective [Zones](/world-of-kenshi) for more details). [Admag](/admag) will have an override with [weakened defenses](/admag/weakened), making further direct assaults into their capital easier.
