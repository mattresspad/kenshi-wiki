**Bast** is a zone named after a now mostly abandoned town of the United Cities in the northeast region of the map. It is one of the two border regions between The Holy Nation and United Cities, with the other one being [Skimsands](/skimsands). The region is under jurisdiction as part of [United Cities](/united-cities) core<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but is in practice a weakly held conflict zone along the frontiers of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

It was once a developing region dotted with 9 hamlets and 3 settlements until [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) smashed through the [United Cities](/united-cities) defenses<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, executed the head noble<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, razed the region<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and pushed the frontlines back from [Okran's Valley's](/okrans-valley) borders to this area<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, before withdrawing their forces and taking the youngsters as captives. This place has been in perpetual conflict ever since<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> for _at least_ 15 years<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> with HN sending patrols to periodically pummel this area<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and UC struggling at securing this area<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

A significant amount of [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) (from [Sinkuun](/sinkuun)) and [Cannibals](/cannibals) (from [Northern Coast](/northern-coast)) spill into this territory and may find themselves embroiled in the fight with one another too.

Both sides have a noticable patrol presence in this region, so it's best to keep an eye out if you are either a non-human or a poor and starving person, as [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and [United Cities](/united-cities) have a tendency to target these types of wanderers, respectively.

While the city itself has almost no loot, the surrounding area witnesses many skirmishing scenes between patrols.

## Tips

-   Holy Nation sentinel and United City samurai gear, as well as [Basic First Aid Kits](/medical-items), can be obtained in abundance here. Wearing faction armor works as a [Disguise](/disguise) and may lead to being attacked by enemies of that faction or the faction itself if the disguise is blown. Faction armor also has their market value significantly reduced, making it less profitable than typical armors to sell.
    -   This place can still be a very easy way to earn [Raw Iron](/raw-iron) by smelting armour and weapons in an [Item Furnace](/item-furnace).
-   This place can be great to train [Field Medic](/field-medic) skill given the large number of casualties from clashes here.
-   To deal with the Holy Nation patrols it is suggested to bring one human male, preferably with a copy of [The Holy Flame](/the-holy-flame) in order to pacify them. Do be aware they will attack on sight if there is a [skeleton](/skeleton) or person with prosthetics in the players Party.
-   To deal with any United Cities patrols avoid being malnourished as it will attract their attention. If all else fails, several dialogue checks from them allow for bribery at the cost of cats.

## Trivia

-   Dialogue from Bard implies that Bast was grown and prospering for only a short period of time before the Holy Nation's invasion.
-   [United Cities](/united-cities) can be inferred to only effectively control eastern-most part of the region, as hinted by forces located only in eastern [Drin](/drin), as any further distance would likely be met with resistance and hampering from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
-   This place is very likely to be razed as a result of one or several large [Holy Nation punitive expeditionary forces](/wrath-of-god) sent after UC [enslaved](/slave-raid)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> a [frontier farm village](/farm-ruins).
    -   The intent clearly isn't conquest, as the forces withdrew from the region after razing it.
    -   The intent likely isn’t wholescale slaughter, since the sources mention only the execution of the head noble, and not of anyone else.
        -   The mention of youngsters getting sent to [Rebirth](/rebirth) would suggest that they valued taking captives instead.
        -   There are visibly alive displaced civilians inside [Endin](/endin) and [Bast](/bast), which wouldn't make sense if eradication of population was the goal.
    -   The intent likely isn't mass enslavement, as only youngsters are mentioned to be sent to Rebirth as opposed to every able-bodied civilian.
        -   During the chaos, many children likely became displaced orphans who were unlikely to survive. They would have both moral and pragmatic reasons to take them in: children are easier to transport and assimilate, and it would be wasteful to let potentially valuable lives [for](/rebirth) [labor](/holy-mines) perish.
    -   The intent is likely to terrorize and weaken [United Cities](/united-cities) through asserting dominance and retribution by executing their noble and destroying infrastructure in the region. By doing so they would create a suitable buffer state and push back the frontlines to a comfortable distance from their [core region](/okrans-valley), with enough patrol presence in this region to serve as deterrence.
-   [History of Kenshi](/history-of-kenshi) suggests that this place was a prosperous region with many farms and towns for trading.
    -   Exactly how densely populated it was is unclear, as the [farms](/ravaged-hamlet) here resemble more of scattered dots than well-developed farming communities. And the defenses were easily overwhelmed by [The Holy Nation's](/the-holy-nation) forces, as hinted by the city falling in a single day.
-   The conflict intensity here could be presumed to be fairly limited, given that a combination of the various squads of [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers), [Slaves](/slaves), [Cannibals](/cannibals) and other squads easily make up just as much of a presence as either [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) or [United Cities](/united-cities). Suggesting that this place is in a state of limited, frozen conflict rather than a full-scale war.
    -   This is further supported by the dialogue of [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), as he doesn't see UC as a significant threat or problem relative to others<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
        -   HN can be considered to have already decisively won the engagement and achieved their objectives considering that they clearly dominated this region by successfully razing the entire region. Doing another large military campaign to wipe out the Empire forces in this area when UC's control in this region is in an obviously weakened state would likely be even easier.
        -   HN's army can be considered to be quite reserved in their actions, as they only [takeover Drin](/drin/the-holy-nation) if _both_ [Inaba](/lord-inaba) and [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) are dead which [scatters off the empire forces](/drin/destroyed). Though [Inaba's](/lord-inaba) death alone shifts 4~5x the military patrol presence in HN's advantage, making overwhelming UC forces a very easy endeavor.
        -   There are clearly large visible patrol groups in the neighboring [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), including the elite [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) squads which HN doesn't send to this front, even though it could quite easily swing the dominance in this region to HN's favor should they just care enough to send them to frontline combat.
        -   Phoenix never mentions about taking Tengu or any of his nobles out to weaken UC, unlike taking out [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) or [Moll](/moll) for instance.
        -   The various pieces of evidence would suggest that they don't care that much about taking over this region or annihilating United Cities, and are merely just sending or stationing enough forces at the borders/frontiers for deterrence.
    -   This is further supported by the dialogue of [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Koin](/koin), who see [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) as a viable buffer between them and the various [Shek](/shek) raiders (i.e. the likes of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) and [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen)) from pillaging their nation<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
        -   Though they clearly care about Bast to a bigger extent than HN as they are still intent on capturing Okran's Shield and pushing HN back<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, rather than almost ignoring completely like [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) does.
