The **Chalice Of Fire** is a [unique item](/unique-items) given by [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) to the player faction for showing considerable value to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).

You receive the Chalice of Fire (as well as a [Holy Seal](/holy-seal) after more dialogue) from the Holy Lord Phoenix as soon as your faction relation with The Holy Nation reaches 75.

From the icon, it looks just like a more polished and shinier version of the luxury good called [Pearl Cup](/pearl-cup).
