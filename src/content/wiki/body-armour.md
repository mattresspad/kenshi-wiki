**Body Armour** includes any piece of equipment of any armour class that is meant to cover the torso in the chest armour slot.

Characters of all humanoid [races](/races) can equip body armour in the armour slot.

For damage and resistance calculations, see [Damage](/damage) and [Resistances](/resistances).

The following values assume \[Standard\] quality.

## No Armour Coverage

These armors provide no protection. Gi provides a 2 [Martial Arts](/martial-arts) bonus while Ninja Gi provides a 1.10x [Stealth](/stealth) bonus. Hive Prisoner Shackles are shackles meant for [Hives](/hive), [Skeletons](/skeleton), or characters without both legs, as the normal shackles cannot be equipped on them.

## Clothing

These armours offer very little protection. They are commonly found being worn by citizens, slaves, or bandits. [Monk Robes](/monk-robe) and [Dyed Robes](/dyed-robes) however, can also be found on [Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) priests and [United Cities](/united-cities) nobles respectively.
