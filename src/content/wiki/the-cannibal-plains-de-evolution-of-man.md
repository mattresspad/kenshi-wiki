**The Cannibal Plains: De-evolution Of Man?** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items). This item is 'unique' to Science HQ in [World's End](/worlds-end). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in [The Great Library](/the-great-library) in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch).

This book was written by the current leader of the [Machinists](/machinists), [Finch](/finch). This book speculates about [Cannibals](/cannibals) in the nearby [Cannibal Plains](/cannibal-plains).
