The **Rag Loincloth (Dyed)** is a piece of [Clothing](/clothing) equipped in the [Legwear](/legwear) slot. This is a [colorable](/colour-scheme) variation of [Rag Loincloth](/rag-loincloth). There is a matching shirt for this "rag set" called the [Dyed Rag Shirt](/dyed-rag-shirt).

It is worn by Skeletons, perhaps a leftover of previous uniforms.

This armour has inventory dimensions of **4x4**.

## Appearance

The hips and legs are mostly covered with torn cloth and rags.
