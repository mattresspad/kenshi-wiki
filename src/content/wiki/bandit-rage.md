**Bandit Rage** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger in response to stabbing the [Dust Boss](/dust-boss) War Leader during dialog in the [Bandit Demands](/bandit-demands) event.

Once the conditions have been fulfilled, the [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits) will send an assault to the [player's base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost). This assault will consist of 3-6 squads, each made up of 0-4 [Dust Bandit Bowmen](/dust-bandit-bowman), 5-10 [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandit), led by a [Dust Boss](/dust-boss).

Once the assault arrives the War Leader will give a short speech before ordering the attack to begin, attacking gates and characters, and liberating captives. If the attackers are victorious, the [Bandit Demands](/bandit-demands) event will trigger in 40-50 hours.
