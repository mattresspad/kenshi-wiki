**Yayoi** is a member of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas).

She is intended to be the leader of an Ally Reinforcements [Event](/events), arriving at the player's Outpost with 4-10 [Flotsam Ninja](/flotsam-ninja) characters to protect the player from base assaults by [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), including [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god). However, she does not actually spawn in game.

Her event is set to not trigger if [Moll](/moll) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states) or if the player is allied with the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).

She has a c.30,000 Bounty from [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). They wear \[Specialist\] armour (5% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths). She has a Brave [Personality](/personality) and no unique dialogue aside from announcement dialogue when she arrives at the player's Outpost.
