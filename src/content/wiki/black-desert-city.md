**Black Desert City** is a town run by [Skeletons](/skeleton) in the middle of the [Deadlands](/deadlands) region. The town is populated by [Skeleton Civilians](/skeleton-civilian) and [Skeleton Residents](/skeleton-resident). As there are no human residents, the town lacks a food supply as is noted by the town’s bartender, however edible food can be found in containers within the other buildings. There are both regular beds and skeleton repair beds on the second floor of the town's bar. Black Desert City was created after the fall of the [Second Empire](/second-empire)_\[citation needed\]_. [Sadneil](/sadneil) can be recruited in this location.

Fighting with the locals is not advised. Like in other cities without a police station, you will be carried outside after being knocked unconscious. Due to the region's [Acid Rain](/weather-effects), this can be dangerous for any characters which are not Hivers or Skeletons.

At the edge of the city there is a bridge which connects it to the hill where the [Scraphouse](/scraphouse) is located.

## Trivia/Tips

-   Each Skeleton Storage appears to be filled with various containers of hi-tech junk loot that may include [Power Cores](/power-core) and [CPU Units](/cpu-unit). They also house a number of [Skeleton Repair Beds](/skeleton-repair-bed) and have a [Water Tank](/water-tank) on the roof.
-   You can fit a Research Bench II (or higher) on the top floor of the purchasable Metal Wallhouse. As there is a [recruitable skeleton](/sadneil) in the bar they could be used to research any tech you may want without fear of being attacked.
-   The Skeleton Residents (not Civilians) in the town have a very high (20) randomise value on their stats. Due to this they can spawn with negative Robotics levels leading to repairing attempts actually hurting themselves instead.
