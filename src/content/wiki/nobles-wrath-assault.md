**Noble's Wrath Assault** is an [Event](/events) that can occur if the player lives within 9,000 meters of a [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) owned location, and has killed or captured one or more of the following [Nobles](/noble-circle), [Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Kana](/lady-kana), [Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Ohta](/lord-ohta), [Sanda](/lady-sanda), [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi), or [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga). It however cannot trigger if all of the following nobles have been killed or captured, [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), [Inaba](/lord-inaba), [Nagata](/lord-nagata), [Sanda](/lady-sanda), [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi), and [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga).

If the conditions are fulfilled, the event has a 50% chance to trigger each day if the player has a base within 2,500 meters, this chance scales down with distance until it reaches a 0% chance at 9,000 meters. The event can only trigger once per noble taken down, up to a maximum of 7 times total. The event is normally triggered by the [United Cities](/united-cities), but if [Kana](/lady-kana) is the trigger for it, the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) will instead trigger the event.

Once triggered, either the [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) will launch an assault. This assault will consist of 2 squads (set to have up to 20 individuals),

-   The first led by [Eyegore](/eyegore), alongside 10 [Samurai](/samurai), 1 [Samurai Scout](/samurai-scout), 1 [Samurai Captain](/samurai-captain), 3 [Samurai Heavies](/samurai-heavy), and 2 [Samurai Sergeants](/samurai-sergeant).

The remaining squad can be one of two variants. With the first being the most common one

-   1 [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant), accompanied by 1-3 [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), 10-15 [Samurai](/samurai), and 8-20 [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript).
-   1 [Samurai Sergeant](/samurai-sergeant), accompanied by 1-3 [Samurai Scouts](/samurai-scout), 5-8 [Samurai](/samurai), and 1-4 [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript).

Once the assault arrives, [Eyegore](/eyegore) and one of his [Samurai](/samurai) will have a short speech about your crimes, then the attack will begin. If you have killed one of the nobles, and not just captured them, [Eyegore](/eyegore) will interrupt the speech and force the attack early. If victorious, the assault's forces should leave your base shortly after. If defeated, an [Elite Hunter Assault](/elite-hunter-assault) event will trigger in 24-72 hours.
