**Search Lights** is [Electrics Tech](/electrics-tech) which unlocks the ability to construct [Searchlights](/searchlight) on the sides of walls and gates.

Searchlights provide light to larger areas than [Torch Posts](/torch-post) and [Electrical Torch Posts](/electrical-torch-post). They can almost cover an entire farm, which can greatly increase farm yields by decreasing the 'working in darkness' stat drain.
