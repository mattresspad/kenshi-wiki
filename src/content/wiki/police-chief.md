**Police Chiefs** are the security chiefs of the [United Cities](/united-cities), [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants), and the [Free Traders](/free-traders). They can be easily found inside of [Police Stations](/police-station), and can be asked about bounty notices, offering bails, and reselling confiscated items.

Despite the loosening of the United Cities' grip on the southern territories after certain [World States](/world-states) are achieved, they still seem to assume the role of law enforcement in the cities. This may be due to the lack of [Rebel Swordsmen](/rebel-swordsmen) presence in the south, leaving security to be handled by the Police and [adventurers](/tech-hunter-gate-guard).

If you show bounties such as [Tinfist](/tinfist), [Bo](/bo), [Jaegar](/jaegar), [Grey](/grey), [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) to them, the Police Chief will not allow you to take them away without a fight. This is not the case with other, less-substantial, bounties.

Most of their dialogue is locked once the player has allied with the [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) (with the exception of the [King Gurgler](/king-gurgler) bounty).

They wear \[Standard\] armour (5% chance of \[High\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
