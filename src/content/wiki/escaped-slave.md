_Not to be confused with [Slave Escapee](/slave-escapee)._

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>It took too much blood and sweat to escape the masters...<p>I won't go back to the slave camp that easy!</p></blockquote></figure>

**Escaped Slave** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They escaped slavery and have a c.2,000 [bounty](/bounty) from the [United Cities](/united-cities). If they are found in a squad alone, they can be talked to and potentially convinced to join the player faction.

If the player is an escaped slave themselves, the Escapee will be surprised and quickly offer to join.

They can be found patrolling as a member of the [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) faction in [Heng](/heng-zone), [Howler Maze](/howler-maze), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), and [The Hook](/the-hook). They might also be found squatting in a [overridden](/town-overrides) version of [Heft](/heft), [Manhunter Base](/manhunter-base), or [Stoat](/stoat) as well as [Mourn](/mourn).

They wear \[Prototype\] armour (5% chance of \[Shoddy\]) and have weapons manufactured by Unknown.
