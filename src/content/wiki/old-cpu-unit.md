**Old** **CPU Unit** is an [item](/items) which can only be found in a few locations. It serves no function outside of being worth a small amount of money.

Found on:

-   [Hydraulic Knight](/hydraulic-knight)
-   [Old Empire Police Captain](/old-empire-police-captain)
-   [Skin Bandit](/skin-bandit)
-   [Legion Thrall](/legion-thrall)

In every case, removing this item will result in the immediate death of the skeleton which was holding it. Aspiring ninja/assassin can clear up [Skinhouse HQ](/skinhouse-hq) from patrolling [skin bandits](/skin-bandits) by [incapacitating](/assassination) them, killing them by removing their CPUs and then kidnap [their leader](/savant) from his bed, all without a single skirmish.

It is possible to remove this item without killing the owner by placing them in a [Prisoner Cage](/prisoner-cage) before retrieving the CPU.
