**The Crater** is a Zone in the southwestern corner of the map. The place is filled with [Beak Things](/beak-thing) feasting upon packs of [Swamp Raptors](/swamp-raptor). It is a very hostile environment and most of the ruins are empty.

Because of shape of the crater, with high borders on all sides, it can be difficult to pathfind to enter and exit this Zone.

## Inhabitants

There are no [Major Towns](/major-towns) or [Minor Outposts](/minor-outposts) in The Crater. This zone does not spawn Camps.

## Tips

There are a lot of [Beak Things](/beak-thing) in this zone, so if you plan to venture here, be sure to either have characters with high [Athletics](/athletics) skills or combat skills, and if needing a damage boost, bring weapons with high Beak Thing damage such as [Falling Suns](/falling-sun) or [Polearms](/polearms).

## Trivia

-   Given the location of this zone overlapping with their neighboring zone, and the Skeleton's dialogue when entering this zone which suggests that this wasn't the appearance back then, this may formerly resemble something similar to [The Grid](/the-grid) before a great explosion or explosion-like impact in the center caused it to become a "crater".
    -   A former description of [Cat-Lon's CPU](/cpu-of-cat-lon) directly states this as a fact with the following line "Science and archaeology flourished in the empire until an explosion in the Grid caused a famine.". However, given the fact that the description is replaced, it can no longer be used to support this theory, and it becomes unclear whether such a theory is still valid or not.
-   Of the Three labs within The Crater, the lab to the south appears to have been hit by a boulder from the blast, and in addition to its top floors being destroyed, its foundation is also damaged from the impact. The lab in the center is seated on the lowest ground of the three labs with a strip of land covering its foundations from the center of the blast. All that's left of the center lab are the foundations, and the walls of the ground floor, indicating that the distribution of force on the building was unbalanced, and ripped the building off of its foundation. The third lab to the north is the only intact lab in the crater. It's seated on higher ground, and leans away from the explosion. This suggests that the force of the explosion was evenly enough distributed throughout the structure to only cause a significant lean. All of this would indicate that all three of these labs were constructed before The Crater explosion.

-   This zone is the only area close to [Arach](/arach) that has no [Skin Spiders](/skin-spider) spawns, suggesting the elevation from the circle blocked out spiders from migrating into the region.
    -   Similarly the [Band of Bones](/band-of-bones) are present in the [Spider Plains](/spider-plains) above as well as [Shun](/shun) and [The Hook](/the-hook) below, suggesting they ignore this region during travels.
