**Prisoner** is someone serving time in [Tengu's Vault](/tengus-vault). They can be [rescued](/slave-recruits) for a possibility for them to join the [Player's faction](/nameless).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
