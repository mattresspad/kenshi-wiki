**Suicide Note** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). This item is 'unique' to Flotsam HQ in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village). However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in the inventories of [Holy Citizens](/holy-citizen).

This letter was written by a [Holy Sentinel](/holy-sentinel). The crimes written about in this letter are similar to those referenced in [Letter From A Freedom Fighter](/letter-from-a-freedom-fighter).
