**Soldierbots**, not to be confused with [Log-heads](/skeleton-log-head-mkii), are a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. They're a specific model of [Skeleton](/skeleton). The Soldierbot is the only guaranteed Skeleton _subrace_ you can recruit (only [one character](/agnu)). They have superior hit chances, (2) vision range multiplier as well as (3) heal rate when compared to a [Skeleton](/skeleton-race) who has a lower (1.25) vision range multiplier and (2) heal rate.

### Weather Immunity

Characters of this race are immune to all types of [Weather Effects](/weather-effects).

  

### Equipment Slots

_Characters of this race can wear any type of equipment in these slots._

-   [Body Armour](/body-armour)

_Characters of this race cannot wear any equipment in these slots._

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal._

-   [Perception](/perception) (+20%)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal._

-   [Dodge](/dodge) (-20%)
-   [Field Medic](/field-medic) (-20%)
-   [Martial Arts](/martial-arts) (-20%) **(Does not actually work as of 1.0.68, will only impact the stats that they spawn with originally)**
-   [Stealth](/stealth) (-20%)
-   [Thievery](/thievery) (-20%)

## Trivia

-   The Soldierbot uses the Log-Head MKII head model, but with a body matching most MKI Skeleton sub-races.
-   It is possible that the [Second Empire](/second-empire) was unable to produce Soldierbots, as the [Hydraulic Knights](/hydraulic-knight), [Police](/old-empire-police-captain), and [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion) are all made up of the lower-quality [Log-Head MKII](/skeleton-log-head-mkii) units - the 'II' may indicate a Second Empire design.
