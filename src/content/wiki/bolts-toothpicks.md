**Bolts \[Toothpicks\]** are ammunition used with [Crossbows](/crossbow-class).

These bolts can be made on an [Arrow Making Bench](/arrow-making-bench) after researching [Crossbow Bolts](/crossbow-bolts-tech). They can be stored in a [Boltbox](/boltbox).

## Used with

-   [Junkbow](/junkbow)
-   [Tooth Pick](/tooth-pick)
