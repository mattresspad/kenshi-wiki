The **United Cities** (A.K.A. the "**Empire**" or the "**Desert Empire**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>") is an expansionist, feudal slaver society. It is the largest faction by land area and has towns of drastically varying population sizes dispersed throughout the [continent](/world-of-kenshi), including [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Heng](/heng-zone), [The Hook](/the-hook), [Bonefields](/bonefields), and the [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

Its northern population is heavily concentrated on the towns of [Stoat](/stoat)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Heng](/heng),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [Heft](/heft),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> while its southern population is concentrated on [Drifter's Last](/drifters-last).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Ruled by [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [his inner circle](/noble-circle), the Empire is plagued with endemic corruption, and monetary worth is the prime measure of a person in Empire culture. Those suffering extreme poverty are considered to be potentially dangerous criminal elements and are often enslaved as a result.

[Slavery](/slavery) and [serfdom](/empire-peasants) are important pillars of the Empire, filled up by the bottom of Empire society such as the [vagrants](/starving-vagrant) and other types of bandits, providing food and material. Another pillar is the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild). The guild brings their cities great wealth and prosperity. The Empire possesses advanced technology, high-quality equipment, and valuable luxuries imported from distant lands. It considers [anti-slavery](/anti-slavers) ideology to be a form of terrorism.

The UC is engaged in an ongoing conflict with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), with the heaviest fighting taking place at the border of [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) - [Bast](/bast-zone). The UC suffered a great defeat at [Bast](/bast-zone) where they were pushed back by HN forces and lost the hold on the region in a single day.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Since the razing of [Bast](/bast), the conflict has frozen, turning into a stalemate.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

The drug [Hashish](/hashish) is highly illegal, and the samurai guards are obsessed with purging it from their streets to such an extent that even the material used to produce it, [Hemp](/hemp), is also outlawed.

The United Cities does not tolerate Hemp and Hemp-related goods and production facilities. Owning any building such as [Biofuel Distillery (Hemp)](/biofuel-distillery-hemp), [Hemp Farm](/hemp-farm), [Hemp Processor](/hemp-processor) or a [Hydroponic Hemp](/hydroponic-hemp) will result in a [Drug Bust](/drug-bust) raid against the player if this building is discovered by the authorities. It is likewise illegal to possess or import [Bloodrum](/bloodrum), [Hashish](/hashish) or [Hemp](/hemp) into the Empire, despite evidence of use and possession of said substances by the privileged Nobles. "Rules for thee, not for me" is very much what the Empire stands on.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

## History

[History of Kenshi](/history-of-kenshi) puts the founding of United Cities and [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) between 100 ~ 1000 years ago, sometime after [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) was founded, and is rumored to come from the ashes of a former empire such as the [Second Empire](/second-empire).

The United Cities and the Traders Guild seized power and began to institute new laws regarding slavery, where people would be free of the tyranny of the former empire, and only the criminals would be relegated forcefully to the mines and farms. However, much like the Second Empire in the past, the term of 'criminal' was found to be more and more loosely applied.

A few decades ago, the southern plains suffered a significant drought, which in turn caused a severe famine that nearly brought the United Cities to its collective knees. Due to the famine, the southern cities were forced to rely on the Traders' Guild to bring them the supplies they needed to survive. Unfortunately, [Shek](/shek) raiders and other bandits blockaded the routes and took these supplies for themselves. What little did get through was instantly set upon by the nobles in a bidding war, and the lower classes were driven to starvation and [rebellion](/the-red-rebellion). The resultant fighting claimed many lives, including many nobles and even [Emperor Anzai](/emperor-anzai) himself, but the rebels were eventually put down and survivors enslaved. This marked the selection of a new Emperor from the [Noble's Circle](/noble-circle), [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu), a cruel and impulsive man known for his lack of common sense. The Red Rebellion had also made much of the farming life unsustainable inside United Cities.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is unknown whether the current reliance on slavery was the norm before the Red Rebellion, however existence of the [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) implies that the reliance on slavery has increased in recent times.

Sometime after [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) was chosen, simmering tensions between [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and United Cities have broken out into open conflict at border regions in recent years, [Bast](/bast-zone), once a prosperous and fertile area, filled with many farmlands and towns for trading, was razed to the ground in a border conflict with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation). The land has since been a warzone where both the Holy Nation and United cities vie endlessly in a stalemate for at least 15 years, trampling underfoot any life that may have once thrived here (with the [neighboring border](/skimsands) used as a relatively low-intensity<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> buffer zone).

## Society

The Empire is ruled by the [Noble Circle](/noble-circle), a cabal of extremely narcissistic, cruel, and entitled lords and ladies. They are known for [hunting peasants for sport](/noble-hunter), making outlandish requests to their guards (such as ordering them to train a pet [Leviathan](/leviathan)), and generally lacking concern for the lives of anyone beneath them.

The nobility is mostly comprised of [Humans](/human) (slightly more [Scorchlanders](/scorchlander)) and [Sheks](/shek), while [Hivers](/hive) are disproportionately more likely to be peasants.

Hivers and Sheks are noted to increase in population and significantly spike criminality levels in UC [newspapers](/united-weekly-series), and their presence have inspired the likes of [City Heroes](/united-heroes-league) who will target non-humans for assaults (although their acts of violence puts them at odds with the law). [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants) are often found in the Empire's locations and areas.

## Military

The United Cities has a considerable size of military forces, charged with securing their borders from [Cannibals](/cannibals)<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Reavers](/reavers)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>/[Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders)<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The Empire army is capable of stationing forces at [Bast](/bast-zone) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) against [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Samurai](/samurai) are the leading military and [police force](/samurai-police) of the United Cities. [General Eyegore](/eyegore) is among the most skilled warriors inside the empire.

Alongside the professional [Samurai](/samurai), the United Cities has been observed to use [Conscripts](/samurai-conscript) to bolster their ranks, especially around the warzone of [Bast](/bast-zone). [Lord Inaba](/lord-inaba) of [Stoat](/stoat) is responsible for managing conscription.

The command of the military is centralized around the nobility, which is split between the [Emperor's authority](/emperor-tengu)<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> whose forces are supplied by the slave camps of [Slave Farm](/slave-farm), [Stone Camp](/stone-camp) and [South Stone Camp](/south-stone-camp)<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Alongside each of the nobles that manage their own respective influence zones. These include:

-   [Sanda](/lady-sanda) to [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert)<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## Dispositions

United Cities, being a major faction, has a long list of relations with factions both inside and outside their empire.

### [Traders Guild](/traders-guild)

Headed by [Longen](/longen), they are a large and entrenched organization forming the backbone of UC's economy<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that runs the supply chain to all UC towns which feeds the vast majority of townsfolk<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The guild also maintains private military forces comprised of [Samurai](/samurai) and various [other](/traders-guard) [guards](/caravan-guard), and is apparently allowed to engage in limited [conscription](/samurai-conscript) as well.

### [Slave Traders](/slave-traders)

Underlings of Traders Guild that engage in the practice of [slavery](/slavery) to run the economy. They engage in the capturing, trading, transferring and working of slaves as means to keep their industrial cogs working, typically capturing the incompetent and poor such as the likes of [Starving Vagrant](/starving-vagrant), [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers) and [Escaped Slaves](/escaped-slave).

They have close ties to the likes of [Manhunters](/manhunters) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) who help capture and re-capture slaves.

### [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)

Headed by [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), they are a [major faction](/factions) that has fought with the United Cities at [Bast](/bast-zone) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) ever since the borders were pushed back from [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley)<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and destroyed the region in the process (possibly causing the ire of some of the peasant populace).<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

United Cities intends to capture/kill [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) to send in forces to take their defensive position [Okran's Shield](/okrans-shield),<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and then [conquer their towns](/bad-teeth/united-cities), [take over their mines](/holy-mines/slave-traders), and [enslave some of their people](/holy-farm/slave-traders) when viable.

### [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)

Headed by [Esata the Stone Golem](/esata-the-stone-golem). The United Cities have a very poor opinion of them, seeing them as barbarians<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which are kept at bay from pillaging them thanks to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> UC has a bounty on [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) for her capture and are content for [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) to wipe out the [Sheks](/kamikaze) for them.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

They are noted to start pillaging the desert once the military of The Holy Nation is sufficiently disorganized<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and are hinted to engage in a power struggle with another<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Rebel Farmers](/rebel-farmers)

Headed by [Boss Simion](/boss-simion), the Rebel Farmers are composed of the most impoverished outcast peasants and farmers who have been pushed out of towns and farms as a result of being unable to pay rent and taxes<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and resorted to banditry and insurgency across the entire UC sector,<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> typically engaging in raids on caravans and travelers. Considered to be an annoyance to UC authorities<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Vagrants](/vagrants)

Beggars, destitutes and thugs from the bottom of society originating from the populated city of [Heng](/heng)<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, their [characters](/starving-vagrant) engage in banditry in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and [Heng](/heng-zone).

### [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)

Headed by [Tinfist](/tinfist), the Anti-Slavers are a group of elite ninja fighters who engage in the disruption and destruction of slavery and it's economy. Considered to be terrorists and freedom-preaching loonies<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that unleashes famine and banditry to cause death and destruction across the Empire<sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

The effectiveness of their raids have been blocked off by the defensive perimeters of [Longen](/longen)<sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Kana](/lady-kana)<sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_47-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-47"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Ruben](/slave-master-ruben)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_47-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-47"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, [Grande](/slave-master-grande)<sup id="cite_ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

### [Western Hive](/western-hive)

A faction made up of Hivers located north in [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), they are the source for much of the Hiver peasants inside United CIties.

### [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants)

[Hiver](/hive)\-dominated<sup id="cite_ref-49" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-49"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> immigrant peasants and serfs that make up the lower working class of the Empire whose presence is mentioned to have significantly sparked crime-rates and poverty.<sup id="cite_ref-50" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-50"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They typically live in [Vagrant Camps](/vagrant-camp) or UC towns dependent on whether or not they reach the legal threshold.

### [United Heroes League](/united-heroes-league)

[Greenlander](/greenlander) xenophobes who see non-[Humans](/human) as problematic elements of their society that need to be ridden of, likely inspired in part by [United Weeklies](/united-weekly-series).

They are hired hitmen of [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) in cases of [Thug Attack](/thug-attack).

### [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas)

Headed by [Sand Ninja Oni](/sand-ninja-oni), they are groups of bandits and ninjas that engage in banditry across the desert.

### [Reavers](/reavers)

A bandit faction headed by [Valamon](/valamon), they engage in the banditry and conscription of various captured individuals into their rank. Blocked off by [Lady Tsugi's](/lady-tsugi) defenses<sup id="cite_ref-51" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-51"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

## Tips

-   Allying with either United Cities or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) early on can be beneficial because:
    -   Allying with either will prevent [Manhunters](/manhunters), [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) and [Slave Raids](/slave-raid) assaulting you.
    -   Allying with United Cities will prevent [Tax Man Visits](/tax-man-visit) and [Tax Collector](/tax-collector) events.
    -   Allying with [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) will prevent [Traders Guild Visits](/traders-guild-visit).
-   The alliance with United Cities and Traders Guild is accounted separately:
    -   Allying with United Cities requires one to have an outpost with at least level 2 (Watchtower on the map) for c.10,000 c fee, or c.100,000 otherwise.
    -   Allying with Traders Guild requires one to pay c.100,000 or c.75,000 fee with an outpost of any level.

## Trivia

-   Settling within or near United Cities territory will result in a [Tax Collector](/tax-collector) coming by weekly. They will demand c.3,000 from you. If you lack funds, the tax collector will insult you for being poor, then remind you that being poor is a crime. You have three days before they return - if you don't cough up enough cash they will attack.
    -   You can avoid paying taxes entirely if you are allied with them. To get to 50 relations, you would have to pay c.100,000, or hand in 25 [bounties](/bounty). This may be a more expensive option, but it will safeguard you from ever being caught at a bad time. Another thing is that if you are allied with the Empire, they will send [Reinforcements](/united-cities-reinforcements) to your outpost whenever you are attacked.
-   It's likely that interracial marriages between Greenlanders and Scorchlanders are fairly common with in UC, given the almost equal ratio of their presence in UC alongside the fact that several unique characters (like [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu)) are "light-skinned" Scorchlanders.
-   It's likely that [Greenlanders](/greenlander) are much more populated while [Hive Princes](/hive-prince) and [Sheks](/shek) are much rarer in the entirety of UC as suggested by the racial proportion of Starving Vagrants, Rebel Farmers, Rebel Swordsmen, Slave Hunters, Slave Traders, United Heroes League.
-   Some level of trading between The Holy Nation and Traders Guild/United Cities can be presumed because:
    -   [Cooked Vegetables](/cooked-vegetables) inside Noble's inventories, [Foodcube's](/foodcube) ingredient in [Empire Bars](/empire-bar), [Samurai's food](/samurai) and ["Tengu's Seal"](/mystery-sack) are [Greenfruits](/greenfruit) that are only farmed in large quantities in HN lands.

    -   [Cactus Rum's](/cactus-rum) description could be interpreted as either [grasslands](/okrans-pride) or [swamplands](/the-swamp). While the drink itself is never sold in HN vendors, the [Bloodrum](/bloodrum) (that includes [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum)) is.
