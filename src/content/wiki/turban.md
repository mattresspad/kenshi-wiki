The **Turban** is a Light Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot. It can be bought from Hat Vendors.

It is equipped by [Holy Farm Leaders](/holy-farm-leader), [Holy Farmers](/holy-farmer), [Slaver Guards](/slaver-guard), and [Slaver Heavy Guards](/slaver-heavy-guard). It can be randomly equipped by [Bar Fishermen](/bar-fisherman), [Bar Thugs](/bar-thugs), [Drifters](/drifter), [Goat Men](/goat-man), [Outlaws](/outlaw), [Slavers](/slaver), [Slaver Caravan Bosses](/slaver-caravan-boss), [Slaver Caravan Guards](/slaver-caravan-guard), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard), [Tech Hunter Guards](/tech-hunter-guard), and [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

The Turban is made from a single piece of cloth and wrapped around the players head so that some hangs to cover the cheeks and neck of the character.
