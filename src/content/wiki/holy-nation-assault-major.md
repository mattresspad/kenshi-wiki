**Holy Nation Assault (Major)**, simply referred to as 'Holy Nation Assault' in-game, is an [event](/events) that can occur if the player has defeated the [Holy Assault](/holy-assault), [Holy Nation Assault (Minor)](/holy-nation-assault-minor), or **Holy Nation Assault (Major)** events. It is closely related to, but not to be confused with, two other events of the same name, [Holy Nation Assault (Minor)](/holy-nation-assault-minor), and [Holy Nation Assault (Inquisitor)](/holy-nation-assault-inquisitor).

The event cannot trigger if the [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta), and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are all killed or captured.

If the requirements above are met, [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) will send an assault to the player's outpost in

-   100-250 hours (~7.3 days) if it was triggered off of a [Holy Assault](/holy-assault) event
-   72-110 hours (~3.8 days) if it was trigger off of a [Holy Nation Assault (Minor)](/holy-nation-assault-minor) event,
-   72-200 hours (~5.7 days) hours if it was triggered off of a **Holy Nation Assault (Major)** event.

The assault will consist of 10-12 [Paladins](/paladin), 1-2 [High Paladins](/high-paladin), and 4-9 [Holy Servants](/holy-servant) being led by an [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) (set number force of 15).

After arriving at the outpost, the War Leader will order his men to attack the outpost and the player characters inside it.

If the raid is victorious, they will occupy the player's base, patrolling around it, using the furniture, freeing captives, and imprisoning your characters.

If the raid is defeated:

-   There is a 30% chance to trigger another **Holy Nation Assault (Major)** event in 72-200 hours (~5.7 days)
-   and a 20% or 40% (depending on [Valtena's](/high-inquisitor-valtena) status, the latter if he is killed/captured) to trigger a [Holy Nation Assault (Inquisitor)](/holy-nation-assault-inquisitor) event in 72-200 hours (~5.7 days).
