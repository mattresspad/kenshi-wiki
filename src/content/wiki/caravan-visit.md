**Caravan Visit** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the player has an outpost within 11,000 meters of [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge). It cannot trigger if the player is an enemy of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

When the conditions have been fulfilled, the event has a 20% chance to trigger each day if the [player's base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) is within 6,000 meters of [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge), this chance scales down with distance until it hits 0% chance at 11,000 meters.

Once triggered, the [Trader's Guild](/traders-guild) will send a caravan to the player's base, consisting of 7-11 [Caravan Guards](/caravan-guard) and 1-3 [Pack Beasts](/pack-beast), led by a [Caravan Trader Boss](/caravan-trader-boss).

When the caravan arrives, the leader will shout out asking the player to come trade. If the player does so, they will be met with a selection from the items below. Note however, the caravan lacks any money to buy items from the player, unless the player has already purchased something from them.
