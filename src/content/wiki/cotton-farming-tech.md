**Cotton Farming** is [Farming Tech](/farming-tech) which allows the player to construct [Cotton](/cotton) farms.

This technology is good for players who have an outpost in a Zone which has a Green [Environment](/environment). Cotton is a legal alternative to [Hemp](/hemp), and is used in the creation of [Fabrics](/fabrics).
