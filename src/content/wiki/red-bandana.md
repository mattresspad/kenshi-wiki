The **Red Bandana** is a type of [Headgear](/headgear) belonging to the [Light Armour Class](/light-armour). The Red Bandana has two other variants: the [Bandana](/bandana) and the [Black Bandana](/black-bandana).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

It is only found equipped on the [Kamikaze](/kamikaze) character.

## Appearance

The Red Bandana is a red cloth tied around the character's face like a headband. It leaves the majority of the face and head exposed.
