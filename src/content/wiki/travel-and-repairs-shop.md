**Travel and Repairs Shop** is a [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters) shop which sells travel items and [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs). This location also sells many [Maps](/maps). The second floor of this building will contain [Skeleton Repair Beds](/skeleton-repair-bed). The shop inventory is similar to the [Travel Gear](/travel-gear) vendor, additionally selling [Robot Limbs](/robot-limbs), but no [Bolts](/crossbows#crossbow-ammunition-types).

### Locations

-   [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon)
-   [World's End](/worlds-end)

Depending on certain [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in:

-   [Blister Hill](/blister-hill)

### Residents

-   [Tech Hunter Shopkeeper](/tech-hunter-shopkeeper)
-   [Mercenary](/mercenary) (3)

This shop is capable of spawning [Artifacts](/artifacts), as such it may spawn items not in the list. These items, however, will not respawn when the shop restocks. Towns marked with an asterisk will have the shop spawn artifacts.
