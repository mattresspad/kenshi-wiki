**Corpse Disposal** is [Defense Tech](/defense-tech) which unlocks the ability to construct a [Corpse Furnace](/corpse-furnace).

Certain [fauna](/fauna), such as [river raptors](/river-raptor), consume corpses. If players do not wish to attract these animals, they should use a corpse furnace to incinerate dead bodies collected near their outpost.
