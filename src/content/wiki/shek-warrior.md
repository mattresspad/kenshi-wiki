**Shek Warriors** are warriors of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). They are the most numerous members of the Kingdom, being found as guards and residents of shops and towns, as well as being led by their stronger brethren - the [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) - into battle.

If a player's base encroaches on Shek territory, these warriors will lead raids demanding tribute in the form of food.

If [Esata the Stone Golem](/stone-golem) is [killed or imprisoned](/world-states) then the Shek Warriors will swear allegiance to [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) and will start to spawn as members of the [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen).

If the player is allied to [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation), they will randomly harrass the player and attack them at the gates.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Interactions

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>An outsider. How... exotic. I'm curious, outsider, do you know much of the Shek custom?</blockquote></figure>

Upon first time talking to a **Shek Warrior**, one may initiate dialogue to learn more about Shek customs and traditions.

Shek Warriors can lead the less important Shek Kingdom assaults against a [player's outpost](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) such as [Tribute Round](/tribute-round), [Return For Tribute](/return-for-tribute), [Return for Blood](/return-for-blood) and [Shek Challengers](/shek-challengers-shek-kingdom).
