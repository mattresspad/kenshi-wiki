**Slavemongers** are foot-soldiers of the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders), tasked with finding new slaves. They can be found roaming the [Border Zone](/border-zone) and many other places. If Slavemongers encounter unconscious characters, they will enslave them. If they find an escaped slave, they will attempt to capture them as well.

In the base game (and specifically in FCS), Slavemongers have their squad named as _"Slave Hunters------"_, with the dashes indicating that they may not have been intended to appear at all and is supposed to be a cut content.

Slavemongers will steal weapons from unconscious characters, including ones from the player's squad.

They are similar to [Manhunters](/manhunter) and [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunter), but rather than being middlemen, they are directly employed by the Slavers Guild. Unlike Manhunters and Slave Hunters, Slavemongers won't engage in opportunistic attacks on players to sell them into slavery. They will, however, still enslave them if they are knocked unconscious by a third party.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (10% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).

## Trivia

-   If you follow Slavemongers long enough, they will end up at [Rebirth](/rebirth).
    -   Often times the Slavemonger squad leader's AI will break once they reach Rebirth, resulting in their goal changing to Aimless, allowing the player to simply run away. (Provided you do not attempt to unlock your shackles in front of them)
-   If certain town NPCs are knocked down and slavemongers pass by, they will enslave them and may result in conflict with the town guards.
-   The dashes indicating cut content is similar to [Sanda's Noble Guard](/sandas-noble-guard).
