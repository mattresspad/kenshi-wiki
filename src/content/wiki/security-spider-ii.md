A smaller version of the standard [Security Spiders](/security-spider). They have far less HP and strength than regular Security Spiders. Their smaller size also lets them maneuver more easily in cluttered terrain or indoors around furniture. They can be found guarding the bar in [Black Desert City](/black-desert-city), as well as [Scraphouse](/scraphouse), the [Secret Drug Farm](/secret-drug-farm) in [Stobe's Gamble](/stobes-gamble), multiple armories and the [Armor King](/armor-king)'s shop in the [Drowned Ruins](/drowned-ruins) of [Wend](/wend).

## Damage Calculations

These values are for when striking a target with 0% damage resistance. Animals can have one of three qualities for their "weapon". (Moving their position in a squad, saving and then reloading can change to another quality of the weapon) The 23 in the cut calculation is from the minimum cut damage (13) combined with 10 piercing damage which Security Spiders II have.

**Variant 1**

**Cut =** 23 + 0.8774025 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0039) + Strength (0.0117)

**Variant 2**

**Cut =** 23 + 0.89232 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0052) + Strength (0.0156)

**Variant 3**

**Cut =** 23 + 0.9072375 (Martial Arts + Dex)

**Blunt =** Martial Arts (0.0065) + Strength (0.0195)
