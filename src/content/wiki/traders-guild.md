The **Traders Guild** is an allegedly neutral faction that deal primarily with commerce between towns. They're based in the [United Cities](/united-cities) and are integral to their economy. That said, they use their status to gain access and trade with many other factions throughout the world, even the enemies of the [United Cities](/united-cities) such as the [Holy Nation](/holy-nation).

They manage the slave camps throughout UC territories through [Noble Circle](/noble-circle).

Traders Guild will always start out neutral to the player's faction, except for the [Wandering Trader](/wandering-trader) start in which you start with 75 reputation with the Traders Guild. If you have a shop set to public near or on a trade route members of the guild will sometimes stop by to purchase particular items from you.

Many [Shop Guards](/shop-guard), [Caravan Guards](/caravan-guard), and even privately-hired [Samurai](/samurai) warriors are employed by the Traders Guild to protect their assets, with the latter notably being deployed around the Traders Guild headquarters in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge), a de-facto district of [Heng](/heng).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

### Player Relations

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Joining [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) by talking to [Grey](/grey) will grant -30 relations.
-   If you kill or Imprison [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) or [Longen](/longen), you will seriously damage this faction's view towards you.
-   Choosing pro-slavery dialogue with [Lady Kana](/lady-kana) or [Longen](/longen) will increase relationship with them, choosing anti-slavery dialogue will decrease relationship with them.

### Alliances

All bad deeds can be forgiven with cats, at least with the Traders Guild. In [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) you can find the Trader Guilds [Pacifier](/pacifiers), you can pay him to make the others in the guild to turn a blind eye to your past deeds.

You can also make an alliance with the Traders Guild, obviously it will cost you a fair bit of cats, c.100,000 to be exact. [Yamdu](/yamdu), located at the Trader Guild's HQ in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) will offer you this deal if you talk to him. If you have an Outpost, you can choose the option to reduce the price to c. 75,000.

This will affect your faction relationships with:

-   +75 [Slave Traders](/slave-traders)

-   +75 **Traders Guild**

-   \-30 [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers)

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Free trade, protection, slave exemption, and 'class', of course. It is the desert man's dream to one day become a member of the fine Traders Guild!</blockquote><figcaption>Yamdu, about the benefits of an alliance with Traders Guild.</figcaption></figure>

Specifically, Yamdu's benefits refer to [not being taxed by settling in their vicinity](/traders-guild-visit), not being targeted by [Manhunters](/manhunters), [Slave Hunters](/slave-hunters) and [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) (in cases of [Slave Raids](/slave-raid)), and having a privilege that others don't have.

## Trivia

-   **Traders Guild** and [Western Hive](/western-hive) are competitive enemies in trade.
    -   **Traders Guild** could be presumed to be the much larger economical industry, considering that they are 5x as frequent in [Heng](/heng-zone) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert) and 2.5x as frequent in [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).
-   [Yamdu](/yamdu) originally belongs to this faction.
-   While representing in-game currency [Cats](/cats), the flag of the Traders Guild resembles an image of the [Ban Liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banliang) coin.
-   Some level of trading between The Holy Nation and Traders Guild can be presumed because:
    -   [Cooked Vegetables](/cooked-vegetables) inside Noble's inventories, [Foodcube's](/foodcube) ingredient in [Empire Bars](/empire-bar), [Samurai's food](/samurai) and ["Tengu's Seal"](/mystery-sack) are [Greenfruits](/greenfruit) that are only farmed in large quantities in HN lands.
    -   [High Inquisitor Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) has a [Catun](/catun-scrapmaster-manufacturer)\-grade [Paladin's Cross](/paladins-cross).
    -   [Cactus Rum's](/cactus-rum) description could be interpreted as either [grasslands](/okrans-pride) or [swamplands](/the-swamp). While the drink itself is never sold in HN vendors, the [Bloodrum](/bloodrum) (that includes [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum)) is.
    -   **Traders Guild** [pacifier](/pacifiers) is located in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill).
    -   [Slavers](/slaver) inside [Holy Mines](/holy-mines) resemble those of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) (underlings of TG), presumably hired as extra help.
        -   [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Shem](/shem) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) close to or inside HN territories can have Slaver patrols once [Tinfist](/tinfist) is dead.
        -   [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) sharing the same (-100) opinion of [Mongrel](/mongrel-faction) as **The Holy Nation** does.
-   Some level of trading between Swampers and Traders Guild/United Cities can be presumed because:
    -   The presence of [Sake](/sake) (and their description) inside [Empire's Bars](/empire-bar).
    -   The presence of [Hashish](/hashish) and [Bloodrum](/bloodrum) inside various Noble's inventories.
    -   Yamdu remarks that Swampers are "very profitable" for Traders Guild.

-   Some level of trading between Shek Kingdom and Traders Guild/United Cities can be presumed because:
    -   [Yamdu](/yamdu) states that they are having weapon sales towards the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom).
        -   [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) and [Shek Weapon Shops](/shek-weapon-shop) in particular tend to have [Catun](/catun-scrapmaster-character)\-grade weapons
        -   [Cactus Rum](/cactus-rum) found inside [Shek Bars](/shek-bar).
        -   Caravans of TG spawns inside [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert).
