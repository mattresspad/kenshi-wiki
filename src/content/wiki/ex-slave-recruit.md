**Ex-Slave Recruit** is a randomly named [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). They are someone who was bought out of slavery. They can be hired for c.3,000.

They can be found in [Crab Town](/crab-town), [Mourn](/mourn), [The Hub](/the-hub), and, depending on certain [World States](/world-states), in [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), or [Stoat](/stoat). They might be found in [Black Scratch](/black-scratch), [Flats Lagoon](/flats-lagoon), [Smugglers Bar](/smugglers-bar), Waystation and Waystation Four, and [World's End](/worlds-end). They can also be found in locations controlled by [Deadcat](/deadcat) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants).

Their race is determined by their faction ([Drifters](/drifters)). They have an Honorable [Personality](/personality) and after recruitment will have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package.
