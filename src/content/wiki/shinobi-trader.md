**Shinobi Traders** are traders of the [Shinobi Thieves](/shinobi-thieves). They can typically be found in the [Thieves Guild](/thieves-guild) Watchtower. They only sell to the player when they ally to the Thieves by talking to the [Thief Boss](/thief-boss).

Their vendor inventory typically contains thief or ninja-related items. They also buy and sell for 50% the price. Because of this, they are a great source of [Thieves Backpacks](/thieves-backpack) and, if you need them, [Small Thieves Backpacks](/small-thieves-backpack).

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (15% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
