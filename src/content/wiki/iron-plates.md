**Iron plates** are a vital resource created at an [Iron Refinery](/iron-refinery) through the smelting of [Raw Iron](/raw-iron). Alternatively, they are also often times found in mechanical animals as loot. It is specifically stored in [Storage: Iron Plates](/storage-iron-plates).

They can be used as is in the production of weapons and construction of buildings, or further refined into [Steel Bars](/steel-bars).

Starting out, the production of iron plates must be operated by at least one worker, though through research, can eventually be turned into an automated process.

### Locations

Iron plates are extremely common loot in the Kenshi world, whether you're scavenging an [Ancient Lab](/ancient-labs), [Lost Armoury](/lost-armoury), or helping yourself to an unwary trader's stock. They can be acquired from merchants in most cities.
