**High Inquisitor Seta** is a member of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and its [Inquisition](/inquisitor), who occupies as a part of the high command for the [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix). He can be captured and turned over to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom), [United Cities](/united-cities), or [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) for a sizeable [Bounty](/bounty) of c.40,000.

He also functions as a regular [Inquisitor](/inquisitor) and players can hand in [Bounty](/bounty) to him.

He is likely to be the talented commander and governor of both **[Stack](/stack)** and **[Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth)**, responsible for keeping the southern cities in order. He also maintains an extremely important role in reinforcing the southern frontlines in defense against the [Shek](/shek) in [Stenn](/stenn-desert) and [Border Zone](/border-zone), going so far as to command a force bigger than the entirety of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> in [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) and [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf),

He is normally located in [Stack](/stack), but can also lead an attack against the player in an event called the [Wrath of God](/wrath-of-god).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (10% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Catun Scrapmaster](/catun-scrapmaster).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from the faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction. The color used is "Holy Nation Level 3"

## [World States](/world-states)

If High Inquisitor Seta is killed there are several World States that will trigger, mostly related to the weakening of The Holy Nation's southern front against the [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior)/[Kamikaze](/kamikaze) after the chain of command is disrupted and demoralization of HN's populace<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

[Stack](/stack) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) would be taken over by, or become:

-   [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive.
-   [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen) if [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is dead while [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive.
-   [United Cities](/bad-teeth/united-cities) (for [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth) only) if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are alive while [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are both killed too.
-   [Destroyed](/stack/destroyed) if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) are all dead too.

Seta's death/imprisonment can also contribute to a World State of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) taking over [Holy Farms](/holy-farms) which would include [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) and [Valtena's](/high-inquisitor-valtena) death/imprisonment while both [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) and [Longen](/longen) are alive & free. It also contributes to another World State where ST will take over [Holy Mines](/holy-mines), which would require the death of Phoenix, Esata, Flying Bull, while Tengu and Longen are alive & free (but it will switch to destroyed override soon after assuming no one is released).

If Seta, Phoenix and Valtena are all killed while [Moll](/moll) is alive and free, [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) will take over [Blister Hill](/blister-hill) and [Rebirth](/rebirth). If Moll is also killed/imprisoned, both settlements will be part of [Holy Nation Outlaws](/holy-nation-outlaws). If Seta and Valtena are either killed/imprisoned while [Moll](/moll) is alive & free, [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas) patrols would start spawning in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) region.

If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem) is alive & free while Seta is killed, [Hundred Guardians](/hundred-guardian) and [Shek Warriors](/shek-warrior) patrol will start spawning at [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) regions.

If [Ghost](/ghost) is alive & free while Seta is killed, it will cause [Berserkers](/berserkers) patrols start spawning in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) region.

If [Flying Bull](/flying-bull) is alive & free while Seta is killed it will cause Kral's Chosen patrols to start spawning in [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) regions. They will increase in frequency at [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) is also killed. If Seta, Phoenix and Valtena are all killed Kral's Chosen patrols will start spawning at [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley) and [Skimsands](/skimsands) regions.

His death/imprisonment also causes the Holy army patrols to decrease in frequency in [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf) (50%) and [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride) (16%) regions.

If Seta is killed/imprisoned alongside with Phoenix and Valtena, [Strayed Paladin](/strayed-paladin) patrols consisting of 4 to 10 people patrolling the following zones: [Bast](/bast), [Border Zone](/border-zone), [Dreg](/dreg), [Okran's Gulf](/okrans-gulf), [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), [Okran's Valley](/okrans-valley), and [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam).

## Trivia

-   The fact that his world state correlates directly with vastly increased [Berserkers](/berserkers), [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) presence would suggest that said factions have been conducting low-intensity raids throughout [Border Zone](/border-zone) and the surrounding regions, but have been almost entirely blocked off and thinned out as a result of Seta's defensive perimeters<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, causing them to become so rare that they don't even appear as homeless spawns.
    -   This is also further supported by various lines in the game<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, suggesting Holy Army forces having retreated from frontier positions amidst the confusion.
    -   This is also supported by the fact that even when [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) is clearly launching assaults into [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation) and depopulating themselves<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> as a result of [Seto's](/seto) death/imprisonemnt<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Seta's status would still prevent significant portions of Shek Kingdom patrols from spawning, indicative that he is blocking many raider forces off.
-   Despite being at war with the UC who produces [Catun](/catun-scrapmasters)\-grade weapons, Seta appears to have a Catun-grade [Paladin's Cross](/paladins-cross) (which may make sense if they traded it through [Traders Guild](/traders-guild), which unlike UC is neutral towards HN).
-   Seta is marked with significantly less (4) faction importance in the FCS than the other High Inquisitor, (50) Valtena.
    -   Ironically, he is marked with less (4) faction importance than even a standard (5) [Inquisitor](/inquisitor).
-   Seta has a significant number of forces under his command, capable of completely blocking off [Kral's Chosen](/krals-chosen), [Berserkers](/berserkers), [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), and around 3/5th of the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) forces in [Okran's Pride](/okrans-pride), with the rest being blocked by [Phoenix's](/holy-lord-phoenix) forces.
    -   The forces and talent of Seta is so great that even if [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) (and to a lesser extent [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena)) are both dead, he is still capable of holding off the pressure from the onslaught of [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom) by preventing their takeover/overrun of [Stack](/stack) and [Bad Teeth](/bad-teeth), even if they [completely depopulate themselves](/kamikaze) in doing so.
-   Seta has the smallest amount of army force tied under his command in terms of homeless spawns, roughly 10% of all HN army, compared to 30% of [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and 60% of [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix).
