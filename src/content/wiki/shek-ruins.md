**Shek Ruins** is a group of destroyed buildings owned by the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). It can be easily accessed on top of a rocky hill east of [Admag](/admag), towards the direction of [Squin](/squin). Various items are strewn about the entire place which consists of 2 [Watchtower](/watchtower), 1 [Snailhouse](/snailhouse), 1 [L-House](/l-house), 1 [Bughouse](/bughouse), 1 [Y-House](/y-house).

From the looks of the ruins, the place may have served as a border outpost during the Shek Kingdom's more prosperous past, perhaps destroyed in their war with [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation).
