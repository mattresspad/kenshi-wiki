**King Shager's Reign** is a [book](/lore-books) [item](/items) related to the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). This item is 'unique' to Shek HQ in [Admag](/admag), as well as a few Shek HQ overrides. However, it can also be found without the 'unique' label in a couple locations.

This book details how [Shek Kingdom's](/shek-kingdom) raiders<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> - after invading the territory of [The Holy Nation](/the-holy-nation)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> - were defeated since the latter launched their [military campaign](/the-holy-crusade). Shager, rather than choosing to withhold his forces to himself after this pushback, decided to double down on their futile assaults instead.

Places which might stock this item:

-   [The Great Library](/the-great-library)
-   Shek residential
-   Shek storage

There's another book on King Shager called "[Shager's Overthrow](/shagers-overthrow)".
