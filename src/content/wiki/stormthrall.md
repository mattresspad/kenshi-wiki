**Stormthralls** are the guards of [Cat-Lon's Exile](/cat-lons-exile). They live in a separate Dome than [Mad Cat-Lon](/mad-cat-lon), but after Cat-Lon is finished talking, he calls for them to swarm his Dome.

There are 120 Stormthralls at Cat-Lon's Exile. They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (50% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient) or [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths).

Stormthralls can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
