**Skeleton Bandits** is a faction native to the southeast corner of the continent. They primarily consist of [Greenlanders](/greenlander) wearing [Skeleton Masks](/skeleton-mask). They are led by [Elder](/elder), a [P4 Unit](/p4-unit) who enjoys watching humans massacre each other. Elder has brainwashed his human followers into believing that they are [Skeletons](/skeleton).

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.

-   [Elder](/elder)
-   [Upgraded Skeleton Guard](/upgraded-skeleton-guard)

-   [Skeleton Bandit](/skeleton-bandit)

\*These characters do not spawn naturally. They may only spawn when certain [World States](/world-states) are achieved or during [Events](/events).

_The reasons for this faction to have a non-zero relation towards you._

-   Players can ally with this faction by talking to Elder.
-   Players will become enemies with this faction if they ally with [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) or [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).

## Trivia

-   Both **Skeleton Bandits** and [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) have neutral relations with each other however they will still fight. They also share the same (100) opinion for [Skeleton Legion](/skeleton-legion). However, the Legion still tends to attack both factions.
-   Skeleton Bandits won't scream in pain when placed in a [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine).
-   Skeleton Bandits will sometimes say "Resistance is futile". This is an Easter egg quote from the Borg in Star Trek.
