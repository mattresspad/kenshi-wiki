**Western Hive Soldier Drones** are a [Race](/races) in Kenshi. They are a subrace of the Western [Hive](/hive).

Hive Soldier Drones don't make great outpost builders because of their many skill penalties. Their inability to wear helmets can make them a bad choice late game, as Soldier Drones have no way to protect themselves from the Duststorm or Gas [Weather Effects](/weather-effects) aside from the meagre 20% protection from [Swamp Ninja Rags](/swamp-ninja-rags).

## Trivia

-   Of all Hiver breeds, the Soldier Drones have the lowest mental faculties.
-   They rarely separate from their Hives, as they are content in defending them.
-   As healrate is % hp healed over time, Soldier Drones heal twice as fast in their head when compared to their other organic parts.
