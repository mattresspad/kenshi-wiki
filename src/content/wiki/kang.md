**Kang** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits) found in the [Shek Kingdom](/shek-kingdom). He spawns in [Admag](/admag), [Squin](/squin) or [Last Stand](/last-stand). He has a Maniacal [Personality](/personality).

He can be hired for c.6000 and should anyone bother you, he will "rip out their spines." Aside from his recruitment dialogue, he has nothing unique to say.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Ancient](/ancient).
