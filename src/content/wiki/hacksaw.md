**Hacksaw** is an [item](/items) which can be used to cut [Prisoner Shackles](/prisoner-shackles) and [Hive Prisoner Shackles](/hive-prisoner-shackles). It can also be used to cut open other locks as well. As a Hacksaw weighs only 1kg (Compared to [Tools](/tools) weighing 3kg) and performs the same job as Tools, they are superior.
