**Ninja Raid** is an [Event](/events) that can trigger if the [Player](/nameless) has a [Base](/guide-to-building-an-outpost) within 5,000 meters of the [Cactus Den](/cactus-den). It has a 15% chance to trigger each day if within 3,500 meters, this scales down with distance until it reaches a 0% chance at 5,000 meters. The event cannot trigger if the [Sand Ninja Oni](/sand-ninja-oni) is killed or captured, or if the player is allied with the [United Cities](/united-cities) or [Traders Guild](/traders-guild).

Once the conditions are fulfilled, the [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninjas) will send an assault to the player's base. This assault will consist of 3-5 squads, each made up of 3-5 [Sand Ninjas](/sand-ninja).

Once it arrives, the leader will give a short speech, then order the assault to begin, breaking down gates, attacking characters, and freeing prisoners.
