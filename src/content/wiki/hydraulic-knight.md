**Hydraulic Knights** are members of the [Second Empire](/second-empire) faction. 5 of them can be found in [Ashland Dome III](/ashland-dome-iii).

They wear \[Specialist\] armour (15% chance of \[Masterwork\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Skeleton Smiths](/skeleton-smiths) and [Edgewalkers](/edgewalkers).

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached.
