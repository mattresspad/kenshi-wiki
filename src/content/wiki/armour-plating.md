**Armour Plating** is the primary component in producing [armour](/armour). This item is specifically stored in [Storage: Armour Plate](/storage-armour-plate) and only used in crafting armour in the [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy). Armour Plating can be produced using a worker's armour smithing skill at the [Plate Beating Station](/plate-beating-station).
