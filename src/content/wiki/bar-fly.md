## Interactions

She can be found lamenting their purchase of [Ray](/ray) and considering making goat-feed out of him due to his uselessness. Not only does the slave have no tongue and thus cannot speak, they are also stubborn, lazy and feisty. The player can offer c.1000 to take Ray out of Bar fly's hands and gain them as a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). Two or more bar flies may spawn spontaneously in different locations, and the dialogue of selling Ray may still be available on the other bar flies even if you have already bought Ray, albeit there won't be another Ray for sale as Ray only spawn once in each playthrough.

Killing bar fly makes it impossible to recruit Ray. Recruiting Ray lowers the player's relations with the [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) by 5.
