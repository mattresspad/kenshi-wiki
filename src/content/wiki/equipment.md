Equipment is a set of [items](/items) equipped to your character. It includes [Armour](/armour), [Clothing](/clothing), [Weapons](/weapons), [Backpacks](/backpacks), and [Lanterns](/lanterns).

The effect equipment has on Combat, [Athletics](/athletics) and [Stealth](/stealth) is determined by which class of [Armour](/armour) it is, or how large and bulky a [Backpack](/backpacks) is.

## Armour

### Armour

[Armour](/armour) is clothing which provides armour protection in battle. Heavier armour provides more protection, however it also detracts from other [Statistics](/statistics), such as run speed and stealth. Armour can be purchased from stores, stolen, or found in ancient locations such as [Lost Armouries](/lost-armoury) and [Ancient Labs](/ancient-labs). After some researching, armour can be created at a [Leather Armour Crafting Bench](/leather-armour-crafting-bench), [Chain Armour Crafting Bench](/chain-armour-crafting-bench), or [Heavy Armour Smithy](/heavy-armour-smithy). The quality of [Homemade](/homemade) armour is exclusively dependent on the character's [Armour Smith](/armour-smith) level.

### Clothing

[Clothing](/clothing), items which are primarily made of [cloth](/fabrics), provide almost no armour protection. However, they can be helpful for protection from [Weather Effects](/weather-effects). Some clothing items may boost a character's [Martial Arts](/martial-arts) stat or provide protection for their fists. After researching the technology, clothing can be created with the [Clothing Bench](/clothing-bench).

## Weapons

[Weapons](/weapons) are the most essential item for your character to have equipped. Every weapon class also has a [skill](/statistics) which is only leveled up while fighting with that class of weapon. When characters are engaged in fights without a weapon, they must rely on their [Martial Arts](/martial-arts) and [Toughness](/toughness) to stay alive.

Weapons can be found on almost all NPCs (non-player characters), excluding enslaved characters and the [Anti Slavers](/anti-slavers). Weapons can be found in some ruins (EX: [Shek Ruins](/shek-ruins)) and [Lost Armouries](/lost-armoury). Weapons can be sold to any shop ([Stealth](/stealth) may be necessary for stolen items), not just a store which sells weapons.

Weapons and their blueprints can be purchased from [Weapons Shops](/weapons-shops). After researching the technology, weapons can be created at a [Weapon Smithing Bench](/weapon-smithing-bench). The quality of [Homemade](/homemade) weapons is determined by the grades researched, the bench used and the [Weapon Smith](/weapon-smith-skill) level of the character creating them.

## Miscellaneous Equipment

### Backpacks

[Backpacks](/backpacks) can be equipped by characters and livestock. Some backpacks allow for items to be stacked while inside them, noticeably increasing the number of items which one character can hold. Most backpacks severely damage a player's [Stealth](/stealth) stat, except for Thieves Backpacks.

### Lanterns

[Lanterns](/lanterns) can be equipped in the Belt slot, helping players see in the dark. Vendors claim that they are necessary when entering the [Fog Islands](/fog-islands).
