The **Crab Queen** is the leader of the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders). She can be found in the [Palace of the Crab Queen](/palace-of-the-crab-queen) in [Crab Town](/crab-town). She has a bounty of c.40,000 from the [United Cities](/united-cities), despite not having a bounty poster for her unlike the other pit gang leaders.

This character can colour armour and clothing due to a [colour scheme](/colour-scheme) attached. The colour is different from her faction's color as it is attached to this character rather than the faction.

## Interactions

The **Crab Queen** can be spoken to in order to secure an alliance with the Crab Raiders. This is done by declaring a positive opinion of [Crabs](/crabs) when prompted.

Saying that [Crabs](/crabs) are dumb, or that you like eating them will lock you out of an alliance though you can still become allied through the [Crab Tournament](/crab-tournament).

## [World States](/world-states)

If the Crab Queen is killed or imprisoned, Crab Town falls to the [Fishmen](/fishmen).

[Crab Village](/crab-village) falls to the [Reavers](/reavers) if [Valamon](/valamon) is alive, or [ruined](/crab-village/destroyed) otherwise.

Crab Raider patrols will cease to spawn in [Sniper Valley](/sniper-valley) and breakdown in [Greenbeach](/greenbeach), [Stobe's Garden](/stobes-garden), [The Crags](/the-crags), [The Pits East](/the-pits-east).

Patrols of Crab Raiders consisting of 8-14 [Crab Raiders](/crab-raider) and 4-10 [Crabs](/crab) and a separate scouting force of 1-2 Crab Raiders and a Crab stop spawning, being replaced by a broken, malnourished squad of 1 Crab Raider with 3-5 slightly younger Crabs. The [Crab Tournaments](/crab-tournament) and the [Crabbed Ones](/crabbed-ones) events will also cease to trigger.

(Requires Crab Queen to be alive)

If [Valamon](/valamon) is killed, [Ark](/ark) becomes overtaken by the [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) if the **Crab Queen** is alive.

If [Tsugi](/lady-tsugi) is also killed, [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) will start spawning in [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

If [Yoshinaga](/lord-yoshinaga) is also killed, [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) will start spawning in [Heng](/heng-zone).
