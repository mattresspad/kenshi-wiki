**Crabs** is an animal faction. This faction will not appear on the factions list as it is "not real".

Crabs and [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) are in mutual alliance, however player allied with [Crab Raiders](/crab-raiders) is free to defend themselves from them without compromising alliance
