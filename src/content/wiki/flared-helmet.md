The **Flared** **Helmet** is a Medium Armour that is equipped in the Headgear slot. It can be purchased from Holy Nation Hat traders and UC Armour shops.

It is the uniform headgear of [Samurai Conscripts](/samurai-conscript). It can also be randomly worn by [Drifters](/drifter), [Strayed Paladins](/strayed-paladin), [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunter), [Tech Hunter Gate Guards](/tech-hunter-gate-guard), [Tech Hunter Guards](/tech-hunter-guard), and [Tech Hunter Ruins](/tech-hunter-ruins).

This armour has inventory dimensions of **3x3**.

## Appearance

The Flared Helmet is made up of several metal plates layered on top of one another to form the helmet. The helmet has an open face and a wide base.
