**Longen** is the leader of the [Traders Guild](/traders-guild) and has [Diplomatic Status](/diplomatic-status). He resides in the Faction HQ in [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge), where he usually spends time in his throne room. He ostensibly manages much of the [slavery](/slavery)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and [trading activities](/caravan-trader-boss)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> throughout the [United Cities](/united-cities).

Longen has his own [noble squire supreme](/yamdu), as well as many powerful slave-owning [n](/lady-kana)[o](/slave-mistress-grace)[b](/slave-mistress-ren)[l](/slave-master-grande)[e](/slave-master-haga)[s](/slave-master-ruben) who are apparently sworn to him rather than [Emperor Tengu](/emperor-tengu) as their immediate overlord. Aside from limited competition from the [Free Traders](/free-traders), Longen is the undisputed king of commerce in the Empire.

He has the Smart [Personality](/personality).

Characters can speak with Longen and hear him complain that not one of his "so-called guards" has put an end to [Tinfist](/tinfist)'s insanity, provided that the player hasn't already gotten rid of Tinfist or [Tengu](/emperor-tengu), and is not allied to [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) or enemies of [Slave Traders](/slave-traders).

If Tinfist has already been defeated, Longen might award the player instead.

They wear \[Masterwork\] armour and have weapons manufactured by [Cross](/cross).

## [World States](/world-states)

(Requires Longen dead)

When Longen is killed, several World States will trigger. Mostly related with the devastation of Traders Guild, slavery, and supply lines across the desert.

[Starving Vagrants](/starving-vagrant) ([Heng](/heng), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert)) and TG [Caravans](/caravan-trader-boss) ([Heng](/heng), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast), [The Hook](/the-hook), [Stenn Desert](/stenn-desert)) would cease to spawn.

If [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive, it will cause Anti-Slavers patrols to start spawning in [Heng](/heng-zone) and [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert).

Trader's Edge would fall into ruins, if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is also killed, it will fall into [Anti-Slavers](/anti-slavers) control if [Tinfist](/tinfist) is alive and the [Free Settlement](/free-settlement) will turn into [The Free City](/the-free-city). If Tinfist is not alive, [Trader's Edge](/traders-edge) will be handed over to Empire Peasants.

[Heng](/heng) would become half-destroyed or handed over to Empire Peasants if [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is also killed, with two different overrides dependent on [Tinfist's](/tinfist) status.

If [Wada](/slave-master-wada) is also killed, Heft will become malnourished if it's under UC rule.

[Slave Traders](/slave-traders) patrols would become less frequent in [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Heng](/heng) and [Grey Desert](/grey-desert).

If Longen or Lady Kana are either imprisoned or killed, the [Traders Guild Visit](/traders-guild-visit) should no longer trigger.

**Longen** is considered to be one of the Northern Slave Masters/Mistresses alongside with [Kana](/lady-kana), [Ren](/slave-mistress-ren), [Grande](/slave-master-grande), [Haga](/slave-master-haga), [Wada](/slave-master-wada) and [Ruben](/slave-master-ruben). Killing them will further decrease [Slave Hunter](/slave-hunter), [Manhunter](/manhunter) and [Slave Traders](/slave-traders) patrols, reducing to none when all of them are killed.

(Requires Longen to be alive)

If [Tinfist](/tinfist) is killed, [settled nomads](/settled-nomads) will become empty.

-   If [Grande](/slave-master-grande) is alive, [Cult Village is taken by slave traders](/cult-village/slave-traders).
-   If [Tengu](/emperor-tengu) is alive, [Fishing Village is taken by slave traders](/fishing-village/slave-traders).
-   Slave Trader patrols start spawning in [Sinkuun](/sinkuun), [Northern Coast](/northern-coast), [Skimsands](/skimsands), [Skinner's Roam](/skinners-roam), [Shem](/shem), [The Outlands](/the-outlands).
-   Slave Trader patrols increase in frequency in [Heng](/heng-zone), [The Great Desert](/the-great-desert), [Grey Desert](/grey-desert), [Stormgap Coast](/stormgap-coast).

If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix) are all killed while Tengu is alive, [Holy Mines get taken by Slave Traders](/holy-mines/slave-traders).

If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Flying Bull](/flying-bull), [Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) and [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) are all killed while Tengu is alive, [Bad Teeth gets taken by United Cities](/bad-teeth/united-cities).

If [Esata](/esata-the-stone-golem), [Valtena](/high-inquisitor-valtena) and [Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix)/[Seta](/high-inquisitor-seta) are all killed while Tengu is alive, [Holy Farms get taken by Slave Traders](/holy-farm/slave-traders) (can switch to [Destroyed](/holy-mines/destroyed) override later).

## Trivia

-   If Longen is brought in front of [Holy Lord Phoenix](/holy-lord-phoenix), he will appear to be dismayed by your action and tell you to leave, stating that he wants "No part in your war".
-   If Longen is taken to [Moll](/moll) the leader of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), She will appear confused and distressed by your action. She will then tell you "We have enough on our plate right now" and tell you take him somewhere else.
-   Longen has an exceptional ability at recognizing escaped slaves/ex-slaves, even the ones in a disguise.
