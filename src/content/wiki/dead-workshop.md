_This article is about the Dead Workshop. For the Deadland Workshop in Deadlands, see [Deadland Workshop](/deadland-workshop)._

<figure class="quote"><blockquote>Old ruins. You gotta look at a map, go way out into the furthest reaches of the ass end of nowhere, the more dangerous the better, and then look around for old ruins and stuff.</blockquote><figcaption>Ronin Construction Trader</figcaption></figure>

**Dead Workshop** is an [Ancient Location](/ancient-locations) in The Pits East. This workshop is a large, hangar-like structure.

This location is one of the few reliable places to obtain [Engineering Research](/engineering-research). Similar locations are the [Deadland Workshops](/deadland-workshop), [Post-Ancient Workshop](/post-ancient-workshop), and [Workshop Complex](/workshop-complex). All of them can be marked by reading the **Engineers [Map](/maps)**.

There is a deep square pool in front of the workshop. It is big enough to be visible on the map but is partially covered by the workshop's icon.

## Dangers

-   This location can spawn 0 to 10 units named [Soldierbot](/soldierbot-character).
    -   Although their name is Soldierbot they are actually [Screamer MKI](/screamer-mki)s.

## Item Spawns

This location has a gear artifacts value of c.25,000 and an item artifacts value of c.10,000, meaning it will spawn additional items through the [Artifacts](/artifacts) system.
