**Skinhouse HQ** is the headquarters of the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits) faction. [Savant](/savant), leader of the Skin Bandits, can be found here.

[Humans](/human) seen here are at risk of being attacked and skinned alive in a [Peeler Machine](/peeler-machine). Skeletons, Shek and Hivers are safe to be seen and can even speak with Savant. Only Skeletons however can successfully ask to join. Although Sheks and Hivers will not be attacked by the Skin Bandits who reside in the Skinhouse HQ via dialogue, if they are knocked unconscious or are foolish enough to try to use one of the repair beds inside, they will be quickly picked up and placed into a Peeler Machine, just like a human would be.

## Trivia

-   The "destroyed" overrides of [Skinhouses](/skinhouse) and the **Skinhouse HQ** are the only [FCS](/forgotten-construction-set) items of type "Town" in use which are not assigned to any faction. In practice, though, the game treats them as if they still belonged to the [Skin Bandits](/skin-bandits).
