The **P4 Unit** is a subrace variant of a [Skeleton](/skeleton). Presently, it is impossible to create a P4 Unit player character at the beginning of an unmodded game. Through random low chance players can still recruit one, as they can potentially spawn as a [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). The subrace's unique feature is the reduced 150 head HP (compared to 200 HP for the other playable Skeletons) as well as a Healrate of 3.0.

Due to their increased experience rates, they are useful for crafting jobs.

The game also features the even more improved [P4 Mark II Units](/skeleton-p4mkii), a presumed further improvement of the P4 Unit line.

## Experience Multipliers

All playable races have a distinct set of [Statistics](/statistics) which they can gain experience at an increased or decreased rate. Players should pay attention to these experience multipliers and use them to their advantage.

### Increased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly faster rate than normal. This increased rate is +20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Armour Smith](/armour-smith)
-   [Engineer](/engineer)
-   [Field Medic](/field-medic)
-   [Robotics](/robotics)
-   [Science](/science)
-   [Weapon Smith](/weapon-smith)

### Decreased Rate

_Characters of this race gain experience in these Statistics at a slightly slower rate than normal. This decreased rate is -20% unless otherwise noted._

-   [Dodge](/dodge)
-   [Katanas](/katanas)
-   [Stealth](/stealth)
-   [Thievery](/thievery)

## Characters

### Game Starts

Players cannot choose to start as a member of this race in any [Game Starts](/game-starts).

### Unique Recruits

There are no [Unique Recruits](/unique-recruits) which can spawn as a member of this race.

### Generic Recruits

[Reportedly](https://www.reddit.com/r/Kenshi/comments/1lm2omn/kenshi_fact_of_the_day_192/), it is possible to get a P4 recruit named Zill by setting squad size to 3 and then loading in Heft and Heng (this needs to be the first time entering these towns, otherwise it likely won't work).

## Trivia

-   Many high ranking skeletons found throughout the game are the P4 Units. This fact, their increased science and crafting skills, as well as their more delicate heads can lead to a speculation that this subrace of Skeletons was designed to be leaders and commanders.
