**Pia** is a [Unique Recruit](/unique-recruits). She is a fast runner. Her sister Naevia was also a member of the [Flotsam Ninjas](/flotsam-ninjas), but recently "Died doing what she loved doing, fighting the Paladins".

She can be found in [Flotsam Village](/flotsam-village) and is recruitable for the cost of c.3,000. She has the Dumb personality.

Like [Miu](/miu) who spots spiders, Pia is always on alert for Holy Paladins, acting as a useful radar for the group. She is the fastest paid recruit ([Bo](/bo) is faster but requires much more to recruit and is free) having a massive 50 Athletics along with a modest 20 Strength/Dexterity.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]) and have weapons manufactured by [Unknown](/unknown).
