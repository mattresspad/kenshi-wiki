A **Megacrab Ganglion** is a unique item for which the only use is to sell. 5 can be found on the bodies of [Megacrab](/megacrab), [Barnabus](/barnabus), [Mr Gigantus](/mr-gigantus), and [The Crabinator](/the-crabinator)

The Megacrab is only found in the [Howler Maze](/howler-maze) and it may not be worth the effort to go there for only this item, especially since the only notable locations in that area are the [Lost Libraries](/lost-library).

If players are after money, it may be easier to simply raid [Nest of Beak Things](/nest-of-beak-things) for [Beak Thing Eggs](/beak-thing-egg), since those are not unique and sell fairly well.
