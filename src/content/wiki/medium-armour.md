Medium Armour is [Armour](/armour) which provides more protection than [Light Armour](/light-armour) and less than [Heavy Armour](/heavy-armour), as well as a generally moderate stats debuff.

The following values are the stats of the armour at \[Standard\] quality.

## Legwear

There are four Medium Legwear options. Armoured Rag Skirts can be found on a [Dust Bandits](/dust-bandits), and some members of the [Western Hive](/western-hive). Armoured Rag Skirts (colored) can be found on [Red Sabres](/red-sabres) (Red), [Berserkers](/berserkers) (Red), and soldiers and princes of the [Southern Hive](/southern-hive) (Blueish Purple). Hack Stopper Pants can be found on [Reavers](/reavers) and Plated Drifter's Leather Pants can be found on drifters.
