**Gunner Recruit** is a randomly named [Generic Recruit](/generic-recruits). They are a ranged combat specialist. They can be hired for c.6,000, unless they are [Hive](/hive), then they can be hired for c.4,000.

They can be found in [Eyesocket](/eyesocket) and [Slave Markets](/slave-markets). They have a chance of spawning in any town controlled by the [United Cities](/united-cities), including when the location is "malnourished". They can be found in [Crab Town](/crab-town), [Mourn](/mourn), [The Hub](/the-hub), Waystation and Waystation Four, and, depending on certain [World States](/world-states), in [Bark](/bark), [Blister Hill](/blister-hill), [Clownsteady](/clownsteady), [Heft](/heft), [Heng](/heng), [Sho-Battai](/sho-battai), or [Stoat](/stoat). They can also be found in locations controlled by [Deadcat](/deadcat) or [Empire Peasants](/empire-peasants). They can be found in [Shark](/shark), no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps.

Their race is determined by their faction ([Drifters](/drifters)). They have a random [Personality](/personality) and after recruited have the "Player TOUGH Merc" Dialogue Package. Although this character can potentially spawn as a [Hive Soldier Drone](/hive-soldier-drone), [Hive Worker Drone](/hive-worker-drone), or [P4 Unit](/p4-unit), due to conditions on recruitment dialogue, they can only be recruited if they are of those races if they also have a Smart Personality.

They wear \[Shoddy\] armour (5% chance of \[Standard\]).
