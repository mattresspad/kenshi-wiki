**Tech Hunter Shop** is a shop which sells travel supplies and some light/medium armour pieces. It is controlled by the [Tech Hunters](/tech-hunters). The shop inventory is similar to the [Travel Gear](/travel-gear) vendor, additionally selling some clothes, [long bolts](/bolts-longs), and more [blueprints](/blueprints).

### Locations

-   [Black Scratch](/black-scratch)
-   [Mourn](/mourn)
-   [Spring](/spring)

Depending on [World States](/world-states), this vendor might spawn in these locations:

-   [Stoat](/stoat) (Tech Hunters)
-   [The Free City](/the-free-city)

### Residents

-   [Tech Hunter Shopkeeper](/tech-hunter-shopkeeper)
-   [Tech Hunter Guard](/tech-hunter-guard) (3)
