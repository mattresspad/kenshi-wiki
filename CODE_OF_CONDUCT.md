# Kenshi Wiki code of conduct

A one-line correction is welcome here. Treat other editors with respect, even when your experiences in the game differ. Describe what you observed, ask for a source when you need one, and keep criticism focused on the edit. Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing someone’s private information are grounds for a maintainer to remove a comment or restrict participation. You can use GitHub’s report menu on an issue or comment to flag abuse.

## Give credit where it belongs

Lo-Fi Games created Kenshi. This community wiki also draws on the work of players, mod authors, artists, and earlier wiki editors. Use the names creators publish under and credit the source when you build on their work.

By submitting article text, you agree to publish it under the terms for that article. Original wiki text uses CC BY-SA 4.0. Articles adapted from the Kenshi Fandom wiki use CC BY-SA 3.0. Preserve their revision and author credits. The [licensing notes](https://kenshi.wiki/contributors#licensing) explain the terms for each kind of material.

Before uploading or embedding an image or other asset, check that you can share it here and include its creator, source, and reuse terms in the [artwork inventory](public/notices/artwork.txt). If permission is unclear, link readers to the creator’s own page. A credit alone does not grant permission to copy a file.

## Make a contribution

Use the [article correction form](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/issues/new?template=article-correction.yml) to tell us what to fix, or the [website form](https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/issues/new?template=website.yml) for problems with the site. To propose wording yourself, follow the [browser editing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md). For a security concern, use the [private reporting instructions](SECURITY.md).
