Kenshi Wiki restoration artwork

The sun, moon, pulse, clock, house, die and contributors glyphs are original wiki line drawings. WebFCS's clipboard and magnifier were drawn for the wiki.
https://github.com/mattresspad/kenshi-wiki/blob/0c9b4348592605169315b6dd613dde1c220d1237/src/components/UiIcon.astro

Meitou's two-moon favicon derivative was selected by the project's creator for its link. A general redistribution license was not supplied.
https://meitou.kenshi.wiki/

The Map and KenshiDB marks came through WebFCS's map-icon.svg and database-icon.svg, which identify SVG Repo as the source. Individual artists, item URLs and terms remain unconfirmed.

The platform marks are 48-pixel derivatives from Prd's wiki resource directory. The marks belong to their respective platforms. The frozen inventory records the filenames and provenance; per-file redistribution grants are unverified.
