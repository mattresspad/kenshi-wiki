## Open question

The first Trivia entry suggests that King Gurgler’s death affects Fishmen numbers and that Catun can still fall regardless of his survival. With Lord Shiro’s status held fixed, does changing King Gurgler’s state affect the selection of Catun’s takeover record, the number of resident squads, or either outcome after reload? Compare the FCS conditions or matched saves before publishing that claim. The reader-facing article carries the Shiro trigger. Fandom revision 93167, Trivia · Recorded import, first Trivia entry.
