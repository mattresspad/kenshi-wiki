# Pages needing work

This generated contributor view uses the permanent article catalog, conversion/reviews.json, curated gap findings, extracted source occurrences and accepted resolution history. It is not another editable status database. Source comparisons and the rendered-output gap audit remain the acceptance authority.

A transferred and format-checked article without editorial evidence is Awaiting editorial review. An article with recorded inspection and unresolved findings is Reviewed, with open findings. Specific restoration work accompanies either state whenever actionable findings remain. Imported WIP, Article stubs and other categories are unchanged. A completed review may retain open questions.

The build resolves route, fragment and media availability from its inputs; the post-build gap audit uses the same resolver against rendered output and requires an identical public projection. Review coverage and task closures remain separate. Historical closure evidence is retained. No previous reports or comparison checkout are needed for a normal build.

The landing page is /pages-needing-work. Static current-work and history lists contain at most 50 article entries per page. Filters progressively enhance the ordinary links using a compact index. Every article note targets the correct static page and anchor. Pagination and history routes are noindex and omitted from the sitemap; the landing page appears once in the title index and sitemap. All maintenance routes are reserved separately from the immutable source inventory.

Local previews show selectable editable file paths; publication uses the configured branch and current file. Evidence remains pinned to original source revisions. Public entries show concise occurrence excerpts; every literal occurrence and investigation note remains in the underlying ledger. The full investigative report remains available locally at reports/gaps/.

Faithful transfer and format acceptance are required for restoration. Outstanding editorial review and documented inherited gaps can continue after deployment. Introduced conversion failures, unsupported closure claims, route dispositions, temporary prototype dependencies and separate release approvals remain gates.
